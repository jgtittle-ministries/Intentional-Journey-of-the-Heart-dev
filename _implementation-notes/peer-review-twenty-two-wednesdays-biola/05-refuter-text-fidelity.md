### L2 — The promotion rule and the missing-data rule are mutually undefined. HIGH → **UPHELD**

The cleanest finding in the three reviews. Both quotations are verbatim; the text genuinely never says whether a

### L3 — The design predicts full forms of "response to load" but schedules no load; no lament. HIGH → **NARROWED**

Verified: **lament = 0, grief = 0, suffering = 0**; doubt is scheduled (Table 1, weeks 7–11) and suffering is not;
the attrition concession is quoted verbatim.

*Refuted by Table 2's own occasion cell.* "The design has therefore scheduled **zero occasions** for the full form
of one of its three signs." Table 2 gives the occasion for response to load as "**Every opening**; the three breaks
are its scheduled load tests." The full form's occasion is every opening — the room's first ten seconds — not the
breaks. The finding's real point is that the design manufactures no adversity, which is a different claim.

*Construct misquoted by omission.* "Adversity is what the construct names." The construct is stated as "confidence
in God under the load of **ordinary weeks**," and the growing form is "a late arrival or an awkward moment taken in
stride." The design's own load concept is ordinary-life load, not crisis.

*Reviewer-supplied premise.* "A family hit by a serious loss in February is, **in my experience**, the family most
likely to disappear." Offered as experience, and the selection argument stands on it.

**Surviving claim.** The manuscript contains no lament practice and no occurrence of lament, grief or suffering,
so a participant whose year turns hard has no socially sanctioned form to put it in and the design reads their
departure only as absence.

### L4 — Three marks and none for desolation. HIGH → **NARROWED**

The theological point is right and the remedy (a second face on the top rung — "telling that God has been silent
and that one kept practicing anyway") is the single best constraint-respecting suggestion in the three reviews.
Two arms of the argument fail on the text.

*Refuted by the marking rule.* "They will be marked '**not yet**,' every week, for a year — the identical mark
given to a person who is indifferent." The marks are not applied to persons: "The team reads **the rung the room
is on**, marks each sign seen, not yet, or no occasion." The finding's own Finding 5 says so ("every reading that
matters is a property of the room"). No participant is scored at the floor of anything, because no participant is
scored.

*Refuted by the growing form.* "It has provided nothing for the person who did not stop and received silence."
The growing form of costly telling is "**Offering: something brought unprompted**, and an honest report of a
lapsed practice is telling with a small price" — the first clause is content-agnostic and covers a report of
silence, and the narrated re-entry credits precisely that kind of report as "an offering-level tell, and the team
marks the sign **seen**."

**Surviving claim.** The full form of costly telling is worded so that only consolation can satisfy it ("Telling
what God said and what obeying it cost"), and no mark distinguishes flatness under desolation from flatness under
indifference.

### L5 — The prototype mitigation is applied at the individual level; every reading is a property of the room. HIGH → **NARROWED**

The core is textually exact and important: "Participants who came through the prototype are marked as such in the
readings" cannot mitigate an instrument whose unit is "the rung the room is on," and the observation that row 4
makes the right move once and does not generalize it is correct. The trust-vocabulary contagion argument
(a phrase put into the room's air is no longer unprompted for anyone) is sound and is not reachable by individual
marking.

*Reviewer-supplied premise — the arithmetic.* "**If that family enrolls whole**, it is the entire minimum cohort
and forty percent of the maximum." The manuscript says "**some of them** are expected to enroll." "The entire
minimum cohort" is also loose: six people from one family cannot be the three-to-six-family minimum.

*Partly already answered.* "This family is not a participant group at all; it is a co-design team." The manuscript
says exactly this: "**That family is not outside the case**," and it discloses the previewing role in the same
paragraph.

**Surviving claim.** The prototype mark is applied to individuals while every reading is a room property, and no
analysis rule states what the mark does in five of the six disconfirmation conditions.

### L6 — Row 4: two failures to fail, possibly empty sample, sessions not on the schedule, incentive against rescue. HIGH → **NARROWED**

*(a) upheld.* "At **both** spring second runnings" — one adult takeover is a pass. Verbatim.

*(b) upheld.* The carve ("counted only where the teen had not led before"), "several of them have led before,"

*(c) narrowed.* "'Both spring second runnings' has **no referent** in Table 1." Table 1 lists three teen-led

*(d) narrowed.* Same as Porter 15: the debrief's surprise line ("what did not go as expected tonight, and what the

**Surviving claim.** Row 4 requires two failures to fail, may have an eligible sample of zero with no reporting
rule for that case, names occasions Table 1 does not locate, and states no exemption for a step-in taken for the
teenager's sake.

### L7 — Nothing in the family-facing corpus asks any participant whether they could say a true thing. MED-HIGH → **UPHELD**

what do I want to do differently." All three pulse items point the respondent at themselves; nothing anywhere asks
about the room. The private, permitted, individually filled channel exists and is unused for this purpose, and the

constraint. The split-by-generation pulse return rate is free and behavioral and is a genuinely new observation.

*Title over-states slightly.* "Adopts Edmondson's construct and rejects her level of measurement **without

### L8 — Which parent goes first is never specified and never rotated. MED-HIGH → **NARROWED**

about which parent opens. The depth-licensing theory is the paper's own ("A parent who goes first with something
true licenses a depth the teen would never volunteer cold"), so the inference that the opener sets the ceiling is
the author's, not the reviewer's. The single-attending-parent observation is grounded in the manuscript's own

*Reviewer-supplied premise.* "Where that is the father — **which in this population is the likelier default**, and

finding does not need the gendered premise: the ceiling problem holds whichever parent is the fixed opener.

**Surviving claim.** The design specifies no rotation of the opening parent and records nothing about it, so a

### L9 — Row 5 is immunized against the outcome it most needs to catch; unblinded coding. MED-HIGH → **NARROWED**

The unblinding half is upheld and is the part worth acting on: the row carries the paper's most speculative
proposal, is coded by the team that invented the house words, from its own scribe's notes, in known chronological
order, with "no blind coding, no second coder, and no inter-rater check" conceded in the limitations. The blinding
remedy (de-identify three self-read transcripts, randomize order, ask the existing outside reader to sort them)
costs two hours and touches no constraint. This is the best cost/benefit remedy in the three reviews.

*The conjunct arm is over-stated.* "The scenario in which the design has failed completely is the scenario this
condition is written to survive." Row 5 tests one specific equation — that trust-stage migration **is**
internalization at group scale. If the sign readings do not advance, a flat vocabulary is uninformative about that
equation, so the conjunct isolates the test rather than immunizing it. A globally failed year is caught by row 1,
which is what row 1 is for. Dropping the conjunct would convert row 5 into a second, weaker test of the ladder.

**Surviving claim.** Row 5's coding is unblinded, done by the proposal's own authors in their own invented
vocabulary, and can be blinded at essentially no cost using structures the design already has.

# The secular-indistinguishability convergence

**It is not a three-reviewer convergence. It is two reviewers, one textual locus, and one weaker satellite.**

*Who actually files it.* A search of all three reviews for the claim shows Coe files it twice (C1: "Every one of
the six [Table 3 rows] would be satisfied … by a competently run secular mentoring cohort"; C8: "readable in full
by someone who does not believe in God, and indistinguishable from group cohesion") and Porter once (P10: "the
readings would be identical in a well-run secular grief group, a good AA meeting, or a strong high-school drama
ensemble"). **Lewis Hall never makes the claim.** Her only use of "indistinguishable" is about a "no occasion"
week (L11), and her nearest analogue, L4, is the *converse* complaint: that the instrument cannot see a
specifically Christian state (desolation) that a secular instrument would also miss. Counting her into the
convergence would be an artifact of paraphrase.

*Are the two making the same claim?* C8 and P10 are the **same finding on the same six table cells**, differing
only in strength. Both ran the same test — search Table 2 for the word "God" — and both got the same true answer:
two of nine cells, both full forms, third sign never. That is one finding independently reached, not two.
C1's Table 3 version is a different and weaker claim about the six *thresholds*, and it does not survive at full
strength: rows 1, 3 and 5 are read on behaviors defined by domain-specific practices (the six-step Scripture
practice, the tell step, the room's own language), which a secular cohort instantiates only by substituting
different content.

*What survives the text.* The finding is real but it is narrower than any of the three statements of it, and it is
about **wording, not inference**:

> The six lower cells of Table 2 — the cells the pilot expects to spend the year marking — are worded so that a
> Companion can mark a full year without ever being required to read for the domain. That matters specifically
> because the design's calibration pages are built so that "a companion who has never read a word of the
> underlying research can still mark honestly," and because the observation protocol freezes at week 1.

*What does not survive.* Three things, and they are the parts a hostile author would attack first.
(i) "No theological content whatever" (P10) is refuted by P10's own remedy, which concedes the growing form of
costly telling "already is by design" a lapse in the practice of listening. (ii) "The readings would be identical
in an AA meeting" conflates the mark with the occasion: the marks are taken at the tell step ("each person tells
one thing they heard" from a passage walked in the room) and at a re-entry check-in about a Scripture-hearing
practice; the mark would be identical, the behavior producing it would not exist. (iii) "The design carries no
condition under which the room's outcome would look different if the Spirit were not the agent" (C1) is true of
the thresholds' wording and not of their referents.

*Verdict.* One genuine convergence, at half the reported width and a third of the reported strength — and it
lands on Table 2's cell wording, not on the design's theology, not on Table 3, and not on the paper's thesis.
Coe C8 is the version to carry forward; P10 should be folded into it rather than filed separately; C1 should be
recast as what remains of it (no scheduled dependence, no stated interpretive limit) rather than as a
secular-equivalence claim. The correct remedy is the cheap one both reviewers converge on independently:
put the domain into the wording of the first and growing rungs before the protocol freezes. That costs one page
of the pre-printed pack and no constraint.
