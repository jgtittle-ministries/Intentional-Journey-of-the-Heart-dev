# Stage C — Adversarial Refuter: Remedies, Capacity, and Double-Counting

**Role.** I file no findings. I test the HIGH and MED-HIGH findings filed by Coe, Porter, and Lewis Hall
against five questions: can the remedy be had without breaking a constraint the reviewer did not argue
against; what does the remedy cost against the design's own economics; is the finding one problem or two;
would the remedy damage the paper; and does the design already contain the remedy under another name.

**Scope tested.** 38 findings rated HIGH or MED-HIGH — Coe 1–8, Porter 1–4, 6, 8, 10–20, Lewis Hall 1–13.
They resolve to **29 distinct problems**. Nine merges and six splits are recorded below.

**The two budgets everything is spent against.**

- *Instrument budget:* constraint 5, one page per week, defended in the manuscript as "the condition of the
  instrument's survival in a real ministry." No reviewer argued against it. It therefore binds absolutely.
- *Manuscript budget:* Porter 4 requires a cut from ~10,500 words to 7,000. Every prose remedy in all three
  reviews is drawn on an account that is already ~3,500 overdrawn.

**The freeze split, which no reviewer states and which reorganizes the whole list.** The manuscript freezes
only "the signs, their rungs, the marking rule, and the thresholds." Everything else — prose, disclosure,
content layer, analysis rules — is explicitly unfrozen ("The content layer is not frozen"). So Porter's
"after week one it is a year too late" is correct for a *short* list and overstated for most of what the
three reviews demand. The pre-freeze list is given at the end. It is much cheaper than the review set as a
whole, which is the single most useful fact in this report.

### P10. Observers do not resolve the compliance/character ambiguity; absence does

**Porter 11 (main claim, HIGH) — NARROWED. Remedy VIABLE.**

- **Where it overreaches.** The sharp form — if compliance and character produce the same observable
  behavior, then observers observe the same thing in both cases — runs against the very literature Porter
  himself tells the author to promote into the body in his Finding 1: Vazire's self–other knowledge asymmetry
  says observers have access to *different* information, not merely more of the same. Porter's Finding 1 and
  Finding 11 are in tension, and the author should be told so rather than asked to satisfy both at full
  strength.
- **Where it is unanswerable, and this is the part that changes the design.** The weekly readings are taken
  inside the liturgy, in the leader's presence, on the occasions the curriculum schedules for exactly that
  behavior. "Settling: the room lands when the container opens" is, as evidence, a measurement of compliance
  with a liturgy. The three absence tests are the discriminators and the paper never elevates that to an
  inference rule.
- **Remedy 1 (state the inference rule; classify every Table 2 reading structure-present or structure-absent)
  — VIABLE.** One paragraph and one column heading. And it is the author's own discipline: the manuscript
  already does exactly this for the vocabulary ("The vocabulary corroborates; it does not decide").
