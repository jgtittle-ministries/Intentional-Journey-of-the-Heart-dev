## The design's real constraints

These are the author's settled theological commitments, not oversights. A remedy that violates one will be
struck unless you argue against the constraint explicitly and on its own ground, which you are free to do.

1. **The covenant.** Signed before any instrument runs. Mirrors never levers; no scores; names stop at the
   shepherd who knows the faces; and a personal test, "measure nothing you will not weep over."
2. **No announced targets.** No numeric outcome goal is ever stated to the room.
3. **Permission is for pages, never for care.** Every family-facing page exists only on a family's yes.
   Shepherd-side watching needs no permission.
4. **Journals are never collected.** Home practice cards are never inspected.
5. **One page per week of team paperwork.** The lightness is defended as the condition of the instrument's
   survival in a real ministry.
6. **A family is never divided.** Families share circles; parents go first in every practice.
7. **Rooms, never people.** The room's quarterly self-read assesses the group, never any individual.

## What the author has disclosed (read these carefully; they are recent and unreviewed)

- The prototype family is not outside the case: both parents and four teens ran the practices for eighteen
  months, three teens have already led sessions, some are expected to enroll, and the prototype group now
  previews each session before the cohort meets it.
- The Holy Spirit appears once, in the conclusion, as the one who forms, with the instruments reading traces.
- The content of hearing is never recorded, only that hearing was told. This is named as a deliberate loss.
