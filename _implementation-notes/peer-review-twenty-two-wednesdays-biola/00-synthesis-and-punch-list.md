## 3. The three problems that live in one table

Table 2's nine content cells carry three separate findings, and one page of the pre-printed pack fixes all three without bending a constraint.

1. **The lower rungs never require reading for the domain.** God appears in two of nine cells, both full forms, which the paper concedes will not show for months. Every reading actually taken in year one is therefore theologically silent in its wording. *(Coe and Porter, merged)*
2. **The top rung admits only consolation.** "Telling what God said and what obeying it cost" cannot be satisfied by someone who practiced faithfully and heard nothing. *(Lewis Hall, narrowed)*
3. **A sign that was seen and has ceased cannot be recorded.** The ratchet promotes and never demotes, so the instrument has no way to write down a room going backward. *(Porter, over Coe)*

**The residue of problem 1 is not repairable and should be disclosed instead:** no instrument in this design can distinguish a Spirit-wrought room from a well-facilitated one. That belongs on the cost list, not on the punch-list.

## 4. Sorted by clock, not by severity

The founder's stated plan, 2026-08-19, is that no submission happens until the companion paper is fully accepted with at least a preprint link, and that Porter is approached first with a short description of the article rather than a manuscript. That splits this punch-list in two.

### Pilot clock — these change what gets written down from Week 1

- **The Table 2 rewrites**, all three problems in section 3. Freeze-bound: the observation protocol is meant to freeze at Week 1, so deferring them to October makes the year's own series incomparable.
- **The marking rule.** Underspecified and one-directional: "three consecutive weeks of seen" is undefined against "no occasion," and there is no path down.
- **Per-row prototype rules.** Row 4 got its carve; the other five conditions got nothing, and marking a participant cannot mitigate a room-level unit.
- **Governance of named records on minors.** No ethics review, no assent statement, and no approving body named anywhere. The pilot has parent consent and CCA's child-protection policy; nothing names who approves the research use.
- **Row 3 re-based on the return-card count**, which is already collected. Only the analysis rule is new.
- **Attrition reasons in the decisions log**, nearly free, since a family meeting real adversity currently exits as unreadable attrition.

### Manuscript clock — these wait for the companion's acceptance

Genre and length, the citation status, the sanctification paragraph, the ecclesial relocation, the "unwilling to go back" sentence, and the whole of Porter's editorial apparatus.

**Note the collision that will define the eventual revision:** the three reviews demand three to four thousand new words from a manuscript Porter says must shed three and a half thousand. Every manuscript-clock item competes against every other one.

## 5. Capacity — the hard ceiling

The three reviews propose **eighteen additions to the one weekly page**, each defended as "one line," collectively roughly tripling it. Constraint 5, one page per week, defended in the manuscript as the instrument's survival condition, binds — because no reviewer argued against it.

Also proposed: Table 3 from six rows to eleven with four rewritten; the permission conversation from one ask to five asks plus two disclaimers; the Thursday call's four moves asked to carry six.

**Take whole (near-free, and where June's evidence comes from):** the Table 2 rewrites · the second face on the top rung · severing the "unwilling to go back" identification · disambiguating row 1 · rebuilding row 4 · deleting row 5's conjunct and restating row 6 · re-basing row 3 on the return-card count · pulse return rate by generation · per-row prototype rules · settle time · a founder-absence row · all pure disclosure.

**Take three or four:** the *borne* mark bundle · a harm condition · a blinded sort · the attrition log · a lament week · parent rotation · the pneumatology and sanctification paragraphs · stray-as-census.

**Dropped if everything above is adopted:** the fourth vital sign, which is the most expensive proposal in the set; the two routed pulse items; the dependence line; the sin and forgiveness marker; the repair-liturgy sign; the two-meanings mark; the counter-signature rule; and the "none" threshold row.

## 6. Remedies struck

- **The outside reader adjudicating de-identified profile pages.** Exports minors' records in a study with no ethics review, contradicts the covenant, and contradicts its own author's other finding.
- **A disconfirmation condition a purely social account would not predict.** Unbuildable; it would produce a fake discriminator.
- **Renaming the construct horizontally.** Concedes the thesis in exchange for honesty available more cheaply.
- **Damaging as written:** a demotion rule taken alone, which manufactures the harm the desolation mark exists to prevent — the three remedies are a bundle; unqualified public weighing of a minor's hearing; and saying the hope aloud, which would seed the very vocabulary the design samples unprompted.

## 7. The cheapest honesty available

The manuscript's cost list has five items. Four more belong on it: the irreducible residue of problem 1, the spouse case in a family where one parent will not go first, the shepherding that preempts the third sign's own occasions, and the theological cost of the single-domain restriction. **Going from five costs to nine is the least expensive and most credible move in this entire punch-list.**
