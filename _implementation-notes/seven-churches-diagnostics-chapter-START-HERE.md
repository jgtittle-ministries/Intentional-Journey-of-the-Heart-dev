## The chapter outline (as designed in-session, confirmed/sharpened by the research)

1. **The problem this chapter solves** — the congregation apparatus is all borrowed (Cameron & Quinn, Burton & Obel, thermodynamics); the method requires Scripture first — and Scripture delivers: seven documented congregation-scale diagnoses by the risen Christ. The genre finding as warrant: all three scholarly genre proposals (prophetic oracle / imperial edict / covenant audit) converge on "an authoritative sovereign conducting a formal, structured, per-city examination with verdict, remedy, sanction, and reward."
2. **The protocol structure** — the seven-part form (Beale/Aune); the Christ-attribute matched per condition; "I know" (the omniscient examination); the complaint/commendation counts (Laodicea the only zero); **"I have this against you" as the dominical minimum factor** (with the honest boundary: the diagnostic-economy thematization is OUR contribution — cite texts + form-analysis, and hand Schwarz's Liebig-derived barrel its scriptural precedent).
3. **The canon's standing procedure** — the corporate-oracle genre (Amos's rhetorical entrapment — each church reads the other six case files); Jesus' Galilee corporate addresses (counterfactual diagnostics; Jerusalem's one will; the fig tree = Sardis in botanical form); **Jonah–Nahum as the canon's own longitudinal study** (corporate repentance does not transfer across generations); the **diagnose→warn→give-time→judge sequence** (Gen 15:16; Lev 26's escalating cycles; Luke 13:6–9's *cultivated* probation year; Rev 2:21 "I gave her time to repent"); **Zechariah 4 as the emblem** (the seven eyes and the seven lampstands come from the same vision — the Examiner examines His own light-delivery system; removal = decommissioned as light-bearer).
4. **The seven diagnoses on the map** — each church's diagnosis precisely (ESV), with the graded local-setting material (social not scenic: guild feasts, imperial-cult milieu, refused earthquake aid, quake-refugee pillars, stealth-taken citadel); the mapping to the phase map/CVF **tiered as illustrative correspondence, not exegesis**: Ephesus = form-outlasting-fire; Sardis = the Pharisee congregation (the audit performed by the un-gameable Witness); Laodicea = lukewarm + "I need nothing" (the absence of the ache — anchors the desolation chapter's differential); Pergamum/Thyatira = pruning neglected; Smyrna/Philadelphia = churches that would fail every modern vitality metric. **The standing rebuke: the two commended churches are small, poor, persecuted — diagnosis decoupled from visible prosperity entirely.**
5. **Corporate diagnosis, individual response** — the grammatical floor (Rev 2:10's singular/plural interleave; Beale's "corporate representation" — use that term, NOT "corporate personality"); Rev 2:23's two-level sentence; every letter's individualizing close; Rev 3:20 to the worst church ("if anyone…"); *no will overridden at church scale — a person can overcome inside a dying church*; the hearing formula as judicial (the response completes the diagnosis). Wink as scholarly company; the secular congregational-studies convergence (a congregation is a single character-bearing entity with a narratable identity — they describe, Christ evaluates).
6. **The prescriptions as transitions** — differentiated medicine (conservation vs correction; memory aimed per failure; the pronoun-split surgery; dosage per condition); the phase map's "heat or container?" question with Christ's own per-state answers; the stakes (lampstand removal — a church can die as a church before it dies as an organization; "I will spit you out"; "like a thief").
7. **Did the diagnoses hold? (the genuine-test section)** — the honest verdict: **suggestive but confounded** (Gibbon's "saved by prophecy, or courage" left unresolved; the confounds; the 1922–23 common terminus; n=7, post-hoc metric) — **and the reframe that is the better story: the letters were conditional pastoral warnings, and the post-letter flourishing (Melito of "dead" Sardis; Laodicea's council; Ephesus's centuries as Asia's Christian capital) is evidence the warnings were HEEDED.** "Unless you repent" working is not falsification. Include the Laodicea-pipes collapse as a worked example of testing sermon-lore (Wilson's eight retired claims; the exegetical core — usefulness, not zeal-spectrum — survives).
8. **What no borrowed instrument can supply** — the examiner is present ("I know"); the diagnosis is love (Rev 3:19 in the zero-commendation letter, beside the knock at the door); the endpoint is table-fellowship; the promises are the book's ending distributed in advance (the envelope structure — seven lampstands open the book; a city whose lamp is the Lamb closes it). Decline the church-ages reading fairly and firmly (Mede→Scofield→Branham; the scholarly case; typological simultaneity is the responsible universalizing). The modern gap named: nobody has fused the Rev 2–3 process with a validated instrument — the space this chapter and Vol 4 occupy, with modern metrics running *under* His diagnosis, never in place of it.
9. **A word to the church that reads its own letter** — the pastoral close: which letter is yours is a question for corporate self-examination under love, not a verdict to fear (whom He loves, He reproves); the Ephesus triad (remember → repent → first works) as the standing renewal protocol; Rainer's "past as hero" = counterfeit remembering vs Rev 2:5's remembering-for-repentance. Certainty note: the protocol structure and corporate addressability **Clearly Taught / Reasonably Inferred** (the texts + near-universal form-analysis); the phase-map/CVF mapping **Speculative** (illustrative correspondence); the survival-correlation **held as unresolved providence** (Gibbon's posture).
