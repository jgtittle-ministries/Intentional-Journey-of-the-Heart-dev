### The Period 3 Row's Structural-Completion Milestone

The v5_6_0_12 admission of FL.XXXIII Community Polity Structure Law produces a structural-completion milestone in the catalog's history: the Period 3 community-scale row holds Foundational entries across all six Group designations for the first time in the catalog's development. The Period 3 row's complete coverage is structurally significant for several reasons that the present sub-section articulates.

**First**, the completion demonstrates that the community-scale operational frame is *operationally complete* at the corporate person-structure level. Each of the six corporate Groups identified in the v5_6_0_11 corporate person-structure scriptural-definition document — corporate spirit (lampstand of Rev. 1–3), corporate heart (one heart of Acts 4:32, Ezek. 11:19), corporate soul (one soul of Acts 4:32, Phil. 1:27), corporate mind (same mind of 1 Cor. 1:10, mind of Christ of Phil. 2:5), corporate body and action (body of Christ master-frame and Group V community-relational substrate), corporate structural frame (the present law's polity-architecture territory) — now holds at least one Foundational Law's operational articulation. **Second**, the completion validates the body-of-Christ master-frame's structural integration at v5_6_0_11. The master-frame holds all six corporate Groups in unified operation; the Period 3 row's completion across all six Groups demonstrates that the master-frame's articulation is operationally substantive rather than merely aspirational. The body-of-Christ master-frame names the integrative identity within which the community's operational dimensions occur; the Period 3 row's Foundational coverage names the operational dimensions themselves. The two together hold the community-scale articulation at both the master-frame level and the operational-dimension level.

**Third**, the completion produces operational benchmarks for the catalog's other rows. The Period 0 row (eternal-pattern scale) currently holds entries across GI (FL.XV Hardening), GIV (FL.X Faith-Sight), GV (FL.I Sowing-and-Reaping; FL.VI Hear-and-Obey; FL.XVI Bondage), and GVI (FL.XXIII Sabbath Rest), with GII and GIII without Foundational entries — territory that may be addressed in subsequent revisions. The Period 5 row (cosmic-eschatological scale) remains sparsely populated and is anticipated as v5_6_0_14-or-later work per the v5_6_0_11 Q4 sequencing.

**Fourth**, the completion makes the Period 3 row available as a reference for understanding the catalog's full operational frame at the community scale. Researchers, pastoral practitioners, ecclesiologists, and seminary instructors who turn to the catalog for the community-scale operational articulation will find a complete Foundational-tier articulation across all six Groups, with the body-of-Christ master-frame providing the integrative-identity articulation and the ten Foundational Laws (and four non-Foundational entries) providing the operational-dimension articulation.

The Period 3 row's completion is not the catalog's terminus; subsequent expansion work will continue at the other rows, at the cross-scale relationships (the parent-child structure, the constitutive bidirectional paired-cell structure, the scale-invariant scope of certain laws like FL.XXIII), and at any additional articulation work the catalog's continued review surfaces.
