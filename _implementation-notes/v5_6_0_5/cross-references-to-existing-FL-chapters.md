# Cross-Reference Additions to Existing Foundational Law Chapters

The third-axis test produced four new Foundational Laws (FL.XIV–FL.XVII) that pair structurally with existing Foundational Laws. The pairings should be visible from both sides — when a reader is reading FL.VIII, the page should name the pairing with FL.XIV, and not only the reverse. This file specifies the cross-reference additions needed in three existing Foundational Law chapters.

The additions are small (a sentence or short paragraph each) and are placed at specific anchor locations in each chapter. Each addition is presented as: (1) the anchor — a distinctive substring in the existing chapter that locates where the addition goes; (2) the addition — the sentence(s) to insert.

---

## FL.III — The Heart-Throne Law

**File:** `volume-1-laws-of-the-spirit/foundational-law-iii-the-heart-throne-law/index.md` (or matching path)

**Anchor location 1:** In the "Why This Is a Foundational Law" section (or equivalent closing section), find the paragraph that names FL.III's pairings with FL.II Confession-Restoration and other existing laws. Add the following sentence at the end of that paragraph:

> The third-axis test (Vol 5 Periodic Table chapter) added two further pairings to the Heart-Throne Law's structural family: FL.XIV Vanity-of-Substitutes at P1/GII names the structural failure of every Object the heart enthrones in God's place — the operational consequence of the idolatry FL.III diagnoses — and FL.XVII Substitution-Cascade at P4/GII names the generational transmission of the inherited enthroned Objects, completing the individual-scale-to-generational-scale arc of the orientation-of-pursuit dimension in the Heart column.

**File:** `volume-1-laws-of-the-spirit/foundational-law-vi-the-hear-and-obey/index.md` (or matching path)

**Anchor location:** In the "Why This Is a Foundational Law" section (or equivalent closing section), find the paragraph that names FL.VI's structural features (its scale-invariance, its bidirectionality in the source texts). Add the following sentence at the end of that paragraph:

## FL.VIII — The Desire-for-God Law

**File:** `volume-1-laws-of-the-spirit/foundational-law-viii-the-desire-for-god/index.md` (or matching path)

**Anchor location 1:** In the "Why This Is a Foundational Law" section, find the paragraph that names FL.VIII's pairings with FL.VII Drawing-Near, FL.III Heart-Throne, and FL.XIII Pure-Heart Vision. Add the following sentence at the end of that paragraph:

> The third-axis test (Vol 5 Periodic Table chapter) named the law's structural counterpart explicitly: FL.XIV The Vanity-of-Substitutes Law at P1/GII names the structural failure of every Object the heart attempts to fill the FL.VIII longing with. FL.VIII names the engine; FL.XIV names the structural failure of every alternative engine. The two laws together constitute the bidirectional treatment of the Heart-column orientation dimension at the individual scale, and answer the fifth-periodicity question this entry's Certainty rationale reserved for future revision — the designed-orientation pattern that FL.VIII flagged as a possible fifth periodicity is now visible in its full operational form as the FL.VIII / FL.XIV pair.

**Anchor location 2:** In the Certainty rationale at the close of the chapter (the italicized closing paragraph), find the sentence that names the fifth-periodicity speculative uncertainty (the one beginning "First, whether the law warrants naming a fifth periodicity..." or similar). After that sentence, add the following clause as a second sentence:

> *The third-axis test that produced FL.XIV Vanity-of-Substitutes has provided a partial answer to this reservation: the designed-orientation pattern operates as a paired law structure (designed-for-X engine + structural-failure-of-every-non-X engine) rather than as a single law, and the fifth-periodicity question accordingly becomes whether the paired-structure pattern itself constitutes the fifth periodicity (creational-pointer with structural-failure-of-alternatives) — a question the corpus continues to reserve for future revision but that is now more sharply posed.*

## Note on Other Existing Foundational Law Chapters

The remaining ten existing Foundational Laws (FL.I, II, IV, V, VII, IX, X, XI, XII, XIII) do not require cross-reference additions, because their pairing with new Foundational Laws is either indirect (FL.I, FL.IX have their idol-ward face named in the Mirror discipline rather than in a paired new law) or constitutive (FL.XIII Pure-Heart Vision's orientation content is already explicit, and the third-axis test's anchor in the first table of the Decalogue is named in the Vol 5 chapter prose rather than requiring a FL.XIII addition).

If a future revision identifies a fifth idol-ward Foundational Law that requires a new cross-reference, the same pattern applies: place the cross-reference sentence at the closing-section anchor, using the same form as the three additions above.
