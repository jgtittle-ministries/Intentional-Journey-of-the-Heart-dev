Two readings are possible: (a) the empty cell is a structural feature of the generational scale — the integrative-internal dimension (Soul) does not have a distinct generational-scale articulation because the integration happens at lower scales (individual at P1/GIII, community at P3/GIII Container Law) and is then transmitted by the mechanisms the row otherwise names rather than by a distinct generational-scale Soul-integration mechanism; (b) the empty cell is a genuine under-population that subsequent revisions should address with a candidate Foundational Law at P4/GIII.
