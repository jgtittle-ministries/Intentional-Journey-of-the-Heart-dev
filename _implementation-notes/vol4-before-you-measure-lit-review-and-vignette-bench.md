## 1. The headline: four conditions for "knowing you can see"

Across nursing, medicine, pharmacy, engineering, the military academies, and teacher education, assessments of affective internalization were trusted only when four things were simultaneously true:

1. **Each level was tied to a logically appropriate evidence type** (not one question-format asked about all five levels);
2. **Trained raters agreed on real artifacts** (ICC/G ≥ ~0.75; reliability is a property of the rater-calibration regime, not the rubric document);
3. **The instrument separated groups it should separate** (known-groups discrimination, pre-specified in direction and magnitude);
4. **Multiple independent methods converged** (self-report alone was never validated as sufficient — anywhere).

Where any condition was missing, the record shows either unvalidated practice (Potgieter's central finding: **16 of 20 studies never touched assessment**; "No guidelines describing the assessment of the affective domain were found") or actively counterfeit results (performed empathy, tick-box caring, gamed portfolios, rubric-punished honesty).

## 2. The evidence-type gradient (the single most transferable artifact)

Yanofsky & Nyquist (J Educ Perioper Med 12(1):E055, anesthesiology; PMC4719552) mapped Krathwohl level → required evidence:

| Level | Evidence that can see it |
|---|---|
| Receiving | attendance, attention artifacts |
| Responding | produced work (journals, task completion) |
| Valuing | **voluntary, unprompted** expression or action |
| Organization | the value visible in real practice; how competing goods are ordered; teaching others |
| Characterization | **longitudinal consistency across contexts** — no single-sitting evidence exists |

An instrument asking the same kind of question (e.g., Likert self-report) about all five levels is category-confused above level 2–3. **Audit every Vol 4 instrument item against this gradient.**

## 3. The observability ceiling (三 independent demonstrations)

- **Krathwohl himself** (Handbook II p. 165, via EBSCO Research Starters): "Realistically, formal education generally cannot reach this level… not attained until at least some years after… formal education."
- **GUALS** (Rogers et al., MedEdPublish 2018;7:2, PMC10701821): 7-point Krathwohl-mapped scale, 125 journals, 7 raters, ICC 0.86 — and **no journal ever scored 7 (Characterising)** in a one-week window.
- **CEBOK3** (Dennis & Hains, ASEE 2019): civil engineering set its entry-to-practice target at **Organization** and placed Characterization deliberately beyond what assessment certifies.
- **West Point** (USMA Gold Book): internalization of Army Values assessed by **360° ratings (self/peers/subordinates/instructors) repeated across four years** — multi-observer time-series, not an instrument.

**Design rule:** every Vol 4 instrument publishes a claim ceiling — "evidence up to Organization; Characterization only by longitudinal, multi-witness consistency of life" (which is also Matt. 7:16, and confirms the review series' witness-senior-to-self-report finding).

## 4. The failure catalog (design against each)

1. **Faking-good / social desirability** — Krathwohl's own warning (quoted by the ASCE committee): students "will provide responses to direct questions that correspond to what they believe the instructor wants, rather than what they truly feel." Paulhus's split matters: **self-deceptive enhancement vs. impression management** — a Receiving-level soul can *sincerely* self-report Characterization. Defenses with meta-analytic support: forced-choice formats (Cao & Drasgow 2019, JAP: faking d≈0.06 vs. substantial for Likert), **other-report** (Connelly & Ones 2010, Psych Bull: informants beat self-ratings for behavior prediction), behavioral indicators.
2. **Performed virtue under observation** — "Fool me once: The illusion of empathy in interactions with standardized patients" (Medical Teacher 38(12) 2016): assessment promoted feigned empathy; "if assessment promotes inauthenticity… empathy is a quality which cannot be assessed."
3. **Documentation displacing the practice** — Sims et al. 2020 (Int J Nurs Stud 107:103580), the tick-box rounding study: "the task had become the documentation not the actual conversation or the care."
4. **Rubric-punished honesty** — Nikolic et al. 2023: lab rubrics that "punish ethical measurement recordings and encourage unethical practices" (writing the 'correct' value over the honest reading). Church analogue: rewarding reported devotional performance over honest desolation — the exact dry-believer harm the corpus guards.
5. **Self-perceived gain without real gain** — Roberts & Kaur 2023 (Nurse Educator): training "improved self-perceived empathy but did not affect empathy."
6. **Rubric reliability doesn't travel** — REFLECT rubric: adequate IRR at origin; replication ICCs 0.35–0.45 (Grierson et al. 2020). Recalibrate locally with exemplars, always.
7. **Gaming reflective portfolios** — "writing to the rubric." GUALS countermeasure: **the full scoring rubric was withheld from students** to preserve authenticity.
8. **The environment fights the instrument** — Hojat et al. 2009 ("the devil is in the third year"): validated empathy instrument faithfully measured *decline* caused by the hidden curriculum. Pre-commit to receiving bad news as signal (bad-news test at instrument scale).
9. **Assessing belief instead of behavior** — teacher-ed "dispositions" controversy: NCATE deleted "social justice" as a disposition (2006) after compelled-belief accusations; *Oyama v. University of Hawaii* (9th Cir. 2015) upheld the university **because** judgment was tied to "defined and established professional standards" of conduct. **Survivable design: observable practice against covenanted, freely-accepted standards — never interior orthodoxy. Feed to any future FC1/legal review.**
10. **Hierarchy assumption has critics** — Nikolic found affective objectives ranked in *reverse* taxonomy order by engineering academics; Song & Nix 2025 (nursing ALO census: median program outcome level 3.5) cites McLeod's objection that attitudes may not arrange hierarchically. Test Guttman-like scaling in OUR population; don't assume the ladder.
11. **Field-wide retreat is possible** — ASCE BOK2 drafted affective statements, then "concluded that the ability to measure attainment in the affective domain was far less certain… and chose to relegate the assessment in the affective domain to an advisory appendix," restating its values outcome **entirely within the cognitive domain**. CEBOK3 returned with affect on only 7 of 21 outcomes. Lesson: an instrument that claims to see everything gets trusted on nothing; **fewer, defensible constructs beat comprehensive ones.**

## 5. What actually worked (the positive toolbox)

- **GUALS** — the strongest direct precedent: Krathwohl-mapped 7-level rubric on journals; derived from phenomenological analysis of intervention-vs-control journals (known-groups discrimination), then rater-validated (7 raters, ~1hr exemplar training, ICC 0.86). Added a "Transitional" level between Organising and Characterising.
- **SJT construction recipe** (Smith et al. 2020, Am J Pharm Educ 84(7), PMC7405297; DeLellis PAD-S 2023): 10–12 SMEs → many independently drafted scenarios from real practice → external screen (kill items not requiring judgment) → pilot → **consensus scoring key with near-miss partial credit** → item psychometrics + subgroup fairness. Known limits: coachable (~0.5 SD), construct-heterogeneous, low alpha is normal (report ICC/test-retest instead). Response-instruction effect (McDaniel et al. 2007): "what is best?" (knowledge) vs. "what would you do?" (tendency) measure different things — **rater benches use knowledge framing**.
- **P-MEX / multisource feedback** — many brief sampled observations by multiple raters across contexts; MSF instruments held to generalizability ≈ 0.70 with published rater-number requirements. Affect became reliably measurable when the unit became **the aggregate of many observers over time**.
- **EPSA** (Ater Kranov et al., ASEE 2013) — observed group discussion of a scenario, scored by rubric; **year one spent entirely on task standardization and rater consistency before any validity data** ("Establishing reliability is a prerequisite for establishing validity").
- **Peabody et al. 2000 (JAMA 283:1715)** — the vignette validation classic: against standardized patients (76.2%), written vignettes scored 71.0%, beating chart abstraction (65.6%). **Written case portraits scored against explicit criteria are a valid, inexpensive proxy for live observation.**
- **Anchoring vignettes** (King, Murray, Salomon & Tandon 2004, APSR 98:191; King & Wand 2007, Political Analysis 15:46) — fixed-truth vignettes calibrate raters/scales; assumptions to honor: response consistency + **vignette equivalence** (the portrait's true level must be the same for every competent reader). 5–7 (up to 12) vignettes per concept; best sets are "equally spaced through the distribution"; vignettes **before** self-assessment (Hopkins & King 2010) — priming a common scale is a feature.
- **Factorial-survey discipline** (Rossi/Auspurg & Hinz) — every vignette is a designed cell in a factor space, ≤20 vignettes per rater sitting.
- **Known-groups method** (Cronbach & Meehl 1955; Hattie & Cooksey 1984) with the Preacher et al. 2005 caution: **extreme-groups-only validation inflates apparent power — middle cases are mandatory.**
- **Historiometry** (Simonton 1990/2009) — psychometric-grade measurement on the documented dead, multi-rater coded biographical records with reliability checks; psychobiography's two-independent-sources rule (Schultz 2005; Ponterotto). **The direct warrant for testing instruments against the historical record.**
- **Content validity machinery** — Lawshe CVR (1975; Ayre & Scally 2014 critical values: small panels need near-unanimity); Delphi rounds; Lynn 1986 (>10 experts unnecessary). **Cognitive interviewing** (Willis 2005) before piloting. The standard ladder: Boateng et al. 2018 (Frontiers Public Health 6:149).
- **Nursing's normative-anchor move** — NPVS validated by anchoring items to the ANA Code of Ethics then standard psychometrics. Direct analogue: item-by-item anchoring to Scripture with documented panel CVI — the recognized method, not an improvisation.

**Novelty finding:** no published precedent exists for validating a Krathwohl-level instrument against scriptural/hagiographic portraits. The bench is a defensible hybrid of three established methods (historiometry + clinical-vignette validation + anchoring-vignette calibration) — genuinely novel in the formation domain; document the method to publishable standard.

## 6. THE VIGNETTE BENCH — design spec

**Size.** Core bench **~25 portraits (≈5 per level)** + negative controls + deliberate middle/ambiguous cases → **30–40 total**. Pretest with more; prune by consistency diagnostics. Administered set per rater sitting: **8–12** (blocks from the bench; ≤20 max).

**Construction rules.**
1. **Peg each portrait to one dominant level by documented behavior** (what the figure did, repeatedly, at cost) — never level vocabulary in the text.
2. **Explicit factor space**: level (5) × life domain of evidence (2–3) × evidence visibility (public acts vs. private writings). Every portrait is a cell.
3. **Vignette equivalence**: only figures with thick, multi-source, uncontested records (scriptural arcs; saints with independent biographies — the two-source rule). If the expert panel can't reach Lawshe threshold on a portrait's true level, **the portrait leaves the bench**.
4. **Strip halo cues**: blind or de-emphasize instantly recognizable identities; pretest named vs. blinded versions to quantify halo.
5. **Equal length, register, literary quality** — or raters score prose, not internalization.
6. **Difficulty spread**: equally spaced across the continuum, with deliberate **adjacent-level discrimination pairs** (especially Valuing↔Organization and Organization↔Characterization).

**Negative controls (mandatory; any failure = hard fail regardless of aggregate statistics).**
1. **The counterfeit** — high verbal piety, contradicting behavioral record (the Pharisee of Matt 23 is canonical). Must score LOW.
2. **The adjacent construct** — natural virtue/temperament without the construct (discriminant validity).
3. **The desolate believer** — documented dryness with a deep-internalization long arc (Teresa of Calcutta is canonical). Must score HIGH. *A bench without this control cannot certify the instrument safe for the population the project cares most about — this is the weep-test made operational at the design stage.*

**Candidate portrait pool (to be argued case-by-case at keying):** scriptural — the Pharisee (counterfeit), the rich young ruler (Responding/Valuing boundary; treasure test failed), the thief on the cross (kind without degree — the instrument must know what it cannot see), Peter pre/post-Pentecost (level movement in one life), Demas (drift), the Bereans (Receiving→Valuing), Barnabas (Organization), Daniel (Characterization arc); historical — Teresa of Calcutta (desolate-believer control), Brother Lawrence (Characterization in the ordinary), Spurgeon (fruitfulness under recurring darkness), Finney's backslider confession (the un-captured peak), Wesley pre/post-Aldersgate (Responding-without-assurance → Valuing), Ephesus via Rev 2 (corporate-scale portrait for the congregation instruments).

**Keying.** Expert panel **7–12** (SJT norm 10–12; >10 unnecessary per Lynn), Delphi rounds, level assignments retained only at Lawshe CVR critical value (7-member panel → near-unanimity). **Key frozen and dated before any rater trial.**

**Rater trials.** ≥3 independent raters per portrait (prefer 5+); knowledge-type instructions ("what does this record show?"); SCID-style calibration loop (train on anchor portraits with known keys → group calibration on divergences → individual feedback → re-test). Portraits before any self-assessment in a sitting; never a forced single self-vs-portrait comparison.

**Scoring.** Graded distance-from-key (partial credit for adjacent level, zero for 2+ off). Expect low alpha (SJT-normal); reliability claims rest on **inter-rater ICC and test-retest**.

**Pass/fail gates (before any live pilot).**
1. Content validity: every retained portrait at Lawshe threshold.
2. Rater agreement: **ICC ≥ 0.75** target; kappa ≥ 0.61 floor on categorical assignment.
3. Known-groups ordering: instrument reproduces the canonical portrait ordering (direction and magnitude pre-specified); adjacent pairs separated.
4. All three negative-control types behave as designed.
5. Only then: cognitive interviews (Willis) with intended users → leadership-cohort pilot → psychometrics, documented as a Kane-style validity argument.

## 7. How this maps onto the Vol 4 review

Proposed new front section — **"Before You Measure (Earning the Right to Ask)"** — carrying the validation ladder: construct audit (item→level→warrant, with the desolation inversion clause, degree-not-kind labels, Fowler-orthogonality check) → **vignette bench** (this spec) → cognitive interviews → leadership-cohort live pilot (the plow-us-first group takes every instrument first) → congregational deployment, ordered by the weep-test principle: *the more a measure could wound, the smaller and later it deploys.* Existing Vol 4 sections then stand as the deployment half. Claim ceilings (§3) and the failure catalog (§4) written into every instrument spec; the Measurement Covenant is the governance wrapper already in place.

**The weep-test is independently validated by this literature.** Detached measurement did not merely miss the affective constructs — it *destroyed* them where it touched them (performed empathy, tick-box caring, punished honesty). The fields that succeeded moved the measurement into relationship: many observers who know the person, over time, under low stakes. That is Baxter's "the catalogue was innocent only because I knew the faces," arrived at empirically by secular fields sixty years after Krathwohl warned them.
