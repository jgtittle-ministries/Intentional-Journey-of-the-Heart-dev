## ⭐ Part 1 — IJH-relevant findings (the actual point of the exercise)

### Finding A — A complete Vol-2 chapter (**V2.Exp0B — The Contemplative Substrate**) is drafted *inside the FC PDF*, is *referenced by the Vol 2 Taxonomy Key as if it exists*, but is **not a chapter in Vol 2.** *(The strongest actionable item of the series — your call to promote it.)*
- **What the volume says:** the [Vol 2 Taxonomy Key](../../docs/volume-2-knowing-to-doing/taxonomy-key-how-this-volume-classifies-its.md) defines a **"Foundational Practice"** type and states: *"**V2.Exp0B (The Contemplative Substrate) is the first entry of this type** and names four practices (fixed-hour prayer, lectio divina, silent waiting, vigilantia cordis) **that the rest of this volume's catalog assumes**. … These are discussed in the Formation Companion discussion in Vol 5 (FC)."* So the volume's own taxonomy gives the chapter a Vol-2 ID, declares the whole catalog depends on it, and **redirects readers to the FC PDF for the actual content.**
- **What's actually in Vol 2:** the chapter file **does not exist.** The Vol 2 directory runs `…exploration-09a…`, `exploration-10…`, `exploration-o-the-christian-companions-framework-tool.md` (the letter-O Companion chapter) — but **no `exploration-0b` / Contemplative-Substrate file.** A reader who follows the "V2.Exp0B" pointer finds no chapter; the only place the four practices are written out is **FC pp 23–30** — a section that isn't even in FC's own table of contents.
- **So:** this is a **drafted-but-unpromoted Vol-2 chapter** — the clearest instance in the whole review series of "a formation-doc concept not yet present in the volume," and a near-dangling reference (the taxonomy key promises V2.Exp0B; the volume doesn't deliver it). The draft is mature: four practices, each with *what it does / why it's substrate / form / scriptural ground / a caution*, plus explicit connections to V2.Exp1, 2, 4, 6, 6C, 7, 8, 10 and to V1.FL.II/III/VII/VIII/X/XI/XIII, plus a "wider tradition" note and Open Trails.
- **IJH action (your call):** **promote the Exploration 0B draft into an actual Vol 2 chapter** (e.g., `exploration-0b-contemplative-substrate.md`), lifting it from FC pp 23–30 — since the taxonomy key and the catalog already assume it. Two clean-ups to do in the same pass: (1) reconcile the **naming** — the Taxonomy Key writes **"V2.Exp0B"** (zero-B) while FC writes **"Exploration OB"** (letter O); pick one; (2) once it's a real chapter, FC can *reference* it rather than *embed* it. **This is the most concrete and highest-value item the five reviews have produced.**

The series' most persistent gap is now answered at the disciplines level. ## Part 2 — The seven-author theological review

### Cross-panel synthesis

**(A) Grace / Spirit-as-agent — the series' #1 press — is, in FC, a structural load-bearing wall.** "Humility as the agent of change. The Companion is not the agent; the Holy Spirit is." "Rescuing is how well-meaning Companions interrupt the Spirit's work." Mode-switching itself "requires a nudging from the Holy Spirit." Keller, Stott, Fee, and Prince all affirm without reservation; this is the most agent-careful of the five papers.

**(B) The "this is not therapy" boundary is a genuine strength.** The three-fold protection (pilgrim / Companion / role), the referral-as-a-competency framing ("referral is not a failure of companioning; it is a competency of companioning"), and the Stephen-Ministry referral criteria together form a discipline the whole panel commends — and that protects the role legally and pastorally. Keller (Powlison-adjacent) and Stott affirm strongly.

**(C) The one genuinely exposed flank: importing non-Christian-grounded tools.** Shadow Work (Jungian/mythopoetic), Voice Dialogue (Stones' *Psychology of Selves*), Big Mind (Zen), the somatic Guts work, Kahneman — FC brings these into Christian formation. This is where Keller and Prince press hardest. FC handles it **better than most**: it runs Powlison's 3-step + Trentham's 4-step, insists "method is never neutral," and names precisely what Shadow Work does *not* supply (sin-as-sin, the Holy Spirit rather than the integrating self as agent, refusal of Jungian metaphysics). The charitable-strengthening note: keep the discernment **per-tool and explicit** (Big Mind's Zen roots especially), and let the Council's character/discernment work govern it — which FC already defers to. This is a *flank to keep guarded*, not a fault.

**(D) The contemplative substrate answers Foster — and grounds the whole role in the Companion's own formation.** The Companion's Affective-Taxonomy stage (HFT) is reframed from a prerequisite checkbox into the *developmental spine*; PROAPT + spiritual direction + a peer group are the "minimum scaffolding." This is the move every prior review implied: formation is something done *to* the Companion before it is done *through* them.

### The seven reviewers

**Timothy Keller (Reformed).** *Affirms:* Spirit-as-agent made structural; the therapy boundary; Powlison's grid applied to tool-discernment. *Presses:* the **non-Christian tool imports** need the firmest per-tool guard (Jungian Shadow Work, Zen Big Mind) — "method is never neutral" is exactly right; hold it tool-by-tool; and the **character-prerequisites** section (deferred) is where Reformed ordination-adjacent rigor will matter most. *Fix:* keep per-tool discernment explicit; develop the character section with the Council. *(Paper-level; FC already defers the latter.)*

**John Stott (evangelical / expositor).** *Affirms:* the fellow-pilgrim humility (the Foster "hear my confession first" story); Scripture-application in the cognitive core; the Navigators' scripture-memory discipline. *Presses:* the **character qualifications** (1 Tim. 3 / Titus 1, read with the Eph. 4:11–12 equipping and the NT women-in-formation examples) deserve the primary-text work the section currently only outlines. *Fix:* the promised next-version character section. *(FC is honest that this is deferred.)*

**Gordon D. Fee (Pentecostal NT scholar).** *Affirms:* the **gift-based, non-gendered** framing of the role (Eph. 4:11–12; Rom. 12:6–8; 1 Cor. 12) and the explicit NT women-in-formation examples (Priscilla, Phoebe, Lydia, Titus 2) — the right corrective to the old masculine "Weapons Master" frame; the Spirit as the agent of all four modes. *Presses:* keep the Spirit (not the protocol, e.g., TPM's tight script) the subject of the healing; honor the **Body** dimension in group mode. *Fix:* none major. *(No IJH change.)*

**Dallas Willard (contemplative — *and a cited lineage*).** *Affirms:* warmly — Spiritual Direction as the oldest, most-developed mode for *the Companion's own ongoing formation*; the contemplative substrate (his *Divine Conspiracy* "sin-management with Jesus added" failure mode is quoted as the thing the substrate guards against); *Hearing God* under the developmental spine. *Presses:* keep mode-switching genuinely Spirit-led, not competence-driven; keep the apophatic discipline inside the doctrinal frame (FC's "receive without confusion" note already does this). *Fix:* none major. *(No IJH change.)*

**Richard Foster (contemplative / Renovaré — *and a cited source*).** *Affirms:* **his standing press is finally answered in full** — the four contemplative disciplines get real structural place (Exp 0B), and the wider tradition (Jesus Prayer, Brother Lawrence, de Caussade, the Exercises, Carmel) is named and received under Christ. *Presses:* hold the practices as *means of grace*, not Companion-competencies to be checked off (FC says exactly this: "not a tool they teach; it is the ground they walk"); add **celebration/feast** so the substrate isn't all silence and watchfulness (FC's own Open Trails already names this gap). *Fix:* the celebration Open Trail. *(Promoting Exp 0B into Vol 2 = acting on this affirmation.)*

**John Eldredge (heart / desire — *and the Band-of-Brothers / Weapons-Master lineage*).** *Affirms:* the group mode and its tool inventory are *his* world (Band of Brothers, *Wild at Heart*, mission-and-calling framing); the recovery of the heart and the contested interior. *Presses:* the **mixed-gender reframe** away from the masculine Weapons Master is right for FotH, but don't lose the gendered specificity of formative wounds where it matters (FC's per-tool "framing notes" already hold this); keep **mission/calling** (Shadow Mission, Mission Clarity) central, not just healing. *Fix:* keep the gender-aware framing notes; preserve the mission axis. *(No IJH change.)*

**Derek Prince (Word-and-Spirit / Pentecostal).** *Affirms:* **TPM-style memory healing** and the inner-healing lineage (Payne, Bennett, RTF, Sozo) are his most native ground; the Word-memory discipline (Navigators TMS); the Spirit as the real agent of transformation. *Presses:* (1) name **deliverance** explicitly alongside memory-healing — TPM/inner-healing and deliverance overlap but aren't identical, and some material is bondage, not a lie-in-a-memory; (2) on the **non-Christian tools**, press hardest of all (a Word-and-Spirit reviewer is least comfortable with Jungian/Zen lineages) — FC's discernment protocol is the right shape; keep the spiritual-discernment guard, not just the epistemic one. *Fix:* name deliverance as (or beside) a mode/tool; keep the discernment spiritual as well as methodological. *(The "missing war" thread again; FC is closer than the others.)*
