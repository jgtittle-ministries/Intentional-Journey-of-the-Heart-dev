## Part 2 — The eight-author theological review

### Synthesis
The theology of HFT is sound and **converges with the TA review's lesson**: the few presses are the *same family* IJH has already largely absorbed.

- **Measurement humility (Lewis, Willard, Foster, Keller).** The central move — an educational taxonomy + a 7-point Likert to *measure* heart formation — is the exposed flank. HFT is already humble (it calls itself a "pilot," a "tractable target," a "starting point… for all its limitations"), and **IJH already wrestles this openly** (the Vol 3 apologia; the new meta-law "Properties of Spiritual Law" draft; Vol 4's hedges). So this *confirms* an IJH stance rather than demanding a change. The guard to keep visible: measurement stays **servant of a Spirit-wrought, relational** work, never its master.
- **"Affective domain" ≠ "the biblical heart" (Keller, Lewis, Eldredge).** Krathwohl's affective domain (values/attitudes/interests) is a secular construct; the biblical heart is more (will, the orientation of love, the wellspring, the imago). HFT grounds the heart in Willard's "direction of the will" + Smith's "what we love," so it's not pure affect — but the mapping deserves the *same "heuristic, not identity" guard* the TA review urged for the Kahneman map. **Already IJH's habit** (Vol 2 Exp 1's Certainty note flags the taxonomy mapping as "new but internally consistent").
- **Keep the telos (love of God) above the proxy (trust in Scripture) (Eldredge, Lewis, Stott).** Trust-in-Scripture is a brilliant *tractable* target, but it's the gateway, not the end — the end is loving/desiring God. HFT says exactly this ("the affective gateway"), so it's a *keep-it* note, not a fix.
- **The Spirit as agent (Fee, Prince).** Already named (Rynsburger & Lamport — "the Holy Spirit is the agent of growth"). Fee/Prince would simply keep it foregrounded over the method. No IJH change.

**Net theological verdict for IJH: confirms, doesn't demand** — same as TA. The actionable IJH items are the two documentation findings above.

### The eight voices (compact; Lewis in full, as he's new)

**C. S. Lewis (the trained affections / imaginative apologetics).** *Strong affirmation.* HFT's thesis — "training the heart is prior to training the mind in the order of transformation" — *is* "Men Without Chests" (*The Abolition of Man*): the chest, the seat of trained emotion, is what makes a person neither mere intellect nor mere appetite; "we make men without chests and bid them be virtuous." He'd also bless the desire-centered frame (Smith's "we are what we love") as kin to his own *Sehnsucht*/Joy — the heart's longing as a signpost to God. **Press 1:** the taxonomy-plus-Likert apparatus flirts with the *Abolition*'s "Conditioners" — the affections are educated by their right *objects* (beauty, story, liturgy, "the Tao"), not principally by being graded on a ladder; keep the instrument a servant. **Press 2:** self-reported "trust" is unreliable (the heart's self-deception — *Screwtape*); the **external-witness track is the truer gauge** — lean on it. **Press 3:** belief often follows the *imagination being captured* ("myth became fact") — so deepen the imaginative/desire dimension; it's *how* the heart is actually formed (this strengthens, not weakens, HFT's affective framing). **IJH relevance:** confirms IJH's heart-first instinct; reinforces the measurement-humility already in the volumes. No new IJH change.

**Keller** — affirms trust-in-Scripture as tractable; presses the affective≠heart guard and the heart's deceit (Jer. 17:9) making self-report suspect → lean on external witness + the Spirit. *Already in IJH.*
**Stott** — loves reflective Bible study as the foundation; keep the **Word's own forming authority** central (it isn't only that we trust it; it forms us — Heb. 4:12). Minor.
**Fee** — affirms the Spirit-as-agent already present; keep the Spirit (not PROAPT/the taxonomy) the subject of the forming; honor the corporate/Body setting. Minor.
**Willard (source)** — HFT rests on his "renovation of the heart" + six dimensions + *Hearing God*; he'd endorse warmly and be glad it's framed as a pilot. Keep it relational, not a regime. Minor.
**Foster (source)** — his disciplines/PROAPT are means of grace, not pedagogical inputs; add the contemplative stream. Minor (same as TA).
**Eldredge** — affirms heart-prior-to-mind; presses that the deepest target is *desire/love for God and the healing of the wounded heart*, not a measured trust-score; this is where the **Sower soils** (the heart's *condition*) belong — which loops back to Finding A.
**Prince** — most enthusiastic about **trust-in-the-Word** as the target (Word-and-Spirit); ground it in the Spirit's witness to the Word and in the new-birth (the regenerated heart can trust); add confession/speaking the Word as formative. Medium-but-already-IJH.
