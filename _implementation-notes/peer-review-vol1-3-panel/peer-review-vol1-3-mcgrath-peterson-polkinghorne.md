# Peer Review — Volumes 1–3
## Panel: Alister McGrath · Eugene Peterson · John Polkinghorne

*Working artifact (dev-only, never mirrored). Drafted 2026-06-17. Method follows the project's standard persona-review convention (cf. the Vol 1 four-tradition and three-stream reviews, and the 7- and 8-author Formation-Document reviews): each reviewer gives **affirmations + specific findings by volume + a single biggest concern**, in their authentic voice; then a **synthesis** (convergences and tensions) and a **prioritized findings list (High/Med/Low)** for adjudication with the author.*

*All three reviewers are personas drawn from their body of work. Every claim below was verified against the texts before it was written; file references are given so each finding can be checked and acted on.*

---

## A note on calibration (read this first)

This review is deliberately **fair, not adversarial**. Before the panel convened, I checked the texts for the guards a hostile reviewer would assume were missing — and found most of them already in place, and well-placed. The project already self-critiques exactly where these reviewers press:

- **A certainty-tier discipline** — *Clearly Taught / Reasonably Inferred / Speculative* — defined in [read-me-first.md](../../docs/read-me-first.md) and mapped to numeric confidence in the claim registry. Vol 3 is held openly at lower confidence; its central claims (F_s, conservation laws, miracles-as-threshold) are flagged *Speculative / Frontier* and frozen against routine revision ([note-before-we-begin.md](../../docs/volume-3-quantitative-framework/note-before-we-begin.md), ¶29).
- **An analogy-class discipline** — every physical analogy tagged *illustrative* or *load-bearing-proposed*, with elevation requiring a deliberate Council vote, precisely "because analogies drift upward silently otherwise" ([note-before-we-begin.md](../../docs/volume-3-quantitative-framework/note-before-we-begin.md), ¶31; [part-1-governance-model.md](../../docs/volume-6-governance/part-1-governance-model.md) §8).
- **A pervasive anti-mechanism guard** — "there is no mechanism, there is a Father" and "never meant to measure how loved you are" appear, foregrounded, at least nine times across the three volumes ([spiritual-force-energy-and-power.md](../../docs/volume-1-laws-of-the-spirit/spiritual-force-energy-and-power.md), [properties-of-spiritual-law.md](../../docs/volume-1-laws-of-the-spirit/properties-of-spiritual-law.md), [exploration-03-spiritual-force.md](../../docs/volume-3-quantitative-framework/exploration-03-spiritual-force.md), [the-complete-causal-loop-set.md](../../docs/volume-3-quantitative-framework/the-complete-causal-loop-set.md), and others).
- **A measurement guard at the front of the assessment work** — "The Affective Taxonomy measures *observable formation* … and nothing more. It does not measure, and must never be read as measuring, a person's standing before God" ([exploration-04-spiritual-distance.md](../../docs/volume-3-quantitative-framework/exploration-04-spiritual-distance.md)); the Pharisee-collapse closure check; external-witness triangulation; "the Holy Spirit … is not a variable any researcher can control" ([volume-4-testing-framework](../../docs/volume-4-testing-framework/)).

So the findings below aim at **tighten and foreground what the project already half-says** — not demolish. Two seeds from the planning notes were *overturned by the texts* in the project's favor, and are corrected here (the dry-season word is not buried; the measurement work is already heavily guarded).

## I. Alister McGrath — the theologian-scientist

*Reading as a systematic and historical theologian who also holds an Oxford chair in science and religion and a doctorate in molecular biophysics — the one member of the panel equipped to weigh the Vol 3 move from both sides of the fence.*

### Affirmations

The first thing to say is that I have reviewed a great deal of work in the science-and-religion field, and this project is **more methodologically disciplined than most of what reaches print**. Three features deserve naming.

First, the **certainty-tier scheme**. To sort one's own claims into *Clearly Taught*, *Reasonably Inferred*, and *Speculative* — and then to hold to that sorting when inconvenient — is an act of intellectual honesty that the genre badly needs and rarely shows. It is the working epistemology of a critical realist, and it is built into the architecture rather than bolted on.

Second, and more impressive, the **analogy-class discipline**. The recognition that "analogies drift from illustrative to load-bearing silently over time … not through any single decision, but through accumulated weight in the prose," and the decision to *tag* each analogy and require a formal vote to promote it — this is genuinely sophisticated. I have watched whole theological programmes founder on exactly this drift, unawares. Here it is named and fenced. That is a contribution in its own right.

Third, the **Grammar of Creation**. The Form-3 ▸ Form-2 ▸ Form-1 ladder, and the insistence that mathematics is the *echo* of the ordering Logos rather than the thing itself, is textbook good natural theology — the "two books" tradition done with proper humility. The claim that a rational creation reflects a rational Creator is mainstream, defensible, and I affirm it without reservation. The project resists the collapse into "the universe simply *is* mathematics" — correctly.

### Findings by volume

**Volume 1.** My one substantive concern is *hermeneutical*, and it is mild. The project moves from specific scriptural passages — narrative and didactic — to trans-contextual "laws." [Properties of Spiritual Law](../../docs/volume-1-laws-of-the-spirit/properties-of-spiritual-law.md) defends these as personal and covenantal rather than mechanical — well done — and the [Held-Tensions / Shadow-Pairs](../../docs/volume-1-laws-of-the-spirit/supplemental-held-tensions-and-shadow-pairs.md) device guards against one-directional misreading. But neither squarely addresses the *descriptive-to-normative* leap itself: by what warrant does "this is how God acted here / taught here" become "this is a law that holds across contexts"? The implicit answer — multi-author, multi-Testament convergence — is sound, and is in fact the project's stated criterion for the top tier. It should be made explicit as a *hermeneutical* principle, not only an epistemic one. A short note would close the gap. **(MED.)**

On the *word* "law" with its latent deistic, mechanistic load: I note the project already weighed renaming laws to "principles" and deliberately kept "laws," with the Held-Tensions device as the mitigation. I am satisfied with that resolution. **(LOW — already answered.)**

**Volume 2.** Theologically careful. The Affective Taxonomy read as *integration, not replacement* — with the Pharisee diagnosed as "Stage N collapsed" rather than a high achiever — is a genuinely good piece of pastoral-developmental theology that keeps grace prior to attainment. No substantive finding.

**Volume 3.** This is where the project is most exposed, and the exposure is real. The question I cannot avoid as a theologian-scientist: does the borrowed apparatus of field theory — Lagrangians, gauge fields, gauge groups, configuration space — *do real work here*, or is it decorative? Having read the TFT appendices, my honest judgment is that in [TFT Explained](../../docs/volume-3-quantitative-framework/appendix-tft-explained.md) and [TFT Interpreted Analogies](../../docs/volume-3-quantitative-framework/appendix-tft-interpreted-analogies.md) the mathematics is, at present, *vocabulary* — it names structures (curvature, coupling, threshold) without deriving anything from them. That is not fatal; illustrative analogy is legitimate, and the project says it is illustrative. The danger is that borrowed mathematical clothing lends an *appearance* of rigor that the content has not yet earned — "theology in borrowed mathematical clothes" — and a sympathetic but careful reader will feel the seams.

Here is the thing that surprised me, and it is the most actionable point I have: **the project's own answer to this concern is locked in a PDF.** The [TFT Challenged](../../docs/volume-3-quantitative-framework/appendix-tft-challenged.md) appendix — the "honest accounting … an adversarial critique … that marks where it is doing real mathematics and where it overreaches (its 'Analogy Boundary Principle')" — is, in the readable markdown, a *single paragraph pointing to a PDF popup*. The two *expository* TFT appendices run to full length in prose; the *self-critical* one does not. The instrument that would disarm my concern is the least visible thing in the volume. Surface it.

Finally, the F_s "equation": the sovereign-G term, by design, absorbs any disconfirmation ("a maximally-ready believer before a freely-withholding God still receives only what God freely gives"). Theologically this is *correct* and I would not have it otherwise. But it means F_s is **not falsifiable**, and therefore not an equation in the scientific sense — it is a conceptual schema written in equation shorthand. The chapter already holds it at *Speculative* and says "there is no mechanism, there is a Father." The small remaining step is to *say plainly, at the point of presentation,* that this is a schema, not a computation — so no reader mistakes the shorthand for a claim it isn't making.

### Biggest concern

**Volume 3's reach for un-cashable formalism risks the credibility of the (excellent) tier-disciplined whole.** The cure is not to retreat from the quantitative ambition — the *question* "how would you measure that?" is a good and clarifying one — but to let Vol 3's own best instincts (the analogy-class discipline, the TFT-Challenged critique) govern the *prose* as visibly as they govern the *registry*.

## II. Eugene Peterson — the pastor

*Reading as a pastor of forty years, a translator who spent a decade getting the Psalms to sound like they were prayed by people with dirt under their nails, and a man constitutionally suspicious of any attempt to manage the life of the Spirit.*

### Affirmations — including a correction I owe the author

I came to this expecting to find the pastoral word buried — a kindness in a footnote after the machinery. I went looking for "A Word for the Dry Season," braced to say *make this the frame, not the afterthought.* I cannot say it. **It is already the frame, in seven places.** It is in Vol 1's chapter on spiritual force, set *before* the language of reservoirs can wound. It is a "standing word for the whole of Part II" early in Vol 2. It is a "guard for the dry and the scrupulous" in Vol 2's confession chapter, with the tender conscience explicitly protected from turning a verse into a club. It is in the Desire-for-God law, distinguishing the dry-but-faithful from the deadened. It is set down again in Vol 3, immediately after the equation that could hurt someone. And the same sentence — *"they were never meant to measure how loved you are; and you are loved, all the way through the dark, whether you can feel it or not"* — appears **word for word** in Volume 1 and Volume 3. That is not an author hedging. That is an author who has been in the dark himself and refuses to let his own book become a rod for someone else's back. I affirm it with my whole heart. It is the soul of the work, and the **Granddaddy notes** are its pulse.

I affirm, too, the things this project sets its face against: the vending-machine god, the technique that "obliges" God, the law-as-lever. "God is a Person, not a machine — and definitely not a vending machine" is the right first word, and it is said first. And — this matters — I find the assessment work in Volume 4 has *already heard* the objection I was going to raise: "the Holy Spirit … is not a variable any researcher can control," and at the very front of the spiritual-distance chapter, "The Affective Taxonomy measures observable formation … and nothing more. It does not measure, and must never be read as measuring, a person's standing before God." Someone on this project has read the same warnings I would have handed them.

### Findings

So my findings are smaller than I expected, and they are about *where the reader meets things*, not whether the right things are present.

**The measurement temptation is still the real one — not because the guards are missing, but because of where they live.** What you measure, you manage; and what you manage long enough, you begin to think you manufacture. The guards I praised live *inside the instruments* — in the facilitator's manual, in the chapter that introduces the taxonomy. But the tired believer holding the book meets "Affective Level 3," "Tell-Rate," "spiritual distance," the causal loops — the *vocabulary of a dashboard* — and may form a scoreboard in the heart long before reaching the paragraph that says *this was never a scoreboard.* I am not asking you to take out the instruments. I am asking that the guard reliably **arrive before the metric** in the reader's actual path through the page, not merely beside it in the architecture. **(MED.)**

**Give the dry one a single door.** Because the dry-season word is in seven good places, it is in no *one* place — there is no single, named home a pastor can point a desolate person to and say "start here." A reader in the dark will not find seven scattered passages; he will find the one chapter he happens to open. I would gather a **named canonical "slow door in"** — one short reading the dry, the new, and the weary are routed to first — and let it link out to the seven. The content exists. This is about a threshold and a signpost, not new writing. **(MED — the "slow door in.")**

**Mind the pace.** Three volumes, a testing framework, six governance documents, a periodic table, appendices in field theory. It is magnificent. It is also *a great deal* — and the person this work is finally *for* is "a person learning to hear God," who may have one tired hour on a weeknight. The author's own Granddaddy note on the causal loops — *amazed at the simplicity* — names the goal exactly. Guard the simplicity. The cure is not to cut the scholarship but to mark, unmistakably, the simple path *through* it for the one who will never open Volume 3. **(Overlaps the slow door.)**

### Biggest concern

**It may be more architecture than a tired soul can pray.** Everything true and tender is here — but it is housed in an edifice, and edifices can intimidate the very people they were built to shelter. The remedy is a *door*, plainly marked and low to the ground, not a smaller house.

## III. John Polkinghorne — the physicist-priest

*Reading as a theoretical physicist who spent twenty-five years in the field before ordination, and the rest of a life arguing that a rational world is the creation of a rational and personal God — and that one must be exact about what physics can and cannot be made to say.*

### Affirmations

The **core is true and I affirm it gladly.** That creation proceeds through the rational Word, and that this is *why* the world is deeply intelligible — open to mathematical description at all — is mainstream Christian theology and, to my mind, one of the strongest pointers from science toward a Mind behind the world. The [Grammar of Creation](../../docs/volume-3-quantitative-framework/the-grammar-of-creation.md) has this exactly right, and its further insistence that the ordering Logos is **personal** — refusing the slide into Tegmark's "the universe simply *is* a mathematical object" — is precisely the discrimination that matters. A mathematical structure does not love anyone. The Logos of John 1 does. Holding that line is the whole game, and the project holds it.

I affirm, too, the candour of the [note before we begin](../../docs/volume-3-quantitative-framework/note-before-we-begin.md): "structural analogy rather than a literal claim," "the certainty ratings in this volume will be lower," the analogy-class discipline. A physicist could ask for no more honest a preface.

### Findings

Now I must do what a physicist on a panel is *for*, which is to hold the analogy to account.

**TFT is borrowed vocabulary, not physics — and should say so even more plainly than it does.** I read [TFT Explained](../../docs/volume-3-quantitative-framework/appendix-tft-explained.md) and [TFT Interpreted Analogies](../../docs/volume-3-quantitative-framework/appendix-tft-interpreted-analogies.md) closely. Curvature, gauge fields, gauge groups, parallel transport — these are *named* and mapped onto Truth, Goodness, and Beauty by verbal resemblance. But there are **no equations of motion, no dimensional analysis, no conserved quantities actually derived, and — decisively — no quantitative prediction that could come out wrong.** The Beethoven's-Fifth gauge analogy is delightful and it is *entirely* illustrative. None of this is physics; it is physics' *vocabulary* used as metaphor. That is a legitimate thing to do — but the apparatus lends an *illusion* of rigour that the content has not earned, and the careful reader feels it. The project's own [TFT Challenged](../../docs/volume-3-quantitative-framework/appendix-tft-challenged.md) appendix concedes much of this — but, as McGrath notes, that concession is hidden in a PDF. **Bring it into the daylight, and push it one step further than it goes.**

The constructive half: **keep the structural analogies, drop the cosplay.** There is an honest and a dishonest way to borrow from physics. The dishonest way borrows *magnitude* and *equations* you cannot cash. The honest way borrows *topology* — direction, sign, threshold, coupling, attractor, the shape of a landscape — claims of *form* you can actually defend. By that test, the [Role Atlas](../../docs/volume-3-quantitative-framework/the-role-atlas-where-each-law-lives.md) and the [Causal-Loop Set](../../docs/volume-3-quantitative-framework/the-complete-causal-loop-set.md) are **honest uses** — they assert structure (this is an attractor, this loop is sign-flipped, this is a constraint), not numbers. The Lagrangian and gauge-field language is **not** — it asserts a mathematical *form* precise enough to compute with, which is not present. Quarantine the latter; hold up the former as the model of how to do it right.

**F_s is not an equation; name it as the schema it is.** No units; the multiplicative form is asserted, not derived; the functions f, g, h, i are admitted open. The chapter is honest — *Speculative*, "there is no mechanism, there is a Father." The one missing sentence is the plain one: *this is a conceptual schema written in the shorthand of an equation — it says "these conditions matter, jointly, under God's free gift," and it computes nothing.* Say that where the equation first appears, and it stops over-claiming.

**Engage divine action — it would strengthen you, not weaken you.** This is my one *additive* finding. The project asserts, rightly and repeatedly, that G is sovereign and exogenous — "outside every loop." But it *asserts* this; it never engages the hard question I spent a career on: **where is the causal room for God's particular action in a world that genuinely runs by law?** There is a serious literature here — the chaos-theoretic and "active information" openness, the CTNS–Vatican divine-action project (Russell, Murphy, and others). The [Open Trails](../../docs/volume-3-quantitative-framework/open-trails-what-vol-3-cannot-yet.md) chapter names many frontiers but not this one. Even a few honest paragraphs gesturing at it would convert "G is real but not a mechanism" from a *bare assertion* into a *motivated belief* — and motivated belief is the whole posture this volume is reaching for. **(MED.)**

### Biggest concern

**A true and important thing is being undersold by an over-reaching dress.** The real gold here — a rational, personal Creator whose world is intelligible because He spoke it — is partly hidden behind formalism that cannot be cashed. Strip the un-cashable apparatus, foreground the honest structural analogies, and the gold shows the better for it.

## IV. Synthesis

### The convergent headline finding

**All three reviewers, for three different reasons, land on the same place: Vol 3's mathematical formalism over-reaches.**

- McGrath: it risks the **theological credibility** of the tier-disciplined whole.
- Peterson: a dashboard vocabulary can **wound and mechanize** the very soul the work is for.
- Polkinghorne: **it is not physics**, and the borrowed dress hides real gold.

They converge on one action: **quarantine and soften the load-bearing equation/QFT language; keep — and hold up as the model — the structural analogies** (Role Atlas, Causal-Loop Set, field-as-landscape) that all three accept as legitimate. And the single most leveraged move is one the project has *already written and then hidden*: **surface the TFT-Challenged critique out of its PDF and let the analogy-class discipline govern the prose as visibly as it governs the registry.**

### The tension between reviewers — and why it dissolves

McGrath wants *more* rigour (a hermeneutical note; the divine-action literature). Peterson wants *less* apparatus (a simple door; guarded pace). On the surface these pull opposite ways. They dissolve once you see that **both are asking for better *signposting*, not for adding or cutting content**:

- The project's certainty tiers and analogy classes already exist — in the *governance registry*. Make them **visible to the reader on the page** (a tier badge, a one-line "what this is / isn't" at each strong claim). That gives McGrath his rigour-made-explicit and gives Peterson his honest framing — the *same* edit.
- A clearly marked **simple path** through the edifice (Peterson's "slow door") lets the scholar have all three volumes *and* lets the tired believer have a short, safe road — without subtracting anything.

So the resolution is the project's own instinct: **foreground the humility you already practice.** Less new writing than re-placement and signposting.

### The shared affirmation (do not lose this in the findings)

All three reviewers affirm: the **tier-honesty**; the **"no mechanism, a Father"** guarding; the **Grammar of Creation** core; and that **Volumes 1–2 are sound**. Vol 3 is where the work is most exposed — *and the project already knows it and has built the very tools (tiers, analogy classes, the TFT-Challenged critique, the dry-season word) to address it.* The work of these findings is to **finish moves the project has already begun.**
