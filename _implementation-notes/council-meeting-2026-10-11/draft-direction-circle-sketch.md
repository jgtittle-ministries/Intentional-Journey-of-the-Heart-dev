# A Direction Circle — a sketch for the Council

Draft v1 · 1 October 2026 · rides with item 5 (spiritual authority) · for counsel; the decisions are John's

## The question

Since September, three people have been asked to serve as John's spiritual director. Two answered with an honest no and offered relationship in place of the office; the third, Bobby Gore, is still considering. While that waits, and perhaps beyond it, **can the work of direction be carried by a few people together**, so that John is not walking without it?

## What direction does, and where each part could live

| What direction gives | Who could carry it |
|---|---|
| Someone attending with John to what God is doing | **A direction circle** of three |
| Outside eyes on blind spots | The circle; at home and on the bench, the witness questions John already practices |
| Continuity over years | John's own personal log, which stays private; he brings the circle a page of his choosing |
| Prayer for him | The circle |
| Standing to say *stop* | **A named person outside the circle**, still to be found |
| A door out | Someone outside both, still to be found |

## The shape

Three people, once a quarter, about ninety minutes. John brings one page of his own choosing. The circle works in the pattern of the Quaker clearness committee (Parker Palmer) and of group spiritual direction: **honest, open questions only, no advice unless it is asked for**, silence allowed, and the time closed in prayer. At the end, one member says briefly what the circle noticed. What is said stays in the room, and anyone may pass or step away without giving a reason.

**Who might sit.** The people who declined one-to-one direction each offered something smaller: Bill Fairback, friendship; Ryan Hammond, a sounding board and encourager. Both are what a seat in the circle asks for. A third person is still to be found. **No one has been asked**, and each ask would be smaller than the one they declined.

## What it is not

- **Not a replacement for a one-to-one director.** That search stays open. If Bobby says yes, the circle may continue alongside him or stand down; that is John's call.
- **Not oversight of the fellowship pilot.** That ask (Ryan Hammond, with Tom Fremont as backup) is unchanged. This sketch is about John's own soul.
- **Not the keeper.** The AI-kept log is a record and a mirror, never a director. It cannot pray, and it has no standing to say no.

**A trial.** Two quarters, then an honest review: is the circle actually doing the work of direction?
