## The two new terms to write in

### 1. Routinization term **R** — APPROVED DRAFT (Foster breadth folded in)

> **Routinization — the wineskin that holds the wine.** The friction term explains why a *single soul* slides back: γ is a drag on a moving person, and when the driving force recedes and γ dominates, the soul that climbed partway slips into the well it came from. But the record forces a second, distinct question friction does not answer: why do some whole *outpourings* leave a lasting body while others evaporate, though the peak looked the same? Friction acts on the individual; this asks about the *container*.
>
> Scripture states the mechanism before any physics can. When three thousand were cut to the heart at Pentecost, the fire did not simply disperse: "they devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching and the fellowship, to the breaking of bread and the prayers" (Acts 2:42). Within the day the outpouring was *lodged in durable practice* — a body with teaching, table, and prayer. And the Lord had already named the law of it: "no one puts new wine into old wineskins… new wine is for fresh wineskins" (Mark 2:22). The wine is the outpouring; the skin is the structure that must hold it. The sower's rocky soil is the same law read as loss — the seed that sprang up fast "had no root," and "in time of testing" fell away (Luke 8:13). Fast rise, no root, no fruit.
>
> So alongside friction stands a second term — call it **routinization, R**: the mechanism by which the transient kinetic energy of the peak is converted into durable *structure* — offices, doctrine, rhythms, relationships, a body — that holds souls on the Glory trajectory after the outpouring recedes. Where friction *bleeds* energy off, routinization *banks* it. The tide goes out (γ) unless there are banks to hold the water (R). As a fraction, the lasting fruit is roughly the share of the peak's energy that lands in a container:
>
> > **fruit ≈ κ · (energy released at the peak)** — where κ, the capture coefficient (0 ≤ κ ≤ 1), is how much of the outpouring the body's structure actually holds.
>
> This is not a metaphor reaching for evidence; it is the plainest reading of the best-measured revival we have. The Billy Graham crusades were engineered around exactly this term — counselor training, the decision card mailed to a local pastor within a day, referral into a congregation: a *routinization machine*, built because the team knew an unattended decision fades. And the numbers obey. Where the receiving church followed up, retention ran 95–96%; where it did not, a crowd of tens of thousands left fewer than a fifth incorporated into any body a year later. Same peak; different κ. The durable fraction even clustered where relational scaffolding already existed — a friend already in the pew. This is why the fruit-test must name its denominator: "still moving a year later" measured as *private* steadfastness reads high — a soul can hold a true decision alone for a while — but measured as *visible movement in a body*, the honest denominator, it reads low wherever κ was low. **The body-visible fruit is the captured fruit.**
>
> And R is dual-valenced, exactly as inertia (mₛ) was — the danger the sociologists name and Scripture named first. Good routinization is the body acquiring *steadfast mass*: the Acts 2:42 devotion, the wineskin supple enough to hold and stretch. But a wineskin can fail two ways. It can grow **rigid** — structure hardens; Weber called its end the iron cage, and Paul named it two thousand years earlier, "having the form of godliness but denying its power" (2 Tim 3:5); over-routinized, the same mass that once held the wine becomes the brittle skin that bursts at the next filling, the institutional analog of the hardened heart, high inertia now resisting the *next* move of God. Or it can grow **narrow** — a single stream of the Spirit's life held in isolation, which sours into its own perversion (holiness into legalism, ardor into emotionalism, justice into performance); Richard Foster's *Streams of Living Water* names this failure and its cure, that the streams must converge into one "great river." So the healthy wineskin is both **supple** (not ossified) *and* **whole** (not sectarian): structured enough to hold, elastic enough not to strangle, broad enough not to sour. Too little structure and the flood spills (all fire, no form — the un-followed-up revival); too rigid and the wine bursts the skin (all form, no fire — the dead institution); too narrow and it curdles (one stream alone). Every awakening lives or dies in that band.
>
> ***Certainty: Reasonably Inferred.*** *The distinctness of routinization from friction, its dual valence, and the breadth condition are strongly consilient with the record (Weber's routinization of charisma; the Graham follow-up data; Foster's streams; the founding-event-fades-while-movement-endures pattern of Herrnhut, Azusa, and the Vineyard). The capture coefficient κ is offered as a picture, not a measured quantity — the honest status of all the mathematics in this chapter.*

### 2. Multi-modal friction
The Engine chapter's friction γ is one-directional (drag/fade only). The record shows three modes: **(a) drag** (fade/backslide — world, flesh, isolation, as written); **(b) productive pruning** — boundary-drawing that *strengthens* (the Vineyard's MC510, Kansas City Prophets, and Toronto-Blessing expulsion each pruned excess and aided endurance); **(c) stewardship-capping** — a spike deliberately *ended* by leadership (Asbury 2023 closed at 16 days), not friction-decayed. Write friction as having drag, pruning, and stewardship modes.
