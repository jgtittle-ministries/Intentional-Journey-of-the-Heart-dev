# Adversarial Verification — MSM ("Measuring Spiritual Maturity in Hearing God")

*Per the Registry's standing method, each consolidated cross-panel finding was handed to a refuter briefed to break it — defending the paper with its actual text in hand. This review added a second assignment: verify the panel's four headline RESOLUTION claims as rigorously as the challenges. Full read completed.*

## Resolution claims

**R1 (Krathwohl: Handbook II used as intended) — CONFIRMED, with a qualifier.** The paper's core move is genuinely instrument/item classification: each of the six instruments is assigned an operating range ("NCD functions at Levels 1–2," "REVEAL operates at Levels 2–3," "The DPA operates at Levels 2–3," FMS "operates primarily at Level 3," SAI factors "intend to operate at Levels 3–4"), and the constructive section is level-specific *measurement criteria*, not a person-staging rubric. The qualifier: person-staging leaks back in at the pastoral edges ("Group A is at Level 2. Group B is at Level 4"; the directee's "Level 2–3 transition") — exactly F8(c). The ruling stands for the analytic spine; the edges qualify it.

**R2 (Fee: Spirit-agent guard HOLDS) — CONFIRMED.** Both quoted guards exist verbatim: "the taxonomy is diagnostic rather than constitutive... The Spirit's sovereign agency is assumed throughout; the taxonomy recognizes the results of that agency, not the mechanism. Second, all measurement here is measurement of effects, not causes" (Affective Domain section), reinforced in the Introduction ("recognizing the observable results of the Holy Spirit's action... not trying to describe how the Spirit actually causes"). Constructs are all human-side. The only friction is F8(d)'s two receiver-calibration phrases, which strain but do not breach the guard.

**R3 (Willard's tally) — CONFIRMED on three of four; the fourth is PARTIAL.** Self-report-junior: resolved — "self-report has a ceiling of validity that falls below the level of characterological transformation, and... must supplement self-report with external observation rather than depending on it as their primary data source." Instruments-capture-low-levels: resolved — the clustering finding is the paper's thesis. Ware's guardrail: resolved (see R2). Edwards's practice-sign: only *half* resolved — the paper adopts practice-as-chief-evidence ("fruit is observed by others, not self-certified: 'You will recognize them by their fruits'") but does not operationalize Edwards's kind-criteria, which is precisely F4. Willard's tally should read 3.5, not 4.

**R4 (Teresa's inversion load-bearing) — CONFIRMED.** The Self-Knowledge Problem is one of four structural pillars of the ceiling argument: "The most spiritually mature person may actually underreport because their behavior feels unremarkable... those most mature are most aware of their deficiency," anchored to 1 Tim 1:15. The inversion is not decoration; the validity-ceiling case partially rests on it.

## Challenges

**F1 (inversion at the deep levels) — SURVIVES.** Strongest refutation: the paper *does* name the dark night at Level 4 ("God withdrawing felt consolations so the person can hear and obey from a deeper place than feeling," Hab 3:17–18), names a "corporate dark night" for groups, and its Level 4–5 criteria are behavior-keyed (fruit, consistency), which the dry-but-obedient soul still satisfies. But the refutation fails where the finding aims: the Level 3 criteria are explicitly affect-keyed ("When you miss Bible reading for a week, what do you experience?"), and the showcase directee illustration scores felt *relief* as regression with no inversion clause distinguishing purified non-noticing from Level-2 relief. The counterfeit-consoled soul defeats the impression-management control, which targets deliberate faking, not sincere consolation. Foundational for Stage 2 stands.

**F2 (source-discernment absent) — SURVIVES.** The paper's only defenses: discernment appears once as a Level-3 charismatic skill ("distinguish the Spirit's voice from one's own thoughts," 1 John 4:1) — note, *two* sources, not three — and group Level 4 has hearing "submitted to the group for testing" with no test criteria. The good soil is defined as one who "hears the word," silently presupposing whose word. A Level-5-characterized person around a wrong voice is nowhere excluded by the taxonomy's machinery. Foundational stands; scope-plea mitigates blame, not the gap.

**F3 (observer's formation) — SURVIVES, cleanly.** "A trained observer can answer this question in three meetings" and "requiring only a trained observer" appear with zero specification of the training anywhere in the paper. No refutation available: at Level 5 the paper's own prescription makes a person the instrument, and that instrument's calibration is unaddressed. Foundational.

**F4 (kind vs degree) — SURVIVES, with a named but unbuilt defense.** The paper's best protection: Luke 8:15's "honest and good heart (interior matches exterior—no Pharisee gap)" *is* a kind-criterion, explicitly named as a good-soil element, and the Pharisee Problem section shows the author knows the failure mode. But the operational Level-5 criteria — "pervasive, consistent, and predictable," longitudinal observation, community corroboration, cross-context consistency — are all degree-criteria, every one of which the lifelong Pharisee passes (the Pharisees were community-corroborated for decades), and Matt 23:15 defeats the Reproducing group test. The honest-heart element never makes it into the criteria. Foundational.

**F5 (Sower on the ladder) — WEAKENED.** The paper's failure-point framing is explicit ("not descriptions of personality types; they are descriptions of points of failure") and it never claims a person passes *through* soils. But the conclusion's "has diagnosed not only the current level but the specific barrier preventing further growth" does treat soils as ordinal way-stations. Surviving core: add the one-sentence explicit disclaimer; no structural repair needed.

**F6 (negative claim outruns survey) — WEAKENED.** The abstract scopes the claim to the six surveyed instruments, the thesis is flagged as "theoretical and taxonomic... not empirical," and the in-principle argument targets *self-report* specifically — the paper already argues most of the proposed restatement. Also, Fowler's coded clinical interview *is* engaged (rejected as impractical, not invalid). Surviving core: "no existing instrument" (thesis sentence) is still universal; the informant-report/self-other-agreement literature and AAI-type discourse methods are the standing partial answers to the ceiling and are genuinely unengaged — cite and bound the claim.

**F7 (observation not operationalized) — SURVIVES; mostly Stage-2-assignable, one item not.** The "three meetings" / "three to six months" tension is real and appears *within a single paragraph* of the second illustration ("Over six months..." then "took three meetings to produce a diagnosis"). No IRR, drift, halo, or dual-role treatment — fairly Stage 2, which the paper explicitly reserves. Not assignable to Stage 2: "more reliable than any survey" asserts a present-tense empirical property no Stage-1 paper has demonstrated.

**F8 (bundle) — items (a)–(d), (f), (g) SURVIVE at claimed severity; (e) WEAKENED.** (a) The group taxonomy is "proposed... parallel to" with Tuckman cited uncritically; work-team→voluntary-group boundary unstated — stands. (b) Fowler "theoretically reaches the highest levels of internalization" is the category slip alleged; the paper's own Fowler critique never notices the orthogonal axes — stands, minor. (c) Verified quotes above; ordinality presented as given via Krathwohl — stands, and qualifies R1. (d) Both phrases verified ("learning to distinguish the Spirit's voice"; "spiritual gifts operating intuitively rather than effortfully") — mild 1 Cor 12:11 friction, minor. (e) Weakened: the paper pre-empts with "tendencies in common practice, not ceilings imposed by the tradition's theology" plus the ideal-types caveat. (f) The Drucker epigraph ("What gets measured, gets improved," instinct called "sound") does sit against the ending's "partially opaque to measurement by design" — minor rhetorical. (g) Longitudinal pastoral observation plus community corroboration carries no consent/safeguarding guard; the paper names performative trust as a *group* pathology but offers nothing protecting the *observed* — stands.

## Meta

**(a) Foundational vs Stage-2.** None of F1–F8 refute the Stage-1 diagnosis; all four Foundational findings *extend* it. But F1–F4 reach past MSM into IJH's own program: F2 touches the hear-and-obey core most directly (the core presumes the voice heard is God's; V2.Exp7A shows the corpus knows this, MSM's taxonomy doesn't carry it); F1 threatens any affect-keyed SIP/Tell-Rate item (the desolate advancing soul reads as regressing — the staged desolation-dynamics four-way differential is exactly the missing module); F3 threatens Vol 4's EWI (the external witness's formation is the instrument's calibration); F4 threatens fruit-based criteria generally. F5–F8 are text edits and Stage-2 specifications.

**(b) Convergences.** The triple convergence on observer-formation (Ignatian director-annotations, Teresian confessor-examination, Willardian apprenticeship — three unrelated traditions, one demand) is the review's strongest single result and survived verification intact. The double convergence on inversion likewise. Together F1–F4 constitute a de facto Stage-2 specification: inversion clause + source-tests as a peer layer + observer-formation curriculum + kind-criteria operationalized — a sharper Stage-2 charter than the paper's own.

**(c) Corpus coverage.** Every Foundational gap except one has a standing corpus asset: F1 → desolation-dynamics staged research (the inversion module, awaiting John's go); F2 → V2.Exp7A discernment-of-voices; F3 → FC1/Leader Lab companion-formation track (the natural observer-formation venue, itself gated); F8(g) → LANGBERG-2 safeguarding. F4 has no named corpus asset — Edwards's kind-criteria would need importing into Vol 4's instrument work. The Stage-2 vehicle for all of it already exists as Vol 4's pilot-ready SIP-Individual / NRP-IoG / EWI framework.
