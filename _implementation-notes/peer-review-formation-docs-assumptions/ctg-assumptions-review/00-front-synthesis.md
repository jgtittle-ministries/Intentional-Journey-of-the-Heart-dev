# Executive synthesis

**This review produced three landmark resolutions and one new triple convergence — the most consequential single review of the series.** The panel judged CTG "the most pastorally intelligent document in the series" (Charry) and "the pastorally wisest of the three" quartet papers (Edwards) — and then, for the first time, formally closed two of the series' longest-running questions.

## 1. The landmark rulings — all verified

1. **The Sower question is SETTLED.** Snodgrass, who has carried the thread since MSFIG: "the co-present-conditions reading is the best of the three available readings for this corpus's purposes, and I judge it the settlement of the series' Sower question." No region is a stage; none is "further along"; the thorny region remains unfruitful exactly as Jesus says; the parable becomes the mirror the pastoral tradition always made it, and it keeps its warning aimed at the reader (Lewis: "if I am simply the good soil, the parable has nothing to say to me... parables are mirrors, not taxonomies of third parties"). His ATB demand is also met — **the birds are back**, with the right citations at the path. One discipline is textually mandatory: the paper claims the reading "grounds" integration-not-replacement three times, and the verifier confirmed "dissolves" is defensible where "grounds" is not — hold the settlement at *sanctioned pastoral application*, and, in Snodgrass's words, "held there, it will not fall."
2. **The formator finding — the series' central standing finding — is ANSWERED IN PRINCIPLE.** Benedict, who demanded it at CSM: the paper requires the Companion to be "under spiritual direction and inside a Fellowship-of-the-Heart community of their own — companioned, confessed over, prayed for, accountable," grounded in survival, not procedure ("a Companion who engages active spiritual opposition while isolated is not a warrior but a target"), sealed by the sons of Sceva. His ruling, verified at three-quarters: "Principle: answered. Structure: begun... and say so with gladness, for at CSM I had nothing." The residue: no verification or removal mechanism — who confirms the Companion is in fact companioned? Charry seconded: "first document in the series to make this structural."
3. **Charry's whose-flourishing question — substantially answered at the population level.** The wide door ("if you're here because Mom said you had to be, that is a true sentence. Say that"), three equally honored outcomes including *no-and-honest*, the shame/guilt distinction with different clearings, "fruit, not perfection." Her verdict: "this pedagogy was built for the church's casualties, and it shows." She also ruled Level −1 a *recovery*, not an innovation — the ancient catechumenate exorcised its candidates daily — and it "passes my sobriety check entirely."
4. **Duty-before-delight confirmed by three traditions at once** (verified): Lewis's act-as-if, Wesley's means-of-grace-while-seeking, Edwards's doctrine of seeking — all landing on the paper's own texts: "regularity precedes delight"; "Valuing is... enacted in small obedience until it is felt"; "Streaks are not the goal. Returning is the goal."

## 2. The two structural findings

**F2 — WEEK ELEVEN (SURVIVES, Foundational — the review's new major convergence).** Wesley, Benedict, and Charry — the 18th century, the 6th, and the patristic era — independently found the same gap: the cohort ends at the threshold, which is, by the paper's own doctrine ("Responding largely stops when the external structure stops"), the moment of maximum load. Wesley: "the structure must not stop at week ten; it must *transform* — cohort into class, class into band, scaffold into fellowship... Tell me what stands at week eleven, and I will tell you whether this system makes disciples or only makes beginnings." Benedict: "ground opened in a temporary community can be left standing open in the rain." Charry: the ancient church invented *mystagogy* for exactly this season. The verifier's crucial note: **this is a documentation gap, not a corpus gap** — FotH exists as an ongoing fellowship; the paper never says so. The fix is to write the corpus's week-eleven answer into the beginners' paper.

**F3 — WHERE DOES THE NEW BIRTH FALL (SURVIVES in core, Foundational).** Edwards, from his Northampton study: "a cohort may be cleared, scaffolded, and honestly known while every member remains unregenerate... Tended ground must not pass for new ground." The verifier confirmed the paper's *theology* is guarded (the tools/Spirit boundary holds at every joint) but its *pedagogy* never locates regeneration in the arc, treats rocky ground's joyful energy as rootlessness curable by rhythm rather than possibly counterfeit, and lets "parts of me are good soil" reach the possibly-unregenerate participant against Ezek 36:26's *created* heart. Edwards's fix is one Companion's-Handbook page: "watch for the new sense, and test it by nature changed and practice sustained."

## 3. The remaining verified findings

**Foundational (insert-tier): the trough (F1)** — Lewis's law of Undulation, the desolation differential's **sixth** series appearance, now at beginner scale: the clearing-grammar knows obstructed and cleared but not the tide — "a cohort taught tool-to-knot matching but not trough-recognition will dig up its garden weekly... dryness is usually the tide and only sometimes the thorn, and the tide is his friend." The fix is a short differential beside the Rhythm Card, distilled from the parked desolation research.

**Significant:** the thorns Jesus named are cares, *riches*, and pleasures — the paper's thorn-map runs grief, shame, fear, anger, doubt, with mammon only as an idol sub-case (Wesley: "I watched wealth choke more Methodists than sorrow ever did"); and the verbatim overreach "more precise than the parable's surface" must be softened (the knots reading is defensible for the first thorn — *merimna* is Matt 6:25's anxious care — with idols carrying the rest). The three missing inventories (F6, all verified): the baptized imagination (Lewis: "Sehnsucht is the beginner's native tongue, and it is nowhere in the toolkit"); the fuller means of grace (Wesley: no Supper — his converting ordinance for seekers — no fasting, no works of mercy); and the creed (Charry: "the ancients did not only clear ground; they sowed the creed into it deliberately... what the church plants in cleared ground has never been a method. It has been a confession"). Level −1's axis: the right warrant is the parable of the weeds (Matt 13:24–30, "an enemy did this"), and the path's causality should follow the paper's own footnote (the enemy *exploits* the hardness — the fix is native to the text).

**Minor (half already weakened by on-page material):** the church-disappointment thorn species (Lewis's pew as attack surface); everyday calibration for Level −1 ("usually wears carpet slippers"); the going-out arm (Wesley's field preaching — FotH's daily tells exist but aren't cited); prevenient grace in the opposer; counterfeit tests for the experiential weeks; a conviction tool; murmuratio (Benedict: the vice that presents as a wound — though the sin/weight diagnostic is "precisely anti-murmuratio"); renewed consent at week five; a word of awe at the warm door; the sending's sacramental anchor.
