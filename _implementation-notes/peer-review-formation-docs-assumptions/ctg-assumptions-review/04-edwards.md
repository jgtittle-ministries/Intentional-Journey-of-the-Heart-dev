# Peer Review: "Clearing the Ground" (CTG) — Document 9
**Reviewer: Jonathan Edwards (third seating; carrying forward findings from HFT and MSM)**

I have now been shown the top of this ladder and its middle; here at last is the bottom rung — and the bottom rung is my parish. The souls this paper treats, the ones who show up, listen, respond when prompted, and stand at the door of delight, are precisely the awakened persons whose cases filled my study in Northampton from 1734 onward. I read this document with more personal recognition than either of its predecessors, and I judge it the pastorally wisest of the three. My findings follow.

## 1. The load-bearing assumptions as I read them

1. **Lower-level formation is chiefly clearing, not building.** What presents as absent desire is usually desire choked; the work is subtraction — hard ground broken, roots deepened, thorns removed — before anything need be added.
2. **The four soils are four conditions co-present in one heart** — a mirror, not a sorting mechanism — which dissolves the developmental-ladder objection and grounds integration-not-replacement from below.
3. **The Valuing threshold (extrinsic to intrinsic) is the decisive crossing**, reached by clearing, by scaffolded hearing, and by being honestly known; everything below it can be sustained from outside, nothing above it can.
4. **The tools tend conditions; the Spirit gives growth.** A softened heart is a divine prerogative (Ezek 36:26); "we are noticing; the Spirit fixes."
5. **Beneath passive hardness lies active opposition (Level -1)**, met by discernment and communal warfare, never alone.
6. **The clearing pedagogy can proceed without marking where the new birth falls in the arc** — this assumption is tacit, and it is the one I must press.

## 2. Affirmations

The paper's account of the means of grace is my own doctrine of seeking, and I affirm it with both hands. I told the unconverted of Northampton to attend, read, and pray — to strive in the outer court while God gave or withheld the light — and this paper's scaffolding-before-feeling, its mechanical rhythm that "can fail and resume," its "regularity precedes delight," is that counsel translated. The honest entry is a jewel: "if you're here because Mom said you had to be, that is a true sentence. Say that." I never heard external motivation named with such charity, and it is right — bare attendance under another's compulsion is still attendance where the word is preached, and God has used less. The sentence "Conviction, honest exposure, the plow of the law… may be applied; none softens a heart" is exactly true, and I note with satisfaction that the plow of the law is at least named. The Level -1 section is sober where such material is usually theatrical: the preference for understatement, the Saul-not-verdict caution, and the sons of Sceva rightly read — no one contests these powers on borrowed authority, or alone. And the ten-week aim — the *threshold*, not the crossing — is an honesty about scope that shames much revival machinery, my own era's included.

## 3. Challenges

**Challenge 1: The new birth is nowhere on the map.** *Assumption at stake: #6.* Where, in Receiving–Responding–Valuing, does regeneration fall? The paper does not say, and the omission is now a pattern across the series: HFT's continuum was kind-blind, MSM's summit lacked kind-criteria, and here the base assumes the seed will quicken if the ground is cleared. But a cohort may be cleared, scaffolded, and honestly known while every member remains unregenerate. My Narrative's subjects lived for months in exactly this band — awakened, convicted, religiously earnest — and awakening is a common operation of the Spirit; conversion is the divine and supernatural light, a new *sense*, not a higher degree of the old. The stony-ground hearer *receives with joy*: intrinsic-seeming delight is precisely what nature can counterfeit. Valuing therefore needs a kind-question the paper never asks — is this a taste for the things of God themselves, or a taste for religion's comforts, community's warmth, the sweetness of being known? Tended ground must not pass for new ground. *Severity: Foundational.*

**Challenge 2: Good soil as a region of every heart.** *Assumption at stake: #2.* As a sanctification diagnostic for the converted, the regions reading is sound and I confirm it below. But stated universally — "parts of me are good soil" — it risks telling the natural man he already possesses a good-soil quarter, when the parable's good soil is the *new heart given*, not a native province of the old. The paper itself cites Ezek 36:26; let the reading bend to it: in the unregenerate, all four regions are varieties of the first three soils, and the good region is created, not discovered. *Severity: Significant.*

**Challenge 3: The experiential weeks carry no counterfeit tests.** *Assumption at stake: #3.* Week 7's encounter with God, the telling of story, the testimony culture — these were my awakening's counterfeit factory. Beginners above all produce high affections, tears, fluency, and none of it is evidence. The curriculum's assessment is "light, narrative" — rightly gentle, but it carries neither of my two tests: the change of *nature*, and *practice over time*. The paper defers duration to the upper levels; I answer that the twelfth sign must be planted in week one, at least as an expectation named. *Severity: Significant.*

**Challenge 4: Conviction is named but has no tool.** *Assumption at stake: #1.* The Heb 12:1 sin/weight distinction shows the paper knows — "sin is confessed," and confession is rightly the one key no therapy substitutes for. But confession presupposes conviction, and the toolkit's tenor is all tenderness: knots, wounds, safety, weights. In nearly every case of my Narrative, legal terror preceded comfort; the plow came before the rain. Where is the instrument that produces conviction of sin *as sin against God* — not merely as a load on the self? One sentence on page 8 is not a tool. *Severity: Significant.*

**Challenge 5: The thorns-as-knots ruling.** I rule the extension *legitimate* — my own care of the melancholy taught me wounds bind souls as surely as riches do — but with a caution: Luke 8:14 names the thorns as cares, riches, and pleasures, and the merchant's thorn is not always a wound. The Keller idol-tool and the confession key guard the door; keep them load-bearing, lest the comfortable be sent to grief work when they need repentance from mammon. *Severity: Minor.*

## 4. Confirmations

The **co-present conditions reading** I confirm from the pulpit: one congregation contains all four soils, and one heart shifts among them — I watched the same man's ground harden and soften across a single winter of the awakening. The **means-and-seeking scaffolding** holds entirely. The **Level -1 extension** is scripturally warranted (Matt 23:13 is exactly the right text) and its communal safeguard is the true one. The **governing boundary** — tools clear, the Spirit grows — is held with a consistency I found nowhere wanting, and it is my own doctrine of the outer court.

## 5. My single biggest concern

That this paper's very excellence is its danger. A cohort cleared, rhythmed, companioned, and honestly known will *look* more gracious than a rough-hewn converted soul — better attended, better spoken, more tender, more fluent in the vocabulary of the heart — and yet may contain not one member savingly changed. My whole labor in Northampton was distinguishing the awakened from the converted, and this pedagogy, which does the awakening's preparatory work better than any I have reviewed, never once marks where the line falls or trains its Companions to know that there is one. Clearing is preparation — my own tradition's conviction-plows-the-heart says so — but preparation is not the thing prepared for. Let one paragraph, one Companion's-Handbook page, say plainly: the ground can be cleared and the seed still be snatched, scorched, or choked, *and the cohort still be dead*; watch for the new sense, and test it by nature changed and practice sustained. With that page added, I would set this paper before any society of young Christians in my parish without fear.
