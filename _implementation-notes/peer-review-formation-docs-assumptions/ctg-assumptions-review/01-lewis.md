# Peer Review: "Clearing the Ground" (CTG) — C. S. Lewis, reviewer

## 1. The load-bearing assumptions as I read them

1. **Formation at the lower levels is chiefly subtraction.** The beginner's ground is not empty but obstructed; the work is clearing (breaking, deepening, untying) rather than construction, and what presents as absent desire is usually desire choked.
2. **The four soils are four co-present conditions of one heart** — not four sorts of men, not four stations on a ladder — making the parable a mirror rather than a sorting-hat, and grounding integration-not-replacement from below.
3. **The Valuing threshold is crossed by clearing plus small obedience**, not by summoned feeling: scaffold the practice of hearing, remove what mortgages the heart's energy, act on what is shown, and intrinsic love arrives afterward.
4. **The lower levels are communal by necessity** — the knots one cannot see are the ones a trusted other can; the container is the condition of the work, not its packaging.
5. **The adversary is active at the point of reception**, and beneath the hard path lies a Level -1 of active opposition to others' receiving, met by sober warfare, never alone.
6. **The boundary**: tools tend conditions; the Spirit gives the growth.

## 2. Affirmations

Let me say at once that this paper concerns exactly the territory of my whole infernal correspondence. Screwtape's patient is a Level 1–2 man throughout, and the entire strategy of the lowerarchy is prosecuted at precisely these levels — which most formation literature treats as a waiting-room. That this paper descends there "deliberately and at length" is its first virtue.

Specific things done well: the honest entry ("if you're here because Mom said you had to, say that") — Screwtape's first move is always to make the patient dishonest about his own motives, and this closes that door at the threshold. "Returning, not streaks" — exactly right; the Enemy (I mean mine, the real one) delights in the man who abandons the whole practice because he missed a morning, for despair over a lapse does far more damage than the lapse. The sin/weight distinction from Hebrews 12:1 is genuinely useful pastoral surgery. And "we are noticing; the Spirit fixes" is the best single sentence in the paper — it forbids the beginner the ruinous habit of perpetual self-excavation. The Level -1 treatment observes, almost verbatim, my rule about the devils: the paper neither disbelieves nor takes an unhealthy interest, "errs on the side of understatement," refuses theatre, and — via the sons of Sceva — forbids solo heroics. Screwtape would recognize the category and resent the sobriety.

## 3. Challenges

**C1. The missing law of Undulation.** *Assumption at stake: #1 (formation is clearing) and #3 (the threshold is crossed by clearing).* The paper's grammar knows two states of a heart: obstructed and cleared. But the beginner's actual experience is governed by a third thing: the trough. Feelings fluctuate by design — peaks and troughs are the natural rhythm of an amphibious creature, and God relies on the troughs more than the peaks; the prayers offered in dryness please Him best. Now here is the danger: a pedagogy whose master-verb is *clearing* will teach the beginner to read every dry Tuesday as a thorn — a knot to be found, a lie to be named, a wound to be excavated. But the trough is not a soil problem; it is weather, not geology. It is to be *walked through* in obedience, not dug up. A cohort taught to match tool-to-knot but not taught to recognize the ordinary trough will dig up its own garden weekly, and each excavation of a non-existent knot manufactures the very introspective anxiety the enemy wants. The paper needs, somewhere near the Rhythm Card, an explicit differential: *when the dryness is a thorn (a real load, traceable, choking) and when it is merely the tide going out* (normal, formative, and — this is the point — the very condition under which duty-before-delight does its deepest work). The "returning, not streaks" material gestures at this but does not name it. **Severity for IJH: Foundational** — this is the one missing category in an otherwise complete lower-level map, and it touches the whole corpus's account of desolation (I note the desolation-dynamics companion is pending; this finding belongs to it).

**C2. The clearing is all excavation; where is the baptized imagination?** *Assumption at stake: #1.* Every tool in the tool map works on the moral-emotional stratum: grief, shame, fear, anger, sin, lie. All real. But beginners are reached through the imagination before the argument — I was myself; Phantastes baptized mine years before my reason surrendered. The word the Sower sows arrives, in Scripture itself, as *story and image*, and much choked desire is not choked by a knot but starved of any picture of what it is desiring. Sehnsucht — the inconsolable longing that every earthly good awakens and none satisfies — is the beginner's native tongue, and it is nowhere in the toolkit. A clearing pedagogy without an awakening pedagogy risks producing tidy, empty ground. The curriculum's "experiential encounter" week may carry this weight, but the paper does not say so. **Severity: Significant.**

**C3. The pew as attack surface.** *Assumption at stake: #4 (community as condition).* The paper's community is the built container — safe, present, clear, intentional. Good. But Screwtape's most reliable weapon against the new convert is the *unbuilt* community: the neighbor who sings out of tune, the grocer's squeaky boots, the half-finished Christians two rows over who make the patient's faith seem ridiculous to him. The ten-week cohort is a greenhouse; disillusionment with the ordinary congregation is the frost waiting outside it, and it strikes precisely at the Receiving-Responding band. The paper should name disappointment-with-church-people as a standard lower-level hazard — arguably a distinct thorn species — lest graduates mistake the greenhouse for the garden. **Severity: Significant.**

**C4. Level -1's methods are drawn too dramatic.** *Assumption at stake: #5.* The paper's own instincts are sober, but its Level -1 exemplars (Saul breathing murder, kingdom-shutters) are the *spectacular* cases. The safest road to Hell is the gradual one — the soft slope, without milestones. Most actual opposition to others' receiving is mundane: the cynical spouse's raised eyebrow, the friend who reliably changes the subject, the committee that schedules the thing to death. If the Companion's warfare-discernment is calibrated only to Sauls, he will miss the eyebrow. One paragraph conceding that Level -1 usually wears carpet slippers would complete the sobriety the paper already intends. **Severity: Minor.**

## 4. Confirmations

Two of the paper's boldest moves I can confirm without reservation. **The co-present-conditions reading of the soils**: as a reading it is psychologically true — every man is a museum of contradictions, and I have never met the pure "good soil" hearer nor the pure path — and, critically, it *preserves the parable's warning function*, which the four-kinds-of-people reading destroys (if I am simply the good soil, the parable has nothing to say to me). Four regions in one heart keeps the warning aimed at the reader. It is also better literary criticism: parables are mirrors held up to the hearer, not taxonomies of third parties. Ruled sound. **Duty before delight**: the paper's claim that Valuing is "enacted in small obedience until it is felt" is precisely the counsel I gave for years — do not waste time bothering whether you love your neighbor; act as if you did, and you will presently come to. The scaffold of Level 2, honestly maintained, *is* the road to Level 3; feelings follow acts far more reliably than acts follow feelings. The paper's mechanical Rhythm Card, far from being sub-spiritual, is exactly how an amphibious creature learns to love. Confirmed. And the governing boundary — tools clear, the Spirit grows — holds at every joint; I probed for the place where technique quietly claims the harvest and did not find it.

## 5. My single biggest concern

It is this: the paper has given the beginner a superb account of his *ground* and almost none of his *weather*. Every diagnostic in it converts an inner state into a soil problem, and soil problems demand digging. But the moment a young Christian most needs a Companion is the fortnight after the glow fades — when the readings are dust, the prayers are cardboard, and nothing whatever appears to be wrong with his soil, because nothing is. That trough is not an obstruction; it is the syllabus. It is where the creature learns to stand up on legs of his own, to obey without the sweetener, and the prayers offered there please Him best. My fear is that a cohort formed wholly in the clearing-grammar will meet its first real trough and conclude — with the enemy whispering agreement — either that some undiscovered knot must be found (endless digging) or that the whole thing has stopped working (quiet drift, the gradual road, no signposts). One firm page teaching the beginner that dryness is usually the tide and only sometimes the thorn, and that the tide is his friend, would guard everything else the paper builds. The tools are good; the gardener must also be taught not to garden in the dark of the moon.
