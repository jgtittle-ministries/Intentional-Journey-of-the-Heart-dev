# Review of "Clearing the Ground" — John Wesley, second seating

I reviewed the foundation paper (MSFIG) and carried three complaints forward: no weekly examination instrument, no house for the backslider, and the collapse of graduated intimacy into mixed maturity. Now the author descends to the beginners — my people. The awakened-but-unconverted filled my classes for fifty years; I require no introduction to this population. I have read the whole paper.

## 1. The load-bearing assumptions as I read them

1. **Lower-level formation is clearing, not building.** The work is subtraction — breaking the path, deepening the shallow, untying the thorn — and the Spirit alone gives growth.
2. **The four soils are four co-present conditions of one heart**, not four sorts of men nor four rungs of a ladder; the parable is a mirror, not a sorting-house.
3. **The duty-to-desire threshold (Responding into Valuing) is crossed by clearing what chokes desire, scaffolding the practice of hearing, and being honestly known** — and practice may rightly precede feeling.
4. **Everything below the threshold can be scaffolded from outside; nothing above it can.**
5. **There is a Level -1 of active opposition**, met by discernment and communal warfare, and the Companion's own life under direction is existential necessity, not best practice.
6. **The work begins with those already in the room** — the pre-cohort conversation discerns among persons who have somehow been brought to the door.

## 2. Affirmations

1. The **honest entry** is my own admission criterion reborn. I asked of candidates only "a desire to flee the wrath to come" — bare Receiving! — and this paper's "if you're here because Mom said you had to be, that is a true sentence; say that" is exactly right. Named external motivation is the floor of grace's work, not a disqualification. Eight honest yeses over twelve pressed ones: I would have this printed on tickets.
2. **"Regularity precedes delight"** is the doctrine of the means of grace, stated better than most of my preachers stated it. I ordered the awakened to pray, search the Scriptures, and attend the ordinances *while waiting for the faith they lacked*, against every Moravian "stillness" that told them to sit quiet until feeling came. This paper's mechanical scaffold, its returning-not-streaks, its obedience-before-feeling at the threshold ("acting on what God has already shown you is the condition for receiving more") — this is my ground, and I confirm it whole.
3. The **evening question** — "Where did I see God today?" — with "I did not see him" counted a complete entry, is a true class-meeting question, and goes some way toward the weekly examination I found missing in MSFIG. Credit given.
4. **Confession before at least one other** (Jas 5:16), the story told and witnessed, the container rebuilt every session — this is my band meeting, and "every Companion has a Companion" is the rule that kept my system alive when I could not be everywhere.
5. **Saul of Tarsus at Level -1, condition not verdict**; the deliberate understatement against theatrical demonism; the sons of Sceva as the law of borrowed authority — sober, scriptural, pastorally safe. Well judged.

## 3. Challenges

1. **What happens at week eleven?** (Assumptions 3, 4.) My classes were perpetual; the ticket renewed quarterly; the band stood open for those desiring more; and I said plainly that awakening souls without joining them in society was begetting children for the murderer. This paper brings a cohort "to the threshold" of the decisive transition — the very hour intrinsic motivation is *beginning* and external structure is *withdrawing* — and then honors the cohort's ending. But the paper's own doctrine says Responding "largely stops when the external structure stops"! To dismiss a soul at the threshold is to remove the scaffold at the moment of maximum load. This is my backslider complaint from MSFIG met again at the intake gate: the system that clears ground beautifully has no named house to receive what grows there. **Severity: Foundational.**
2. **The thorns Jesus named are riches and cares; the thorns this paper clears are wounds.** (Assumption 1.) I accept — my mature reflection accepted — that grief and shame and the believed lie bind souls. But Mark 4:19 says "the cares of the world and the deceitfulness of riches and the desires for other things," and I watched wealth choke more Methodists than sorrow ever did; religion produces industry and frugality, and these produce riches, and riches choke the word. The paper's one economic remnant is the idol paragraph (Keller on money, sex, power) — a footnote's standing for what the Lord made the headline. If the thorn-map runs grief, shame, fear, anger, doubt — and mammon appears only as a sub-case — the pedagogy has partly traded Jesus' economics for our therapeutics. Restore riches-and-cares to named, first-rank standing in the tool map. **Severity: Significant.**
3. **Where is the going-out?** (Assumption 6.) Every tool here operates on persons already at the door: the pre-cohort conversation, the container, the cohort. But my societies filled because I first submitted to be more vile and preached in the fields to twenty thousand who would never have entered a room like this. The hard path of Level 0 and 1 is mostly *out there*, unhearing, unapproached. A beginners' paper whose clearing pedagogy is wholly in-group has designed the class meeting without the field preaching that supplied it — and will inherit only the pre-cleared. Name the awakening arm, or name honestly that another ministry must supply it. **Severity: Significant.**
4. **The means of grace are truncated to small-group process.** The keyword list says "means of grace," but the toolkit is prayer, Scripture, and conversation. Where is the Lord's Supper? I taught constant communion *even for seekers* — a converting ordinance, not a badge for the converted — precisely for the almost Christian waiting at Level 2 with duty and no desire. Where fasting? Where works of mercy, which I ranked means of grace as surely as prayer, and which do more to carry a Responder across the threshold than one more discussion? The scaffold is right in principle and thin in inventory. **Severity: Significant.**
5. **Prevenient grace at Level -1 is implied but never preached.** (Assumption 5.) Saul is rightly invoked, and warfare is rightly *for* the person. But the discernment question is framed wholly as "is this opposition genuine?" — and never "what is grace already doing in this opposer?" My doctrine is that grace goes before every preacher; Saul's very violence was the goad he kicked against. Add expectancy to the discernment, lest the label foreclose what the Spirit has already begun. **Severity: Minor.**

## 4. Confirmations

The clearing-not-building thesis holds; it is the gardener's true humility. The one-heart reading of the soils is the best resolution of that parable's pastoral use I have seen in this series, and it kills the ladder-pride that ruins beginners. Practice-before-feeling is confirmed by fifty years of my societies. The communal necessity of the lower work — confession witnessed, the doubt spoken, the Companion companioned — holds entirely. And the secret-doubt process is a genuine instrument against the almost Christian: the man with the form of godliness is exactly the one whose recited answers conceal a withheld trust, and naming the real question is how I preached him awake.

## 5. My single biggest concern

I built the largest beginner-machine in Christian history, and its secret was not the intake but the *perpetuity*: the class met every week, forever, and the ticket was renewed or it was not. This paper has written a superb ten-week intake and set its terminus at the exact point my whole system began — the threshold where duty is dying and desire is not yet strong enough to walk alone. A cohort brought honestly to that threshold and then honorably dismissed is a field plowed, cleared, sown — and left unfenced in the week the shoots are tenderest. The author's own taxonomy convicts the design: if Responding stops when the structure stops, then the structure must not stop at week ten; it must *transform* — cohort into class, class into band, scaffold into fellowship — or the clearing so carefully done will be found, at week thirty, growing thorns again. Tell me what stands at week eleven, and I will tell you whether this system makes disciples or only makes beginnings.

— J.W.
