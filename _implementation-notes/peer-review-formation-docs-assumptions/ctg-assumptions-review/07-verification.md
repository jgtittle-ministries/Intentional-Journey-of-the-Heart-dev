# Adversarial Verification — CTG ("Clearing the Ground")

*Per the Registry's standing method, each consolidated cross-panel finding was handed to a refuter briefed to break it — defending the paper with its actual text in hand. Full paper read (21 pp.).*

## Resolutions

**R1 — Snodgrass settlement + tier discipline. VERIFIED, and his caution is textually mandatory.** The co-present-conditions reading is stated exactly as attributed: "four conditions of my own heart at any given moment… four regions of a person's interior landscape," "not which soil am I but what is the current condition of each part." The ATB demand is met — the birds are back at the path with the right citations: "the evil one comes and snatches away what has been sown (Matt 13:19; cf. Mark 4:15)." But his tier discipline is not optional courtesy: the paper claims the reading "grounds the integration-not-replacement account" **three times** (abstract, body, conclusion), and adds "more searching than either… reading." "Dissolves" is defensible; "grounds" claims exegetical warrant for a construct the parable does not carry. The settlement stands *with* the demotion to sanctioned application.

**R2 — Benedict's formator ruling. VERIFIED at three-quarters.** All four texts check out: "not best practice but existential necessity" (abstract); "The point is existential, not procedural… under spiritual direction and inside a Fellowship-of-the-Heart community of their own—companioned, confessed over, prayed for, accountable"; "Every Companion has a Companion… heading for a ditch" (Handbook footnote); sons of Sceva at Acts 19:13–16. The residue is real: the rule is declarative — there is no mechanism anywhere in the paper for verifying a Companion is in fact companioned, or removing one who is not. One scoping note: the existential-necessity claim is made in the Level -1 warfare context specifically; the general Companion rule remains handbook-tier. Answered in principle; enforcement unanswered.

**R3 — Charry. VERIFIED on both counts.** Population-level answer: three honored outcomes "yes-and-ready, yes-but-not-yet, no-and-honest" with decline protected; "a cohort may complete the series and stop, and that ending is honored"; shame/guilt drawn exactly ("I am a bad thing" vs "I did a bad thing"); "The point is fruit, not perfection." And the formator's own life is made structural for the first time in the series — the passage is architecture, not advice.

**R4 — Lewis/Wesley/Edwards convergence. VERIFIED.** "Regularity precedes delight"; "The scaffold is mechanical by design"; "Valuing is not first felt and then enacted; it is, as often, enacted in small obedience until it is felt"; "Streaks are not the goal. Returning is the goal." The paper's texts carry all three traditions' practice-before-feeling doctrine.

## Challenges

**F1 — Undulation. SURVIVES.** Strongest refutation: the Rhythm Card counts "I did not see him today" as *a complete entry*, and the soil-week practice is "no fixing—we are noticing; the Spirit fixes" — both tolerate dry days without pathologizing them. But tolerance is not a differential. The paper's governing diagnostic — "which parts are blocking the fruit," match tool to knot — has exactly two states, obstructed and cleared; nowhere does it teach that some dryness is tide, not thorn. A conscientious cohort applying the tool map to weather will excavate. Foundational stands: the fix is a short differential beside the Rhythm Card. Sixth appearance of the desolation differential confirmed at beginner scale.

**F2 — Week Eleven. SURVIVES, foundational.** The paper convicts itself: Responding practice "largely stops when the external structure stops"; the honest target is "the threshold of the Level 2-to-Level 3 transition"; and then "a cohort may complete the series and stop, and that ending is honored." The sending (week 10) has no stated destination; graduates are never routed into ongoing FotH community — only the *Companion's* ongoing community is specified. Partial defense: Rhythm Card and PROAPT are portable, and "intrinsic motivation begins to take over" implies some internal sustainment — but "begins" is the paper's own word, and by its own doctrine the scaffold is still load-bearing at the threshold. The strongest mitigation is external: FotH exists as an ongoing fellowship, so this is a documentation gap, not a corpus gap — but the document must say it.

**F3 — New birth. SURVIVES in core, with materials for the fix on-page.** Strongest refutation: the paper explicitly warns against "mistaking the lower-level behaviors a person can perform for the inner change only the Spirit can give," cites Ezek 36:26 with "the gardener… cannot soften the heart," and holds the tools/Spirit boundary at every joint. So "unmarked" is too strong as a claim about the paper's *theology*. But Edwards' structural point holds: nowhere in the ten-week arc or the taxonomy descent is regeneration located; awakening and conversion are indistinguishable in the pedagogy; rocky ground's "real, even joyful, energy" is treated as rootlessness curable by rhythm and witness, never as possibly counterfeit; and the regions reading tells every participant — including the externally motivated and possibly unregenerate — "Parts of me are good soil," which is precisely the good-soil-quarter risk against Ezek 36:26's *created* heart. Tended ground can pass for new ground in this arc.

**F4 — Thorns. SURVIVES.** Mark 4:19's cares/riches/pleasures are never quoted; the thorn-map runs grief, shame, fear, anger, doubt, idol, with riches only inside Keller's idol sub-case. The knots reading genuinely fits *merimna*; the indefensible sentence is verbatim: the account "is more precise than the parable's surface" — a corpus reading claiming to out-resolve Jesus' own interpretation. Significant; fix is one calibrating paragraph plus first-rank standing for riches.

**F5 — Tier and axis. SURVIVES (Significant/Minor), with one partial self-correction.** (a) "Grounds" verified thrice — hold at application tier. (b) Level -1 is placed on the soil map by the paper itself ("extends the soil map downward," abstract) while its defining content — obstructing *others'* reception, Matt 23:13 — is a different axis than the parable's hearer-sorting; Matt 13:24–30 ("an enemy did this") is indeed uncited and is the right warrant. Mild defense: the paper includes self-obstruction, which sits on the hearer axis. (c) Causality inversion verified: "it is closed *because*… the evil one comes and snatches" — but note the paper's own footnote already carries the correct structure ("the wound provided the entry point; the enemy provided the interpretation"): exploitation, not causation. The fix is native to the paper's own footnote.

**F6 — Three missing inventories. ALL SURVIVE (Significant).** (a) No imagination: the tool map is entirely excavation (grief, identity, soma, forgiveness, confession, truth); week 7's "experiential encounter with God" is unspecified and cannot be credited. (b) Means of grace: no Supper, fasting, or works of mercy anywhere; the only mild defense — a parachurch cohort cannot administer the Supper — is exactly Wesley's point: the document never even *points* to it. (c) No creed: the arc is container/soil/story/known/confession/PROAPT/encounter/doubt/mission/sending — process throughout, no traditio symboli.

**F7 — Bundle.** (a) Church-disappointment thorn: absent; SURVIVES (minor). (b) Carpet slippers: **WEAKENED** — the paper already says "wittingly or not" and roots Level -1 in the respectable Pharisee of Matt 23:13, not only dramatic Saul; residue = no everyday calibration examples. (c) Going-out: SURVIVES — the pre-cohort discernment presupposes arrivals; week 9's mission discovery is not a recruiting pipeline (FotH's "daily tells" exist in the corpus but not here). (d) Prevenient grace at Level -1: **WEAKENED** — warfare "always for the person and never against them" plus Saul-to-Paul carries part of it; residue = no account of grace already at work in the opposer. (e) Counterfeit tests: SURVIVES — assessment is "light, narrative"; no change-of-nature or practice-over-time sign. (f) Conviction tool: **WEAKENED** — "Conviction, honest exposure, the plow of the law… all may be applied" names it at the hard path; residue = no operational tool in the tool map. (g) Murmuratio: **WEAKENED** — the sin/weight diagnostic ("Sin is confessed. Weight is laid aside") and "only confession" clears unconfessed sin are precisely anti-murmuratio instruments; residue = no treatment of complaint-as-vice inside the honest-entry ethic itself. (h) Mid-series re-reading: **WEAKENED** — a "mid-series pulse at week 5" exists but is assessment, not renewed consent with freedom to depart. (i) Fear of the Lord at the door: SURVIVES (minor) — Safe/Present/Clear/Intentional has warmth without tremendum; the gravity arrives only at Level -1. (j) Sending unlocated sacramentally: SURVIVES — "a sending" with no congregational anchor.
