## Executive synthesis

This is the series' hinge review — the first paper about the **person** rather than the process, and the paper where the series' two great open person-findings (the formator's formation; who tells the founder) had to land. The panel's verdict divides cleanly along a line Gregory the Great drew: the paper answers the **interior** half of the guardian question magnificently — and the **exterior** half not at all.

**The landmark resolution:** the formator's-formation finding — five traditions converging across ten prior reviews on "the instrument at Level 5 is a formed soul" — is **substantially answered by this paper**, and the verifier confirmed every claimed element in the text: hearing-and-obeying as "the developmental spine" (an explicit self-correction of the 2005 Weapons Master paper, made twice in the author's own voice); affective-stage floors that are "not optional and cannot be finessed"; the three-fold scaffolding; "Every Companion has a Companion. **The landmark challenge:** a six-voice convergence, the widest of the series, on a single absence repeated at every altitude — **the exterior hand**. The paper gates the door in but not the door out (Gregory: "the canon of descent is missing"; Benedict: "floors and no trapdoor, entrances and no exit, warnings and no watchman"); no one can remove a drifted Companion, no pilgrim grievance channel exists, and nothing stands above the founder (Fowler: supervision by "a practitioner more mature than themselves" is "empty by construction at the apex"). The verifier confirmed the textual basis exactly: the words *remove, suspend, de-certify, grievance, complaint* do not occur in the paper. Gregory's confirmation section carries the series' most personal witness — the pope who had no structure above him: *"My begging was sincere, and it was not enough, and I knew it was not enough. Do not leave your founder where I stood."*

**And the hear-and-obey core is confirmed an eleventh consecutive time** — with the verifier's new observation that this review's surviving findings do not merely spare the core, they **presuppose** it: desolation matters because God sometimes goes silent on purpose; consent matters because the Creator deals directly with the creature; governance matters because drift is measured against a Voice that does not drift.
