# Adversarial Verification — The Formation Companion Assumptions Review

Verifier's note on method: every finding below was hunted against the full text of `fc-extracted.txt` (v5_5_6, April 2026 draft) with the intent to refute it from the paper's own words. Quotations are normalized from the pypdf extraction's spaced-letter artifacts. Where the paper is silent, I say so; where the corpus (not the paper) holds the answer, I say that explicitly and separately.

## Verdicts on the panel's challenges

**A. The exit/governance convergence** — no verification/removal/de-certification mechanism, no pilgrim grievance channel, no visitation, supervision without teeth (Gregory C1, Benedict C1, Langberg C4, Ignatius C5, Fowler C2-part).
**SURVIVES.** I attempted refutation on three fronts and all three fail. First, the Level III powers are exclusively upward-facing: "certifying Level I, mentoring Level II toward Level III, and helping the Council (or other oversight body) assess whether a particular Companion is ready for a given level" — assessment for advancement, never for descent. Second, the required supervision has no described authority: the three-fold scaffolding exists so "the Companion keeps their own material in their own awareness," and the paper itself concedes the failure mode it cannot catch — capacity "will begin to erode even if their skills-level competencies remain," i.e., the drifted Companion still looks competent. Third, the words *remove*, *suspend*, *de-certify*, *grievance*, and *complaint* do not occur in the paper; the pilgrim has no named channel anywhere in the text. Benedict's formulation is textually exact: floors and no trapdoor. At corpus level the LANGBERG-2 safeguarding architecture (gates, floors, triggers, data governance) is implemented and mirrored, and Council governance drafts (SCHEMA, CONTRIBUTING) exist — but none of the given corpus facts evidence a removal rite or grievance channel, so the "canon of descent" remains a genuine gap even at corpus level.

**B. Who tells the founder** — no structure above the founder; "more mature than themselves" empty at the apex; bootstrap circularity (Gregory C2, Benedict C4, Langberg C6, Fowler C2-part).
**SURVIVES.** The refutation candidates are "everyone directs, everyone is directed, a form of mutual submission" and "Every Companion has a Companion... This is non-negotiable" — but Fowler's construction argument holds against both: supervision must come from "a practitioner more mature than themselves," which is unsatisfiable at the apex, and the Council appears in the text only downstream of the author's pen (consulted on the deferred character section; assessing Companions inside the structure he authored). The bootstrap is likewise unanswered: Level III certifies Level I, but nothing in the paper says who certified the first Level III or who verified the founder's own stage. At corpus level the remedy is in motion — the seven-churches research bearing on who-tells-the-founder is drafted and parked, and an IJH Council exists with drafted governance docs — but drafted-and-parked is not constituted, so the finding survives at both levels until those land.

**C. Consent at the mode-switch** — the capstone illustration shifts a direction session into TPM-style memory work without the pilgrim's renewed consent (Langberg C3, Ignatius C4; Benner C2 adjacent).
**SURVIVES.** The strongest refutation available: the paper requires the Companion to be "transparent with the pilgrim about what they are and what they are not," forbids "authority over the other person's formation," and TPM-style work is prayer the pilgrim must actively cooperate in, so a literally unilateral session is practically impossible. But none of that is consent at the threshold. The illustration reads: "discloses a childhood memory that is clearly active, unresolved, and asking for attention. A mature Companion can shift into TPM-style work for that session" — the *memory* asks; the pilgrim is never asked. The capstone's three named requirements (multi-mode competence, integration, "a nudging from the Holy Spirit") omit the pilgrim's yes, and no sentence anywhere in the paper states a consent-renewal principle for mode changes. The fix is one sentence; the absence, in the paper's flagship illustration of its central competency, is real. No given corpus fact covers this — a genuine gap, and the cheapest one to close.

**D. Desolation differential absent from the common core** (Ignatius C1, Benner C3).
**SURVIVES.** Textually verified: *desolation* appears exactly once in the operative role material, inside the Level II Spiritual Direction mode specialization ("what consolation, what desolation, what is God inviting"); the cognitive core that every Companion must know contains "referral criteria for licensed care" and "how formation actually happens" but no four-way differential (neglect / over-obligation / enemy's desolation / God's stripping). The best refutation — the substrate section carries John of the Cross's dark-night lineage and the hearing-of-Presence mode ("rests in Presence when no direction is given") — supplies raw materials, as Ignatius conceded, but teaches no differential and gives the Level I Companion nothing. The inward half also survives: the spine says an unpracticing Companion's capacity "will begin to erode," and nothing distinguishes erosion from a dark night, so the measurement reads the Companion's most formative passage as regression. Corpus coverage is the strongest of any finding: the desolation-dynamics research containing the four-way differential verbatim is fully drafted, parked dev-only, awaiting the author's go — this seating is the finding's eighth appearance, and the remedy already exists in the corpus's own hand. The paper still lacks it; the finding survives until that draft ships and is cross-wired into the FC common core.

**E. Character gate deferred while the competence ladder is finished; threat model has no predator** (Gregory C3, Benner C7, Ignatius C3, Benedict C2, Langberg C2-part).
**SURVIVES.** The deferral is not merely alleged; it is the paper's own statement: "These will be developed later in a separate paper based on consultations with the Council," while the 3×3 matrix, tool inventory, and credentialing pathways are built to full resolution. The partial refutation — the affective floors are character-adjacent gates, and the deferred section's outline (1 Tim 3, Titus 1, specific character markers) points true — reduces severity but not the sequencing hazard Gregory named: what is measurable is finished and what is weightiest is postponed. Langberg's predator half survives cleanly: every failure the text contemplates is sincere (projection, rescuing, drift, burnout, delayed referral); nothing anywhere in the paper contemplates the helper who seeks the role for access, and the natural home for that screening is the deferred section. Corpus mitigation is real: the paper is explicitly a Speculative-confidence first draft, and the FotH FC1 track is hard-gated on CCA and Virginia legal review before any cohort runs, so the corpus already refuses to train before review. The finding survives as a binding sequencing constraint: no certification before the character paper exists.

**F. The therapy line by self-recognition** (Langberg C1).
**WEAKENED.** The paper is less self-referential than the finding claims. The referral trigger list is substantially condition-based, not felt-sense-based: "clinical depression, trauma beyond what a non-licensed helper can competently hold, acute crisis, psychosis, or any condition that meaningfully impairs functioning"; the Level I cognitive cell requires "referral criteria for licensed care"; and — against the claim that no criteria exist for the TPM-style mode — the Level II cognitive cell requires "contraindications for their mode; legal and ethical edges of their specific mode," and TPM-style practice requires TPM's own facilitator certification. What survives: the Stephen criteria are cited but never stated ("Stephen Ministry provides exact criteria"), the one trigger that matters most in memory work ("beyond what a non-licensed helper can competently hold") is delegated to the helper's self-assessment, and Langberg's clinical observation that the overwhelmed helper cannot self-diagnose is unanswered in the text. Corpus note: the LANGBERG-2 safeguarding architecture includes triggers, which is where the operational statement now lives; the paper should state or cite them explicitly.

**G. Floor measurement unspecified** (Ignatius C2, Benedict C3, Fowler C5).
**WEAKENED.** The paper answers more than the reviewers allowed: the floor is "measured as described in HFT" ("HFT provides the operational definition") — a direct delegation to the corpus's measurement document, not an unspecified gesture; the assessors are named in kind (Level III Companions plus "the Council (or other oversight body)"); and Vol 4 treats the Companion's stage "as an explicit independent variable," tying the role to a live measurement program. What survives is real but narrower than "unspecified": the paper never describes the assessment *event* (who examines, on what evidence, against the candidate's motive to present well), Benedict's point that a research variable is not a practice gate stands, and the first-generation bootstrap (who certified the first examiner) is untouched — that residue transfers to B. Fowler's deeper objection about what HFT measures is finding H, not G.

**H. Fowler's orthogonality** — Krathwohl measures depth-of-conviction where Level II/III work requires structure-of-knowing (Fowler C1).
**SURVIVES.** I looked hard for a structural-capacity gate and found none: every floor in the paper is a Krathwohl-lineage internalization stage (Stage 3 "scripture personally binding," Stage 4 "value system reorganized around scripture"), while the capstone the floors are supposed to protect is described in structural terms — holding four modes "as complementary rather than competing," moving between frames "without losing grounding." The paper's own honesty half-concedes the gap: the capstone "cannot be reached through training alone" and Stage 5's modeling is "largely unconscious," but nothing in the gating apparatus measures *how* belief is held as opposed to *how deeply*. Fowler's counterexample — the ardent, wholly-reorganized believer with a tacit, external authority structure — passes the paper's Level II floor as written; the text has no answer. This is a genuine instrument-validity finding against the paper's third load-bearing assumption, and none of the given corpus facts cover it; it belongs in Vol 4's testing program and the Research Register.

**I. Benner's taxonomy critique** — being treated as developable by competency logic (Benner C1).
**WEAKENED.** The strong version does not survive the paper's own text. The paper repeatedly refuses competency logic for the affective domain at exactly the decisive points: the Spirit, not technique, is the agent ("The Companion creates conditions; the Spirit does the work"); staying present is "developed through practice," not instruction; the capstone "cannot be reached through training alone"; the substrate capacities "are formed by years of contemplative practice, or they are not there at all." The taxonomy is used to *specify and gate* what must be present, not to claim a manufacturing process — measuring being is not producing it. What survives is Benner's institutional prophecy, which no text can refute because it is about implementation: matrices become curricula, and the substrate's governing status is asserted in prose rather than in structure. That is a real risk, but it is a governance-of-implementation concern, not a foundational error in the paper's anthropology as written — the two-anthropologies tension is present but confessed and held, in a document that calls itself a first pass.

**J. Convergence-flattens-direction** — the four-traditions claim erases direction's constitutive agendalessness; non-intervention should be the default (Benner C2).
**WEAKENED.** The flattening claim overlooks direct counter-text. The paper names, as what Spiritual Direction contributes to the synthesis, "the default posture of attending before acting" — default posture, the very thing Benner says is missing; the affective core makes non-rescue foundational ("Rescuing is how well-meaning Companions interrupt the Spirit's work"); the psychomotor core includes "when to let the moment continue"; and the third capstone illustration prescribes exactly Benner's remedy — a plateauing group needs "not a new tool but a season of simpler, spiritual-direction-style attending." What survives: the flagship illustration models intervention (the TPM shift) rather than magnificent restraint, and the sentence claiming the traditions do not differ in "what the practitioner fundamentally does or seeks to achieve" remains contestable as stated — direction's agendalessness is arguably an end, not a mode. The paper contains Benner's default; it has not promoted it from posture to rule ("departure requiring justification"). Paragraph-tier fix.

**K. Taxonomies not run through the paper's own discernment protocol** (Benner C4).
**SURVIVES** (as to this paper, with an upstream caveat). Textually verified: the discerning-use section runs Powlison/Trentham on Shadow Work explicitly, flags Big Mind and Kahneman, and states as a rule that "method is never neutral; method always assumes an answer to what the person is, how change happens, and who the agent is" — and then the educational-psychology framework that structures the entire paper is never examined under its own rule. The one available refutation is jurisdictional: the taxonomies and the protocols both arrive from TA ("consistent with the three-taxonomy framework proposed in TA"), so the discernment may have been performed upstream; nothing in this paper or the given corpus facts shows that it was. Until that is shown or written, the paper is in visible non-compliance with its own stated discipline. The remedy is a section, not a rebuild — the finding is procedural, not foundational.

**L. Notes without governance** (Langberg C5).
**WEAKENED.** The alleged self-contradiction is softer than claimed: "They do not take clinical notes" governs the clinical register, while the psychomotor core's "Reflection, notes, and supervision between sessions" describes the Companion's self-review ("what the Companion themselves was doing that helped or hindered"), whose *results* go to supervision — and confidentiality is required at clinician-grade discipline "with the specific exceptions... clearly understood in advance." What survives: session reflection inevitably contains the pilgrim's disclosures, supervision transmits them to a third party, and the text never tells the pilgrim either fact, nor specifies what is written, where it lives, who may see it, or what is destroyed when companioning ends. The corpus already owns the governing principle — the MSM surveillance guard (formation data must never become a management tool) and LANGBERG-2's data governance — so this is a corpus-answered, paper-lacking finding: WEAKENED at corpus level; the paper needs the cross-reference and the pilgrim-disclosure sentence.

**M. Level I floor not protective** (Langberg C7).
**WEAKENED.** The floor is not purely spiritual as claimed: the Level I cognitive cell of the matrix requires "referral criteria for licensed care," the common core requires knowing "when to refer, to whom, and how," and the confidentiality bullet requires the legal exceptions ("harm to self or others, as a legal requirement") to be "clearly understood in advance" — which entails some reporting-duty knowledge at Level I. What survives: named trauma-recognition training is nowhere required of the people leading Hot Seat, Clearings, and Tell Your Story — tools whose stated purpose is "confidential, heartfelt exposure of a range of emotions, sins, faults, and joys" — and "understood in advance" is asserted without a training requirement to produce the understanding. Corpus note: this is squarely inside the implemented LANGBERG-2 safeguarding architecture's territory (gates and floors); the paper should name the requirement rather than inherit it silently.

**N. Mixed-gender structural risk** (Langberg C8).
**SURVIVES.** Attempted refutation: the Stephen-style mode imports "the institutional practices around assignment, confidentiality, and supervision that Stephen Ministry has developed," and the deferred character section outlines marriage stability and related markers. But the assignment/supervision practices attach to one mode only, and every gender-aware sentence in the text attaches to tool *naming and framing* (Hot Seat safety framing, renaming Guts Commando, Guts training in "how the work lands differently for women and men") — none to the structural questions of where years-long one-on-one meetings happen, who knows they are happening, or what governs contact between sessions, in a paper that is explicitly mixed-gender by design. The corpus's safeguarding architecture may house the remedy, but the given facts do not evidence these specific structural rules, so I cannot credit them. Significant, section-tier; it does not threaten a load-bearing assumption, but it is the kind of silence case files answer too late.

**O. Developmental referral missing** (Fowler C3).
**WEAKENED.** The paper contains the principle Fowler asks for, in its own text: "A participant whose contemplative practice grows beyond what the cohort can hold should be referred to the wider church — to a spiritual director in her own tradition... This is not a failure of the cohort; it is the cohort doing its work." The refutation fails only in scope: that sentence lives in the Exploration OB and covers contemplative-practice growth, while the core role's referral criteria remain exclusively clinical ("clinical depression, trauma..., acute crisis, psychosis"), and nothing trains the Companion to read a directee's stage transition as growth rather than backsliding — the misreading H makes likely. The remedy is to give the substrate sentence a sibling in the core role: paragraph-tier, already half-written by the author.

**P. Cumulative levels hide Companion regression** (Fowler C4).
**WEAKENED.** The map is less accretive than the finding claims: the spine section makes the Companion's stage "not a one-time checkpoint but a continuously developing variable that governs what the Companion can and cannot do," and the paper explicitly models downward movement — capacity "will begin to erode even if their skills-level competencies remain." So the architecture can represent a Companion's decline and ties function to it. What survives is the category error the erosion language creates: the only downward movement the paper names is culpable neglect (stopped practicing), so the disruptive Stage 3→4 transition — or a dark night — has no non-failure category, and a supervisor applying the text as written will read formative dismantling as drift. This residue merges with D and is answered by the same parked desolation research.

**Q. Capstone power concentration** (Gregory C5, Benner C5).
**WEAKENED.** Against "unaudited," the paper supplies one genuine structural control the reviewers under-credited: a Companion II or III must be "in ongoing supervision with a practitioner more mature than themselves" — the certifier's own supervision is a standing requirement, not mere ethos, and "every Companion has a Companion" binds Level III too. What survives: no audit of certification *acts*, no requirement that the certifier's current companionship be verified in the certifying proceeding (Benner's concrete fix), no counterweight to the accumulation of defining-the-moment, dispensing, credentialing, and Council-advising in one person, and no de-certification — which is finding A's territory. The guild-formation risk is real but is the downstream shadow of A and B rather than an independent foundation; it falls when the canon of descent is written.

**R. Minor cluster** — sub-verdicts:
- **Stage-grading as rank (Gregory C6): WEAKENED.** The paper carries explicit anti-rank text at the levels themselves — "The levels are deliberately named so that no level sounds like or remains as a permanent station," "A Companion at Level I is not lesser," "The stage is not an honorific; it is an operational measure" — which is most of the caution Gregory requested. Unaddressed residue: pilgrims learning they are being read on a scale.
- **Referral-as-mode framing (Benner C6, Ignatius C7): WEAKENED.** Ignatius's claim that referral is absent from the matrix is textually wrong — "referral criteria for licensed care" sits in the Level I cognitive cell — and the paper calls referral "the most important mode-switch a Companion can make." What remains is Benner's language point (referral as the role's confession of limits rather than a fifth repertoire item) and referral's absence from the capstone illustrations: stylistic, paragraph-tier.
- **Substrate lacks desolation counsel for the candidate (Ignatius C6): SURVIVES** (minor). "The drift and return is the practice" addresses attention-drift, not desolation; there is no Fifth-Rule equivalent for the candidate three months into silence. Folds into D and is covered by the same parked research.
- **Reproducibility treated as a safety property (Langberg C9): REFUTED** as stated. The paper never offers reproducibility as safety: it is a selection/sustainability principle for Level I tools, while the trauma-adjacent deep tools are explicitly gated out of Level I ("They are not list items for Companion I to attempt after reading a description") behind formal training, supervised mentorship, and credentialing — and the paper itself states the mechanistic risk ("the process is at risk when applied mechanistically without practiced spiritual discernment"). The genuine residue — Level I exposure tools like Hot Seat — is finding M, already ruled.
- **Four-traditions claim rests on one participant-observer (Fowler C6): WEAKENED.** The paper openly owns it: "The synthesis framing is new and has not yet been tested," held "at Speculative confidence," with workshop testing invited. Fowler's live ask — that testing reach the convergence claim, not just the tools, with a disconfirmation criterion — stands as a note to the testing program.
- **Capstone certification by fruits-over-time (Benedict C6): SURVIVES** (minor). The paper says the capstone "cannot be reached through training alone" but never says how its possession is verified; Benedict's remedy (years and witnesses, not a demonstration) is a one-paragraph addition to the certification language.

## Audit of the panel's resolution claims

**R1. The formator's formation substantially answered — VERIFIED.** Every quoted element is in the text verbatim or near-verbatim: hearing-and-obeying is "the developmental spine of the Companion role" that "continue[s] to develop across all three levels"; the floor "is not optional and cannot be finessed. HFT provides the operational definition"; the scaffolding is exact — "every Companion has their own PROAPT practice, their own spiritual direction or supervision, and their own peer accountability group. These three together form the minimum scaffolding"; "Every Companion has a Companion. Every director has a director. This is non-negotiable"; and the substrate specifies prior practice with durations ("Two or three appointed hours, sustained for at least six months... Twenty minutes daily, sustained for at least three months"). Ignatius's assessment ("more specification of the observer's formation than the ten papers before it gave together") is fair on the evidence, and Gregory's split is accurate: the interior half is answered at depth; the exterior half (accountability with teeth) is finding A and is not answered. The resolution claim, as scoped to the interior, is verified.

**R2. Gregory's structural confirmation — VERIFIED**, with one thin leg noted. The four anchors exist in the paper: stage floors gating who may lead (Part 1); the Affective Core / Four Connects on the life the worker must live (Part 2); the mode-switching capstone as medicine fitted to the hearer (Part 3 — "reads what the moment is and responds with the mode that serves the moment"); and the substrate's vigilantia cordis with "a short examen in the Ignatian sense" as the daily self-watch (Part 4). The homology is real and independently assembled. Caveat: the Part 1 mapping rests on the affective floor alone, because the character half of who-may-come is the deferred section — Gregory himself flagged this in C3, so the confirmation and the challenge are consistent.

**R3. The Weapons Master self-correction — VERIFIED.** The paper states it twice, explicitly and in its own voice: the introduction ("the 2005 Weapons Master paper understated the role of the small group facilitator's ongoing internalization of hearing and obeying God's word. That was named as a prerequisite but not explicitly developed") and the spine section ("One move from the original Weapons Master paper requires explicit correction in this paper: the original treated scriptural engagement and hearing-and-obeying God as prerequisites (checkbox items...)... they are the developmental spine of the Companion role"). This is a genuine self-correction, named as such, twenty-one years on.

**R4. Fowler's capstone confirmation — VERIFIED.** The textual basis is verbatim: "Integration is the Stage 4 to Stage 5 movement of the Companion's own Affective Taxonomy journey and experience. It cannot be reached through training alone" — written by the author before any reviewer demanded it, as Fowler notes. The structural elements Fowler maps to conjunctive faith are all in the text: holding modes "as complementary rather than competing," real-time reading of the moment, moving between frames "without losing grounding." The identification of this with Stage 5 conjunctive structure is Fowler's disciplinary interpretation, but the paper independently supplies every feature he cites, including the rarity claim ("Few Companions reach Stage 5. For some, that is as it should be.").

**R5. Hear-and-obey confirmed an eleventh time — VERIFIED.** All three named confirmations are explicit: Benner ("making, I believe, eleven consecutive confirmations across this review series — the hear-and-obey core itself... It is not false"); Langberg ("I can add my confirmation to the ten before me"); Fowler ("The hear-and-obey core stands an eleventh time... The core survives my instruments intact"), with Fowler adding the stage-availability gloss that strengthens rather than qualifies it. I independently checked every surviving finding against the core: A–E, H, K, and N challenge governance, consent, curriculum, and measurement instruments; none disputes that formation is the progressive internalization of hearing and obeying God, and several (D especially) exist *because* the reviewers take the core seriously. The eleventh confirmation stands clean.

**R6. Bilgere provenance — VERIFIED.** The paper handles it correctly at all three touchpoints: the discerning-use section locates the non-Christian provenance in the lineage, not the practitioner ("Shadow Work is from the Jungian and mythopoetic lineages"); the credentialing section attaches the non-Christian-grounded label to Shadow Work Seminars' certification (Cliff Barry's curriculum), not to Bilgere; and the tool inventory and references treat Bilgere as Christian appropriation — "Bilgere's work has mixed-gender applications that are well developed," and the reference note reads "Gateways to God... later integrated with Christian inner healing practice. [Already in Vol 5.]" Nowhere is Bilgere called secular. Benner's review independently confirms the reading ("its Christian appropriation — Bilgere's *Gateways to God*... stands inside the faith"). The convention holds.

## Meta

**1. Foundations vs paragraphs.** Of the surviving findings, four threaten load-bearing assumptions. A and B are, as Gregory said, one finding with two halves, and together they falsify the paper's unstated ninth assumption (Benedict's formulation): that entrance floors plus voluntary scaffolding suffice for a house with no exit procedure. H threatens the paper's third load-bearing assumption directly — the gating variable (Krathwohl-lineage internalization) does not measure the structural capacity the paper's own capstone requires, which means the floors could pass exactly the Companion most likely to harm a directee in transition. E is foundational conditionally: it is confessed and scheduled, and becomes a live foundation failure only if training outruns the character paper — which the corpus's existing gates currently prevent. The remaining survivors are paragraph-to-section tier: C is a principle-level omission with a one-sentence remedy plus a protocol line; D is a section addition whose content already exists in draft; K is a compliance section; N is a structural-safeguards section; the R-minors are paragraphs. Notably, nothing that survives requires dismantling the synthesis architecture — the convergence claim (J), the taxonomy anthropology (I), and the reproducibility principle (R) all substantially withstood adversarial reading.

**2. Convergences.** The exit/governance finding was reached independently by five reviewers across six challenge seatings (Gregory, Benedict, Langberg, Ignatius, Fowler), and who-tells-the-founder by four — reviewers spanning the sixth century to the twentieth, a trauma clinician, two monastic legislators, a pope, and a developmental psychologist. When a Benedictine visitation instinct, a clinical safeguarding instinct, and a stage-theory bootstrap argument all land on the same absent mechanism, the absence is structural, not a matter of reviewer taste. The consent finding (Langberg + Ignatius, with Benner adjacent) is a second, smaller convergence with the same signature: two traditions with utterly different vocabularies (trauma-informed consent; the Fifteenth Annotation's balance-pointer) independently flagged the same sentence of the same illustration. Convergences of this kind are the review series working as designed — they mark where the paper's silence is audible from every direction at once.

**3. Corpus coverage of the surviving Foundational findings.** A (canon of descent): partially owned — the LANGBERG-2 safeguarding architecture (gates, floors, triggers, data governance) is implemented and mirrored, and Council governance drafts exist; but none of the established corpus facts evidence a removal/de-certification rite or a pilgrim grievance channel, so the descent half is a genuine gap. B (who tells the founder): the corpus owns a drafted remedy — the seven-churches research bearing directly on this question is fully drafted and parked awaiting the author's go, and the Council with draft SCHEMA/CONTRIBUTING is the natural constitutional home; the gap is publication and constitution, not conception. D (desolation differential): fully owned in draft — the desolation-dynamics research contains the four-way differential verbatim, parked dev-only; this is the series' most persistent finding (eighth appearance at these seatings) precisely because its remedy sits complete and unshipped. C (consent at the mode-switch): a genuine gap — no corpus fact covers it; it is also the cheapest fix on the board. E (character gate): owned by design — deferred to Council consultation with the paper's knowledge, and the FC1 gating precedent shows the corpus already enforces review-before-running; the predator-screening half has no evidenced corpus home and should be named in the Council's character work. H (orthogonality): a genuine gap and the review's one truly new finding — nothing in the established corpus addresses the structure-vs-content distinction in the AT measurement; it belongs in the Research Register and Vol 4's testing design. The pattern is striking: of the six foundational survivors, four are already answered or half-answered by parked or implemented corpus work. The series' open ledger items — write-the-signs and who-tells-the-founder — are confirmed by this review as the corpus's two real structural debts, and both drafts are waiting on one decision.

**4. The hear-and-obey core.** No surviving finding touches it. Eleven consecutive confirmations, and this review's adversarial pass adds an observation the panel did not make explicitly: the surviving findings do not merely spare the core, they *presuppose* it. The desolation differential matters because God actually speaks and sometimes goes silent on purpose; the consent finding matters because the Creator deals directly with the creature and no Companion may stand between; the governance findings matter because drift is measured against a Voice that does not drift. The core is not the paper's unexamined assumption — it is the standard by which every reviewer measured the paper's failures. It stands.

**5. What this review means for IJH.** The Formation Companion paper is the corpus's hinge document — the first paper about the person rather than the process — and this review found its interior architecture (spine, floors, substrate, scaffold) sound enough that six adversarially-briefed reviewers spent most of their fire on a single absence repeated at every altitude: the exterior hand. The paper answers who may come, what they must be, and how they must watch themselves, more thoroughly than the ten papers before it combined; it does not answer who may say *come down* — to a Companion, to a Level III, or to the founder — and its own doctrine of invisible erosion proves that someone must. The good news carried in this verification is that IJH has already written most of its own medicine: the four-way differential sits drafted, the who-tells-the-founder research sits drafted, the safeguarding architecture is live, and the Council exists to receive the canon of descent and the character gate. What the review asks of the project is therefore not new research but three acts of will — ship the two parked drafts, write the removal chapter, and add the consent sentence — after which this paper's Rule would stand on the ground its reviewers, from Gregory to Fowler, have spent fourteen centuries and one career apiece confirming is real.
