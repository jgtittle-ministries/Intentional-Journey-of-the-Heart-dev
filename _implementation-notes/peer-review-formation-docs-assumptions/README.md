# Formation-Docs Foundational-Assumptions Peer Review Series — Archive

**Series run 2026-07-07 to 2026-07-08 · COMPLETE, 12 of 12.** Dev-only archive; never mirrored to prod.

This series reviewed all twelve IJH Formation Documents with a single question: *what are the basic assumptions this corpus is built on, and do they hold?* It is distinct from the earlier seven-author reviews (archived in `../peer-review-formation-docs/`), which asked whether each paper implied volume changes. Ground rules: one document at a time, panel proposed and approved by John before each run, **no changes to the formation documents themselves** — findings inform IJH foundations only.

## Headline result

**The hear-and-obey core was confirmed twelve for twelve, with zero qualifications** — and upgraded along the way: LOAD-BEARING (Krathwohl, MSF — it rescues the taxonomy from vacuity) and PROTECTIVE (Langberg, MSF — "sheep who know the Shepherd's voice are harder to steal"). The verifier's repeated meta-verdict: the surviving challenges don't merely spare the core, they presuppose it.

## Where the close-out lives

The series close-out and the **final open ledger** are in the MSF verification (§5 of `msf-assumptions-review/07-verification.md`, also in the MSF docx). Biggest standing items: the surveillance guard in writing, consequence design (Campbell's law), write-the-signs (desolation differential, 8 appearances), who-tells-the-founder / canon of descent, Fowler orthogonality, consent architecture, confession/lament/return rows, the least-powerful audit / bad-news test, construct resolution, and a mission-telos indicator. Note: the canon of descent has since been published in CPR's "Who Watches the Shepherds?" chapter.
