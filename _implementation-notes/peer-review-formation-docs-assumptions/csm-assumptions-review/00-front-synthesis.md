# Executive synthesis

**The warmest confirmations of the series, and its two most persistent findings finally named.** CSM drew from the tradition's master cultivators the strongest endorsements any document has received — and the verifier confirmed each against the text. At the same time, the review crystallized what eight documents of challenges have been circling: two standing findings that recur regardless of who is empaneled, both of which the corpus has already substantially answered elsewhere.

## 1. The confirmations — verified

1. **Teresa: "My son, you have written my book."** Her four waters — the labor changing by stage, well-rope to rain — are the paper's thesis, "a thing I proved on my own hide." The verifier spot-checked her three prescription-comparisons (beginners' scaffolding; restraint at the hinge; receiving in the deep waters) and all three hold.
2. **Ignatius: "the paper's core move carries my imprimatur."** His annotations (adapt the Exercises to the disposition; the Weeks as stages) are level-appropriate pedagogy four centuries before the name. Verified too: his MSM findings receive partial answers here — the time barrier as "a duration to be endured," the dark night "received, not programmed," and group-scale testing ("one member receives, another tests").
3. **Willard: "This is the paper I have been asking this series to write."** The boundary sentence — *formation tends conditions; the Spirit gives the growth* — **resolves his standing indirection concern, substantially** (verified; with the correction that the causal-verb lapses number three, not one — a copy-edit list, not a doctrine). He called the duty-to-desire treatment "the finest thing in the eight documents": "The will to desire is not yet desire; one cannot, by deciding, make oneself delight." And the wrong-intervention warning — "plowing a heart that is already broken and weeping only wounds it" — "alone justifies the framework."
4. **Fee: the guard held in its prescriptive edition** (verified: every prescription cultivates attention, obedience, or testing; none manufactures the voice) — and his Sower ruling marks a series milestone: **the prescriptive use of the soils stands on firmer ground than the diagnostic use ever did**, because Jeremiah 4:3 and Hosea 10:12 make soil-tending a scriptural *imperative*, and footnote 7's concession is "exactly the honest architecture I asked for."
5. **Foster:** the boundary sentence "*governs* here — it does not merely caption"; the traditions-toolkit is his *Streams* argument "executed with the nuance I would insist upon"; and the paper's honesty about his own book's popular fate ("a Level-2 scaffold mistaken for the building") earned his trust. **Benedict:** the restraint at the hinge is his chapter-64 tempering; the four-traditions borrowing is his own method; "regularity precedes delight" confirmed with both hands.

## 2. The two Foundational findings — and the series-level pattern they complete

**F1 — The desolation differential is missing below the summit (SURVIVES).** Ignatius's central finding, his MSM challenge in prescriptive dress: the dark night is quarantined at the final transition, "as if God withdrew consolation only from the nearly-perfect. But He does not consult the taxonomy." The verifier confirmed the paper's own composite proves the gap — its differential is verbatim two-way (over-obligation vs. neglect) where it must be four-way (neglect / over-obligation / the enemy's desolation / God's stripping), "two of them opposite to what the paper prescribes"; and the lifted obligation collides with Rule 5 (in desolation never make a change). The demand: a **state layer** (consolation/desolation) crossing the transition layer — a second axis, not a paragraph. **The verifier's series note: this finding has now appeared in some form at HFT, SST, 4Cs, MSM, and CSM — the single most persistent finding of the whole series** — and the staged desolation-dynamics research holds a four-way differential (night / drift / depression / acedia) that is Ignatius's demand almost exactly, with a clinical lane his rules lack. The remedy is drafted, held dev-only, awaiting the go.

**F2 — The prescriber is unformed (SURVIVES).** Benedict: "it has built a fine set of tools and no house to keep them in... Who diagnoses the diagnostician's disordered loves?" Teresa, doubled from MSM: "the watcher now holds a prescription pad... Without it, I would hide it from half my confessors." The paper concedes skilled directors are scarce, then rests its prescriptions on precisely such judgment. **The verifier's second series note: the observer/prescriber-formation finding has now surfaced from five distinct traditions across two papers — the quartet's central standing finding.** The venue exists: FC1/Leader Lab is precisely the formator-formation structure. The verifier's summary judgment deserves quoting: *"The panel's two foundational findings are therefore best read not as holes in IJH but as cross-references CSM has not yet drawn."*

## 3. The remaining verified findings

**Significant:** Vision and Intention still missing (Willard's carry-forward lands cleanly — the paper's "the will to desire is not yet desire" is true but overreaches into omitting the *willable* act, the decision to become a disciple; and no section paints the kingdom life whole — "the rich young man did not lack a level-appropriate intervention; he lacked a vision of treasure"). The streams' gaps, all three verified: the social-justice and incarnational streams are absent from a *hearing* pedagogy ("God speaks in the cry of the poor and at the workbench" — Foster), the toolkit has no celebration ("a pedagogy of plow and weed and wait, unaccompanied by festival, forms grim disciples... what I miss in these pages is singing"), and the charismatic stream is credited with its warm-up act while the paper uses its actual gold (1 Cor 12–14) uncredited — 1 Cor 14:1's *commanded* eager desire belongs in the toolkit (Fee: "the paper is so disciplined about what we cannot do that its readers may under-expect what God will do"). The corporate listening-format quietly obligates reception — the holy-silence norm ("nothing" honored as data, not failure) is one paragraph. The anti-shopping guard is absent (Benedict's stability: a matching pedagogy "hands the restless disciple a respectable vocabulary for quitting in month three").

**Weakened or minor (verified):** the unity of the disciplines is *not* missing — the paper states it fully ("the same Word that breaks the fallow ground becomes, later, the honey"); co-presence across levels needs only a category; individual listening does get tests, one transition late; the Spirit-outruns-the-map sentence (Acts 10:44) is genuinely absent; John 10:27 belongs at Level 1 as promise, not the summit as trophy; humility as the directee's floor (Teresa), the receptive posture at the deep transitions ("the soul should let itself be held — receiving is the new labor"), and Teresa's virtue-before-disclosure ordering for groups are each a paragraph.
