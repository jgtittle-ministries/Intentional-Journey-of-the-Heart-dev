# Adversarial Verification — CSM ("Cultivating Spiritual Maturity in Hearing God")

*Per the Registry's standing method, each consolidated cross-panel finding was handed to a refuter briefed to break it — defending the paper with its actual text in hand. Full read (34 pp.); every quoted phrase located in the text.*

## Resolutions/Confirmations

**R1 (Willard — boundary sentence). VERIFIED, with a correction to his count.** The boundary sentence is real (abstract) and is reinforced by a conclusion-level guard the panel underweighted: "where the preceding pages speak of practices that 'cultivate' or 'produce,' the claim is throughout dispositional: the means place a heart where the Spirit may act, never that they compel the growth." Willard's hunt was sound but incomplete: the causal verb is not one lapse but **three** — "converts the group's performative trust… into normative trust," the same verb repeated in the composite ("to convert performed trust into the real thing"), plus "withdraws for a fortnight to **force** distributed ownership." His resolution stands; the copy-edit list grows.

**R2 (Fee — guard + Sower). BOTH VERIFIED.** The hearing-cultivation section prescribes only attention, listening, weighing, and testing; the summit is explicitly "recognized rather than manufactured." No prescription promises the voice. On the Sower: footnote 7 concedes "illuminating analogy and pastoral heuristic, not an exegetical claim" and adds the Heb 3–4/Rev 2–3 license; more decisively, Jer 4:3 and Hos 10:12 are themselves *imperative* tillage texts addressed to covenant people — prescriptive tillage has direct scriptural precedent in a way diagnostic staging does not. Fee's "firmer ground" judgment is correct.

**R3 (Ignatius — partial answers). VERIFIED.** "Not a defect to be removed but a duration to be endured"; "Neither can be programmed; both can be received"; "One member receives, another tests" plus "regular submission of individual leadings to the group for testing." Partial exactly as he says — the summit and the group get what the beginner and the individual do not.

**R4 (Teresa — four waters). VERIFIED on all three spot-checks.** Beginners: "a rule of life, an accountable relationship, a fixed time and place, an assigned reading." Hinge: "The decisive pastoral discipline here is restraint"; the director "lifts the obligation." Deep: received-not-programmed as above. Her confirmation holds.

## Challenges

**F1 (desolation differential below the summit). SURVIVES — Foundational.** Strongest refutation: the idealization caution (barriers "co-occur and recur… seldom the clean ascent") plus suffering appearing as a *plow* at Level 0→1, so darkness is not wholly quarantined at the top. But neither supplies a grammar. The composite directee's differential is verbatim two-way: "depending on whether the trouble is genuine over-obligation or mere neglect" — exactly as charged, and the lift-the-obligation prescription is issued with no test against Ignatian desolation, where Rule 5 forbids the change. The paper's own escape hatch ("a discerning, situational judgment rather than a rule") *presupposes* the discernment it never teaches. The state-layer demand stands untouched.

**F2 (the prescriber unformed). SURVIVES — Foundational.** Refutation attempt: the receding-leader section does form leader *behavior* per transition, and the means-of-grace section insists the taxonomy is "only a map for the gardener's hand." But nothing forms the hand's owner: the examen and direction are aimed exclusively at directees; the paper concedes direction "depends on scarce skilled directors" and then rests its composite prescriptions on precisely such judgment; and it raises its own stakes — the wrong intervention "can harden the very ground it means to soften." The triple-convergence finding is confirmed doubled.

**F3 (common life / unity of the disciplines). WEAKENED.** The unity half is substantially rebutted by the paper's own text: it does not "half-know" it — it states it fully: "The same Word that breaks the fallow ground… becomes, later, the honey… the same prayer… the same fellowship." The frame is same-means-different-work, which *is* the classical unity plus a map. Surviving core: (a) co-presence-across-levels as itself formative has only one clause ("the company of others whose desire proves contagious"), no category; (b) the "clerisy of soil engineers" risk is real but collapses into F2 — the matcher, not the matching.

**F4 (listening without testing). WEAKENED (individual) / SURVIVES (corporate).** Refutation: the individual *does* get an in-practice testing framework — "weighs Scripture, prayer, counsel, and circumstance together" (Prov 3:5–6, Willard) — so "the individual has no tests" is false as stated. Surviving core: it arrives one transition late; listening prayer at the hinge — where suggestibility is being deliberately watered — carries no 1 Thess 5:21 guard. The corporate charge verifies exactly: "shares what each received" grammatically obligates reception; no holy-silence norm anywhere.

**F5 (Vision and Intention). SURVIVES.** Refutation: vision is present at the hinge — "a true sight of God in the gospel," Edwards's apprehension — so the lack is not total. But no section paints the kingdom life whole, and the decision-point charge lands cleanly: the paper argues "It cannot be willed directly, because the will to desire is not yet desire" and lets that overreach into omitting the *willable* act — the intention to become a disciple, which VIM distinguishes from willing desire. Genuine gap.

**F6 (the streams). ALL THREE SURVIVE.** (a) Footnote 13's left-aside list disclaims only the six *framework* families, not the four-tradition toolkit, which is offered as full coverage ("read as pedagogy, that sentence is a toolkit"); social-justice and incarnational streams appear nowhere — though *Streams of Living Water* sits in the bibliography, the paper citing the very book whose missing streams Foster names. (b) No celebration: Foster's own book is cited for chapters 3 and 5 — fasting and simplicity, the ascetic tools; the titular feast is absent; "worship, music, and beauty received" is aesthetic reception, not festivity. (c) Confirmed with teeth: the corporate-hearing section runs on 1 Cor 12:7 and 14:26 — the charismatic canon — while the toolkit credits the stream only with "God is encounterable now" as desire-watering; 1 Cor 14:1's commanded eagerness is absent.

**F7 (stability and time). WEAKENED.** Refutation: time-language is *not* only at the top — "regularity precedes delight," the "slow watering," liturgical formation "on a long horizon… over years"; and the means-of-grace section (instruments, not levers) largely answers the plumbing worry. Surviving core: the anti-shopping guard is genuinely absent — a matching pedagogy hands the restless disciple a respectable vocabulary for quitting in month three, and the paper vows nothing.

**F8 (bundle).** (a) **SURVIVES** — no Acts 10:44/sovereign-compression sentence exists; the idealization caution covers messiness, not the Spirit outrunning the map. (b) **WEAKENED** — the theology Fee wants is already present ("God has been speaking all along to ground that would not listen"); only the John 10:27 *placement* at the summit misleads; a citation-level fix. (c) **WEAKENED** — "not rungs to be climbed by effort but conditions to be tended" does real tempering; a word to the discouraged low self-locator is still missing. (d) **SURVIVES** (minor) — humility appears only as the formator's ("humility of labor"), never the directee's floor. (e) **SURVIVES with trim** — the paper gives continuance-posture ("keep the ground… stay in the field") but not receptive-posture; it rules out the wrong labor without describing being held. (f) **SURVIVES** (minor) — founding norms are "genuine question, honest disclosure, shared discernment," all interaction-practices; Teresa's virtue-precondition ordering (love/detachment/humility before disclosure) is absent. (g) **SURVIVES, doubled** — see R1.

## Meta

**(a) Foundational vs. additive.** Only F1 and F2 threaten architecture: F1 demands a state layer (consolation/desolation) crossing the transition layer — a second axis, not a paragraph; F2 demands a formation-of-the-formator section the paper's own scarcity concession makes obligatory. F5 is a section-level addition (the decision square). F3, F4, F6, F7 are paragraph-to-section additions; F8 is sentences and citation moves.

**(b) Convergences.** The **prescriber/observer-formation finding** has now surfaced at MSM (who is formed to *see*?) and CSM (who is formed to *prescribe*?) from five distinct traditions — Ignatius, Teresa, Willard, Benedict, and implicitly Krathwohl's own observer-training demand. It is the quartet's central standing finding and should be named as such. The **desolation differential** has now appeared in some form at HFT, SST, 4Cs, MSM, and CSM — the single most persistent finding of the whole series, and the only one that recurs regardless of which panel is empaneled.

**(c) Corpus coverage.** Both foundational findings are already substantially answered *elsewhere in the corpus*: the staged desolation-dynamics research holds a four-way differential (night / drift / depression / acedia) that is Ignatius's four-way demand almost exactly — with a clinical lane his rules lack — so F1's remedy is drafted, held dev-only awaiting John's go. F2's venue exists: FC1/Leader Lab is precisely the formator-formation structure (currently gated). F4's missing testing grammar exists as FL.XLVI Interrogating Reality with its Tell-step and as V2.Exp7A discernment-of-voices; the FotH GS handbook already operationalizes group founding-norms. The panel's two foundational findings are therefore best read not as holes in IJH but as cross-references CSM has not yet drawn.
