# Executive synthesis

**The headline is double.** This paper drew the warmest verdicts of the series — Charry: "the best paper of the four... for the first time in the series I watched Scripture *amend the instrument*"; Willard: "the healthiest document of the set"; Wesley blessed it as Deuteronomy 6 in practice — and, at the same time, its single most load-bearing scriptural claim took the review's most decisive verified hit. Four findings survived outright; three weakened to precise cores; and the panel produced two cross-disciplinary convergences as strong as any in the series.

## 1. What the panel confirmed

1. **The hear-and-obey meta-discipline was exegetically confirmed by the field's foremost parables scholar.** Snodgrass, emphatically: "the paper has seized the parable's actual theme. The Sower is *about hearing*... A formation model whose core is hearing-and-obeying God has not imported a foreign concern into this text — it has found the text's own concern." The *shema* backing, Luke 8:15's "noble and good heart," heart-as-soil, fruit-as-test: all confirmed. "The hear-and-obey core of IJH is the parable's own theme, and no exegete can take that away from this project."
2. **The Pharisee counter-example was praised by all six reviewers** — Wesley: "worth the whole paper"; Willard: "the paper's finest structural feature"; Bonhoeffer: "proves the author knows the difference between compliance and transformation, which is the difference between cheap and costly grace." Building the counterfeit into the model as a permanent warning is the series' best self-critique.
3. **Scripture amends the instrument — twice.** Charry's long-standing senior-partner challenge finally met its first working escape routes: the spiral-not-staircase section forces an explicit concession ("the taxonomy needs extension"), and the corporate column is theologically generated — Krathwohl never wrote a group taxonomy; Paul did.
4. **The corporate claim holds triumphantly** (Wesley: "fifty years of classes are its evidence"); Bonhoeffer confirmed the paper treats corporate hearing as its own reality, "more Pauline than most of your sources." Suffering-as-engine, the humility marker, Spirit-primacy at every transition, indirection ("we put ourselves where we can listen... to receive what we cannot achieve"), and measurement humility all held.
5. **Fowler credited the paper with heeding prior cautions** — and called the corporate extension "the boldest move in this corpus," potentially "its most important contribution" once its construct is named.

## 2. What survived adversarial verification

**HIGH — the Sower mapping (F1, SURVIVES; the exegete, the theologian, and the developmentalist converged independently).** The parable's own attached interpretation presents the first three soils as three kinds of *unfruitful* hearing — a typology of outcomes, not a developmental sequence. The paper maps thorny soil (which "proves unfruitful," Mark 4:19) to Stage 3 "Valuing — real growth," and reads 30/60/100-fold as maturity grades the text never ranks (Matthew reverses the order). Charry's summary of the stakes: "the instrument has overruled the Lord's own exegesis of the Lord's own parable — gently, without anyone intending it, because the grid needed a rung there." The verified fix is a re-tier, not a retreat: from "primary scriptural test" to *illustration with thematic confirmation* — the parable confirms the model's center (hearing, heart, fruit) and illustrates its categories; it does not prove the five stages, which stand on Krathwohl, Deuteronomy 28, John 15, and the tradition. Snodgrass: "the paper has made its strongest text carry its weakest claim... a project this honest will be stronger, not weaker, for saying so plainly."

**HIGH — the community-brokenness convergence (F3 SURVIVES + F4 SURVIVES; Bonhoeffer and Wesley found the same hole from opposite sides).** Bonhoeffer: the group column has no doctrine of sin within the fellowship — the Stage 5 group description is a *Wunschbild*, a wish-dream; there is no category for the group whose shattering may be its first true encounter with grace; and confession of sin brother-to-brother — the breakthrough to real community — is absent from the practice table ("in a group that knows it is 'moving toward Stage 4,' who will admit he no longer prays?"). Wesley, from fifty years of machinery: no house for the backslider (declension is a different disease from disorientation and wants different medicine — the penitent path), and the missing measurement instrument is not a better survey but *the pastoral question asked weekly in community* — "How does your soul prosper?" — tested against tempers, words, and works. The corpus already holds much of the answer: FL.XXX communal soul-care, the Tell-step, the Fellowship's confession-adjacent protocols, and the desolation differential (which distinguishes declension from desolation — exactly Wesley's demand).

**HIGH — the group construct (F2, SURVIVES).** "Stage-N group" is undefined: mean of members, modal member, or emergent property? Fowler, from his own ecology work: a group is not a large person; its stage can mask, cap, or outrun its members. The paper's own Stage 5 row already speaks in emergent-culture language — the repair is to name it: **groups have cultures of reception, not stages of soul; the two columns are analogues, not identities.** This matters directly for the Group SIP / Tell-Rate instruments.

**HIGH (architectural) — one map does all the work (F5, WEAKENED but corpus-decisive).** The refuter cleared MSFIG itself (its Bridge honestly scopes the paper to the Affective Taxonomy), but the surviving core lands on the corpus: the soul and spirit taxonomies appear in the Bridge and do zero operational work anywhere — every working claim, table, and application runs on the affective map alone. Willard: "the integrating paper quietly demonstrates what the corpus has not yet confessed... one formation, one map. Say the simpler, truer thing out loud: the affective taxonomy is the map; the soul and spirit papers are lenses — gauges of integration rather than separate engines." This confirms SST's redundancy finding *in practice* and is now the standing architectural question before the whole taxonomy program.

**MEDIUM — hypothesis discipline (F7, SURVIVES).** Roughly seven distinct testable interdependency claims with no confidence-ranking; the leader-ceiling asserted as law is contradicted by the paper's own pneumatology (the Spirit distributes; sovereign work at any level) — "ordinarily" is the proportionate fix. **MEDIUM — the senior-partner question (F6, WEAKENED as Charry herself anticipated):** the skeleton is still Krathwohl's, and Powlison's step two has now been run once (on linearity) but not on the taxonomy's deeper assumptions. **MEDIUM — persistent items (F8, WEAKENED):** person-staging language returns; the contemplative register still shapes the upper stages (the six Streams enter only as extensions); and the group column lacks the lament row the paper's own individual sections believe in — with the specific caution that Schwarz's minimum-factor logic at person-scale would misread the desolate member as the group's bottleneck, "when she may be its formation."

## 3. Tensions within the panel

- **What the group's summit is:** portable culture (the paper, confirmed by Wesley's own history) vs. the community saved beneath the wreckage of its dream (Bonhoeffer). Both can be true; the table currently only says the first.
- **Sorting vs. mixing:** a stage-map tempts leaders to sort by level; Wesley's system mixed maturity while grading *intimacy* (class vs. band) — two dimensions the model currently collapses into one ladder.
- **Where measurement lives:** Fowler wants the group construct defined before any claim; Wesley wants the weekly question instead of the instrument; Charry wants the sufferer protected from the metric. All three converge against the self-scored scale — and toward examination-in-relationship.

## 4. What this review means for IJH, in one paragraph

For the fourth consecutive review, the core held: the hear-and-obey center was not merely spared but positively confirmed by the one reviewer best qualified to take it away. What MSFIG's review adds is an agenda with unusually clear ownership: re-tier the Sower mapping in the corpus's own confidence-language (illustration-with-thematic-confirmation — the fix is one tier-label, and the parable goes on funding the project's center); decide the architecture question Willard has now pressed three times and this paper's own practice answers (one master map, two lenses); name the group construct before Group SIP hardens (cultures of reception, not stages of soul); and write the missing pastoral rows — confession, the backslider's honorable door, the lament row — for which the corpus already owns the material (FL.XXX, the Tell-step, the Fellowship's protocols, the desolation differential). Bonhoeffer's last word is the one to keep: teach the grandchildren that the actual, disappointing, sinful group is the very place where Christ is present — "then the model will describe not only how communities ascend, but how they are saved."
