# Adversarial Verification — "The Heart in Formation" (HFT)

*Per the Registry's standing method, each consolidated cross-panel finding was handed to a refuter briefed to break it — defending the paper with its actual text in hand — before any finding was weighted. The paper is ~2,500 words, explicitly a proposal ("run the pilot"), with one global self-qualification: the taxonomy is endorsed "for all its limitations and need for updating" — never specified. That vagueness matters below: it concedes limitation in general while defending against nothing in particular.*

## F1 — Validation direction backwards; Spirit named, not structural. **SURVIVES.**
**Refutation attempt:** The paper does more than name the Spirit: "the transformation isn't just pedagogical; the Holy Spirit is the agent of growth, and the disciple's part is learning to listen and respond" — an explicit agency claim, anchored to Willard 2012's hearing-God framing. Also, strictly, the mapping sentence runs Krathwohl→Willard ("That maps remarkably well onto... the renovation of the heart"), not Willard→Krathwohl as the finding words it.
**Verdict:** SURVIVES. The Spirit-agency sentence is exactly one sentence, sourced to Rynsburger & Lamport as the finding says, and it leaves no trace in the actual machinery: the five-stage mapping, the assessment design, and the pilot criterion ("evaluate whether the taxonomy framework actually helps coaches... set meaningful objectives") contain no Spirit term, no discernment step, and no theological test of the taxonomy — fit is asserted ("These aren't arbitrary categories — they describe a genuine phenomenology"), never adjudicated. Whichever direction the mapping sentence runs, correspondence is celebrated as validation; the operating system is imported intact.

## F2 — Objectives→persons category shift. **SURVIVES** (remediation smaller than the finding implies).
**Refutation attempt:** The pilot construct is a *single value* — "the progressive deepening of a disciple's trust in scripture" — which is already close to Krathwohl's prescribed value-by-domain scoring, not whole-person staging.
**Verdict:** SURVIVES. The paper's own language repeatedly commits the shift: the levels "describe a genuine phenomenology of how people actually come to own a value" (re-reading an objectives-classification as developmental phenomenology); "a person who is excited to share... is demonstrating Stage 5 formation"; and the payoff claim is "the language to say what stage a disciple is at." Stage 5 is defined whole-person ("their entire orientation has been reorganized"). The single-value test case means the *fix* is nearly free — rewrite person-staging language as value-specific internalization scores — but the finding itself stands on the text.

## F3 — Unvalidated ordinality assumed, not tested. **SURVIVES.**
**Refutation attempt:** The "limitations and need for updating" concession, and the pilot is framed as a test.
**Verdict:** SURVIVES. The ordering is asserted as fact ("aren't arbitrary... genuine phenomenology") with no citation to the Handbook's own hedges, and the pilot's stated success criterion tests *usefulness to coaches*, not whether Receiving→Characterization is actually cumulative and ordinal. An instrument reporting stage-position inherits exactly what the pilot never checks.

## F4 — Kind vs. degree; neutral-learner assumption. **WEAKENED.**
**Refutation attempt (strong):** The paper explicitly *denies* the neutral learner in its anthropology: via Smith, "we are affective animals shaped by what we love... The heart's orientation determines what the mind will do with what it learns"; via Willard 2012, formation "requires a person whose heart is oriented toward God, whose spirit is responsive to the Holy Spirit." The attribution "the taxonomy assumes a neutral learner where Scripture assumes an already-worshiping heart" is contradicted by the paper's stated anthropology — the author already holds the reviewers' anthropology.
**Verdict:** WEAKENED. **Surviving core:** the *instrument* is kind-blind even though the *author* isn't. The continuum starts at "showing up" and ascends smoothly to Job 42:5 with no regeneration discontinuity; nothing in the two-track assessment can distinguish the stony-ground hearer's joyful Level-2/3 response from saving reception, and the scale has no place to register a difference of kind. The paper's anthropology and its measurement grammar are out of alignment — that misalignment survives.

## F5 — No category for grace, rupture, or fruitful desolation. **SURVIVES.**
**Refutation attempt:** Sin appears once — "patterns of sin get addressed systematically" at Organization — and PROAPT opens and closes in prayer.
**Verdict:** SURVIVES; that is the whole inventory. The grammar is uniformly monotone ("continuum of deepening internalization," "progressive transformation"); there is no repentance-as-rupture, no dry-season category, no account of a falling Likert score that isn't regression. Nothing in the text anticipates the dark night; the pastoral hazard (a desolate believer reading their own instrument as a verdict) is unaddressed. The paper offers no hint of a downstream answer — though per project context (not the paper), IJH's later desolation-dynamics work (a four-way differential distinguishing desolation from decline) is precisely the missing module; see Meta.

## F6 — Self-report unreliability; external track must be senior. **SURVIVES.**
**Refutation attempt:** The paper insists the external component "is not incidental" and expects "actual life adjustments that group members and external witnesses can notice" from Valuing onward — behavior, not just self-report.
**Verdict:** SURVIVES. The tracks are structurally parallel ("Assessment runs on two tracks") with no evidentiary hierarchy, and the design *aggravates* the concern: witnesses are "(spouse, close friend) chosen by the disciple" — self-selected, affectively entangled observers. Nothing addresses faking, social desirability, or the beginner-overrates/mature-underrates inversion, which is fatal precisely at the high end the taxonomy cares about.

## F7 — Conscious appropriation vs. pre-reflective habitus. **SURVIVES.**
**Refutation attempt:** The paper cites Smith 2009/2013 as "the anthropological claim that makes this whole project necessary," and its behavioral-evidence emphasis targets more than talk; MOVE's "reflective bible study" is itself a practice.
**Verdict:** SURVIVES — and the Smith citation sharpens rather than blunts it. Every observable marker in the stage-mapping is discursive: answering fact questions, "venturing interpretations," verbal application, being "excited to share." PROAPT is a discussion liturgy; there is no immersion, memorization, or psalmody carrying formative freight. Invoking Smith's anthropology while building an articulacy-measuring discussion process is the performative tension the finding names.

## F8 — Attitude-toward-text vs. fiducia; Barna's cognitive cap. **WEAKENED.**
**Refutation attempt (strong):** The paper's own telos is personal encounter: "Job 42:5 captures it: moving from hearing about God to seeing him directly"; trust in scripture is "the affective gateway to spiritual development"; Willard 2012's hearing-God framing makes the text a place where God speaks. The fiducia-in-a-Person half of the finding is thus partially answered by the paper itself.
**Verdict:** WEAKENED. **Surviving core:** the *operationalization*. Stage 5 is explicitly "Barna's (2003) endpoint," and Barna is defined as assent to "six specific biblical statements" — a propositional worldview checklist capping an affective taxonomy. The paper gestures at seeing God directly but measures agreeing with statements; the John 5:39 warning applies to the instrument, not the author's intent. Fix: operationalize the endpoint the paper already names (Job 42:5), not the one it borrowed.

## Metacognition mini-finding — **SURVIVES.**
The paper says the external-witness component "introduces the metacognitive dimension that Krathwohl (2002) added to the updated Cognitive Taxonomy." In the 2001/2002 revision, "metacognitive" is a *knowledge dimension* — knowledge of one's own cognition (self-knowledge, strategic knowledge). Third-party behavioral observation is the opposite of metacognition; if anything, the *self-report* track is the metacognitive one. The best defense — witness feedback prompts the disciple's self-awareness — is a stretch the sentence doesn't make. Genuine misreading; trivially fixed by deleting the Krathwohl-2002 warrant (the community-grounding rationale stands on its own).

## Meta — What threatens IJH's foundations vs. what needs reframing

**No finding touches the hear-and-obey core.** F1, F4, F7, and F8 actually *converge on* it: Spirit-as-agent, an already-oriented heart, practice-borne formation, trust in a Person who speaks — these are IJH's own commitments, deployed against a paper whose measurement scaffolding hasn't caught up with its anthropology. F8's fiducia correction points *toward* hearing-God, not away.

**Genuinely foundational for the taxonomies program:** F2 + F4 + F7 jointly. If the affective taxonomy requires (a) value-by-domain rescoring, (b) a kind/degree discontinuity, and (c) a habitus layer it cannot see, then the planned soul and spirit taxonomies inherit all three burdens by design. This is the finding-cluster that should gate the "developed taxonomies for the heart, soul, and spirit" horizon. Notably, IJH's later corpus already moved to an "integration-not-replacement" framing for the Affective Taxonomy — the right posture; this paper predates it and should be read (or annotated) under it.

**Foundational for Vol 4 measurement, but partially answered downstream (project context; the paper itself makes no such connections):** F5's pastoral core is met by the desolation-dynamics four-way differential (research staged); any HFT-derived instrument should require that module before deployment. F6's seniority demand maps onto Vol 4's External-Witness Instrument (EWI) design — the fix is making the hierarchy explicit, not inventing it. F1's untiered confidence claims are answerable with IJH's existing confidence-tier convention.

**Reframing/procedure only:** F3 (add ordinality as a pilot hypothesis), F8 (swap Barna for a Job-42:5-shaped endpoint), and the metacognition citation (delete one warrant). F2's remediation is likewise small in practice because the pilot already targets a single value — only the person-staging rhetoric must go.
