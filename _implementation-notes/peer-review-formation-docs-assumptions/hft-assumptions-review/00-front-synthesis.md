# Executive synthesis

## 1. What the panel confirmed — including the two confirmations that matter most

1. **The heart-priority anthropology holds, unanimously.** "Training the heart is not supplemental to training the mind; it is prior to it in the order of transformation" was affirmed by all six — Charry called it Augustine's own instinct; Smith called it the recovery of the church's oldest wisdom; Edwards recognized his *Treatise*'s premise. This is the paper's foundation, and it stands.

2. **The stages are real — and the tradition's own witnesses said so most strongly.** Teresa staked her Interior Castle on it and identified a genuine convergence: Krathwohl's Characterization is "a true cousin" of her seventh mansion, where union shows itself in works, not raptures. (The TA review's Orthodox reviewer had already noted the East's purification–illumination–union map.) A staged account of deepening internalization has centuries of company inside the church.

3. **The instrument's own author ruled the extension legitimate.** Krathwohl's persona — the review's most unexpected confirmation — found the paper's rendering of the 1964 scheme "substantially accurate" (rare, he noted, in secondhand uses), the PROAPT objective-mapping "squarely inside the envelope," and the church application a genuinely interesting restoration: the church possesses exactly what levels 4–5 require and schools lack — long horizons, whole-life scope, and a community licensed to care about operative values. The 1964 indoctrination worry is dissolved, not violated, by a church candid about which values it forms.

4. **The external-witness design was praised by all six reviewers** — the strongest single convergence of either review so far. Edwards: "my twelfth sign in modern dress — the paper should claim the lineage." Powlison: "the best design decision in the paper." Charry: "quietly profound." Whatever else changes, keep it — and per the verified findings below, promote it.

5. **Trust-in-Scripture as the test case is well aimed** (Edwards: the Word is the chief means of grace, and reflective engagement with it is where the new sense will show itself), and the paper's honesty — pilot framing, the Hall alarm faced, limitations conceded — earned credit across the panel.

## 2. What survived adversarial verification

Seven of nine findings survived outright; two were weakened to precise cores. Ranked by verified significance for IJH:

**HIGH — the three-part cluster that gates the taxonomy program (F2 + F4 + F7, all verified).** These three findings interlock, and together they are the review's central result:

- **Objectives ≠ persons (Krathwohl, SURVIVES).** The 1964 taxonomy classifies educational *objectives*; assigning a disciple a *stage-position* is a category shift its authors explicitly declined to make. Persons occupy different levels simultaneously for different values and life-domains — a disciple may organize her finances around Scripture and merely receive it regarding forgiveness of one enemy. The fix is nearly free (the pilot already targets a single value): score *value-by-domain*, never *person-by-stage*.
- **The instrument is kind-blind (Edwards/Powlison, WEAKENED to a sharp core).** The author's anthropology is *not* the neutral-learner one — the refuter showed the paper explicitly holds the reviewers' own view. But the *measurement grammar* hasn't caught up: the continuum ascends smoothly from "showing up" to Job 42:5 with no place to register regeneration as a difference of kind. The stony-ground hearer receives the word with joy — Level 3; pre-Damascus Saul scores Stage 5. Edwards's sentence deserves foundation-level standing: *the taxonomy measures the growth of what God alone plants.*
- **The taxonomy maps the conscious lane; habitus runs in the other one (Smith, SURVIVES).** Every observable marker in the stage-mapping is discursive — answering, interpreting, applying, telling. If the deepest heart-formation is pre-reflective (the paper's own Smith citation argues exactly this), a discussion process measures articulacy while formation happens (or fails) in daily practices nobody assessed. Let the practices carry the freight and the taxonomy read the gauges.

Because the planned soul and spirit taxonomies inherit all three burdens by design, **this cluster should gate the SST review** (Document 3, next in this series).

**HIGH (pastoral, for Vol 4) — no category for grace, rupture, or fruitful desolation (F5, SURVIVES).** The framework's grammar is uniformly monotone; a believer in a dry season will read as regression, and instruments catechize — a falling score will be heard as a verdict. Charry, Teresa, Powlison, and Edwards all landed here independently, and Teresa gave it its name: the framework "knows how to draw water but not how to wait for rain." **The corpus has already built the missing module** — the desolation-dynamics research (the four-way differential distinguishing the dark night from drift, depression, and acedia) is precisely what F5 requires; the verified implication is that no HFT-derived instrument should deploy without it attached.

**HIGH — the self-report track must be explicitly junior (F6, SURVIVES).** The tracks are structurally parallel in the paper, witnesses are chosen by the disciple, and nothing addresses faking or the beginner-overrates/mature-underrates inversion (Teresa: the beginner in consolations marks herself a seven; the saint in the sixth mansion marks herself a two; both wrong). The behavioral/witness track must be senior in print. This maps directly onto Vol 4's External-Witness Instrument design — the fix is making the hierarchy explicit, not inventing it.

**MEDIUM — the discernment protocols still have not been run on the taxonomy (F1, SURVIVES).** The Spirit-as-agent confession is one sentence, and it leaves no trace in the machinery: no Spirit term, no discernment step, no theological test of the taxonomy anywhere in the mapping, assessment, or pilot criteria — fit is asserted, never adjudicated. Powlison's seat on this panel supplied what was missing: his protocol, actually run (§3 of his review), with the verdict *usable as descriptive scaffolding, provided the reframing is made explicit and load-bearing*. His four-step run — the taxonomy's genuine gold, its exposed operating system (neutral learner, no Fall, no grace, pedagogy as engine), what survives reinterpretation, and the verdict — is the single most directly usable artifact of this review, and could be adapted as the documented discernment-pass the corpus has owed itself since the TA review. It is also fair to record: the corpus has already moved to an "integration-not-replacement" framing for the Affective Taxonomy; this paper predates that move and reads differently under it.

**MEDIUM — ordinality is assumed, not tested (F3, SURVIVES).** The Handbook's own authors flagged the cumulative ordering as logical-hypothetical with limited validation. The pilot, as designed, tests usefulness-to-coaches, not the ordering. Krathwohl's procedural counsel: run the pilot as a test of the ordering itself, and it becomes a contribution to the field rather than a borrowing from it.

**LOW / procedural —** the Stage-5 endpoint should be operationalized as the paper's own Job 42:5 telos rather than Barna's propositional checklist (F8, weakened: the author's telos is already encounter; only the operationalization needs the swap); the Krathwohl-2002 "metacognitive" citation is a genuine misreading (verified — metacognition is a knowledge dimension, not third-party observation; the community-grounding rationale stands without the warrant); two canonical-label slips ("Value Set" for "Value or Value Complex"; "selective attention" for "controlled or selected attention").

## 3. Tensions within the panel

- **How much can a map do?** Krathwohl (a filing system that may prove a serviceable map, pending evidence) vs. Teresa (the stages are real rooms, but God opens the doors) vs. Smith (the map's staircase isn't the territory's soil). All three accept staged language; they disagree about what it is language *for* — description, objectives, or gauge-reading. The verified findings favor gauge-reading.
- **The Tell as evidence.** The paper reads eagerness to share as Stage-5 evidence; Edwards lists forwardness about one's experiences among his *no-signs*, and Teresa flags vanity's imitation of zeal. Both, however, accept the remedy the paper already contains: witnesses, behavior, time.
- **Grace vs. design.** Teresa's fourth water is not drawn by any wheel; yet Teresa, Edwards, and Powlison all explicitly permit the framework as a description of how given grace *consolidates*. The panel's shared landing: the ladder describes appearances; God alone works the reality.

## 4. What this review means for IJH, in one paragraph

The heart-priority foundation, the staged shape of the journey, the trust-in-Scripture test case, and above all the external-witness principle were confirmed — several by the most authoritative voices available (the instrument's own author; the tradition's own cartographer of the heart). What survived verification asks the project for three structural moves, none of which is a retreat: score values in domains rather than persons on ladders (and let that gate the soul/spirit taxonomies); make the measurement grammar match the theology the author already holds — a discontinuity for grace, a category for fruitful desolation (the corpus's own desolation research is the module), and the witness track senior to the self-report; and let the practices carry the formative freight while the taxonomy reads the gauges. Powlison's protocol-run supplies the documented discernment-pass the import has owed itself since 1964 was first borrowed. And the hear-and-obey core? The refuter's finding bears repeating: the surviving challenges *converge on* it — Spirit as agent, an already-oriented heart, practice-borne formation, trust in a Person who speaks. The panel did not shake the foundation; it asked the scaffolding to be worthy of it.
