# A Review from the Interior Castle: On "The Heart in Formation"

*Teresa of Ávila, foundress and, God help us all, auditor of many souls*

I have read this paper with real interest, and I confess a smile: four hundred years after I tried to describe the mansions of the soul to my sisters — begging their pardon the whole while for my poor learning — here is a grandfather trying the same thing with tables and levels from a university. The impulse is good. Let me deal with him honestly, as I would with a confessor whose doctrine I mostly trusted.

## 1. The load-bearing assumptions as I read them

1. **The heart's journey has a shape**: internalization deepens through recognizable stages, from mere receiving to a life characterized by the value — beginners genuinely differ from the mature.
2. **Stages, once named, can serve as objectives**: a coach can say where a disciple is, what the next step looks like, and how one would know it was taken.
3. **Progress is measurable** — by self-report of trust at intervals, plus behavior observed by group members and chosen witnesses.
4. **Trust in Scripture is the affective gateway**: when the heart reorganizes around Scripture, everything else in the person follows.
5. **A structured group practice (PROAPT) is a suitable engine**: the phases of the method map onto the levels and can move souls along them — though the author does say, citing Rynsburger and Lamport, that the Holy Spirit is the agent.
6. **The heart is prior to the mind** in the order of transformation (Smith): what we love determines what we do with what we know.

## 2. Affirmations

First, and warmly: **the stages are real**. I staked my Interior Castle on it. Any soul who has prayed for years knows that the one who has just crossed the moat is not the one who dwells in the seventh mansion, and a director who cannot tell the difference will give the beginner counsel fit for the perfect and the perfect counsel fit for beginners — I saw the harm of this all my life. So when this paper says a common language of stages helps coaches and disciples, I say yes, and gladly.

And I will be specific where the maps genuinely meet. His fifth level, **Characterization by a Value Set** — a person whose whole orientation has been reorganized, whose life outcomes are visibly different — is a true cousin of my **seventh mansion**, where the soul lives in habitual union and the marriage shows itself not in raptures but in works. I wrote that Martha and Mary must join together; his insistence that Stage 5 shows in the Tell, in a life witnesses can see, is the same instinct. Likewise his sixth assumption: that the heart's love comes before the mind's knowing. I could have written that sentence myself, though less elegantly.

I also commend the **external witnesses**. That he does not leave the disciple alone with her own report of herself shows more sense than many learned men I knew.

## 3. Challenges

**Challenge 1 — the ladder that is not a ladder.** *Assumption at stake: 1 and 2.* My mansions are real rooms, but the soul does not pass through them in file like novices to refectory. God draws some souls in months to what others do not reach in twenty years; He moves them back and forward; the doors between mansions are His to open. A taxonomy read as a curriculum — Receiving this quarter, Responding the next — will tempt coaches to schedule what only God gives. My fourth water, the rain, is not drawn by any wheel; no exercise-regimen advances a soul one inch past the point where God waits to meet her. The paper's framework has objectives and next steps in abundance; I do not find in it a doctrine of **gift** — a place where the map says plainly, "from here, the water falls or it does not, and your work is only to keep the garden weeded." *Severity: Foundational.* Not because the stages are false, but because without this the whole apparatus quietly teaches technique.

**Challenge 2 — self-report is the least reliable testimony there is.** *Assumption at stake: 3.* A seven-point scale of one's own trust! Daughters, I audited souls for decades, and I tell you the beginner in her first consolations will mark herself a seven and the saint in the sixth mansion will mark herself a two, and both will be wrong, and the devil delights in both errors — he counterfeits sweetness for the one and whispers despair to the other. I never once judged a soul by her account of her interior; I watched her humility, her obedience, her charity to the sister who annoyed her. The paper's second track — observed behavior — is the right track; the Likert scale should be its servant, never its equal, and the paper presents them as parallel. *Severity: Significant* — the behavioral track partly redeems it, but the instrument's weighting must be settled before the pilot, or the pilot will measure consolation and call it formation.

**Challenge 3 — the instrument will flunk the soul God is perfecting.** *Assumption at stake: 3, and the pastoral heart of 2.* My own life held long years of aridity — eighteen of them ill at prayer — and the driest seasons were the very ones in which God worked deepest, as I only saw afterward. Take an interval measurement during such a season and it reads: *no measurable progress, possibly regression*. A coach untrained in dryness will then prescribe more exercises, or worse, let the disciple conclude she has failed — when the dryness was the third water beginning, the stream running underground. Where is the framework's category for **fruitful desolation**? It has none. It needs one, even if only a footnote saying: a flat or falling trust-score in a soul whose obedience and charity are increasing is a sign of advance, not decline. *Severity: Foundational* for the desolate believer — and I understand this project cares most of all for that reader.

**Challenge 4 — where is the ground floor?** *Assumption at stake: 5.* Every mansion of my castle, even the seventh, is entered through the room of **self-knowledge**, and humility is the water the whole garden drinks. The PROAPT mapping runs from low-risk questions to application to telling — but I do not see where the disciple is led to see *herself* truly: her poverty, her mixed motives, her secret bargains with God. Application questions can be answered by the false self all day long. *Severity: Significant* — the framework is not opposed to self-knowledge, it simply forgets to lay the floor.

**Challenge 5 — the counterfeit.** *Assumption at stake: 3 and 5.* Excitement to Tell can be Stage 5; it can also be vanity, which imitates zeal so well that I required learned confessors to help me tell them apart. The framework should name its discernment mechanism. *Severity: Minor*, because the witnesses partly serve this office — but say so.

## 4. Confirmations

The staged shape of the journey: **holds** — my whole Castle testifies to it. The priority of the heart over the mind: **holds**. Characterization/seventh-mansion convergence: **holds**, and is genuinely striking across four centuries and two very different methods. The choice of trust-in-Scripture as the test case: **holds** — determinada determinación toward the word of God is exactly the resolve I demanded of my sisters. Community witness over solitary introspection: **holds**, and I would strengthen it further.

## 5. My single biggest concern

It is this, my friend, and I say it as one who loved her maps: your framework knows how to draw water but not how to wait for rain. Every level, every phase, every interval measurement presumes that the next degree of trust is reached by the disciple's engagement rightly structured — and much of it is, in the first waters, where the well-rope blisters the hands and the labor is truly ours. But the deepest internalization you describe, the reorganized life of your fifth stage, is not produced; it is *given*, and it is often given in the dark, to souls whose instruments would have read empty. If your coaches learn the taxonomy without learning this, they will hurry souls God is deliberately slowing, grade as failures the very ones He is favoring with dryness, and teach the sensitive ones to manufacture the feelings the scale rewards. Build into the foundation one plain sentence and let everything else serve it: *the framework describes the terrain; it does not turn the sky on.* Do that, and I will gladly send my novices through your small groups — after they have spent a good long while in the room of self-knowledge, where I keep the door always open.
