# Peer Review: "The Heart in Formation" (HFT)
**Reviewer: David Powlison (simulated) — foundational-assumptions review, discernment protocol run in full**

John, thank you for letting me read this. You are after the right thing — the heart, not merely the head — and you are honest about borrowing. My job here is to do what the prior review found had not been done: actually run the protocol on the borrowed instrument itself. I do so gladly, and charitably. What follows strengthens; it does not demolish.

## 1. The load-bearing assumptions as I read them

1. **The heart is trainable, and its formation can be given stated objectives and measured progress** — formation without measurement is drift.
2. **The Affective Taxonomy's internalization continuum (Krathwohl et al., 1964) validly maps the renovation of the heart** — that Receiving-through-Characterization describes the same terrain Willard calls the progressive transformation of what a person loves.
3. **Trust in Scripture is the affective gateway** — the right first target, because movement from awareness of the Bible to life organized around it is precisely staged internalization.
4. **The formation site is the structured small group (PROAPT), with the Holy Spirit named as the agent of growth** (via Rynsburger & Lamport).
5. **Heart is prior to mind in the order of transformation** (Smith): the heart's orientation determines what the mind does with what it learns.
6. **A five-dimension taxonomic architecture (heart, mind, body, soul, spirit) is buildable and desirable** — this paper being the heart piece.

## 2. Affirmations

- You refuse to collapse formation into information transfer. That refusal is biblical to the bone (Prov 4:23; Jas 1:22), and rarer in the Christian-education literature than it should be.
- The Hall finding — prayer up, Bible engagement down — is exactly the kind of unsettling observation that ought to drive a project like this. You let the data disturb you. Good.
- The external-witness assessment track is the best design decision in the paper. Hearts are known in community, over time, under load — not by introspection alone. A spouse's report is nearer to biblical epistemology than a Likert scale.
- You name the Spirit as the agent of growth. Many taxonomic proposals never do. Hold onto that sentence; I will press on whether the model lets it do any work.
- Job 42:5 as the picture of Stage 5 is lovely and apt.

## 3. The protocol, actually run

**Step 1 — What the taxonomy genuinely observes well.** Krathwohl, Bloom, and Masia watched real people, and they saw something true: commitment deepens by degrees. A man first tolerates, then attends, then answers, then prefers, then organizes his life, then simply *is* a certain kind of person. This is honest common-grace observation of the surface of internalization — the same terrain Scripture describes when the sown word moves from path to soil to thirty-, sixty-, hundredfold. As *description of observable engagement*, the continuum is real gold. Secular psychology at its best is a sustained, careful attention to what human beings actually do. This is that.

**Step 2 — Its underlying assumptions exposed.** But every observation is made through a gaze, and the 1964 gaze has an operating system. (a) *The self is a neutral appropriator of values*: the learner at "Receiving" is pictured as uncommitted, a blank ledger onto which values are progressively entered. Scripture knows no such learner. Every heart arrives already at Stage 5 — already characterized by a value set, already worshiping something (Rom 1:25). Formation is never first-formation; it is contested *re*-formation, the displacement of ruling loves. (b) *No Fall, no grace, no divine agency*: the model's change agent is instruction; its change site is the classroom; its engine is pedagogy. God does not appear in the machinery. (c) *Values are human constructions*, curricular contents selected by educators — the taxonomy is deliberately content-agnostic and will map the internalization of Marxism, brand loyalty, or Pharisaism with equal facility. (d) *Ascent is smooth and monotonic*: no war, no repentance, no dying and rising, no Psalm 88.

**Step 3 — What survives reinterpretation; what must be rebuilt.** The descriptive vocabulary survives: degrees of engagement are observable and worth naming. What must be rebuilt is the deep structure. Take the crown level: does "Characterization by a Value Set" equal "the law written on the heart"? No — and the difference is the whole gospel. Jeremiah 31:33 has a first-person subject: "*I* will put my law within them, and *I* will write it on their hearts." Ezekiel 36:26 likewise: "*I* will give you a new heart." In the taxonomy, the learner ascends; in the covenant, God descends. The taxonomy describes what internalization *looks like from outside*; it has no account of who does the writing. Rebuilt within a biblical gaze: (i) the agent is the Spirit, and pedagogy is means, not motor; (ii) the starting point is competing loyalty, not vacancy — so "Receiving" must be reread as the first crack in an existing allegiance; (iii) the path includes repentance, relapse, pruning, and desolation — a sawtooth, not a staircase; (iv) the criterion of arrival is fruit the Spirit grows (Gal 5:22), verified in love under trial, not sustained self-reported trust.

**Step 4 — Verdict on HFT's specific use.** Better than most integration attempts I have reviewed. You target trust in *Scripture*, not generic values; you name the Spirit; you assess in community. But the taxonomy is deployed with its operating system largely intact: the five stages function as your developmental model, PROAPT phases are mapped onto them, and your theology of the Spirit sits *alongside* the machinery rather than reorganizing it. Verdict: **usable as descriptive scaffolding, provided the reframing is made explicit and load-bearing** — a paragraph that says plainly: this ladder describes appearances; God alone works the reality; and the disciple's part is Willard's own — listening and responding.

## 4. Challenges

**C1. The neutral learner.** *Assumption at stake: #2.* The taxonomy assumes an uncommitted starting heart; Scripture assumes a worshiping one. A framework that cannot describe idolatrous counter-formation cannot fully describe Christian formation, which is warfare against it. IJH's own catalog knows this; the imported model does not. **Severity: Foundational.**

**C2. Who writes on the heart?** *Assumption at stake: #1 and #4.* If the stages become objectives and the coach the objective-setter, the teacher quietly becomes the functional agent of change — the very move Jer 31:33 forbids. Your Rynsburger & Lamport citation gestures at the correction; the model's architecture undoes the gesture. **Severity: Foundational.**

**C3. The Pharisee scores Stage 5.** *Assumption at stake: #2 and #3.* Saul of Tarsus, pre-Damascus, was maximally "characterized by a value set" organized around Scripture (Phil 3:4–6). Internalization is not regeneration; zeal is not grace. Your instrument, as designed, cannot tell them apart. **Severity: Significant.**

**C4. Linearity versus desolation.** *Assumption at stake: #1.* A disciple in the dark night may report *declining* felt trust while being formed most deeply. The Likert trajectory will misread Psalm 88 as regression. (Your project's own desolation research is the remedy — connect them.) **Severity: Significant.**

**C5. Trust known under trial.** Self-reported trust is weak evidence; trust is revealed when obedience costs something. Your external-witness track already mitigates this — weight it accordingly. **Severity: Minor.**

## 5. Confirmations

- **Heart-priority is confirmed**, not merely assumed: Smith's claim that we are lovers before thinkers is Proverbs 4:23 in academic dress. Your foundation holds here.
- **Trust-in-Scripture as test case is well aimed**: the Hawkins/Parkinson finding converges with the Psalms' own testimony about the word as the instrument of formation.
- **Community-based assessment is confirmed** by the ecclesiology of Ephesians 4: we grow up into Christ *together*, speaking truth in love; your witnesses are that, in miniature.
- **Your own instinct that the taxonomy needs "updating"** is confirmed — and should be taken all the way down, from update to reframe.

## 6. My single biggest concern

Here it is, plainly, from one who loves what you are attempting: my concern is not that you borrowed from Krathwohl — the church has always plundered the Egyptians — but that this particular instrument's silence about God is contagious. A ladder is a seductive thing. Give a coach five stages and a scale, and within a year the stages will have become the goal, the scale the verdict, and the group a place where people learn to perform ascent — a new law, administered kindly, measuring what only God can give. The taxonomy describes the footprints; it does not know the Person walking. Your whole larger project — a grandfather's testimony that God speaks and can be heard and obeyed — is precisely the frame that must *swallow* this instrument rather than sit beside it. Write that into the foundation: the stages are a way of noticing what the Spirit is doing, never a program for producing it; and when a beloved disciple's numbers fall while God prunes him, the coach must know how to say "this, too, is formation." Get that sentence load-bearing, and you may use the ladder with a clean conscience — because everyone holding it will know the writing on the heart is in Another's handwriting.
