# Peer Review: "Bearing Fruit with Patience" (BFP)
## Jonathan Edwards — fourth seating, Document 10

I have read this final paper with more pleasure than any of its three predecessors, and I will say at once why: the quartet ends where true religion ends, in waiting. "We plow and clear and order and wait, and the seed sprouts and grows, we know not how" — this is Mark 4 rightly handled, and it is the temper of a man who has watched God work and not confused himself with the Worker.

## 1. The load-bearing assumptions as I read them

1. **The polarity reversal.** As the levels rise, the cultivator's role recedes; at Characterization it nearly vanishes. The tools tend conditions; the Spirit gives the growth.
2. **Unengineerability.** Characterization cannot be aimed at, for the aiming is itself a deliberation and Characterization is deliberation's cessation. It is given, not made.
3. **The external, unself-conscious diagnostic.** The summit is recognized in a person by others before it is noticed by the person; "no gap between public and private life as reported by external witness."
4. **Three engines only** — time working through obedience, suffering in the form of the dark night, and the Spirit on an eschatological horizon; "the decades are not optional."
5. **Rigidity, not stagnation, is the peril of the top.** Maturity is the integration of all five levels; "a Level 5 person whose Level 1 Receiving has died is not at Level 5; they are rigid."
6. **The summit is corporate and reproductive** — the hundredfold multiplies, and a closed maturity breeds closed disciples.

## 2. Affirmations

First, the misdiagnosis section is pastoral gold. I watched Northampton misread its saints in both directions — the comfortable pronounced gracious, the desolate pronounced backslidden — and this paper's warning that "the same behaviors mean opposite things and the wrong remedy interrupts the deepest work the Spirit does" would have spared souls I knew. To refuse to rescue a person out of the night the Spirit has appointed, and instead to accompany — this is exactly right.

Second, the Rhythm Card's ruling that "I did not see him today" is a complete and honest entry. Brainerd's diary is full of such entries. That the framework counts them as part of the arc and not a failure of it is a mercy the measurement literature rarely extends.

Third — and this I must credit loudly, for I have demanded it through three papers — **the paper finally names the counterfeit at every level.** "Pharisee-Characterization collapse is the settled rigidity whose spiritual life no longer surprises anyone." The quartet has at last admitted that the top of the scale has a false twin.

Fourth, the honesty: "some leaders never reach Level 5; that is honest." It is.

## 3. Challenges

**Challenge 1: The summit is defined by its form, not its matter.** *Assumption at stake: #3, and the whole account of Characterization.* I preached thirteen sermons on 1 Corinthians 13 and ended them in heaven as a world of love, because the definition of the summit is not integration, consistency, or reflexiveness — it is *love*. BFP's Characterization is described almost wholly by formal properties: no deliberation, no public-private gap, settled background awareness, self-replication. "Christlikeness" and "the mind of Christ" gesture at the matter, but the paper never unpacks them, and the gesture will not bear weight, for the question is precisely *what in Christ* the person resembles. A man of great natural sweetness, durable temper, and integrated life exhibits every formal property named here. The discriminator is divine love — love to God for His own excellency, and the labor of love that seeks not its own. Absent 1 Corinthians 13, the kind-gap survives in its subtlest form. **Severity: Foundational** — though, as I shall say below, three genuine kind-marks are present elsewhere in the paper, which is why this is a gap to be filled and not a wall to be torn down.

**Challenge 2: "Recognized by others first" does half the work.** *Assumption at stake: #3.* The criterion has two parts. The *unself-consciousness* is genuinely a kind-mark — it is evangelical humiliation operationalized, for the gracious under-claim and the counterfeit over-claim, and a diagnostic the subject cannot administer to himself defeats the self-advertising hypocrite entirely. But the *corroborating community* is only as sound as its own eyes. The Pharisees were recognized by others first; all Jerusalem called them the holy ones. Where a community is itself closed — and the paper's own gnostic-community warning concedes such communities exist — its unanimous witness to a member's Christlikeness certifies nothing but shared blindness. The criterion needs a rider: the witnesses must include the outsider, the injured, the servant. **Severity: Significant.**

**Challenge 3: "The decades are not optional" — but Brainerd died at twenty-nine.** *Assumption at stake: #4.* He was converted at twenty-one; his whole race was eight years, most of it consumption, darkness, and Indians. Yet I gave his diary to the world as the pattern of holy perseverance, because the pattern was there. The corpus's own hedge — "the Spirit reserves the right to bypass it" — must be allowed to govern the timeline claim too, or the framework has no room for the dying young saint, nor for the thief who was characterized in an afternoon. **Severity: Minor** — the hedge exists; it wants only extension.

**Challenge 4: The dark-night differential is named but not stated.** *Assumption at stake: the misdiagnosis thesis.* The paper says the distinction between depth and regression "requires the discernment of someone who has walked the road" — which, unspecified, is a circle: the discerner is certified by possessing the discernment. John of the Cross gave *signs*: the soul finds comfort neither in God nor in any creature; it remains anxiously solicitous for God, fearing it does not serve Him; it cannot meditate but is drawn to a loving attention. Mark these — they are all *direction-of-desire* tests, kind-tests. The regressing man stops wanting God; the man in the night wants nothing else and cannot find Him. The differential exists in the paper's own cited source and should be stated. **Severity: Significant.**

## 4. Confirmations

The waiting posture holds; the unengineerability holds and is the deepest Reformed instinct in the quartet; the anti-rigidity rule holds and is, I judge, a true kind-criterion in disguise — for the openness to receive a new word like a beginner is my eleventh sign, the gracious soul's *increasing* appetite, which no settled hypocrite sustains. The reproduction claim, which I feared would repeat Matthew 23:15, in fact escapes it: BFP does not offer multiplication as proof of grace but observes that reproduction transmits *kind* — "a closed leader forms closed groups... the closure travels into the next generation." That is the Pharisee's proselyte-making accounted for, not ignored.

**My ruling on the standing finding: PARTIALLY ANSWERED, and more nearly than any prior paper.** Three kind-criteria are present and load-bearing: unself-consciousness (evangelical humiliation), perseverance through the stripping of consolations (costliness against self-interest — the hypocrite's religion dies when the comfort is withdrawn, for the comfort was what he loved), and sustained openness (the enduring appetite). The residue is twofold: the material definition — love — is never named as the summit's content; and the corroborating community is never itself tested.

## 5. My single biggest concern

That the quartet ends with the summit described but not defined. Everything formal is here, and much of it is true and finely said; but "Heaven is a world of love," and Characterization, if it is anything, is that world begun in a person. The paper knows the shape of the hundredfold — reflexive, integrated, open, reproducing — and does not say what fills the shape. Write one paragraph placing 1 Corinthians 13 at the center of Level 5 — that the tree is known by *this* fruit, love that suffers long and seeks not its own — and the three kind-marks already present (humility, costliness, hunger) fall into place as love's evidences rather than free-standing properties, and the demand I have carried through four papers is discharged. The house is built and the rooms are swept; it wants only the fire lit on the hearth.
