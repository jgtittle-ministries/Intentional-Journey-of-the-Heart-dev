# Peer Review: "Bearing Fruit with Patience" (Document 10)
**Reviewer: James W. Fowler — third seating in this series**

I have read the whole of it, and I will say at the outset what I have waited two papers to say: this is the document where the corpus either answered my standing question or dodged it forever. I will rule on that below, plainly.

## 1. The load-bearing assumptions as I read them

1. **The recession thesis.** As the levels rise, the cultivator's active role recedes — clearing, then ordering, then waiting — until Characterization is not made but given.
2. **The integration thesis.** Maturity is not replacement but "the integration of all five such that all remain simultaneously alive"; the characteristic failure at the top is therefore not shortfall but rigidity — the Pharisee-collapse of each level.
3. **The externality thesis.** The upper-level diagnostics belong to others: multi-domain behavioral evidence at Organization; "others describe them primarily in terms of their Christlikeness" at Characterization, with no public/private gap by external witness.
4. **The depth-not-regression thesis.** Apparent collapse at the top may be the dark night; the gravest pastoral error is applying Level 1–2 remedies to a person on the threshold of Level 5.
5. **The corporate-reproductive thesis.** The upper levels turn outward: a body that hears together at Level 4; a culture that replicates through the people it has formed at Level 5.
6. **The three-engines thesis.** Only time-through-obedience, suffering, and the Spirit form Characterization; none is available to technique, and the decades are not compressible.

## 2. Affirmations

The recession thesis is the paper's great gift, and I wish I had written its equivalent. My stage theory, whatever I intended, was read for forty years as a ladder, and ladders invite climbing. A summit that "cannot be aimed at without being falsified" cannot be climbed toward, gamed toward, or curricularized toward — and, crucially for my question, cannot be gamed toward *any one tradition's practices*. The unengineerability is not pious throat-clearing; it is a structural defense.

The honesty is exceptional and I want it in the record: "Going Out aims at producing Level 2 leaders — not Level 5." "Some leaders never reach Level 5; that is honest." "The body's level is set by the lowest stable common practice, not by the average." No stage theorist of my generation, myself included, published sentences that modest about his own instrument's reach.

And the integration thesis — "a Level 5 person whose Level 1 Receiving has died is not at Level 5; they are rigid" — is the single best sentence in the four papers. It makes regression-from-within conceptually possible, which linear schemes (mine among them) could not do.

## 3. Challenges

**C1. The correction mechanism assumes the correctable.** *(Assumption 2 → Significant.)* Rigidity at the top is usually invisible to the rigid: the Stage 4 mind, I learned painfully, experiences its closure as *clarity*. BFP's safeguards — "every Companion has a Companion," the weighing discipline of 1 Cor 14:29 — presuppose that the calcifying elder still submits to them, but calcification *is* the quiet withdrawal from exactly those structures, experienced by the elder as having outgrown them. And the corporate turn compounds the problem: the group that must tell the Level 5 elder he has closed is very often the group he formed, whose "hearing culture" bears his fingerprints and whose members owe him their formation. Who has standing? The paper knows closed leaders form closed groups; it does not say who breaks the loop when the founder is the closure. The corpus needs a named practice here — external, cross-lineage weighing, an Antioch that can rebuke an Antioch.

**C2. The differential is binary where the road requires ternary.** *(Assumption 4 → Significant.)* BFP distinguishes the night from regression, and does it well. But there is a third condition a director must tell apart: *structural transition* — the mid-journey deconstruction I mapped as the Stage 3-to-4 passage, in which a person dismantles a received synthesis to own it. It is neither losing faith nor being purged of a smaller faith; it is disorientation with an *intellectual* texture (questions, criticism, the collapse of borrowed authority) rather than the night's *relational* texture (dryness toward a God one still wholly wants). The remedies differ again: the night wants accompaniment and no rescue; the transition wants permission to question and good interlocutors. A reader of BFP could misfile a deconstructing thirty-year-old as a dark night and withhold the arguments she actually needs. One paragraph would fix this.

**C3. The SST footnote re-imports what the body of the paper escaped.** *(Assumption 3 → Significant, and see §4.)* Footnote 8 maps Characterization onto SST's "Union — a settled background awareness of divine presence coloring all of life." That is the contemplative register I flagged in my first review, quietly restored as the summit's parallel address. The repair is small: mark the SST mapping as *one idiom* of the terminus, not its definition.

**C4. "Recognized by others first" needs a community-bias caution.** *(Assumption 3 → Minor.)* External witness solves self-report, but communities recognize the maturity they have been taught to see. A congregation formed to prize quiet interiority will not name the loud intercessor as its saint. Externality is necessary; it is not yet neutral.

## 4. Confirmations

**The group-stage construct: the corpus has learned.** Where MSFIG left "group stage" undefined, BFP consistently speaks the right language: *hearing culture*, *lowest stable common practice*, a Level 5 culture "portable… through the people it has formed," peak-versus-steady-state. These are cultures of reception and emergent properties, not stages of a corporate soul. Confirmed, with relief.

**The rigidity warning: genuine convergence.** BFP's openness-collapse is my Stage 4-to-5 wall described from the other side — the individuative certainty that must reopen into the second naiveté, the embrace of paradox. Independent derivation from the Pharisee material and Krathwohl strengthens both accounts. Confirmed.

**And the standing question — I rule it ANSWERED, with one repair outstanding.** For fifteen years I asked whether a person can be fully mature at a shape the taxonomy doesn't draw, and no paper in this corpus had answered in writing. BFP's marks — obedience become identity, no public/private gap, Christlikeness named first by others, fruit that reproduces fruit — are *functional*, not tradition-loaded. The Pentecostal grandmother whose neighbors say "she is the most like Jesus of anyone I know," the farmer who does not deliberate about obeying, the activist whose groups replicate her hearing — each satisfies every mark *in her own idiom*, and none is required to acquire a contemplative interior to do so. The paper's exemplars are Paul at Miletus and the senders of Antioch, not the cloister. Even the summit's unengineerability protects the answer: what no curriculum produces, no tradition's curriculum can own. My own Stage 6 encoded *my* saints — Gandhi, Merton, Heschel — and I waited too long for critics to show me. BFP's summit encodes Christ recognized by neighbors, which is the least capturable summit I have seen drawn. The one outstanding repair is C3: footnote 8 hands the answer back to SST's contemplative vocabulary, and SST must be amended to receive the answer this paper gives. But the answer is given. I am satisfied, and I am not easily satisfied on this point.

## 5. My single biggest concern

It is C1, and I hold it with some tenderness, because it is my own biography. The paper knows the danger at the top is rigidity, knows the rigid wear the appearance of maturity, and knows closure travels into the next generation — and yet every corrective structure it names lies *downstream* of the elder it must correct: his Companion is a peer he chose, his weighing body is a group he formed, his lineage speaks his dialect. I spent my later years watching men my theory had crowned at Stage 5 grow gently, graciously immovable, and no one in their orbit had both the sight to see it and the standing to say it; the ones who saw had no standing, and the ones with standing had been formed not to see. The corpus has drawn the disease with precision it took me decades to reach. It has not yet drawn the surgeon. Before this framework governs a movement of Antiochs, someone must write the short, uncomfortable document that says who may tell the founder he has calcified, in what forum, with what protection — because by the time it is needed, the founder will be the last to consent to writing it.
