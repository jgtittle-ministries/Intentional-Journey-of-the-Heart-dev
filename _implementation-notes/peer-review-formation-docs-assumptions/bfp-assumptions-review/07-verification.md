# Adversarial Verification — BFP ("Bearing Fruit with Patience")

*Per the Registry's standing method, each consolidated cross-panel finding was handed to a refuter briefed to break it — defending the paper with its actual text in hand. This review carried the series' largest audit: six resolution claims, seven challenges, and the full quartet ledger. Full read (15 pp.).*

## Resolutions

**R1 — John of the Cross: "answered in posture and prescription, half-answered in doctrine." VERIFIED.** All four posture texts are present: the misdiagnosis error named verbatim ("The external observer… will apply Level 1-2 remedies to a person who is actually on the threshold of Level 5"); accompany-don't-rescue ("one does not rescue a person out of the one the Spirit has appointed; one accompanies"); the director warned ("The worker who has not internalized that sentence will, with the best intentions, interrupt the deepest formation the Spirit does"); and "stripped of a smaller faith so that a larger one can be formed." The residue also checks: John's three signs appear nowhere; the differential is strictly two-way (night vs. regression — depression and the enemy are absent); and the night's only in-paper address is upper-level. **Ruling stands as stated.**

**R2 — Fowler's fifteen-year question ANSWERED. VERIFIED.** The Characterization marks are functional, not tradition-loaded: non-deliberation, no public-private gap, external recognition, reproduction. The exemplars are missional — Paul at Miletus (Acts 20:36–38), the Antioch sending (Acts 13) — not the cloister. Residue confirmed: footnote 8 maps the terminus onto SST "Union" ("settled background awareness of divine presence coloring all of life") as "the same terminus from other angles," unmarked as one idiom. Group-construct confirmation verified: "the same hearing culture" and "the body's level is set by the lowest stable common practice, not by the average." **Stands.**

**R3 — Teresa's inversion ANSWERED. VERIFIED.** The abstract: "recognized in a person by others before it is noticed by the person"; the diagnostic "is given over to others — 'others describe them primarily in terms of their Christlikeness; it is the first thing you notice.'" Complete at the summit. **Stands.**

**R4 — Edwards PARTIALLY ANSWERED. VERIFIED, with one sharpening.** The three kind-marks are textual: unself-consciousness (recognized by others first; cessation of deliberation), costliness-through-stripping (the dark-night engine), sustained openness ("refuse to stop receiving"). Residue: 1 Cor 13 is never cited (1 Cor 14:29 is); "love" does appear — the obedience→love→faith loop and "one ordering love now governs the rest" — but never as the summit's material definition. The Matt 23:15 escape verifies exactly: "the closure travels into the next generation through the leaders those groups send out" plus "a closed maturity breeds closed disciples while an open one seeds a lineage" — reproduction transmits kind, so the framework can name a Pharisee-lineage as counterfeit. **Stands.**

**R5 — Willard's ledger. VERIFIED on all four.** Indirection held ("One cannot aim at Characterization, because the aiming is itself a deliberation"). Humble regress as architecture ("every Companion has a Companion… heading for a ditch"). Footnote 29 exists and does exactly what he says: disambiguates the individual Affective Taxonomy, the group taxonomy, and a leader-development taxonomy, applying the integration check to each. Reproduction as discipleship: "not by following a manual but by having been formed by it." **Stands.**

**R6 — Brother Lawrence's insider verdict. VERIFIED.** Reflexive ("they obey as a matter of who they are"), recognized-by-others, long-obedience fruit ("lived obedience over decades… the decades are not optional"), unengineerable ("cannot be solely engineered by curriculum design"). **Stands.**

## Challenges

**F1 — The summit's verb. WEAKENED.** Strongest refutation: the paper defines its waiting against quietism — "not idle waiting, but the active patience that keeps the conditions and refuses despair through long fruitlessness" — and "worker" throughout the series denotes the cultivator, not the disciple. But the defense fails at the summit, where the two figures merge: the Characterized person IS the Companion, the sender, the former of others, and the conclusion's "almost nothing to do but wait" reads on the level itself while the reproduction section shows that level sending, laying on hands, and multiplying. Quietism is never named as the counterfeit. **Surviving core: the reproduction section contains the correction but is never reconciled with the banner sentence; "waits" carries the framing alone.**

**F2 — Write the signs. SURVIVES.** The paper's only self-protection is "The distinction cannot be made from behavior alone, because the behaviors converge" — but John's three signs are direction-of-desire tests, not behaviors, so the sentence doesn't even conflict with writing them; it just doesn't. The discernment is lodged in "someone who has walked the road" — the certification circle exactly as charged. Citing Peers's *Dark Night* points at the signs without transmitting them. Two-way, not four-way, throughout. **The remedy already exists corpus-side: the staged desolation-dynamics research contains the four-way differential — drafted, unpublished.**

**F3 — The night filed at the top. SURVIVES.** The night's every in-paper anchor is upper-level: "a mature person under loss," "threshold of Level 5," engine of Characterization. The generic dryness note ("I did not see him today… a complete and honest entry") is about ordinary seasons, not an early night of sense. Nothing in BFP permits a Valuing-level soul a genuine night; the map as drawn would tell that soul its darkness cannot be depth. Note the internal tension with F7(h): the paper's one address is precisely the threshold address Teresa asked for — which makes the single-address problem structural, not accidental.

**F4 — Love never named as content. WEAKENED.** Refutation: the hearth is not bare — love sits inside the deepest engine ("obedience deepens love, love deepens faith") and Organization is defined by "one ordering love" governing the rest; "Christlikeness" is arguably material, not formal. But the Characterization definition itself lists only formal properties (reflexiveness, no gap, external recognition), 1 Cor 13 is absent, and a man of great natural sweetness does pass the listed marks. **Surviving core: the summit's definition never states its discriminator; one paragraph would light the fire.**

**F5 — Who tells the founder. SURVIVES.** Every corrective the paper offers — Companion's Companion, annual retreat, remaining "genuinely correctable" — is entered and exited at the mature leader's own discretion; the paper even describes the failure ("closure travels into the next generation") without naming any authority upstream of the founder. The group-hears-about-itself discipline (Rev 2–3) is the nearest structure, and a calcified founder's withdrawal from it is precisely the presenting symptom. No self-protection found. (Corpus-adjacent: the staged seven-churches research — Christ's own congregation diagnostics — bears on this but is unwritten and held.)

**F6 — The pots. WEAKENED.** Refutation: the Organization definition includes "daily decisions in a systemic way," and the openness-protecting practices are called "unglamorous" — smallness is nominally inside the frame. But every drawn exemplar wears honor: leaders, sendings, lineages, Antioch, Miletus. **Surviving core: the portrait never says the hundredfold may look like forty years of washing dishes gladly; the framework's imagery climbs past the door.**

**F7 — Bundle.** (a) *Scattered pole* — **SURVIVES** (minor): rigidity is prosecuted throughout; false openness/novelty-chasing never appears. (b) *Community-bias rider* — **SURVIVES**: "as reported by external witness" is unqualified; the paper's own Pharisee category proves communities honor rigidity, yet the witnesses are untested. (c) *Brainerd/decades* — **WEAKENED**: the bypass hedge exists in-paper ("the Spirit reserves the right to bypass it") but is never extended to "the decades are not optional," which stands flat beside it. (d) *Falls at the summit* — **WEAKENED**: "Progress is spiral, not linear" admits return-after-regress, but the Characterization portrait itself contains no stumble, and "no gap between public and private" could convict a fallen-and-returned saint. (e) *Ternary differential* — **SURVIVES**; folds into F2's four-way demand. (f) *Footnote 8 SST re-import* — **SURVIVES** (minor): "the same terminus from other angles" is presented as fact, not idiom. (g) *Who weighs the weighers* — **SURVIVES** (minor): 1 Cor 14:29 weighing is given; faction among discerners is not addressed (the gnostic-community warning covers sealed consensus, not split weighers). (h) *Give the night its address* — **REFUTED**: the paper already files the night at "the threshold of Level 5," which is the Organization-to-Characterization threshold; the demand is met — and that very address is what F3 correctly attacks as the only one. (i) *The body* — **SURVIVES** (minor): non-deliberative obedience is asserted but never treated as a bodily achievement; the retreat is the closest gesture.
