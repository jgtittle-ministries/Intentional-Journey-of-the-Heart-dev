# Executive synthesis

**The quartet closes with the series' richest harvest of resolutions — six verified — and three structural items that now form the corpus's clear remaining agenda.** The panel included, for the first time, an insider: the man the corpus itself cites as the picture of the top level, who ran the one test no one else could run.

## 1. The resolutions — all six verified

1. **The series' most persistent finding — its seventh appearance — is ANSWERED IN POSTURE AND PRESCRIPTION.** John of the Cross's ruling, verified in full: the misdiagnosis error is named with precision; the prescription is right (accompany, don't rescue; don't re-fill supports the Spirit is removing); the director is warned; "stripped of a smaller faith so that a larger one can be formed" is "my own counsel in a language not my own." The residue — half-answered in doctrine — is the review's central structural finding (below).
2. **Fowler's fifteen-year question is ANSWERED.** "Can a person be fully mature at a shape the taxonomy doesn't draw?" The verified marks — obedience become identity, no public-private gap, Christlikeness named first by others, fruit that reproduces — are functional, not tradition-loaded; the exemplars are Paul at Miletus and the senders of Antioch, not the cloister. His words: "BFP's summit encodes Christ recognized by neighbors, which is the least capturable summit I have seen drawn... I am satisfied, and I am not easily satisfied on this point." (One repair: footnote 8 hands the summit back to SST's contemplative "Union" vocabulary — mark it as one idiom.)
3. **Teresa's inversion is ANSWERED at the summit** — the Characterization diagnostic is given entirely to others; the person at Level 5 does not know it, and the instrument no longer asks them. "That is not a patch; that is the correct foundation."
4. **Edwards's kind-vs-degree is PARTIALLY ANSWERED — more nearly than any prior paper.** Three kind-criteria verified as load-bearing: unself-consciousness (evangelical humiliation operationalized), costliness-through-stripping, sustained openness (his own eleventh sign — "the gracious soul's increasing appetite, which no settled hypocrite sustains"). 5. **Willard's seven-seat ledger, verified on all four counts:** indirection held at the summit's maximum pressure; the humble regress now architecture; footnote 29's disambiguation of the three parallel taxonomies ("exactly the discipline I asked for"); and reproduction verified as discipleship, not program — "this is the Great Omission answered structurally."
6. **The insider's verdict.** Brother Lawrence, on the level the corpus names for him: "the paper's marks of Characterization are true marks. Reflexive — yes; my hands learned to pray before my mind gave permission. Recognized by others first — yes; I never knew what M. de Beaufort saw... And your warning that a Level 5 person whose Receiving has died is only rigid — I confirm it from the inside; the day I stop being surprised by Him, suspect me."

The panel also confirmed the rigidity thesis as the paper's finest original work (Willard: "'Pharisee-Characterization collapse is the settled rigidity whose spiritual life no longer surprises anyone' earns its place beside anything in the literature of formation"; Fowler: "the single best sentence in the four papers... it makes regression-from-within conceptually possible, which linear schemes — mine among them — could not do"), and the honesty as exemplary ("some leaders never reach Level 5; that is honest").

## 2. The three structural findings

**F2 — WRITE THE SIGNS (SURVIVES; the residue of the series' most persistent finding).** The differential between the night and collapse is commended but not transmitted: it is lodged in "someone who has walked the road" — a certification circle. John of the Cross: "a lineage reproduces only what it writes down or embodies, and the embodied discerner dies. I wrote the signs precisely because I would not always be in the parlor at Beas... Write the signs. They fit on a card smaller than the Rhythm Card, and they will save more souls than any other paragraph the corpus could add." His three signs are direction-of-desire tests, writable; the four-way differential (night / regression / depression / the enemy) is still two-way. **The remedy is drafted: the parked desolation-dynamics research contains exactly this.**

**F3 — THE NIGHT IS STILL FILED AT THE TOP (SURVIVES).** Every in-paper anchor of the night is upper-level — but the night of sense comes early and is common, and Brother Lawrence's own ten years of dryness came at the *beginning*: "a young soul in my old trouble will be told his darkness cannot be depth because he has not earned it yet — the very misdiagnosis you warn against, committed by the map itself. Let the night fall where God sends it, at any level."

**F5 — WHO TELLS THE FOUNDER (SURVIVES).** Fowler's hardest-won finding: every corrective structure the paper names lies downstream of the elder it must correct — his Companion chosen by him, his weighing body formed by him, his lineage speaking his dialect — and calcification *is* the withdrawal from those structures, experienced as having outgrown them. "The corpus has drawn the disease with precision it took me decades to reach. It has not yet drawn the surgeon... someone must write the short, uncomfortable document that says who may tell the founder he has calcified, in what forum, with what protection — because by the time it is needed, the founder will be the last to consent to writing it." (The staged seven-churches research — Christ's own congregation diagnostics — bears directly on this.)

## 3. The remaining verified findings

**The summit's verb (F1, WEAKENED to a sharp core — triple convergence).** "At the top, the worker mostly waits" conflates the cultivator (who rightly waits) with the mature disciple (who wills easily and serves more). Teresa: "the seventh mansion is a sending dock," not a rocking chair — "I did not spend my last fifteen years in a mule cart so that the castle could end in a cul-de-sac." Willard: "the strain recedes, not the will... The worker waits; the disciple wills, easily. Say both." Brother Lawrence's third voice: there are two waitings — "the one who waits at the top is not waiting *for* God but attending *to* Him; the fruit is delayed, the company is not." The reproduction section contains the correction; the banner sentence needs its second clause.

**Love as the summit's content (F4, WEAKENED — Edwards's residue).** The Characterization definition lists formal properties a man of great natural sweetness passes; love appears in the engines but is never named as the material definition. "Write one paragraph placing 1 Corinthians 13 at the center of Level 5... and the demand I have carried through four papers is discharged. The house is built and the rooms are swept; it wants only the fire lit on the hearth."

**The pots (F6, WEAKENED — Brother Lawrence's Foundational, narrowed).** Integration's exemplars all wear honor — leaders, sendings, Antioch — while integration is tested in little things where no honor attaches. "The top of this mountain smells of onions... if your framework does not say that the hundredfold harvest may look like forty years of washing dishes gladly, the people it forms will climb past the very door it means to open. Put the kitchen in the paper."

**Minor (verified):** the community-bias rider (the Pharisees were recognized by others first; witnesses must include the outsider, the injured, the servant); extend the Spirit-bypass hedge to "the decades are not optional" (Brainerd died at twenty-nine); the fall-and-return belongs in the summit's portrait ("a Characterization drawn without stumbles will convict every true saint of fraud"); the scattered pole (false openness as rigidity's unprosecuted twin); the ternary differential (structural deconstruction vs. the night); who weighs the weighers; the body's role in reflexive obedience; footnote 8's idiom-marking. One demand was REFUTED: the night's threshold address already exists — which is precisely why F3's single-address problem is structural.
