## 2. What survived adversarial verification

**HIGH — the disorganized-attachment cluster (F1 + F2, both SURVIVE): the review's decisive scientific result.** Granqvist — who led the field's 2017 consensus statement on exactly this — was blunt: **there is no adult trait "disorganized attachment"**; the adult analogue is a state of mind regarding unresolved loss or trauma, not a personality type; and ATB's Future Work trains lay facilitators to recognize and *record* the four patterns — "precisely the over-attribution pipeline our statement warned against." Bowlby added the measurement floor: no validated instrument warrants any pattern-assignment the paper's diagnostics propose, adult research is dimensional rather than categorical, and Section 11's "disorganized awareness" as a retained virtue "romanticizes a breakdown." The verifier confirmed the internal contradiction (the body text credits Ainsworth with four patterns; the paper's own bibliography correctly says disorganized came later) and the meta-verdict: **what falls is the four-category diagnostic apparatus and its lay deployment — the theological triad, the firewall, and the spiral survive intact.** The convergent fix, in Granqvist's words: *keep the care; retire the taxonomy to the footnotes; let the practices be offered to anyone who recognizes the struggle rather than assigned to those a facilitator has typed.*

**HIGH — safeguarding architecture (F5, SURVIVES — LANGBERG-2 again, with higher stakes).** Real safety *practices* exist (the covenant, consent, SUDS, Exit/Return, referral language — Langberg credited them as "real safeguards, not decoration"). The *architecture* does not: no screening, no facilitator competence floor or supervision, no protocol for when TPM's memory-descent surfaces more than a lay group can hold, no named referral triggers or decision-holder, no data governance for pattern-records (the dual pastoral/research use needs separate consent), no provision for the leader-as-rupture case. Langberg: "Instincts do not protect people; structures do... The person in front of that facilitator is holy ground." This folds directly into the standing LANGBERG-2 item reserved for the Council's Character-Prerequisites pass — ATB raises its stakes because it brings trauma histories into the room by design.

**MEDIUM-HIGH — the soils table, second edition (F4, SURVIVES with its confirmation attached).** The structural verdict improved on MSFIG (type-to-type), but the verified cell-level items stand: the hard-path cell omits the parable's own named agent — "the text will bear a substrate the enemy exploits; it will not bear a substrate that replaces him" (the corpus's own V2.Exp2B supplies the fix); thorny↔disorganized is the weakest cell (the text's mechanism is rival loves and riches, not approach-avoid trauma conflict — "a secure wealthy man is thoroughly thorny"); the cells speak causally while the caption hedges; rocky↔anxious is the tight cell and should be marked as such; and Section 13's own "decades of godliness with attachment wounds" concedes good soil without secure pattern — the strongest internal evidence the correlation is loose, worth owning explicitly. Langberg added the pastoral edge: mapping trauma-born patterns onto the parable's language of failure risks re-moralizing trauma — "hedges in a working draft do not survive into a Tuesday-night small group."

**MEDIUM — the earned-security overstatement (F3, SURVIVES).** "Granqvist's research demonstrates that earned security through religious experience is empirically real" attributes to Granqvist a demonstration he has not made in those terms: *compensation* is established; durable earned security via religion is the plausible, still-under-tested extension. His softening language is ready to use. The missing socialized-correspondence pathway also matters for design: the secure arrive by socialization, the insecure by compensation — two doors into the same church, two formation logics.

**MEDIUM — grace vs. machinery (F6, WEAKENED to a precise core).** The document-level safeguard exists and is strong (the Conclusion is nearly Charry's own sentence) — but it lives in the captions, not the cards: the Reassurance Cooldown has no failure-condition grace ("what the anxious person hears from God when she re-asks anyway"), and there is no door marked *extraordinary* — Julian's sovereign showing, the gift no protocol schedules. Julian's watermark line is the repair: *this practice cannot make you more kept than you already are.*

**LOW-MEDIUM — two verified absences worth restoring.** The mother-tongue (F7, SURVIVES as absence): no maternal imagery for God anywhere in the paper — Isa 49:15, Isa 66:13, Matt 23:37 — though the science was born watching mothers, and "for the believer whose father-wound is the very wound, Father-language alone bars the door the paper means to open." And the genealogy (F8, WEAKENED — Edwards and Calvin are present): Augustine's restless heart, the Psalms as secure-base grammar, and Julian herself are absent — "the church's own attachment literature, sixteen centuries deep."
