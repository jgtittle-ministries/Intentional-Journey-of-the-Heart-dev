# Adversarial Verification — ATB ("Attachment Theory and the Biblical Triad")

*Per the Registry's standing method, each consolidated cross-panel finding was handed to a refuter briefed to break it — defending the paper with its actual text in hand. Full read; all quoted text verified against the source.*

## F1 — The disorganized cluster (Granqvist + Bowlby)

**Refutation attempt.** The paper self-arms at three points: Section 2 — "attachment patterns are tendencies, not destinies… descriptive, not prescriptive"; Section 8's caption — "The claim is not that these mappings are one-to-one or causal"; and Section 8 point 5, which explicitly says "adult attachment is more domain-specific than was first appreciated" and that one heart may be "secure with one set of people or topics, anxious with another." That domain-specificity clause is a genuine partial answer to "stable congregational type."

**But the pipeline is real.** Future Work item 9 says exactly what the finding alleges: "Train facilitators to recognize the four patterns in group dynamics and to record observations in a way that supports both pastoral care and research." Sections 4–7 speak of "Disorganized believers" as a class with predictable behavior; Section 8 maps thorny soil to "Disorganized patterns." The Ainsworth contradiction is verbatim: body text credits "Mary Ainsworth's empirical work in the 1960s–1970s identified the now-classic four attachment patterns," while the paper's own Ainsworth 1978 annotation says the system was "later supplemented by the disorganized category." And Section 11's "retains disorganized awareness — the capacity to hold contradictions" repackages what Section 2 itself defines as the *inability* to "organize a coherent strategy" — a collapse, not a capacity.

**Verdict: SURVIVES.** Domain-specificity softens "stable type" rhetoric but cannot rescue categorical lay recognition-and-recording of a fourth adult category the field doesn't recognize as a trait; the internal Ainsworth contradiction is undeniable.

## F2 — No measurement warrant

**Refutation attempt.** The paper does not claim validation — Section 8 flags "Open empirical question: does the parallel mapping hold in the FotH practice data?" and Future Work items 7–9 propose the tests, correctly citing Beck & McDonald's AGI. The paper frames itself as hypothesis, not finding.

**But the operations don't wait.** Practice cards are assigned by pattern *now* (Section 10); Section 12 trains leaders to "name and normalize attachment dynamics"; the Group SIP extension has facilitators observing and recording patterns before any instrument exists. The paper's four-category architecture never engages the dimensional (anxiety × avoidance) structure of adult research or the AAI/self-report split; its only concession is the domain-specificity aside. Hypothesis-framing in Section 8 does not license categorical deployment in Sections 10–12.

**Verdict: SURVIVES.** The paper's own open-question honesty confirms rather than rebuts the finding: everything diagnostic currently rests on impression.

## F3 — Earned-security overstatement

**Refutation attempt.** The sentence's second half describes "secure attachment *to God*" — arguably a compensation claim with real support (Bradshaw & Kent longitudinal prayer evidence is in the bibliography).

**But the words are the words.** Page 4: "Granqvist's research demonstrates that earned security through religious experience is empirically real" — and the Granqvist 2020 annotation repeats "supplies the documentation that 'earned security'… is empirically real." "Earned security" is a technical AAI construct; conflating it with attachment-to-God change is precisely the overstatement alleged. And the correspondence description (secure God-image mirroring secure history) omits the socialized-correspondence finding entirely — the paper's two-pathway summary has one door per population but never says so, so the "two formation logics" implication is missed.

**Verdict: SURVIVES.** The claim as written attributes to Granqvist a demonstration he has not made in those terms.

## F4 — The soils table

**Refutation attempt.** (a) Nothing rebuts it: the hard-path cell reads "the relational system is deactivated before the Word can land; the defense is 'I don't need this'" — no birds, no snatching enemy, despite Mark 4:15 naming Satan as the parable's only explicit agent. (b) The thorny cell claims approach-avoid conflict "generates exactly the 'thorns' (oscillating cares, deceitfulness of competing securities…)" — but the parable's thorns are cares and *riches*; the paper's own V2.Exp1 mapping (Level 2 "competing values") fits mammon better than trauma. A secure rich man remains thoroughly thorny. (c) Verified: the caption disclaims causation while the cells speak mechanism — "tribulation breaks the connection *because* the working model could not sustain it." (d) Rocky↔anxious is indeed the tight cell; its mechanism matches the parable's (joy, no root, collapse under tribulation). (e) Section 13 concedes it: "Some believers will struggle with attachment wounds while growing in godliness and service for decades" — good soil without secure pattern, in the paper's own hand, though the table's "(whether by original development or by earned security)" hedge papers over the tension rather than owning it.

**Verdict: SURVIVES**, confirmation and critiques both. Every cell-level claim checks against the text.

## F5 — Safeguarding architecture (LANGBERG-2)

**Refutation attempt.** Component 4 is more than nothing: group covenant, consent for touch/prayer/questions, SUDS scale, grounding menu, safety plan, rupture repair, and explicit positioning as "complementary to (not a replacement for) licensed mental-health care." Section 12 urges collaboration with licensed counselors.

**But the named gaps are all real.** Section 13's seven cautions are entirely theological-hermeneutical. No screening/intake exists. Referral is exhortation ("build active collaborative relationships") with no named triggers or decision-holder. Exit/Return is verifiably a pause: "return when SUDS ≤ 5; tell leader 'Ready to return'" — the script assumes return. TPM's memory-descent (EMOTION → MEMORY → BELIEF) has no over-capacity protocol. Future Work 9's dual "pastoral care and research" use of pattern-records has no consent split, no data-holder. Nothing addresses the leader-as-rupture case or an independent complaint path.

**Verdict: SURVIVES.** Real safety *practices* exist; the *architecture* — gates, floors, triggers, governance — does not.

## F6 — Grace vs. machinery (Charry + Julian)

**Refutation attempt.** The paper carries stronger grace-governance than the finding admits: Section 7 — "Grace is patient with the substrate"; Section 13 — "ATB does not promise complete repatterning"; and the Conclusion delivers Charry's own safeguard almost verbatim: "Hope, finally, does not rest on achieved secure attachment. It rests on the God who promises…"

**But location matters.** All of that lives in Sections 7/13/14 — captions around the machinery. The Reassurance Cooldown itself (rate, wait ten minutes, ask once, don't re-ask) contains no failure-condition grace; an anxious reader who re-asks has failed the protocol with nothing in the card to catch her. "Earned security" remains the operative vocabulary. And there is genuinely no extraordinary door: "the Spirit's ordinary mode is incremental" is the paper's only register; sovereign bypass gets one clause in a Section 2 list.

**Verdict: WEAKENED.** Surviving core: the document-level safeguard exists and is strong, but it is not embedded where the anxious reader will actually meet the machinery — in the cards' failure conditions — and the extraordinary door is absent.

## F7 — The missing mother-tongue

**Refutation attempt.** The science sections consistently say "caregiver," not "father" — the paper is gender-neutral about the human substrate.

**But for God, only Father.** Full-text check: no Isa 49:15, no Isa 66:13, no Matt 23:37, no maternal image anywhere. "Abba, Father," "the Fatherhood of God," "preaching adoption" — exclusively paternal, while the theory's evidentiary base (Ainsworth's dyads) was maternal. The believer whose wound is the father meets only father-language at every door.

**Verdict: SURVIVES** (as the absence it claims to be — verified absent).

## F8 — Truncated genealogy

**Refutation attempt.** The bibliography contains Calvin (1559) and Edwards (1746) — *Religious Affections* is annotated as "foundational" to ATB's substrate claim. The charge that the bibliography "implies the deepest truths about hearts were discovered in 1969" is demonstrably too strong.

**But the specific genealogy is missing.** No Augustine, no Julian, no pre-modern *attachment* witness; the Psalms appear only as proof-text anchors (Ps 23, Ps 18:2, Ps 56:3 on a practice card), never as the secure-base grammar the finding describes.

**Verdict: WEAKENED.** Surviving core: the church's own attachment literature (Augustine's restless heart, the Psalms as relational grammar, Julian) is absent and worth restoring; the "1969" framing is refuted by Edwards and Calvin.

## Confirmations verified

- **Romans 5:1–5 as structural spine — CONFIRMED.** Section 3 calls it "the canonical" sequence; Section 6 builds hope-theory on it; Section 7's Phase 3 enacts it. The triad's architecture is genuinely scriptural, not psychological with verses attached.
- **Sufferer centered — CONFIRMED.** Cruciform hope ("does not promise relief in this life"), normalized lament, and Section 13's decades-of-godliness-with-wounds passage put the unhealed believer inside the model, not outside it.
- **Saving-faith firewall — CONFIRMED, stated twice.** Section 4 and Section 13: "not produced by secure attachment, and… not prevented by insecure attachment… substrate on which sanctification operates, not… ground on which justification rests." As clean a firewall as claimed.
- **Section 13** covers seven distinct caution heads including anti-prosperity and anti-psychologizing; the panel's "best in series" praise is defensible.

## Meta

**(a) Foundational vs. procedural.** F1+F2 are foundational *for ATB's empirical program*, not for IJH: the theological triad, the firewall, and the spiral survive intact; what falls is the four-category diagnostic apparatus and its lay deployment. F5 is foundational for *any* deployment (a gate, not a critique of content). F3 is a citation-precision fix; F4, F6, F7, F8 are revision-level.

**(b) Convergences.** The scientists (validity: the category doesn't exist as an adult trait; no instrument warrants assignment) and the trauma clinician (harm: pattern-records without governance, no complaint path) converge on the same artifact — the facilitator-recorded label — from opposite directions; Future Work 9 is the single sentence both would strike. Charry and Julian converge independently on grace-as-caption-not-governor, and F6's verification shows the paper already half-agrees in its Conclusion.

**(c) Corpus coverage.** F5 folds directly into the standing LANGBERG-2 item (Character-Prerequisites pass) and can draw on existing FotH safeguarding conventions. F4(a)'s fix is internal — V2.Exp2B already supplies "the enemy exploits what deactivation leaves exposed." Section 11 is the corpus's own integration-not-replacement discipline correctly extended — but F1 shows it was extended over a category that needs reframing first. The anxious "dryness-as-abandonment" thread (Sections 4, 6) should eventually reconcile with the desolation-dynamics differential in staged research, which distinguishes exactly what ATB currently lumps.
