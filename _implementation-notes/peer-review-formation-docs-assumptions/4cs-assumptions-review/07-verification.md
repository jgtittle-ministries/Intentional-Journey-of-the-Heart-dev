# Adversarial Verification — "Four Connects" Deck (Document 6)

*Per the Registry's standing method, each consolidated cross-panel finding was handed to a refuter briefed to break it — defending the deck with its actual slides in hand. Full deck read (50 slides). Genre standard applied: facilitator-operated teaching deck, not a treatise.*

## F1 — Sanctification equation without justification (Calvin/Charry)
**Refutation attempt:** Slide 43 (1 Cor 3) is the deck's hidden justification note: "He himself will be saved, but only as through fire" — salvation secure even when the work burns. And slide 4's texts do let God speak ("I, the Lord, sanctify you"). A facilitator could land the duplex gratia from slide 43 alone.
**Verdict: SURVIVES.** Slide 43 arrives at slide 43 — after every exercise — is about eschatological testing of *work*, not prior acceptance before the work begins; and slide 7's "Also called sanctification" equates the *process the group runs* with what slide 4's own grammar says *God* does. The anxious-participant ladder risk stands. Genuinely a one-slide fix, and slide 43 is the natural hook for it.

## F2 — The anchor teaches felt-state retrieval (Bonhoeffer/Benner/Willard/Charry — THE ANCHOR)
**Refutation attempt:** Three defenses exist in the deck itself. (1) Slide 21 hedges: "a time when you *may have felt* connected." (2) Slide 20 cites 1 Cor 6:17 — "the one united with the Lord IS one spirit with him" — an indicative of union that holds regardless of feeling; the seed of the fix is already on the slide. (3) Slide 27 hands agency back: "Invite in Jesus, give Him permission to do His thing" — not pure technique.
**Verdict: SURVIVES.** The hedge and the indicative are present but inert: the *procedure* (slides 20–22, 26–27) is unambiguous — recall the felt state, "Anchor before going on," "Change with anchor," and step 4 explicitly treats the felt-connected/felt-disconnected contrast as *diagnostic pattern data* about one's connection with God. Verbatim: "A time when you felt connected to God… A time when you felt disconnected." Mother Teresa cannot complete step 1; nothing in fifty slides says dryness ≠ disconnection. 1 Cor 6:17 sitting unused on slide 20 makes the fix trivial — and the omission more visible.

## F3 — Discernment tests not wired to the imagery (Calvin/Willard/Benner)
**Refutation attempt:** Two wirings the finding missed. Slide 28's six tests directly *follow* the hearing-God anchor exercises (26–27) — in live delivery they retro-cover what was just heard. And slide 36 wires mission: "Lines up with Scripture" is criterion one for the gold mission.
**Verdict: WEAKENED.** Surviving core: the Garden (slides 10–11) — where Jesus is invited in and participants "record what happens" — sits eighteen slides before any test, and no test is ever pointed back at it. The most theologically loaded imagery output in the deck (words from the invited Jesus) is the one exercise with no stated filter. Mission and hearing-God are covered; the Garden is not.

## F4 — Mission fourth, personal-vocation, no public square (Newbigin)
**Refutation attempt:** Slide 36's criteria are less self-referential than charged: "Brings the Kingdom of God in those around you," and it ends in *sending* language — Isaiah 6:8, "Here I am, send me." Slide 33 defines shadow mission by damage *to others*. Slide 48 names a "collective mission." Slide 47 extends "to our kids and others."
**Verdict: WEAKENED.** Surviving core: sequence (mission is fourth, slides 6 and 29) and vocabulary (slide 29 "the reason you, personally are on this earth"; slide 36 "It lights your heart up… It brings you alive") verified verbatim; the John 14 trinitarian material (slide 19) never reappears in slides 29–37; and the public square is genuinely absent — slide 49's furthest horizon is the CCA board, trustees, staff, students: expansion *inward* into the institution, never outward to workplace, neighborhood, the poor, or the pluralist world. The Isa 6:8 sending note and kingdom-in-others criterion refute "the self remains the raw material" as an absolute, but not the structural point.

## F5 — Confession as sovereign-share (Bonhoeffer)
**Refutation attempt:** The permission structure ("Share whatever you wish," slide 34; "whatever they have permission to say," slides 35/37) is arguably *deliberate safeguarding* — a mixed, semi-public CCA pilot should not compel disclosure; the corpus has a standing LANGBERG-2 safeguarding architecture that would demand exactly this consent gating. And slide 17's Q3 ("How have I wounded you?") goes past mirroring — it invites the other to name my sin against them.
**Verdict: SURVIVES, with the safeguarding caveat attached.** Verified: no slide names sin as sin; no forgiveness is ever spoken aloud; the pair structurally gives *feedback and suggestions* (slide 15), never Christ's word of absolution. Sovereign-share may be right as a floor; the finding stands because the deck provides no path *beyond* the floor. The Four Questions do walk to the door — confirmed.

## F6 — Double knowledge as sequence, not mutual dependence (Calvin/Bonhoeffer)
**Refutation attempt:** Benner's counter-evidence verifies exactly: slide 10 opens self-connection with a "relaxation exercise to get present to the great 'I Am'" and slide 11 invites Jesus into the garden. Self-knowledge is structurally staged *coram Deo*, not as autonomous introspection.
**Verdict: WEAKENED — and one attribution corrected.** The quoted guard sentence ("we look at ourselves only to be driven to God…") **does not appear anywhere in the extracted deck**; it is Calvin's own formulation, not the deck's. The guard is not one sentence — it is *zero* sentences; what exists is structural (I-Am framing, invited Jesus). Surviving core: the deck's own texts call the heart dull (slide 24) and its treasury capable of evil (slide 9), yet the self reports on the self with no stated external check — this collapses into F3's unwired-Garden gap.

## F7 — Willard triple
**(a) Intimacy-definition vs character-texts.** Refutation: the deck repeatedly refuses session-vividness as the measure — "What will you do or say different?" recurs (slides 11, 37, 41), slide 16 demands a *dated action*, slide 42 sets SMART goals, and the ecology check exists precisely to force valley-transfer. **WEAKENED.** Surviving core: slide 7's stated telos is still "deeper, more intimate relationship," while slide 3's texts end in discernment and distinguishing good from evil; the behavioral scaffolding is means-tracking, not a character-formation definition.
**(b) Four Connects as the corpus's working anthropology.** The deck cannot refute this; it *is* the evidence — fifty slides navigate entirely by four relationships, zero faculty taxonomy. **SURVIVES** (corpus-level).
**(c) VIM vision-light.** Refutation: kingdom material is not absent — Matt 13 kingdom mysteries at slides 23–25, Talents at 30–31, kingdom criteria at 36. **WEAKENED.** Surviving core: kingdom appears as *exercise material*, never as an opening vision of the kind of life on offer; slides 1–7 open with definitions, not vision.

## F8 — Bundle
**(a)** Slide 48's contract = Scripture, intentionality, logistics, confidentiality; no discipline/hard-word provision. **SURVIVES.** **(b)** No solitude architecture outside meetings — verified; though between-meeting structure isn't wholly absent (dated actions, SMART/3x3x3). **SURVIVES (narrowed to solitude).** **(c)** Anchoring, VAK (slide 26 verbatim), "Ecology Check" (an NLP term of art, used as two slide titles), "What's at risk" — all unattributed; and the deck *does* cite when it chooses ("Relational Discipleship," slide 48), so genre doesn't excuse it. **SURVIVES.** **(d)** Slide 28 verbatim: "What does it feel like to be a white male?" — intended as fish-in-water invisibility analogy; still a needless flashpoint, trivially swappable. **SURVIVES.** **(e)** "Theosis is the goal" bare on slides 46 and 50, undefined. **SURVIVES.**

## Confirmations — all verified

Slide 5 Philippian radical social mix — verbatim. Four Questions (slide 17) — verbatim. Slides 47–49 sober re Wunschbild: "test bed," draft social contract, explicit exit ("Decide if this FotH is for you at this time") — Bonhoeffer's non-carryover finding confirmed. Garden-in-divine-gaze (slides 10–11) — confirmed.

## Meta
**(a) Foundations vs. one-slide fixes.** Nothing here threatens IJH/FotH foundations. F1, F2, F3/F6 (one problem: the unwired Garden), F8d/e are one-slide or one-leader-paragraph fixes. F4 is the only *structural* finding (mission's position and horizon), and F5 is a deliberate-design question, not an error. F7b is a compliment wearing a critique's clothes: the operational anthropology works.

**(b) Convergences.** The anchor finding (F2) survived the hardest refutation attempt available — the deck's own 1 Cor 6:17 and slide 27 — and it came independently from a Lutheran martyr-theologian, a depth-psychologist director, and a formation philosopher: three traditions with almost no shared method converging on the same pastoral casualty, the dry believer. That triangulation is as strong as simulated peer review gets, and it lands exactly on the person the author cares most about.

**(c) Corpus coverage.** The corpus already holds F2's missing module: the desolation-dynamics four-way differential (staged research) is precisely the "dryness is not disconnection" doctrine the anchor slides need, and Vol 2's weary-reader/dry-believer material is its plain-language form. Calvin's leader-level items (F1 paragraph, F8a discipline provision) have a natural home in the FotH GS handbook/FC1 track. F5's sovereign-share sits inside the standing LANGBERG-2 safeguarding architecture — any move toward Bonhoefferian confession must be designed *with* it, not around it. F8c's provenance fix has a precedent: the corpus's integration-not-replacement framing is the honest way to name the NLP-derived toolkit's origins while keeping it. The deck needs roughly three slides and one handbook section — all of which the corpus has already half-written.
