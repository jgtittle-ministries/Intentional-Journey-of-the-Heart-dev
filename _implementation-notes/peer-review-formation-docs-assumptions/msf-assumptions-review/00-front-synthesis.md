## Executive synthesis

The series ends at the paper of scale — and the panel, spanning the sixth century to the twenty-first, converged on a single discovery seen from five unrelated seats: **the instrument's relationship to power is undesigned.** A trauma clinician (from her caseload), a measurement scientist (from Campbell's law), a Puritan pastor (from his own parish catalogue), the builder of history's greatest formation-measurement system (from watching it die), and a missionary bishop (from the center's power over the parishes) independently found the same absence: the paper builds the management apparatus — reporting structures, health evaluations, leadership audits, credentialing gates, funding flows — and never writes the guard. The MSM surveillance guard ("formation data must never become a management tool") is not restated in the one paper that most needed it; the verifier confirmed that *consent, confidentiality, safeguarding,* and *misuse* do not occur in eighty pages.

**Baxter's debut gave the review its spine:** "The dynamics may be scale-invariant; the knowing is not." His confirmation is the series' most costly kind — he kept exactly the catalogue this paper proposes, for 800 families, and testifies "the catalogue was innocent only because I knew the faces, and the faces knew me." His counsel: *"Measure nothing you will not weep over."*

**Bonhoeffer delivered the review's hardest test:** the levels are content-neutral — "a lie can be characterized to Level 5" — and in 1934 the instrument would have crowned the Deutsche Christen at Level 4 and flagged the Confessing Church as the problem. Finkenwalde fails every Level 5 test in the paper; John 12:24 appears nowhere. (Krathwohl reached the identical finding in measurement idiom, via the FC review's Fowler-orthogonality finding — the series replicating its own results across documents.)

**Wesley's double testimony is unique in the whole series:** he VERIFIED that the paper's Level 4–5 epistemology is practicable — "multi-source, longitudinal, consequence-bearing soul-measurement of an entire population is not a research proposal. It is history" (70,000+ souls under quarterly examination) — and testified to its failure mode from inside: the class ticket that became a membership card, "a dead sect, having the form of religion without the power." His MSFIG thorns demand was formally VERIFIED as answered: riches stand at first rank at every scale of the paper.

**And the hear-and-obey core was confirmed a TWELFTH consecutive time — twelve for twelve, zero qualifications** — with two reviewers converting confirmation into strengthening: Krathwohl ruled the core *load-bearing* ("an internalization taxonomy without a specified value measures nothing"), and Langberg ruled it *protective* ("the most abuse-resistant element in the entire corpus. Sheep who know the Shepherd's voice are harder to steal").

## Verified challenges

**Foundational tier (SURVIVES):**
1. **The surveillance/governance convergence (A)** — five seats, one finding: the management is built and the guard is unwritten. The single largest structural finding of the review. *Corpus status: guard established at MSM, architecture at LANGBERG-2, Council governance drafted — but no data-governance/consent text anywhere.*
2. **Campbell's law (B)** — formation scores routed to credentialing and funding will corrupt the indicator; the paper diagnoses this in others and prescribes its conditions in itself. *Corpus status: genuine gap — no corpus work models consequence design.*
3. **The Deutsche Christen test (D)** — content-neutral levels; the applied hear-and-obey chapters stop at the group scale; no institutional indicator asks the apostasy question. *Corpus status: the parked seven-churches research IS the dominical apparatus.*
4. **No grammar of the fall (E)** — no backslider path, confession row, lament row, or direction at any scale; the shattered, confessing church reads as regressing. *Corpus status: partially drafted (desolation research; seven-churches repentance calls); the shattered-church station is a gap even there.*
5. **No consequence / no keys (F)** — diagnosis terminates in diagnosis; the one consequence built (credentialing) is the one B condemns. Wesley: the paper has "neither the keys nor the guard, the worst of both."
6. **Routinization of the instrument itself (G)** — no examination of the examiners, no appointed death and rebirth of the machinery. *Corpus status: the wineskin/κ term is the theory, unapplied.*
7. **Internalization without sending (H)** — every tier's Level 5 is ecclesial reproduction; no indicator measures the church's effect on the world; the connection-rate metric scores the frontline layman as failure. *Corpus status: genuine gap — no mission-telos indicator anywhere.*
8. **The construct question (I)** — aggregate vs culture vs corporate person unresolved; Krathwohl's operationalization unmet; esse/bene esse deployed only at the top; Rev 2–3's addressable hearer absent. *Corpus status: pieces exist (cultures-of-reception, seven-churches, Vol 4 group instruments) — unintegrated.*
9. **Krathwohl's misattribution (J)** — Handbook II never claimed cross-scale scope; the extension is the author's own construct wearing the 1964 citation, its validation burden unpaid.
10. **Multi-source without consent (L)** + **no predator (M)** + **no measure of the least powerful (N)** + **trauma absent (O)** — Langberg's block, all surviving; the Willow Creek/REVEAL case is the paper's own uncited counterexample; the bad-news test has no analogue in the instrument. *Corpus status: N, O-administration = genuine gaps.*
11. **Level 5 as equilibrium (R)** + **reproduction-as-apex (S)** — arrival enshrined where sending belongs; the paper's own pastoral text ("more dependent on grace") contradicts and corrects its formalism; Finkenwalde/martyrdom scores as extinction.
12. **TFT load-bearing (U)** + **self-applied standards (V)** + **one stream's idiom at the catholic tier (W)** + **entity-staging shorthand (K)** + **riches engine (Q)** — all survive as stated.
