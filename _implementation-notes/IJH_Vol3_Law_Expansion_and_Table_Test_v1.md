# IJH Vol 3 — Law Expansion & Table Stress-Test (Engine chapter)

*Working analysis, 2026-06-17. Does the new "Engine Beneath the Force" exercise (energy / time / inertia / work / friction / temperature / phase map / gift-of-faith / Schwarz energeia) surface any new Foundational Laws? Candidate-generation + stress-test against the live catalog. **No manuscript edits in this pass — analysis only.** Dev-only.*

## 1. The structure under test (restated)

**Inclusion bar — a candidate is a Foundational Law only if it clears all four** (from the Vol 1 Foundational-Laws intro):
1. **Multi-author scriptural support** — ≥ 2 biblical authors.
2. **Broad acceptance** — affirmed across mainstream traditions, not contested.
3. **Directionality** — causal/conditional (X → Y), not merely descriptive.
4. **Principle-level** — a governing principle, not a situational command.
Bands of closeness handle principle↔command proximity (Band 1 clearly-distinguishable; Band 2 scripturally-specified pathway; Band 3 tradition-by-tradition operational forms).

**The table address (3 parts):** Period **P0–P5** (scale) × Group **GI–GVI** (part of the person) × direction **V/H/B/I**. Plus **law-type** (Structural / Operational / Mixed / Gateway; Vol 2 adds Diagnostic / Tool-Application / Developmental) and **Band 1–3**. Vol 3's **Role Atlas** adds a *third address* — the **field-role** (attractor / axis / term / gradient / well / substrate / coupling / constraint / conservation) — for laws viewed inside the dynamical picture.

**Catalog:** 47 FLs (38 wide-consent FL.I–XXXVIII + 9 newer FL.XXXIX–XLVII).

**Ambiguity worth naming up front (and it turns out to be the whole finding):** the table is built to hold *laws* — directional cause-and-effect relationships. The Engine chapter mostly produces *quantities and field-roles* (inertia, friction, temperature, work, energy) — a **different kind of object**. The right question is therefore not "which cells do these occupy?" but "are any of them laws at all, or are they the field-roles the Role Atlas already exists to hold?"

## 3. The four that clear (or nearly clear) the bar, walked through the table

Only two candidates even reach the bar as *laws* (the rest are field-roles/quantities — see §5). Both, walked through the table, prove to be **facets of an existing FL**, not new rows.

- **C4 — Proportional cost (widow's mite).** *Bar:* multi-author **yes** (2 Cor. 8:12 "according to what one has"; Mark 12/Luke 21 widow; Luke 12:48 "to whom much is given"); broadly accepted **yes**; directional **partly** (it is more *evaluative* — how God weighs — than cause→effect); principle-level **yes**. *Table:* it would sit P0/GV, B — **exactly where FL.IX Generosity-Provision already sits.** It is the *weighing rule* implicit inside FL.IX (and FL.IV Humility), not a separate law. **Disposition: ABSORB into FL.IX** (could be named explicitly there; see §5).
- **C5 — Wholeheartedness / lukewarm.** *Bar:* multi-author **yes** (Jer. 29:13; Deut. 4:29; Rom. 12:11; Rev. 3:15–16); broadly accepted **yes**; directional **yes**; principle-level **yes**. *Table:* P1/GII, V — **the cell FL.VIII Desire-for-God already occupies** ("sustained desire → increased capacity to receive"). Lukewarmness is the negative pole FL.VIII already implies. **Disposition: ABSORB into FL.VIII.**

The other "near" item, **C7 (God gives the growth, 1 Cor. 3:6–7 + Mark 4:28)**, clears multi-author and acceptance but is about *agency* (God, not us) rather than a person-side X→Y; it is the catalog's **grace frame** (stated in the Foundational-Laws intro: "every one rests on grace, God before us") and the content of **FL.XL Abiding-Fruitfulness** ("apart from me you can do nothing"). **ABSORB.**

## 4. Synthesis — what the test revealed

1. **The structure held; nothing bent or broke.** Every Engine concept that *looked* like a law turned out to be either (a) a **field-role/quantity** with no row because the Role Atlas already holds it, or (b) a **re-description** of an existing FL. A law that "has nowhere to sit" would be a finding; here the things with nowhere to sit are precisely the ones that *aren't laws* — which is the table working correctly.
2. **The Role Atlas is vindicated as the right instrument.** Inertia, friction, temperature, work, and energy are the *dynamical* faces of laws already in the catalog — exactly the "third address" the Role Atlas was built to carry. The exercise is therefore confirmation that the Engine chapter belongs where it sits (an organizing instrument, like Grammar / Role Atlas / CLD set), **not** a source of new rows.
3. **Saturation confirmed.** Consistent with the hear-and-obey result (~1 mint per 12): this exercise yields **0 mints**. The catalog remains saturated around its core.
4. **One genuine observation for the Council (not a law):** the **proportional-cost / widow's-mite weighing rule** is a real, multi-author, broadly-held principle that the catalog currently carries only *implicitly* inside FL.IX. If anything from this exercise ever warrants a manuscript touch, it is naming that weighing rule explicitly within FL.IX's chapter — a clarification, not a new entry.

## 5. Recommendations

- **Add outright:** **none.** Zero new Foundational Laws.
- **Hold (Council's call, as clarifications inside existing FLs — never new rows):**
  - The **proportional-cost weighing rule** → consider naming explicitly within **FL.IX Generosity-Provision** (2 Cor. 8:12; Mark 12:43–44; Luke 12:48).
  - The **lukewarm/wholehearted** pole → already implicit in **FL.VIII Desire-for-God**; optional one-line strengthening.
- **Structural edits:** **none.** The stress-test *validates* the existing structure and the Role-Atlas field-role layer; it asks for no new column. (If the field-roles were ever to be tabulated, that is a Role-Atlas extension, not a Periodic-Table change.)

### Noted but not recommended (demoted — disposition: ABSORB / DECLINE)
- **C1 Inertia** → ABSORB (FL.XV Hardening; FL.XVI Bondage; FL.XXII Endurance — the resistance/steadfastness already named). Field-role, not a law.
- **C2 Friction/damping** → ABSORB (the negative pole of the Community-as-Amplifier cluster: FL.XVIII, FL.XX, FL.XXIX, FL.XXX). Field-role.
- **C3 Kairos-time** → ABSORB (FL.VI Hear-and-Obey + FL.XL Abiding; "delay" is a *Property of Spiritual Law*, not a law). Measurement reframe.
- **C6 Gift of faith** → DECLINE as a law (sovereign gift, not a person-side X→Y; already held as open question V3.Exp3.Open2).
- **C8 Revival cascade** → ABSORB (Community-as-Amplifier, V2.Exp9; FL.XX Gathered-body). Collective dynamic, not a new law.
- **C9 Energy received / energeia** → already present as the catalog's grace frame. Not a candidate.
- **C10 Dual-valence of inertia** → ABSORB (the Held-Tensions/Shadow-Pairs and "easy-down, hard-up" Property already carry it). A property, not a law.

**Overall verdict: 0 new Foundational Laws.** The Engine exercise produces field-roles, quantities, and re-descriptions — it deepens *how* the existing laws are seen (the Role Atlas's job), and it surfaces one weighing-rule worth naming inside FL.IX, but it discovers no new law. The catalog holds at 47.
