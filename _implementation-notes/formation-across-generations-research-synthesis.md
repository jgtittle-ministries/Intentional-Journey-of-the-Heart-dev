## The central claim — CONFIRMED from every direction

**Faith is non-heritable; the conditions for faith are heritable.** τ is the integral of a soul's *own* engagement — no parent can accumulate it for a child; every generation's integral starts at zero.

- **Scripture states it in its own grammar:** John 1:13 — the new birth is "not of blood**s**" (plural, *ex haimatōn* — targeting biological descent as such) "nor of the will of the flesh nor of the will of man, but of God." Three negations in one verse. Ezek 18 + Jer 31:29–30 abolish the sour-grapes proverb — and Jer 31 does it *inside the new-covenant oracle*: the end of hereditary religion and the promise of first-person knowing ("they shall all know me") are the same oracle. "God has no grandchildren" (attr. Corrie ten Boom) is canonical grammar.
- **The "did not know the LORD" formula, three uses:** Judges 2:10 (one generation after Joshua); 1 Sam 2:12 (Eli's sons — priests' sons raised *inside the sanctuary*); 1 Sam 3:7 (Samuel "did not *yet* know" — until the LORD calls him by name). Maximal proximity to the things of God is not knowledge of God.
- **Sociology's version:** Niebuhr's one-generation law — "By its very nature the sectarian type of organization is valid only for one generation… **you cannot be born a voluntary convert.**" Smith/NSYR: "**We'll get what we are**" — children absorb the parents' *actual* religion, not the professed one; parents set a "glass ceiling of religious commitment above which their children rarely rise."
- **Bengtson's symmetric finding (p. 164):** warmth transmits *whatever the parents actually are* — warm unaffiliated homes transmit non-affiliation at 63%, better than most churches transmit faith. Conditions-not-content, photographed.

## What the child inherits (the model's ledger)

**Heritable:** position (raised near or far), soil condition, friction environment (the household as the community-that-lowers-γ), the exemplar repertoire (Ezek 18:14 — the son "**sees**… and does not do likewise": the parent's life is the child's *data*, readable as script or warning), the story (Deut 6:21 — answered in first person: "**We** were Pharaoh's slaves"), the practices (Exod 12), the writings (2 Tim 3:15 — which "are **able to** make you wise for salvation **through faith**": exposure delivers "able to," never "did"), the blessing/anointing (FL.XIX), the **habitus** (Bourdieu via Smith's *Imagining the Kingdom* — inherited disposition acquired before doctrine), and the idols (FL.XVII — by default, no effort required; Judges 2's rival gods are "ever fresh").
**Non-heritable:** guilt (Ezek 18:20; Deut 24:16), righteousness (Ezek 18:5–13), knowledge of the LORD (the triple formula), velocity, τ, the new birth (John 1:13). Each generation crosses its own activation barrier with its own yes — *no will overridden, at the family scale*: the parent cannot compel the yes, only warm the room.

## The asymmetry as dynamics — CONFIRMED

FL.XXI's lopsidedness (deliberate/thousand vs automatic/three-or-four) has its mechanism: **idol-transmission is passive diffusion downhill** (no energy input; children soak it up; but bounded — "third and fourth generation" ≈ one living household's radius, and per the *ləśōnəʾāy* reading only where the hating continues); **faith-transmission is active pumping uphill** (requires deliberate energy input every generation; compounds where the work continues — Deut 7:9's thousand generations, mercy outscaling cascade ~250:1). Current data: the nones' retention hit **73%** (Pew 2023–24, up from 53% in 2014) — the passive channel now out-transmits most churches; **six leave Christianity per one convert**. The Shema (sit/walk/lie/rise; *šinnantām* = teach-by-incising) is κ-engineering for a household: maximize the child's engagement-density across the whole day.

## The three-generation problem = the wineskin problem — CONFIRMED, with Weber's own category

Generation 1 fire → generation 2 form+fire → generation 3 form-without-fire. The revival chapter's routinization runs on the family time-axis, and this is not a stretch: **Weber has a named category — *Erbcharisma*, hereditary charisma** — the routinization of fire into bloodline (his Type 5). And **Weber literally quotes Wesley** in *The Protestant Ethic* ch. 5 — Wesley 1786 (verbatim, verified): "religion must necessarily produce both industry and frugality; and these cannot but produce riches. But as riches increase, so will pride, anger, and love of the world… although the form of religion remains, **the spirit is swiftly vanishing away**." (Wesley's remedy: gain all you can, save all you can, **give all you can** — breaking the riches loop.)

- **The anchor historical case — the Half-Way Covenant:** founding ~1630 → institutional crisis by ~1660. **Thirty years, one generation.** And the arc runs continuously to Edwards: 1662 accommodation → Stoddard's open communion (~1700, the Supper as "converting ordinance") → **Edwards, Stoddard's own grandson, fired 10-to-1 in 1750** for trying to re-tighten the wineskin. The Great Awakening = history's largest re-ignition campaign aimed at form-without-fire covenant members (Whitefield/Tennent preaching new birth to the baptized).
- **The declension debate (keep both sides):** Morgan — the covenant was *rigor*, not laxity; Pope — the "myth of declension": second-generation adults abstained from full membership out of **scrupulosity, not indifference**; Bercovitch — the jeremiad was a *genre*, the lament partly a ritual the form uses to keep pleading for the fire. Honest framing: the fade is real (a synod forced in 30 years; Methodism's curves; Pew's retention data), AND the story of the fade needs critical handling.
- **MTD is the degraded product, in Smith's own words:** "a **parasitic** faith… merely becoming the new spirit living within the old body" — wineskin kept, wine swapped. Kenda Creasy Dean's sharpening: "we are doing an exceedingly good job of teaching youth **what we really believe: namely, that Christianity is not a big deal**." The degradation happened in the parents' generation first. Modal exit is *drift*, not deconversion (*Back-Pocket God*: God as an app — "readily accessible, easy to control, useful only for limited purposes"; only ~10% of raised-Christian 18–29s are "resilient disciples," Barna).

## The warmth finding — the master variable (Scripture had the guardrail first)

- **Bengtson (LSOG, 357 families, 8 waves, 1971–2005):** transmission surprisingly stable (~7/10 in 1970 → ~6/10 in 2005); closeness roughly doubles it; **"parental piety will not compensate for a distant dad"** (67% close-to-father vs 51% not; exception: Judaism, where the maternal bond dominates; the father effect echoes up a generation — grandfathers > grandmothers). Coercion backfires: "shoved down my throat" → rebels; **zealot parents tend to produce rebel children**; latitude-with-warmth beat prohibition. Rank-order of chain-breakers: **interfaith marriage worst** (same-faith >2/3 follow; mixed <1/4 follow either) — divorce costs only ~10 points.
- **The mechanism has a name — Grusec & Goodnow's two steps:** internalization = accurate **perception** of the parent's value + **acceptance** of it as one's own. **Talk gives accuracy; warmth gives acceptance** (Okagaki & Bevis instantiate it on religious content). Coercion succeeds at step 1 and guarantees failure at step 2 — the prodigal-producing backfire, mechanistically explained.
- **Scripture's anti-provocation clause precedes the data:** Eph 6:4 ("do not provoke your children to anger"); Col 3:21 ("lest they become **discouraged**" — *athymeō*, drained of the very engagement transmission depends on). Calvin on Col 3:21: unreasonable severity "would excite hatred and lead them to throw off the yoke altogether."
- **Smith & Adamczyk (*Handing Down the Faith*, 2021) triad:** authoritative warmth (beats permissive AND authoritarian — refining Bengtson's "latitude") + **natural faith-talk at home** (the strongest practice variable — stronger than attendance alone; must be conversation, not program: bidirectional child-active dialogue beats unilateral transmission, Boyatzis & Janicki) + authentic parental practice ("Kids are smart. They're perceptive"). Congregations are support structure for parents; **outsourcing fails** — "the home, not congregations, is where meaningful religious socialization occurs."
- **Gender honesty:** meta-analytically (Stearns & McKinney, 30 studies) **mothers are the modal transmitter**; the father is the high-leverage *marginal* variable; **warmth is the master variable for both**, and overprotection actively undermines.
- **Smith's (JKA) gap, filled:** the *ex opere operato* critique — thickly-liturgized homes still produce leavers (Turnau's dead-church counterexample) — is answered by this lane: **liturgy transmits only when embodied love is credible.** Thick practice is necessary scaffolding; warmth is the carrier; hypocrisy-detection defeats habituation.

## The design principles — what the successful traditions engineered

1. **Daily/weekly thick practice in the home, parent-officiated.** "The table is the altar" (Berakhot 55a — after the Temple fell, the altar relocated to the home table); Shabbat as weekly parent-administered liturgy; Luther's Small Catechism headed every section "**As the head of the family should teach them in a simple way to his household**"; Scotland's 1647 Directory made household worship *disciplinable* (elders inquired; negligent heads suspended from the Supper); FAITH5/Earley as modern forms ("**We are discipled by our normal**").
2. **Question-provoking, participatory ritual.** The design intent is *explicit* in the Talmud — Pesachim 116a: the seder's odd actions are done "**so that the children will notice that something is unusual and they will ask**"; the telling (Exod 13:8, whence "Haggadah") is triggered by the question. The **Four Sons** (~2,000 years old): the Torah's four question-texts read as four kinds of child, each answered differently — including "the one who does not know how to ask," where the parent must open it. Gilgal's stones = a deliberately anomalous object whose function is to be asked about ("What do these stones mean **to you**?"). **The dark twin, canonized:** 1 Kgs 1:6 — David's failure with Adonijah stated as *the absence of the question*: "His father had never at any time displeased him by asking, 'Why have you done thus and so?'" The Deuteronomic household runs on questions in both directions.
3. **A demanding personal-appropriation threshold = entry into obligation, not completion of education.** Bar mitzvah (onset of obligation; ceremony optional); Amish adult baptism after rumspringa (**~85–90% choose in**, retention *rising* over the 20th century); LDS missions (the mission converts the missionary; Riess: ~80% of *marginal* childhood-attenders who served stayed — selection-confounded, treat as suggestive). **Confirmation failed where it became graduation** (the Confirmation Project's "graduation effect"). Niebuhr's law answered by design: nobody is born a member; each generation walks in as a convert. Cost + agency + timing at the identity-formation age. What none can engineer: the encounter itself — **means of grace, never grace** (Edwards's own distinction).
4. **High-boundary adult community reinforcement** (Bengtson's "institutional support"; the strictness thesis — robust correlation, curvilinear at the extreme).
5. **Parent as practitioner, not delegator** — the single most consistent finding across lanes.
6. **Warmth as the carrier medium** — harsh thick practice transmits *rebellion against* the tradition.

**The thickness gradient, cleanly measured:** raised-Orthodox retention **83%** (18–29 cohort, post-war thick rebuild) vs **22%** (65+ cohort, thin accommodationist Orthodoxy) — same denomination, different thickness, opposite outcomes. And the **Conservative-Judaism dead zone (41%, worst of the three streams): demand without daily practice** — doctrine thicker than Reform, homes no thicker. Botticini & Eckstein (*The Chosen Few*): Jewish survival ran on the universal-literacy-and-home norm — portable, text-and-table religion (economic causal story debated; the literacy datum solid).

**The baptismal design fork (present both charitably):** Bushnell verified — "the child is to grow up a Christian, and never know himself as being otherwise" — vs believer's-baptism (each generation converts). Symmetric failure modes: covenant presumption vs perpetual-outsider childhood. The tell: **Baptists invented child dedication; paedobaptists invented confirmation** — each design smuggles in the other's insight. Transmission needs *both* inherited conditions *and* a personal-appropriation threshold.

**Youth-ministry question, honest middle:** NSYR — youth-group/mission-trip participation did **not** independently predict lasting faith; but no study shows age-segregation *causes* apostasy (the *Divided* claim fails scrutiny). The villain is *children never seeing adults practice* (Allen's intergenerational-formation research; Bergler's "juvenilization" history; Sticky Faith's intergenerational-worship correlate; *Growing Young*'s warmth-beats-production). The causal arrow runs through the home; programs cannot substitute.

## The grace pathway — God as direct actor on every generation

- **Compensation, meta-analyzed:** Granqvist & Kirkpatrick (11 studies, N=1,465) — **sudden, intense conversions concentrate among the insecurely attached**: God as substitute attachment figure for the children of broken chains. A replicated finding, not a pious hope. (Correspondence is the ordinary means: parental religiosity predicts the teen's *mainly among the securely attached* — warm bonds are the channel. Both pathways true of different people.)
- **Scripture's cases:** Samuel called by name in Eli's corrupt house (Eli's one good transmission act: teaching the boy how to answer); Josiah seeking "while he was yet a boy" *before the Book resurfaced*, reaching back to "David his father" when the near fathers failed; Ezek 16 (the abandoned newborn: "Live!"); Ps 68:5–6 (Father of the fatherless, who "settles the solitary in a home" — conditions re-issued to those who inherited none).
- **The grandparent backup channel:** Copen & Silverstein — grandmother→grandchild transmission **survives parental divorce**, which damages the mother→child link; grandparents as the redundant circuit when the middle link breaks; ~4 in 10 grandchildren share the grandparents' tradition (stable across 30 years); grandparental warmth among the strongest predictors. *The peer-reviewed base for what a Granddaddy's testimony is for.*
- **Return dynamics:** Bengtson's prodigal factors — warmth maintained *through* the apostasy, latitude, a consistent foundation to return to ("Her warmth, openness, and example left them with the freedom to leave, and then return to, her faith"). Longitudinal numbers from Wave 9 (1971→2016, 45 years): **21% of boomers became more religious in later life** — a named driver: **"the desire to pass beliefs to grandchildren."** LifeWay: top reason returners came back = family encouragement (37%). Luke 15: "he came to himself" — the household's deposit ("my father's hired servants have bread") germinating in the far country under famine (Bailey's counter-reading flagged: the father's run completes what memory began — either way serves). Isa 55:10–11 (the word does not return empty — on *its* timetable; cf. FL.XLVII); Eccl 11:1.
- **The closing case is the researcher himself:** Bengtson — raised in a Swedish-pietist revival line; "I was to become the weak link in [the] chain"; then, in his 60s–70s, *while finishing the book*, "an unexpected spiritual experience" — returned, sang in the choir at Trinity Episcopal every Sunday, buried from the church he came back to (d. 2019). **The researcher of prodigals was a prodigal who returned.**

## Myth-checks the chapter performs for its audience

1. **The "Swiss study" father statistic** (if dad practices, 33–44%; if only mom, 2–3%): the Haug/Warner source is real (1994 Swiss survey, Council of Europe monograph 2000) but it is a **descriptive cross-tab** — no controls, no causal design, one country — that entered ministry culture via a 2003 *Touchstone* essay. Demote to illustration; the defensible father-claim is Bengtson's warmth finding.
2. **The "93% of fathers" conversion statistic: a genuine myth** — traced to an undocumented reflection question in a 1996 Promise Keepers book. Never use.
3. **Sticky Faith's 5:1 adult ratio: aspirational rule of thumb**, not a measured threshold. Cite as FYI's proposed goal.
4. **"85% evangelical retention"** doesn't match Pew's tables (~65–70% citable, wave-dependent).
