## Verdict

**Commend warmly; revise lightly — the fixes are surgical and the spine stands.** This is the panel's strongest opening posture of any IJH review to date, because the chapter pre-empts the very faults the prior Vol 3 nine-author panel raised.

### What the panel credited as already solved (the fairness ledger)
All nine explicitly *declined to re-raise* the prior panel's complaints, crediting them as handled in this chapter:
- the **analogy boundary** stated up front ("schemas… no units… structure, not magnitude"), pinned to the TFT-Challenged appendix;
- **G sovereign throughout**, "there is no mechanism, there is a Father," held Speculative/Frontier;
- **F_s named a schema** that "computes nothing";
- the **in-chapter pastoral guard** ("A word for the one who feels stuck") — the guard the prior panel found had "migrated to Vol 1" is now *present and cross-linked*;
- **"energy" grounded in the NT word *energeia*** (Schwarz) — recovered, not borrowed (Weinberg: "the most intellectually honest thing in the chapter… it inverts the usual sin").

So every finding below targets *this chapter's new material*, and the leaks cluster in two predictable places: **the collective/congregational tier** (where the individual-level guards stop reaching) and **the newly-minted quantities** (temperature, the phase map, the gift-of-faith recast).

## Per-reviewer (affirmation · biggest concern)
- **Keller** — affirms grace kept genuinely upstream ("God reads the integral" preaches grace). *Concern:* the **numbered revival sequence and phase "levers" teach an operator's posture** the prose then has to keep correcting — put grace *inside* the verbs, not only around them.
- **Grudem** — affirms the open-system/monergism handling and the careful *energeia* citations. *Concern:* the **gift of faith (1 Cor 12:9) is recast as a private inertia-drop**, bypassing the "for the common good" charism-for-the-body sense (12:7, 11) — flag it as an extension or honor the body sense.
- **Lewis** — affirms the chapter as honest *Transposition* (the higher read into the lower without reduction). *Concern:* **"temperature = average agitation" flattens ordered ardor into entropic disorder** — physical heat means *disorder*, but "hearts burning" is love rightly *aimed*; the metaphor as worded says what the author doesn't believe.
- **Willard** — affirms agency kept with God; the widow's-mite *work* reading; the friction→discipleship cure. *Concern:* **strong on God's agency, thin on the believer's responsive part** — the revival sequence and phase diagnostic lean operational with no stated *receiving/waiting/abiding* posture; the disciplines that gradually lower mₛ are underweighted.
- **Foster** — affirms the real in-chapter word for the stuck and the eschatological reserve. *Concern:* **more apparatus than a soul can pray** (a dozen symbols); guard the *community* against the "are we hot enough?" performance pressure; consider a closing turn that *lays the apparatus down*.
- **Weinberg** — affirms the boundary stated before the first symbol and the refusal to compute. *Concern:* the **boundary ("structure, not magnitude") under-describes the chapter's real practice of ordinal more/less reasoning** — tighten it to "direction, sign, threshold, and ordinal more-than/less-than — never a number," and every leak dissolves.
- **'t Hooft** — affirms the category-error met head-on (the "verdict on a soul" guard) and the determinism trap disarmed at F_s. *Concern:* **"revival as a phase transition" + the (Tₛ, κ) phase map import *collective* determinism** — the freedom-of-the-Person guard is installed for the individual and left off the *congregation*; extend it.
- **Penrose** — affirms aₛ=Fₛ/mₛ correctly inverted, the dual-valence of mₛ, the literate mₛ-vs-γ (inertia vs damping) distinction, latent heat used accurately. *Concern:* **the boundary forbids magnitude, yet the symbols carry it** (the ½ coefficient, the implied exponential, the integral on τ) with no spiritual referent — police the *symbols*, not only the prose.
- **Carroll** — affirms the open-system claim and the velocity-proportional γ as formalism that genuinely *earns* its weight. *Concern:* the **"schema, not computation" discipline is applied to the borrowed physics but not to the new constructs** — the **congregation phase diagram is a real two-parameter classification scheme dressed as a metaphor** (its single most load-bearing move, filed as decorative).

## Convergences (for adjudication)
1. **The collective tier is under-guarded (HEADLINE — 6 reviewers, both clusters).** 't Hooft (phase transition = collective determinism, *High*), Carroll (phase diagram is load-bearing, not decorative, *High*), Lewis ("matter has no will"), Willard & Foster (the minimum-factor "raise it and watch it flow" hands a leader a control panel), Keller (the lever language). The individual guard ("never a verdict on a soul") does not reach the *body*.
2. **The boundary under-describes the chapter's real practice (3 physicists).** Weinberg, Penrose, Carroll converge: the chapter trades in *ordinal* magnitude and carries notational constants with no spiritual referent — the boundary as worded claims *less* than the chapter does, which paradoxically makes honest comparison look like over-reach.
3. **The temperature picture needs both a theological and a physics fix.** Lewis (ordered ardor, not disorder) + Foster (community performance pressure) + Carroll (Tₛ slips between "average of motion" and "driving knob" — name the Spirit as the heat *reservoir*) + Weinberg/Penrose (the unwritten exponential the word "steeply" leans on).
4. **Agency asymmetry (Willard, Keller, Foster).** Rich on God's part, thin on the believer's responsive part; the revival steps read as an operable pipeline.

**Tension between reviewers (and its resolution):** Foster wants *less* apparatus (simplicity); the physicists want the apparatus made *more precise/honest* (name the real claims). Same resolution as the prior panels — **better signposting + the Speculative framing**, not adding or cutting: tighten the boundary, mark which constructs are real claims vs. decoration, and (Foster) close by laying the apparatus down.

## Prioritized punch-list

**HIGH**
- **H1 — Extend the guards from the soul to the congregation.** Add a freedom-of-the-body sentence *inside* the phase section (a congregation, like a person, retains a freedom no point in (Tₛ, κ) can necessitate); give revival step 5 ("cascade, a phase transition") the same "without overriding any one person's yes" rider step 1 has; return the minimum-factor "raise it and watch it flow" to God's hand at the point it's offered. *(6 reviewers.)*
- **H2 — Repair the gift-of-faith section.** Flag the inertia-drop reading as an *extension* of 1 Cor 12:9 (or honor the "for the common good" body sense, 12:7/11); change "movement… becomes effortless" → "suddenly possible" (Gethsemane was costly *work*); pick mₛ *or* Eₐ (or say why both). *(Grudem, Lewis, Carroll, Keller.)*

**MED**
- **M1 — Tighten the boundary + police the symbols.** Reword to "direction, sign, threshold, and ordinal more-than/less-than — never a number," and add one line that the numerical constants and operators (the ½, the integral signs, any implied exponential) are inherited notation with no spiritual content. *(Weinberg, Penrose, Carroll.)*
- **M2 — Fix the temperature picture.** (a) Say spiritual heat is *ordered* ardor, not entropic disorder; (b) guard the community against "are we hot enough?" performance pressure (fervency is a fruit, not a target); (c) name the Spirit as the heat *reservoir* so "raising the temperature" is coherent. *(Lewis, Foster, Carroll, Weinberg.)*
- **M3 — Name the believer's responsive part.** Add the receiving/waiting/abiding posture *inside* the revival sequence; name the disciplines that gradually lower mₛ (the ordinary apprenticeship). *(Willard, Keller, Foster.)*
- **M4 — *energeia* precision.** "Scripture spoke of God's energy long before… joules" conflates the NT *energeia* (working/operation) with the physics scalar; claim Scripture names God's *working*, not "energy" in the physicist's sense. *(Grudem.)*
- **M5 — Commensurability note.** mₛ, γ, and Tₛ each bundle incommensurable things under one scalar; a line acknowledging they are not even a single ordinal scale would be honest. *(’t Hooft.)*

**LOW**
- "a revival… becomes a sequence" → "shows an anatomy without ceasing to be a mystery" (Grudem).
- Own τ as "engagement-weighted time" — the integral is rhetorical (dτ/dt = engagement) (Penrose).
- End the Work section by handing the cost to Christ (Matt 11:30) (Keller).
- Signal the deliberate clock-change to dτ in Pₛ (Penrose).
- "God reads the integral" — own it as a deliberate, named exception (a real quantity) rather than smuggling it (Carroll).

## Disposition
The headline was that the chapter's *own* guards are excellent and simply needed to **reach one level up — from the individual soul to the congregation — and one level in — into the new quantities.** None of the fixes touched the structural analogies (the Role Atlas / CLD kind), which all nine affirmed.
