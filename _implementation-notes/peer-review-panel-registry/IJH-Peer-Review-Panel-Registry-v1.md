# IJH Peer-Review Panel Registry — v1

**For the Council of Stewards · first meeting, June 28, 2026 · prepared by John G. Tittle**

## 1. What this registry is (and is not)

Over the development of the IJH project we have run a number of **simulated, charitable-strengthening peer reviews** — panels of reviewers, each a persona drawn from a real figure's published body of work, brought in to stress-test a piece of the project before it is considered settled. This document gathers every panel run to date, the reason each reviewer was chosen, and the specific target each was given, and proposes a standing way to **empanel the right reviewers for new material** as the work continues.

A few honest qualifications, so the Council knows exactly what it is looking at:

- These are **simulated personas**, reconstructed from each figure's writings — not the persons themselves, and several are no longer living. They are a *diagnostic instrument*, not endorsements by those authors.
- The method is deliberately **charitable** — it strengthens, it does not demolish — and it is **diagnostic only**: nothing is ever changed in the manuscript on a panel's say-so. Findings are adjudicated by the author (and, going forward, by this Council).
- **What the panels are *for*.** This work is, at heart, the author's **testimony** to his children and grandchildren — what he has discovered of how God works — not an apologetic for skeptics. The panels therefore exist to keep that testimony **true, pastorally safe, and clear** for the family it is written to. They are not here to win arguments or to armor the work against opponents.
- A simulated panel **does not replace** this Council, nor real outside expert review, nor the testing program of Vol 4. It is a way to hear, early and cheaply, what a thoughtful reader from a given tradition would most likely press.

**The Council is invited to extend this bench.** Section 6 names the lenses we have and the lenses we lack, and leaves room for each Steward to nominate the reviewers they would most want consulted on future material.

## 2. The standing method (the guidelines developed so far)

Every panel below was run the same way. This is the reusable protocol:

1. **Charitable-strengthening personas**, each drawn from a real body of work, briefed to assume the project is in good faith and to make it stronger.
2. **Per reviewer:** affirmations first, then findings by section, then a single biggest concern — in that reviewer's authentic voice.
3. **Adversarial verification:** each consequential finding is handed to a "refuter" who tries to break it before it is weighted, so plausible-but-wrong findings do not survive.
4. **Synthesis:** convergences and tensions across the panel, then a prioritized **High / Medium / Low** punch-list.
5. **Diagnostic discipline:** no manuscript edits without the author's explicit go; every claim is checked against the actual text; the panel must **be fair** and credit what the work already self-critiques.
6. **Artifact:** a working `.md` plus a Council-ready `.docx`, kept under `_implementation-notes/peer-review-*/`, dev-only, never published to the site.

## 5. Proposed empanelment guide (target → panel)

| A **cross-volume / quick conscience** pass | McGrath · Peterson · Polkinghorne | the tight three-voice panel |

*First live run (done):* the new Vol 3 chapter **"The Engine Beneath the Force"** was reviewed by the **quantitative** panel — the registry's first end-to-end use. The nine-author panel produced nine findings; all were adjudicated, implemented dev-first one at a time, and mirrored to prod, and the review artifact is marked resolved (`_implementation-notes/peer-review-engine-chapter/`). The empanelment, method, verification, and mirror all worked as designed.

## 6. Invitation to the Council — extend the bench

The bench above is strong but uneven. Stewards are asked to **nominate additional reviewers** for the lenses we lack. A good addition (1) brings a distinct, defensible lens not already covered; (2) is drawn from a substantial body of work we can reconstruct faithfully; (3) represents a tradition or discipline the project means to serve; and (4) can be charitable and rigorous at once.

**Coverage we have:** Reformed · evangelical-Anglican · Pentecostal/charismatic · contemplative-Western · systematic · imaginative-apologetic · theologian-scientist · pastoral; physics (four rival interpreters); spiritual direction · inner-healing · lay-care · group-facilitation · trauma/safeguarding · biblical-counseling.

**Lenses we lack (suggested directions — the Council's own favorites are exactly what is wanted here):**

| Gap | Why it matters | (Council nominee) |
|---|---|---|
| **A woman theologian / biblical scholar** | Only two women are seated (both practitioners); the project has a standing, openly-named gap on the "women's side" of formation and character work | |
| **Eastern Orthodox / patristic voice** | Theosis and the Fathers are a deep test of the union-and-formation material we have only from a Western frame | |
| **Roman Catholic sacramental / spiritual-theology voice** | The sacramental flank surfaced in FL.XXVII; no formal Catholic systematic voice is seated | |
| **Majority-world / global-South voice** | The project aspires to be "the church catholic's framework," not a Western one | |
| **Analytic philosopher of religion** | To stress the *arguments* (e.g., the descriptive→normative move) harder than a sympathetic panel does | |
| **Old-Testament / biblical-theology & hermeneutics specialist** | The "by what warrant does a passage become a law?" question deserves a dedicated exegete | |

*One kind of panel has been deliberately **set aside** — not offered for the Council to fill: an openly adversarial / apologetic panel. This work is the author's **testimony** to his family, not a defense for skeptics; the panels keep it true, pastorally safe, and clear, and do not exist to stress the foundations against an opponent.*

## 7. How a review is run, in one line

*Pick the target → empanel from §5 (or a Council-chosen set) → run the §2 method → adjudicate the High/Med/Low punch-list with the author and Council → implement dev-first, mirror on the author's word.* The artifacts are kept so the next Steward can see what was tried and why.
