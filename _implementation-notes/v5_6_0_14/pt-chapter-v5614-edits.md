### What the Attachment-Theory Pass Revealed

The v5_6_0_14 attachment-theory exploratory pass surfaced one finalist (FL.XXXV Trust-Substrate Law) and demoted thirteen other candidates to noted-but-not-recommended. The pass's stress-test produced three structural observations about the catalog's shape that warrant explicit articulation independent of the FL.XXXV admission. Each observation is information the pass produced about the table's structural state, surfaced by pushing a genuinely-new lens through the existing structure. The observations are addressed below in the order they were surfaced.

**Observation 1: The P0/GII empty cell and its prior unarticulated status.** Prior to the v5_6_0_14 admission, the Period 0 row's Heart cell held no Foundational entry, while the same row's Mind & Will cell held FL.X Faith-Sight Law, the same row's Body & Action cell held three Foundational entries (FL.VI Hear-and-Obey, FL.XVI Bondage, FL.IX Generosity-Provision), and the same row's Structural Frame cell held two Foundational entries (FL.I Sowing-and-Reaping, FL.XXIII Sabbath Rest). The Period 0 row's Spirit cell held one Foundational entry (FL.XV Hardening). The empty Heart cell was a structural asymmetry whose existence had not been previously articulated in the chapter's "What the Shape Reveals" section. The attachment-theory lens — whose primary territory is precisely heart-level substrate dynamics — pointed directly at the empty cell, and the candidate the lens surfaced cleared the inclusion bar on substantial scriptural footing independent of the lens's framing. The structural lesson is that the previously-existing structural asymmetry at P0/GII was a real gap rather than an artifact of the catalog's emphasis, and that the gap's prior unarticulated status was itself a feature of the chapter's prose worth acknowledging — the catalog's gaps are not all visible from within the catalog's existing framing, and the v5_6_0_14 admission's surfacing of the gap retroactively is part of the structural lesson the pass produced.

**Observation 2: The three-Group scale-invariant articulation of the faith-family.** With the FL.XXXV admission, the Period 0 row holds the catalog's first three-Group articulation of a single operational family at the scale-invariant scale: heart-relational face at GII (FL.XXXV Trust-Substrate), cognitive-instrumental face at GIV (FL.X Faith-Sight), embodied-active face at GV (FL.VI Hear-and-Obey alongside FL.XVI Bondage's Mirror). The three-Group articulation is structurally novel for the catalog. Prior to the v5_6_0_14 admission, the catalog held two-Group articulations of operational families at various scales (the FL.III Heart-Throne / FL.XIV Vanity-of-Substitutes pair at P1/GII as a single-Group articulation of the throne-and-substitution family; the FL.XVII Substitution-Cascade / FL.XXI Household Formation pair at P4/GII as a single-Group constitutive bidirectional articulation of the generational transmission family; the FL.VI / FL.XVI pair at P0/GV as a single-Group bidirectional articulation of the obedience-constitutes-state family), but had not held a three-Group articulation of a single operational family at any scale. The three-Group pattern teaches the catalog something about the Group axis's operational reach: a single operational family can hold articulation across the Heart, Mind & Will, and Body & Action dimensions at the same scale, with each dimension's articulation operating by its dimension-specific mechanism (heart-relational substrate; cognitive-instrumental substance; embodied-active operation) while the family's underlying operational unity holds across the dimensions. The pattern may extend to other operational families at other scales — the love-family, the worship-family, the obedience-family, the hope-family each may admit three-Group articulation at appropriate scales — and the structural observation invites subsequent work to test whether the three-Group pattern is generalizable across the catalog's other operational families.

**Observation 3: The substrate-to-operation relationship across distinct Groups at the same scale.** The FL.XXXV admission produces the catalog's first articulated substrate-to-operation relationship at the same scale across distinct Groups (FL.XXXV at GII as substrate; FL.VI at GV as operation; both at Period 0). The substrate-to-operation relationship is structurally distinct from the parent-child relationship the catalog has held across adjacent scales (e.g., FL.XXII Endurance-Hope at P1/GIII as individual-scale child of the Suffering-as-Formation Loop at P5/GIII as cosmic-scale parent), from the constitutive bidirectional pair relationship the catalog has held within the same cell (e.g., FL.XVII / FL.XXI at P4/GII), and from the Mirror relationship within the same cell (e.g., FL.VI / FL.XVI at P0/GV). The substrate-to-operation relationship operates at the same Period across distinct Groups: the substrate-at-GII produces the operational conditions under which the operation-at-GV occurs in its God-ward form, with the substrate's absence producing the operation's substitutional forms (anxious-performance or avoidant-self-protection). The relationship pattern teaches the catalog that the Group axis is not merely a categorical-classification axis but encodes operational relationships among Groups — substrate-dimensions produce operational-dimensions; orientation-dimensions condition substrate-dimensions; architectural-dimensions hold the operational-dimensions in unified form. Subsequent work may surface additional substrate-to-operation relationships at other scales — the P2 dyadic-scale heart-substrate-to-action-operation relationship the pre-pass Candidate 7 (Pair-Bond Attachment Substrate Law) tested but did not finalize; the P3 community-scale corporate-heart-substrate-to-corporate-action-operation relationship that FL.XXXII Communal Worship Heart-Alignment may already implicitly hold — and the structural observation invites subsequent work to articulate whether the substrate-to-operation pattern is generalizable across the catalog's other Periods.

**Cumulative significance.** The three observations together establish that the v5_6_0_14 attachment-theory pass produced both a substantive admission (FL.XXXV) and structural learning about the catalog's shape (the three observations). The combination is what the table-stress-test methodology the catalog uses is designed to produce. The lens was used as a candidate-surfacing heuristic rather than as a source of doctrinal authority, and the lens's primary value was the lens's ability to surface candidates the catalog's existing framing had not previously emphasized — with the candidate the lens surfaced clearing the inclusion bar on its own scriptural footing and with the structural observations the candidate produced being information about the catalog's shape rather than information from the lens itself. The pattern — exploratory lens used as candidate-surfacing heuristic, with admissions cleared on scriptural footing and structural observations preserved as information about the catalog — is generalizable to future passes using other exploratory lenses, and the v5_6_0_14 methodology is documented in the present sub-section as a pattern for subsequent application.
