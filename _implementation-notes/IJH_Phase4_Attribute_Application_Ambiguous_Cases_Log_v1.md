## Pre-pass framework-level questions deferred to Phase 5

The grill that scoped Phase 4 surfaced three framework-level questions whose resolution is held for a deliberate Phase 5 work-stream rather than handled as a side-effect of Phase 4. Each is listed here so the log doesn't lose track of them.

**FW.1 — V directionality tag's extended scope.** The V tag currently covers both Person↔God (the original scope) and Christ→participant (the matured-catalog extension introduced for FL.XLII Kingdom-Confrontation in Phase 2 and applied to FL.XLI, FL.XLIV, FL.XLV, V1.Open Miracle Frame in Phase 3). The dual-meaning is documented in HOW TO READ but not formalized. Phase 5 should resolve whether to (a) keep V informal, (b) add formal K tag for kingdom-confrontation, (c) add formal new tag for Christ-toward-participant directionality, or (d) redefine V definitionally.

**FW.2 — V1.Open Miracle Frame tier under the new framework.** The Miracle Frame is at Reasonably Inferred; its territory has been substantially articulated through the Phase 3 admissions (FL.XL, FL.XLI, FL.XLII, FL.XLIV). The structural question is whether the Frame should now be (a) promoted to Foundational, (b) held at Reasonably Inferred with the precedented broader-above-specific structure named (parallel to FL.XXII / Suffering-as-Formation Loop), (c) demoted or absorbed, or (d) decided in a deliberate Phase 5 tier-review pass that handles all accumulated tier candidates together.

**FW.3 — V2.Exp10 Skill Development anomaly at P3/GVI.** The entry spans Individual and Community scales simultaneously and has been flagged with ⚠ since the original chapter articulation. Phase 4 will tag it with "attributes-deferred" rather than forcing a single-scale scale-range. Phase 5 should resolve whether to (a) split into two entries (P1/GVI individual + P3/GVI community), (b) relocate/rename, (c) articulate the Scale Transfer Meta-Law the chapter hints at, or (d) other.
