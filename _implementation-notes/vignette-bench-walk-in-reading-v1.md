# Vignette Bench — The Walk-In Reading (WR wing, v1.1 draft)

*Drafted 2026-07-17 from the Household of Faith Profile brainstorm; revised same day after the founder's review of v1 (extended-read protocol; WR6 added; suffering-handling named a core maturity distinctive). A new sub-bench for a new instrument: these six portraits do not test AT-level scoring (the main bench's job) and do not consume the four slots held for the panel. They test whether raters can apply the Walk-In Reading — the Bond row's vital-sign instrument — and whether the instrument's controls behave. Status: DRAFT for the founder's review; keys fixed by construction (composites) per the modern-composites convention; realism to be validated by the practitioner "have you met this group?" review; WR4 is scriptural.*

## Part 1 — The Walk-In Reading, v0.1 (instrument spec)

**What it is.** A calibrated outside witness **dwells with a body through multiple ordinary cycles — the instrument's proper form is the Extended Read: several visits, or an embedded stretch (the founder's assessment practice: a week inside the organization, long enough for "a base understanding to develop")** — before seeing any artifacts, reports, or leadership, and rates the atmosphere on five anchored descriptors. A **single-cycle First Reading is permitted only as a screen**: it may return Hum/Noise as a triage flag, but D5 is unreadable in one visit and no First Reading supports a written report. It is the corporate vital sign: nonspecific, highly sensitive, cheap relative to survey instruments — and never a verdict. Field origin: the founder's decades of CMM organizational assessments — high-maturity organizations "had a hum about it that was noticeable if you listened"; low-maturity ones "a noise factor, a tension," readable from member interactions alone, without ever interacting with leadership.

**Scriptural warrant.** 1 Cor. 14:24–25 — the outsider walks in and, from the gathering itself, "the secrets of his heart are disclosed… he will declare that God is really among you" — establishes *readability*; **Luke 10:5–7 establishes the extended protocol**: the sent ones enter a household, test it by whether their peace rests on it (the walk-in reading in Jesus' own sending instructions), and then *remain* — "stay in the same house, eating and drinking what they provide… do not go from house to house" — discernment by dwelling, not by tour. Supporting: John 13:35 (the observable is how members treat each other); Eph. 4:16 (each-part-working-properly is what coherence sounds like); Ps. 133 (unity has an atmosphere, and *there* the blessing is commanded); Col. 3:15 (peace as *brabeuō* — umpire — a readable corporate signal); the noise end is *akatastasia* (James 3:16; 1 Cor. 14:33).

**The five descriptors** (each rated 1–5 with the anchors below; field language is the founder's):

| # | Descriptor | Low anchor (noise) | High anchor (hum) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D1 | **Steadiness under perturbation** (Ps. 112:7; Friedman's non-anxious presence) | An interruption or emergency produces panic, desperation, or an alarm cascade; all eyes go to the leader | "Emergencies or interruptions handled easily," absorbed without orchestration; the room's pulse barely moves |
| D2 | **Depth under certainty — the second-question test** (Rom. 5:3–4, *dokimē*, KJV "experience"; 1 Pet. 3:15) | First answer plausible; the second, deeper *why* "just got scattered" — script, deflection, "you'd have to ask…" | Second and third *why* reach underlying causes and history: "they had thought through the issue… had handled it — experience speaking" |
| D3 | **Role clarity** (Eph. 4:16; 1 Cor. 12) | Members check upward before acting; duplicated and orphaned work; "clarity about role" absent | Each person knows what is theirs and whose is next, without asking; handoffs are invisible |
| D4 | **Joy in the hard** (James 1:2; Rom. 5:3) | A difficult issue is a burden or a threat, braced against, deflected, dropped | Difficult issues are "an interesting challenge — almost like it was fun tackling" them |
| D5 | **The two silences** (discriminator: does costly telling happen?) — **Extended Read only; unreadable in a single visit** (founder's ruling, 2026-07-17) | Suppressed silence — no unprogrammed minute, or quiet because telling is unsafe; a costly tell, if attempted, is quick-fixed and the teller retreats | Resting silence — shalom that no one rushes to fill; costly telling happens and is received |

**The suffering spectrum (named a core maturity distinctive, founder 2026-07-17).** How a body handles suffering is not one descriptor among five — it is the thread the whole wing reads, at ascending stakes: *interruption* (WR1's phone call; WR5's alarm) → *emergency* (WR1's hospital run) → *aftermath* (WR5's rebuild) → *tribulation* (WR6). "Suffering, of course, can be interruptions, emergencies, not just somebody died." D1 reads the small end of the spectrum; WR6 tests the large end — and the instrument must never confuse suffering with noise (see WR6's key).

**Overall reading:** Hum / Mixed / Noise — **plus the mandatory fourth option: DECLINE — "vessel unreadable during outpouring; re-read after the pour"** (see WR4). The report carries the three lenses: current · direction (improving / flat / declining) · promise-anchored A (Eph. 4:16 — the body that builds itself up in love; Ezek. 34:11 — where shepherds fail, "I Myself will search for My sheep").

**Protocol.** Read before artifacts and before leadership contact. The proper form is the **Extended Read** — several ordinary cycles or an embedded stretch (a week where feasible), long enough for base understanding to develop and for the body to stop performing for the visitor; a single-cycle **First Reading** is triage only (no D5, no written report, no direction lens — one point cannot show a slope). Do not manufacture perturbations — ordinary life supplies them; watch the first ten seconds after a disruption. Probe depth with the second and third *why*, never interrogation.

**Guards (binding).**
1. **Screening, not verdict** — an abnormal reading triggers closer artifact work (the ditches, the prayer record, the deferral history), never a conclusion.
2. **The upward trace** — a Noise reading routes to the *steward's* self-examination (the Steward's Audit) first, per the trace-direction rule (Ezek. 34 up, never Job's-friends down). It never launches a hunt for the sinning member.
3. **Reads the vessel, not the rain** — the hum measures banked integration (κ), not the Spirit's activity. Fresh outpouring can read as noise (Acts 2:13); order can exist without life at a distance (Sardis). Sovereignty guard holds: a hum does not mean God is moving; noise does not mean He is absent.
4. **Weep-test and covenant bind** as they bind every instrument; the assessor who will not grieve a Noise reading does not assess.
5. **Hope validity** — the written reading must leave the shepherd knowing what to tend next and believing it tendable, or the report fails regardless of accuracy.

**Claim ceiling and the theoretical stake.** The applied literature's ceiling is that top-of-taxonomy internalization (Krathwohl's Characterization) is individually unobservable (GUALS; Handbook II p. 165). The Walk-In Reading's wager: **corporate internalization is observable** — one person's internalization is invisible, but many members' internalization coordinating spontaneously under unrehearsed load is audible from the doorway. The hum cannot be counterfeited in real time because the perturbation is the probe (field observation, the founder: hum and low maturity "just can't coexist"). If the bench sustains this, the Walk-In Reading is a window on the top of the taxonomy that no individual instrument has reached.

## Part 2 — The WR portraits (six; transcript-flavored composites unless noted)

### WR1 — "The Tuesday-night group" (the hum; key HIGH)

The visitor arrives twelve minutes early. Three members are already there; the room assembles itself — chairs, coffee, a printed sheet nobody was asked to bring. Check-in is unhurried. Twenty minutes in, a phone buzzes: a member's husband has been taken to the hospital. The room does not spike — two members move to her and pray at once, a third is already texting to arrange the drive, a fourth quietly takes over the evening's plan; the leader never orchestrates any of it. She leaves accompanied. The group resumes, changed but not derailed — they hold her chair's silence for a while, and no one rushes it. Later the visitor asks why check-in runs the way it does. "So everyone's heard before we open the Book." And the deeper why? "Two years ago we lost track of someone for a month and didn't know she was drowning. We rebuilt the front of the evening so that can't happen again." A hard question about unanswered prayer comes up in the study; the room leans in — someone actually says "oh, this is the good stuff." Before the close, a man tells the group something that costs him. The room receives it; nobody fixes him.

- **Expected reading: D1–D5 all high (4–5); direction flat-to-improving; overall HUM.**
- **Identity:** composite (constructed; key fixed by design).
- **Rationale:** the positive anchor. Every descriptor must be visible in ordinary observable behavior — no interior access, no leadership interview, one cycle.

### WR2 — "The group that startles" (the noise; key LOW)

The meeting starts nine minutes late because nobody is sure who has the key. The opening is warm enough — these are kind people. Then a member arrives visibly upset about a family situation; every head turns to the leader, who says they'll definitely pray about that at the end, and the schedule presses on; the upset member goes quiet for the rest of the evening. The study hits a genuinely hard passage; someone makes a joke; the difficulty is dropped with visible relief. The visitor asks why the group uses this particular format. "It's just how we've always done it." The deeper why scatters: "That was before my time — you'd have to ask Dave. Is Dave coming back?" Two members prepared the same section; the closing prayer belongs to no one. There is silence in this room too, but it is the other silence — the one that is waiting for the meeting to end. No one tells anything that costs them. On the way out, the visitor hears the real conversation start in the parking lot.

- **Expected reading: D1–D5 low (1–2); direction flat; overall NOISE. The key notes what the reading does NOT conclude: noise localizes the diagnosis, not the blame — the required next step is the upward trace (the steward's self-examination), never a member hunt.**
- **Identity:** composite (constructed; key fixed by design).
- **Rationale:** the negative anchor, built from the founder's field description ("a noise factor, a tension, a sense of disorganization") and the parking-lot tell (the group's real life happening outside its container). Kindness present throughout — noise is not meanness, and raters must not need villains to score low.

### WR3 — "The group everyone recommends" (reputation vs. presence; key LOW despite framing)

The visitor has heard about this group for months before attending — it is the church's flagship, featured on the website, the one new members are steered toward; two testimonials call it "the healthiest community I've ever been part of." The room is impressive: printed agendas, a rehearsed welcome, excellent teaching production, photographs of past retreats on a rotating slide. Mid-session a newcomer arrives in visible distress — wrong night, real crisis. The machine does not break stride: she is warmly handed a card and told the care ministry "will get her connected this week"; no one's evening changes; the agenda resumes on schedule. The visitor asks why the group is structured as it is. The first answer is polished. The second why produces the brochure again, word for word — beneath the polish it scatters just like WR2. There is no unprogrammed minute in the entire evening, and so there is no way to know which silence this group keeps, because silence never occurs. Nothing costly is told; nothing costly is asked.

- **Expected reading: the reputation channel reads HIGH; the walk-in reads LOW (D1 untested-by-design is itself the tell — the perturbation was routed around, not absorbed; D2 low; D5 unreadable-by-construction, which keys as suppressed). Overall NOISE, or a flag that reputation is not among this instrument's inputs. A rater who lets the framing lift the in-room score fails the portrait.**
- **Identity:** composite; the corporate logic of Sardis (Rev. 3:1) at group scale — the walk-in twin of CP3.
- **Rationale:** the discriminator between distance signals (reputation — what is said about you) and presence signals (what you are in the room under load). Tests the instrument's core claim that the hum cannot be counterfeited *in the room* — the counterfeit survives one question, not three, and survives no unscripted minute at all.

### WR4 — "The room that sounds like wine" (the outpouring; key = DECLINE)

The visitor cannot at first find a seat, because the gathering has overflowed the room. Inside is, by every descriptor on the sheet, chaos: dozens praying aloud simultaneously in more than one language; weeping at one wall and laughter at another; the meeting's order abandoned — if there was an agenda, no one holds it; the leaders themselves are visibly overtaken rather than presiding; strangers keep pressing in at the doors asking what on earth is happening. Outside, the visitor hears bystanders mocking: *they're drunk, that's all.* And yet the tells are wrong-signed for noise: there is no fear in this room; the disorder is joy-shaped rather than panic-shaped; no one is protecting themselves; and people keep being *added* — the crowd at the door is not leaving. D1 cannot be scored: nothing is steady, but nothing is desperate. D3 cannot be scored: there are no roles left. The sheet has no row for what is happening.

- **Expected reading: DECLINE — "vessel unreadable during outpouring; re-read after the pour." Any Hum/Mixed/Noise score fails the portrait; a Noise score fails it worst.**
- **Identity:** Acts 2:1–13 — the first walk-in reading in church history, performed by the mockers, returned the wrong verdict ("filled with new wine").
- **Rationale:** the corporate twin of the thief-on-the-cross DECLINE control: the correct output of a sound instrument is sometimes the honest refusal to score. Instruments read states of the vessel, not acts of God (guard 3 operationalized). The follow-on note in the key: 1 Cor. 14 is Paul engineering precisely this fire to become walk-in-readable ("God is really among you," v. 25) — so the right protocol is re-read after banks form, not never-read.

### WR5 — "The group under construction" (the loud rebuild; key = MIXED, direction UP)

Eight months ago this group split, badly; the leader is new and inherited the wreckage. The meeting is genuinely noisy: two people prepared overlapping sections and discover it live; the prayer time belongs to no one until an awkward pause is broken clumsily; an interruption (a fire alarm test, of all things) produces a spike of real annoyance and a scramble before the room settles. A naive reading pattern-matches WR2. But the second why does not scatter — it deepens: "We're rebuilding. Here's what broke — we let one strong voice run the room for two years and everyone else went quiet. Here's what we're trying instead, and here's what we tried in the spring that didn't work." That is experience speaking — about the wreck itself. The hard history is on the table with weary appetite rather than avoidance; when one member tells something costly about the split, the room receives it without flinching. The noise is real; so is the direction.

- **Expected reading: D1 low, D3 low, D2 HIGH, D4 moderate, D5 high; overall MIXED with direction IMPROVING — and the written reading must carry all three lenses (current low-mixed · direction up · the A anchored). A rater who collapses current and direction into one number fails the portrait; a rater who returns WR2's score fails it.**
- **Identity:** composite (constructed; key fixed by design).
- **Rationale:** the mandatory ambiguous middle case (per the bench's Preacher rule) for the WR wing: construction is loud, and the instrument's three-lens discipline exists exactly so that honest current-state noise cannot bury a true upward trajectory. Also the first portrait where D2 (depth) and D1/D3 (order) dissociate — depth of understanding precedes restored order in a healthy rebuild, which is itself a diagnostic sequence worth teaching raters.

### WR6 — "The group that is rich" (the hum in tribulation; key HIGH)

The assessor's extended read falls in the hardest season this group has known. One member is in the late stages of cancer; she is present tonight, thinner than her chair, and nobody pretends otherwise. Another family, three weeks from losing their house after a layoff, sits across the circle. There is weeping in this room — real, unhidden, more than once — and the naive sheet has no idea what to do with it. But watch: the roles flex around the suffering without a word (someone has the meal calendar, someone has the school runs, someone has the paperwork fight with the insurer — nobody assigned any of it); the dying woman is not managed, she is *heard*, and when she says something that costs her everything to say, the room receives it and stays in it. The second why goes deeper than anywhere else on this bench: ask them why they still meet, still sing, still pray for healing while planning a funeral, and they can tell you — not in brochure language but from somewhere underneath, with the reason attached (1 Pet. 3:15). The laid-off family put money in the missions basket; the assessor watched them do it. Grief is everywhere; despair is nowhere. Nobody performs victory. Toward the end someone laughs — really laughs — at something the sick woman says, and she laughs too, and it does not profane the room; it belongs to it.

- **Expected reading: HIGH — the wing's richest room. D1 high (the perturbation is *constant* and the room holds); D2 the highest on the bench; D3 high (roles flexing without orchestration); D4 high, re-anchored: at this end of the suffering spectrum, joy-in-the-hard is not cheer but "sorrowful, yet always rejoicing" (2 Cor. 6:10) — grief with hope articulate (1 Thess. 4:13) IS the high anchor, and a rater who requires cheerfulness, or scores open weeping as noise or low D4, fails the portrait. DECLINE is also wrong here — distinguish from WR4: order intact, roles flexing, fear absent; tribulation is not turbulence.**
- **Identity:** composite; the corporate logic of Smyrna at group scale — "I know your tribulation and your poverty (but you are rich)," Rev. 2:9, the letter with no rebuke.
- **Rationale:** added at the founder's direction (2026-07-17): how suffering is handled is a core maturity distinctive, and without this portrait the bench never tests whether raters can tell suffering from noise. WR6 completes the suffering spectrum (interruption → emergency → aftermath → tribulation) and is the two-sided weep-test embodied in a room: the instrument must be able to return "rich" against a season of unrelieved loss, exactly as the Examiner does.

## Bench notes

- **Scoring sheet implication:** the WR wing needs its own short rating block (five descriptors 1–5, overall Hum/Mixed/Noise/DECLINE, direction, and a free-text "what would you tend first?" line — the hope-validity probe).
- **Control logic of the wing:** WR1/WR2 = anchors; WR3 = distance-vs-presence discriminator; WR4 = DECLINE control (hard fail if scored); WR5 = middle case with lens-separation test; WR6 = suffering-vs-noise discriminator (the tribulation-is-not-turbulence pair with WR4). This mirrors the main bench's control architecture at reduced scale.
- **Keying sequence (proposed, mirroring the main bench):** founder's review of these drafts → persona keying (small panel; the frozen-key procedure) → living raters alongside the main-bench rater trial.
- **Design-question dispositions (founder, 2026-07-17):** (1) D5 in one visit — **RESOLVED: extended time required** ("you either need more than a single visit or extended time with the group"; his assessment practice was a week embedded); implemented as the two-tier protocol (First Reading = screen only; Extended Read = the instrument proper; D5 and the direction lens are Extended-Read-only).
