> **What this is.** The exegesis-in-context audit pointed at Vol 3 — the most mechanism-heavy volume, predicted to carry the highest verse-as-mechanism risk. **Context:** Vol 3 went through the **nine-author peer review** (5 theologians + 4 physicists) earlier this session, which targeted exactly this fault profile. So this pass checks the post-review state. **Diagnostic only.**

## Headline finding (the surprise)

**Vol 3 is the BEST-grounded volume in the corpus on the verse-as-mechanism issue — better than Vol 1 or Vol 2.** The volume most at risk of pressing Scripture into mechanism duty is the one that most consistently *refuses* to. The nine-author review forced an explicit discipline that now runs through every chapter: **the text is stated, and then the mechanism reading is flagged as an analogy laid over it, never a claim the text makes.** Verbatim instances:

- **Exp 3, James 5:16:** *"The resonance language here is an analogy I am laying over the verse, not a claim the verse makes."*
- **Exp 6, Parable of the Talents:** *"The atrophied-muscle picture is an analogy I am drawing from the parable's outcome, not the lesson Jesus is teaching, which is about whom you trust."*
- **Exp 8, persistent widow:** *"The Vol 3 hypothesis (held as an illustrative model, not a claim about how God must act)…"* and the resonance mechanism *"is an illustrative analogy, not a load-bearing equation."*
- **Exp 3, Mark 5:30:** the incarnate-Son guard — *"never a current we generate or aim… Read the data point for what it shows about how He works, not as a technique we inherit."*

**This is the audit's own gold standard, deployed pervasively.** It is precisely the move I had to *add* to Vol 2 in #15 — and Vol 3 already does it everywhere.

## Supporting observations
- **Certainty is honestly calibrated.** Vol 3's labels are mostly **Speculative** (Exp 3, 4, 6, 7, 8) or **Reasonably Inferred** (Exp 1, 2, 5); the volume repeatedly says so in its own voice ("I hold the following section at lower certainty than anything else in this volume," Exp 6). The only **Clearly Taught** chapter is Exp 9 (Glory/sanctification), whose anchors (2 Cor 3:18; Rom 8:18) genuinely are clearly taught. No over-claim.
- **The pastoral edge is guarded.** The Force equation leads with **G** = God's free, sovereign self-giving ("not a factor the believer operates"), states plainly "there is no mechanism, there is a Father," and carries "A Word for the Dry Season" inside the chapter. The widow's-oil abundance is fenced against a prosperity formula (2 Cor 12:9; Heb 11:39).
- **The narrative/parable anchors I expected to be stretched are the ones most carefully guarded** — Elijah's seven prayers ("not a power to oblige God"), the Talents (re-rooted in distrust-of-Master), the widow's oil, the persistent widow.

## Findings
**None worth implementing.** Vol 3 passes clean on the verse-as-mechanism audit. There is no residual stretch, no over-claimed certainty, no unguarded mechanism. (If one wanted to be maximally picky: the centurion → `F = F_base × g(authority)` move in Exp 3 turns a narrative into an equation term — but it is owned as Speculative and analogical, and the volume's pervasive analogy-vs-text framing covers it.)

## Recommendation
1. **Vol 3 needs no grounding work.** The nine-author review already did it, and did it thoroughly.
2. **The corpus-wide picture is now clear (Vols 1–3 audited):** the proof-text/verse-as-mechanism fault the audit set out to hunt is **largely already addressed** — by the multi-witness discipline (Vol 1 laws), the #15 fix (Vol 2 explorations), and the analogy-vs-text discipline (Vol 3). The single real gap found in three volumes was FL.II's missing dark-night guard, now fixed.
3. **The audit's most durable product is the rubric itself.** Vol 3's "analogy I lay over the verse, not a claim the verse makes" is the in-house gold standard, alongside FL.VII / FL.X / FL.XIX (multi-witness + genre-aware + guarded + calibrated). Codifying that as a short **Scripture-Grounding Standard** would hold every *future* addition (new laws, new chapters, the Formation Documents) to the bar the corpus already mostly meets — the highest-leverage forward-looking move.
