> **What this is.** The exegesis-in-context audit pointed at Vol 2 — the application volume the Vol 1 POC predicted would carry most of the proof-text load. **Important context:** Vol 2 has already been through (a) the eight-author peer review and (b) the **#15** proof-text fix *this session* (John 8:32 contextualized + re-anchored in John 16:13; John 8:44 re-anchored in 2 Cor 4:4/11:3; 1 Kgs 19 left as already-mitigated). So this pass is checking what those two passes did **not** catch. **Diagnostic only.**

## Method
Inventoried every formal scripture-block anchor (`***Ref (ESV)***`) across the Vol 2 explorations + scanned for the "verse-as-mechanism" tell (a verse made to carry a named mechanism/law). Genre/context-checked each; checked certainty labels. (Formal anchors are the load-bearing citations; a deeper line-by-line of every *inline* citation could surface more LOW items, but the pattern is already clear.)

## Headline finding

**After #15, Vol 2's explorations are in good shape.** The systematic pass found **no new HIGH or MEDIUM proof-text faults** — the worst verse-stretches (John 8:32 as "the mechanism statement," John 8:44 as a general doctrine of installed lies, 1 Kgs 19 as technique) were exactly the ones #15 already fixed. Most "mechanism" language in Vol 2 refers to the *theoretical* knot-feedback-loop model, not a verse pressed into mechanism duty. The residual is **light** — one certainty-calibration item and a few mild wisdom-genre/devotional stretches.

This validates two things: the #15 fix + eight-author review did the heavy lifting, and the audit method reliably distinguishes "already handled" from "still open."

## Findings (light, severity-ranked — all optional, all diagnostic)

1. **[LOW–MED — certainty calibration] Exp 2 (Emotional Knots) "Clearly Taught" headline covers an experiential mechanism.** The law's Certainty note is headed **Clearly Taught**, but its own body already distinguishes tiers: the *weight-vs-sin distinction* (Heb 12:1) is genuinely scriptural, while the *self-sustaining feedback loop* and the *specific release mechanism* are experiential/inferred ("confirmed over multiple decades"). This is the same shape as the Sower fix (**#8**): the headline over-labels the inferred part. **Fix (optional):** split the headline like #8 — weight/sin distinction = Clearly Taught; knot-feedback-loop + release mechanism = Reasonably Inferred (experientially strong). The body nearly says this already; it's a headline change.

2. **[LOW — optional clause] Exp 2 Heb 12:1.** *Sound as used* — the weight/sin distinction is in the text, and the four-type taxonomy is correctly owned as hypothesis. The only refinement: a half-clause making explicit that IJH *borrows* Heb 12:1's two-category structure analogically (the verse exhorts laying aside *both* weight and sin; IJH uses the distinction to say weights need a different tool than sins). Not a correction.

3. **[LOW — wisdom-genre] Exp 5 Eccl 4:12 ("a threefold cord").** Ecclesiastes' pragmatic observation on companionship used for spiritual "Connecting with Others." Within normal homiletic bounds, but it's wisdom-pragmatic, not a teaching on spiritual community. A one-line framing ("the Preacher's practical wisdom, which the law extends") would tighten it.

4. **[LOW — wisdom-register] Exp 9 line 22.** Prov 11:14 / 27:17 are said to "describe a structural feature of how the Spirit works" — mild inflation of wisdom literature into a Spirit-mechanism claim (though hedged "in the IJH framework"). Soften to "illustrate," not "describe a structural feature."

**Used soundly (no action):** Mark 4 (Sower, already worked in #8), Prov 4:23, 1 Pet 5:8, John 10:10 (thief→agent, mild but standard), Eph 6:12 + Col 2:15 (now balanced, #13), Isa 43:1, Mark 9:24 (already worked in #18), Lam 3:22-23, the Container four (Rom 14:3-4 / Rev 1:8 / Matt 7:5 / John 5:19), Acts 13:2 / 1 Cor 14:29, **Jer 1:17-19 (well-guarded by the self-suspicion bar)**, 1 John 1:9 / Ps 66:18 / Jas 5:16 (Ps 66:18 already guarded in #6).

## Recommendation
1. **Vol 2 needs no urgent grounding work** — #15 closed the substantive faults. Items 1–4 above are optional polish; #1 (the Exp 2 certainty split) is the only one with real merit, and it's a headline tweak.
2. **Point the audit at Vol 3 next.** That is where verse-as-mechanism risk is genuinely highest — the resonance / Spiritual-Force / quantum-analogy material recruits Scripture to underwrite a *mechanism*, which is the exact fault profile. (The Vol 3 nine-author review already hardened much of this — G as God's free self-giving, the analogical-resonance caveats — so expect medium, not heavy, findings.)
3. **Net across the POC + this pass:** the corpus is far better grounded than a cold reading would fear. The laws (Vol 1) are multi-witness and genre-aware; the application volume (Vol 2) was just swept; the live frontier is Vol 3's mechanism analogies.
