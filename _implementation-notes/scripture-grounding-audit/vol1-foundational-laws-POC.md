> **What this is.** A proof-of-concept pass over Vol 1's Foundational Laws, asking of every *load-bearing* Scripture citation: is the verse used in accordance with its own genre and context, or is it stretched to do mechanical work it wasn't written for? And is the certainty label calibrated? This generalizes the Vol 2 **#15** fix (John 8:32 as "the mechanism statement"; John 8:44 as a general doctrine of installed lies; 1 Kgs 19 as apophatic technique) to test whether the same fault recurs in the foundational spine. **Diagnostic only — nothing edited until John picks a finding.**

## The rubric (three questions per load-bearing citation)
1. **Genre/context** — does the text *teach* this, or is the claim merely *consistent with* it? (Narrative ≠ prescription; poetry ≠ mechanism; a polemic ≠ a doctrine.)
2. **Derive vs. decorate** — does the claim visibly *rest on* the text, or is the verse cited nearby for color?
3. **Certainty calibration** — does an inference wear a "Clearly Taught" label?

**The in-house gold standard.** Three laws set the bar for how a claim should ground + guard a verse, and the audit measures everything against them:
- **FL.X (Ask-Seek-Knock)** — builds in the Jas 4:3 "safety catch," names the vending-machine misreading and refuses it, adds a full *Shadow Pair* (over-asked / under-asked), and "free to be told no."
- **FL.VII (Drawing-Near)** — "God moves first," "not a vending machine," distinguishes the dark-night hiddenness (Isa 54:7–8) from judicial withdrawal, Shadow Pair + lament (Ps 13).
- **FL.XIX (Spirit-Anointing Transmission)** — the most abuse-prone law, yet it opens with the sovereignty guard (1 Cor 12:11; John 3:8), *corrects in-text* the "contagion/proximity mechanism" misreading of 1 Sam 19, fixes "double portion" → firstborn's inheritance (Deut 21:17), carries the Cornelius counter-case (Acts 10:44–46), and is calibrated to **Reasonably Inferred**.

## Headline finding

**Vol 1's Foundational Laws are exceptionally well-grounded in Scripture — markedly more so than the Vol 2 explorations were.** Across a representative deep-dive (FL.II, VII, X, XI, XIX — spanning confession, reciprocity, petition, mind-renewal, and the mechanism-prone transmission law) plus an anchor-scan of all 46:

- **Multi-witness grounding is the norm** — 4–6 passages, multiple writers, usually both Testaments, per law. This is the discipline the Vol 2 *explorations* sometimes skipped (reaching for a single verse to name a mechanism).
- **The laws are genre-aware and actively self-correcting** — FL.XIX corrects two misreadings in its own text; FL.VII and FL.X build Shadow Pairs against the over-claim; FL.XI carries a de-mechanizing guard ("keeps the law from shrinking into a self-improvement technique").
- **Certainty is calibrated** — the mechanism-prone laws (transmission, surrender-multiplication, generational-nested) are rated **Reasonably Inferred**, not Clearly Taught.

**The Vol 2 #15 proof-text fault does NOT substantially recur in Vol 1's laws.** The two verses I just fixed in Vol 2 both reappear in Vol 1 — **Ps 66:18** in FL.II, **John 8:32** in FL.XI — but here they are deployed *carefully*: as secondary witnesses inside a multi-verse base, not as load-bearing "mechanism statements." The same verse is stretched in the application volume and guarded in the foundational one. That is the whole story in miniature.

**Strategic implication for scaling:** the audit's payoff is **not** in Vol 1. It is in the **application / mechanism material — the Vol 2 explorations especially, and the more speculative stretches of Vol 3** — where a single verse is more often recruited to carry a mechanism. A corpus-wide pass should target Vol 2–3 (and the Formation-Document PDFs), not re-comb Vol 1.

## Proposed Vol 1 fixes (light, severity-ranked — all optional, all pending John)

1. **[guard-propagation — recommended] FL.II Confession lacks the dark-night/felt-silence guard.** Its Vol 2 child (Exp 4) now carries it, and its sibling FL.VII already has it — but a reader meeting **Ps 66:18** in FL.II gets the negative law with no guard that *felt silence is not by itself proof of cherished sin* (the lament psalms; Christ's mediation). **Fix:** one sentence, mirroring FL.VII's dark-night clause and the new Vol 2 Exp 4 guard. *This is the one real consistency gap.*
2. **[cosmetic] Mechanism-register residue.** FL.II still pictures sin as "resistance building up in a circuit" / "weight on the line"; the L-pipe/barnacles imagery recurs. The Vol 1 four-tradition review already softened most of this register; a light touch could finish it. Low priority.
3. **[optional genre note] FL.XI John 8:32.** Used as a near-synonym for "truth renews the mind" — its context is freedom from sin's slavery via abiding in Christ. It is a *secondary* witness (Rom 12:2 / 2 Cor 3:18 carry the law), so this is a one-clause tightening at most, not a fix.

**Deep-dive candidates for a full Vol 1 pass** (not yet read; flagged by the scan as mechanism-prone or already-RI, where residual stretching is likeliest): FL.XVII Substitution-Cascade ("held loosely on deep mechanics"), FL.XXVIII Generational-Nested (RI), FL.XXXIX Surrender-Multiplication (RI; de-mechanized last session), FL.XXXVI Eschatological-Glory. Expectation, based on the sample: light findings, not heavy re-grounding.

## Recommendation
1. **Apply fix #1 to FL.II** (the genuine consistency gap) — a one-sentence guard. Optionally #2/#3 as cosmetic.
2. **Scale the audit to Vol 2–3, not Vol 1.** That is where the proof-text/verse-as-mechanism fault actually lives. The FL.VII / FL.X / FL.XIX gold-standard rubric becomes the test.
3. Conclusion John can bank: **the foundation is solid.** The grounding work the corpus still needs is in the application volumes, and it is bounded.
