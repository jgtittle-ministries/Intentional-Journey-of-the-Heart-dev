## Pattern of cuts applied (matched against v5_6_0_20 FL.XVIII anchor)

The v5_6_0_20 simplification pass calibrated against FL.XVIII established these cuts:

1. **Catalog-ordinal openings** — "the eighteenth foundational law operates at…" → "this law operates at…"
2. **Architecture-asides** — "The Mirror field of the Periodic Table is designed to carry exactly this kind of structure" cut

## Per-chapter notes — Group B (FL.XXXV–XXXVIII), the heavy cuts

These four chapters were authored as catalog admission documents (v5_6_0_14 for FL.XXXV; v5_6_0_15 for FL.XXXVI; v5_6_0_16 for FL.XXXVII; v5_6_0_17 for FL.XXXVIII). Each carried 4–5 paragraphs of catalog-architecture in the opening, an entire "## Structural-Relationship Articulation" section listing 7–11 numbered cross-references by P/G code, and a closing catalog-meta paragraph. The pattern of cuts was:

1. **Opening collapsed from 4–5 paragraphs to 3.** Cut catalog-ordinal framing, P/G placement statements, v5_6_0_X admission narratives, pre-pass methodology paragraphs. Kept: the law's substance, the heart/soul/etc. dimension positioning (in plain language), the scale-invariance content.
2. **Entire `## Structural-Relationship Articulation` section cut.** Each chapter had 7–11 numbered cross-references by P/G code (e.g., "**FL.X Faith-Sight Law (P0/GIV):** Heart-relational face and cognitive-instrumental face…"). Following the FL.XVIII anchor's pattern (the v5_6_0_20 simplification replaced FL.XVIII's similar section with one short pointer to the PT chapter), these were cut entirely. The Periodic Table chapter is the authoritative source for cross-law relationships.
3. **Placement-justification paragraphs cut.** Each chapter closed Why-This-Is with paragraphs like "The law's placement at P0/GII (Scale-Invariant / Heart) is the right one for two reasons. First, the operational mechanism is heart-relational…" These were cut as pure catalog-architecture.
4. **Closing catalog-meta paragraph cut.** Each chapter ended with a "This is Foundational Law XXXVII — the operational law of wide consent that names…" paragraph reiterating the catalog placement and what the admission completed. Cut.
5. **"Candidate N of the pre-pass" references stripped.** FL.XXXV had several references to specific pre-pass candidates ("the partial-trust state operating as the double-minded condition Candidate 4 of the pre-pass surfaced"); these are catalog-internal methodology and were stripped.
6. **Closing markers normalized to next-chapter pointer.** Each chapter's closing was set to "**Foundational Law XXX[+1] — Operational Law of Wide Consent**" matching the FL.XIV–FL.XXXIV convention; FL.XXXVIII's closing was set to "**End of the canonical Foundational Laws as currently articulated**" (the canonical end-marker per CLAUDE_CODE_HANDOFF notes).

Net per chapter: FL.XXXV `~30` lines of architecture cut; FL.XXXVI `~30`; FL.XXXVII `~50`; FL.XXXVIII `~60`. Total `~170` lines net deleted across the four.
