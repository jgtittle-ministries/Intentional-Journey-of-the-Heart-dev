## The four findings — and our disposition

### 1. Corpus omission (methodological strike) — **ADOPTED; corpus completed below.**
The reviewer caught that the Phase 1 corpus **omitted the canon's strongest faith-as-magnitude vocabulary** — Luke 17:5 ("increase our faith"), Rom 12:3 ("measure of faith"; we had only 1 Cor 12:9), 2 Cor 10:15 ("as your faith grows"), 2 Thess 1:3, the 30/60/100-fold (Matt 13:8, 23), and the talents/minas parables. Leaving them out makes the "0 candidates" tally look curated. **Fix: add them and defeat them on the merits** (addendum below); reframe the tally as *"several faith-magnitude **vocabulary** candidates; **zero scaled-outcome** candidates."*

### 2. Matt 9:29 κατὰ τὴν πίστιν under-weighted — **ADOPTED (honest weighting).**
κατά + accusative is the standard idiom for a measuring norm ("according to your **works**," Matt 16:27; Rom 2:6), so the grammar tilts toward B more than Phase 2b granted. It remains **non-decisive**: the narrative outcome is *complete sight*, not graded sight; and the framing question "Do you believe that I am able?" keys κατά to the *object/genuineness* of belief. We will name it as the **strongest residual pro-B datum** and rebut it on the *outcome* (nothing in the story scales), not wave it off.

### 3. Gift-of-faith "orthogonality" too convenient — **ADOPTED (reworded as concession-plus-limit).**
Claim #4 as written ("orthogonal to faith-quantity") is too tidy, because Scripture plainly does give faith a *measure* dimension (Rom 12:3 μέτρον; 1 Cor 12:9 differential gift; Luke 17:5 "increase"). The honest form: **faith genuinely has a measure / capacity dimension, but Scripture never lets that measure *meter the size of a given outcome* above the floor.** The differential gift relocates faith-quantity to *endowed capacity / who is enabled* — a sovereign-distribution axis — and even there it governs *which works are attempted*, not a proportional throttle on a given act's magnitude. Concede the dimension; deny the operational scaling.

### 4. "Persistence vs. faith" partition too neat — **ADOPTED (softened).**
Persistence-in-prayer (Elijah's seven; the Canaanite woman; Bartimaeus) is plausibly **faith enduring over time** (James 5:15, "the prayer *of faith*"), not a factor wholly *other* than faith. Reword: above-floor magnitude is carried by faith **expressed as aligned, persevering prayer and exercised authority** — i.e., faith's *mode and direction over time*, not its *quantity at an instant*. This keeps the distinction that matters (it is not raw amount) without pretending persistence is unrelated to faith.

**Net effect on the conclusion: none.** B's core claim still fails. The amendments make the analysis more honest and more robust and *sharpen* the finding rather than weaken it.

## Corpus addendum — the omitted texts, coded

| Ref | What it contributes | Scaled miracle-outcome? | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Luke 17:5–6** ("Lord, **increase** our faith" → mustard seed / mulberry) | faith voiced as augmentable | **No** | The request for *more* is answered with the *threshold* logic — "if you had faith like a mustard seed…" Jesus redirects "increase" to "genuine, however small." The omitted text most friendly to B, in context, **defeats** B. |
| **Rom 12:3** ("the **measure** of faith God has apportioned") | μέτρον — faith comes in measures | **No** | About sober self-assessment and differing roles/gifts in the body — the *sovereign-distribution* axis (amended claim #4), tied to function, not miracle-size. |
| **2 Cor 10:15** ("as your faith **grows**", αὐξανομένης) | faith-growth vocabulary | **No** | Context = Paul's hope his *ministry sphere* expands with their maturing faith. Scope of ministry, not outcome-magnitude. |
| **2 Thess 1:3** ("your faith is **growing abundantly**", ὑπεραυξάνει) | faith-growth vocabulary | **No** | Commendation of maturing faith + love; no miracle in view. |
| **Matt 13:8, 23** (30/60/100-fold) | proportionate **yield** | **No** | The variation tracks **soil quality** (good vs. rocky vs. thorny), a *condition/quality* variable — and fruitfulness of the received word over time, a different mechanism than faith→miracle. In IJH this is the heart-soil / affective-taxonomy axis (V2.Exp1), and it *supports* the quality reading. |
| **Matt 25 (talents) / Luke 19 (minas)** | proportionate **reward** | **No** | Reward ∝ faithful stewardship ("faithful over little → set over much") — eschatological reward, not miracle-output ∝ faith-amount. Confirms the Kingdom *has* proportionate patterns, but not in the faith→miracle channel. |

**Result:** the canon does contain (a) faith-as-magnitude *vocabulary* (measure / increase / little / great) and (b) proportionate *reward* and *yield* logic. The reviewer is right that these belong in the corpus. **None is a per-act miracle scaled to faith-amount**, and two of them (Luke 17:5's answer; the 30/60/100 soil) actively reinforce the quality/threshold reading. The "zero scaled-outcome candidates" finding survives with the full corpus in view — and is now stated honestly rather than by omission.
