## General steelman for Model B (proportionality)

**The case:** Scripture itself uses *magnitude* words for faith — "**great** faith," "**little** faith," "**all** faith" (1 Cor 13:2), "the **measure** of faith God has assigned" (Rom 12:3). If faith has a measurable magnitude, and Jesus correlates "great faith" with commendation and "little faith" with failure, then outcome tracks faith-magnitude. That is proportionality.

**Rebuttal.**
1. **The magnitude words are used qualitatively.** *oligopistos* ("little faith") is everywhere a rebuke for **fear / anxiety / doubt** — a quality contaminant — applied even where the stakes are small (worry over food, Matt 6:30; the storm, Matt 8:26). It never reads "you had 3 units of faith and needed 5." "Great faith" (centurion, Canaanite woman) is applied for **precision, structural understanding, and tenacity**, not volume.
2. **The outcomes do not grade.** The centurion's "great faith" and the bleeding woman's silent touch both yield **full** healing. "Great faith" does not buy a *bigger* miracle — it buys Jesus' **astonishment**, which is a comment on the rarity and quality of the man's understanding, not a larger output.
3. **Decision-rule #1 returns zero.** Across ~59 entries there is no case where a *larger* outcome is credited to *more* faith.
4. **Two texts are fatal to clean proportionality:** Heb 11:39 (genuine faith, outcome *withheld*) and 2 Cor 12:8–9 (great, persistent, aligned prayer → sovereign "no").

**Verdict:** Model B is **not supported**, even steelmanned. The great/little language is qualitative; outcomes do not scale with it.

## The five flagged cases

### #7 — "According to your faith be it done to you" (Matt 9:29) · *the strongest pro-B text*
- **Steelman B:** the preposition **κατὰ** ("according to / corresponding to") is the most naturally proportional in the healing sayings. The verse literally indexes the result *to* their faith — the clearest grammatical hook for proportionality in the canon.
- **Rebuttal:** Jesus first asks, "**Do you believe that I am able** to do this?" — a yes/no question about the *object and genuineness* of faith, not its amount. "According to your faith" then means "in keeping with the faith you have just professed (that I am able)" — it indexes to faith's **presence and object**, not a measured quantity. κατὰ + accusative routinely means "in accordance with a kind/standard" (qualitative), not "in proportion to a magnitude." And the outcome is **binary** — both men simply see; there is no "more faith → see better."
- **Verdict:** genuinely ambiguous on the bare preposition, but the "do you believe I am able" frame + the binary outcome mean it **does not exhibit graded magnitude**. Consistent with A/C; **does not establish B**. (Leave a noted crack: a B-defender can lean on κατὰ, so the resolution should address the verse explicitly.)

### #40 — The widow's oil scaling to the vessels (2 Kgs 4:1–7) · *the clearest "scaling" miracle*
- **Steelman B:** output literally scaled with a human-side input — the more vessels she gathered, the more oil. A miracle whose magnitude is set by what the human prepared. That *is* proportionality.
- **Rebuttal:** the scaling variable is **vessels** (obedient preparation to Elisha's word: "gather vessels, not a few"), **not faith-amount**. The text never grades oil by faith; it grades it by prepared capacity in obedience. Model C already locates above-floor magnitude in **alignment / obedience / persistence** — vessels-gathered is exactly such a variable.
- **Verdict:** does **not** support B (faith-amount). It **enriches C**: above the floor, magnitude can scale with **obediently-prepared capacity**. Worth carrying into the resolved model — the system is not merely on/off above the threshold; magnitude is real and scalable, but the scaling variable is capacity/alignment, not faith-quantity.

### #55 — The "gift of faith," differentially distributed (1 Cor 12:9; Rom 12:3) · *the serious back-door*
- **Steelman B:** the Spirit gives "faith" as a gift "to each individually **as He wills**" (12:11), in differing measure (Rom 12:3, "the measure of faith God has assigned"). If God gives some people *more* faith and faith drives miracle-force, then differently-gifted people produce differently-sized results — proportionality smuggled in at the gifting level.
- **Rebuttal:** (a) The charismatic **gift of faith** (a special operational endowment for particular bold works) is a different thing from the ordinary/saving **trust** that is the `f()` threshold for a given faith-action; conflating them is a category error. (b) Even granting differential gifting, it concerns **who is enabled to attempt** certain works, not output scaling continuously with a measured quantity within an act. (c) Rom 12:3's "measure" is most naturally the apportioned **sphere/assignment** (humility about role), not an output dial. (d) The differential is **God's sovereign distribution** ("as He wills") — it sits on the sovereignty side, consistent with C's sovereignty bound, not a human faith-lever.
- **Verdict:** does **not** establish B's claim (per-act output ∝ faith-amount), but it identifies a **real, separate axis** — *differential operational capacity, sovereignly assigned* — distinct from per-act faith-quantity. **Preserve as an open sub-question** the resolved model must name, not a win for B.

### #16 — Peter walking, then sinking (Matt 14:30–31) · *gradient vs. floor-crossing*
- **Steelman B:** he walked while faith held and **sank as it waned** — a continuous, graded response of the supernatural effect to the level of faith.
- **Rebuttal:** the text frames a **binary** failure — "why did you **doubt**?" (*edistasas*, wavered / became double-minded), echoing the double-minded doubter of James 1:6–8 who "receives nothing." The wind frightened him → he looked away from Christ → dropped **below the floor**; "beginning to sink" is the consequence of crossing the floor, not a read-out of a faith-magnitude.
- **Verdict:** better explained as **floor-crossing (A/C)** than gradient (B). Illustrates the **sharpness** of the floor; does not establish B.

### #9 — "This kind comes out only by prayer" (Mark 9:29) · *type-dependent difficulty*
- **Steelman B:** different works need different "amounts" — this demon required *more* (prayer/fasting) than others. Quantity-graded difficulty.
- **Rebuttal:** "this kind by prayer" introduces a **type-dependent condition** — some works require deeper aligned prayer/resonance — not a faith-**quantity** scale. It is about the operator's **depth of aligned relationship** (`h()`), not how much faith-substance is dispensed.
- **Verdict:** **refines C** (thresholds and required resonance can be type-dependent); not support for B.

## Updated decision-rule status (post-adversarial)

1. **Model B — rejected.** No graded-output-by-faith-amount case survives the steelman; the one grammatical hook (#7 κατὰ) lacks a graded outcome, and the one scaling miracle (#40) scales on obedient capacity, not faith.
2. **A vs. C → C.** Above-floor magnitude/timing tracks persistence, alignment, authority, and obediently-prepared capacity — `h()` and `g()` (and an obedience/capacity term), not faith-quantity. Bare A (pure on/off, magnitude entirely in the *other* factors) is too thin: #40 shows above-floor magnitude is itself real and scalable on alignment/capacity.
3. **Whose faith.** The gate sits primarily on the **operator's** genuine trust + alignment + authority; recipient faith is **sufficient when present** (the "your faith has made you well" family) but **not necessary** (the divine-initiative class). Intercessor faith counts (the four friends; Jairus; the Canaanite mother).
4. **Sovereignty bound (firm).** Genuine + persistent + aligned does **not** compel a specific outcome (Heb 11:39; Paul's thorn). The resolved model describes the conditions under which God *characteristically* works, never a lever that obligates Him.

## What survives as live / to carry forward
- **Preserved open sub-question (from #55):** does differential, sovereignly-assigned charismatic-faith gifting constitute a *form* of proportionality at the **gifting** level, distinct from per-act faith-quantity? (A candidate **preserved open question** in its own right, attached to the resolution.)
- **Capacity/preparation enrichment (from #40):** above the floor, magnitude can scale with **obediently-prepared capacity** — add this alongside resonance/authority in the `h()`-region description.
- **Type-dependent thresholds (from #9):** some works are gated higher (require deeper prayer/resonance) — the floor is not uniform across miracle types.

## Implication for the equation (for Phase 5 to state)
- **`f()` (trust):** a near-**step / sharp floor** on the *genuineness* of trust — not a smooth sigmoid scaling with amount. (mustard seed #18, look-and-live #34, doubter #52, Peter #16, Nazareth #19 all read as floor, not gradient.)
- **`h()` (resonance) + `g()` (authority):** carry the **above-floor magnitude and timing**, joined by an **obedient-capacity / preparation** term (#40). This is where persistence (Elijah's seven, Daniel's 21 days), alignment ("according to His will," 1 John 5:14), and authority (centurion, "in His name") do their work.
- **Sovereignty:** an outer bound on the whole product — the equation maps the **traceable human-side shadow**, not a guarantee.

The result is therefore **not** merely the coder's prior confirmed — B was given its best case and still failed the decision rule. The one genuinely live proportionality candidate (#55 differential gifting) is preserved, not dismissed. **Independent confirmation of this adversarial pass is still recommended** before the Phase-6 write-up is submitted as a resolution.
