### 3.2 "great / little / no / all faith" — what does the magnitude word do?

**Tally — magnitude word used as a quantity-of-substance metric tied to output size:** **0 of all occurrences.** Every use is qualitative (genuineness, fear/doubt, precision) — or, in 1 Cor 13:2, an explicit *demotion* of faith-quantity beneath a quality.

### 3.4 Whose faith — distribution and necessity
| Locus | Representative cases | Necessary? |
|---|---|---|
| **Recipient** | bleeding woman #2, Bartimaeus #4, blind men #7, Lystra #26, bronze-serpent #34, Daniel #42 | sufficient when present; **not necessary** |
| **Intercessor** | centurion #1, Jairus #3, Canaanite mother #5, the four friends #8, Zarephath/Shunem #37/#39 | substitutes for recipient faith |
| **Operator** (Jesus / prophet / apostle) | #13, #15, #17, #23–25, #29–33, #36–37 | the **consistent locus** of the gate |
| **None named (divine-initiative)** | Bethesda #11, blind-man #12, Nain #14, storm #15, compassion healings #20–21, Malchus #22, OT deliverances #49 | a **large** class — recipient faith demonstrably not required |

**Finding:** the threshold-gate sits primarily on the **operator's** genuine trust + alignment + authority. Recipient faith is *sufficient* when present and intercessor faith can stand in for it, but neither is *necessary* — a large divine-initiative class proceeds with no human faith named at all (the "noticing → responding" structure of V3.Exp8).

### 3.5 Sovereignty bound
Heb 11:39 (#50), Paul's thorn (#57), Eph 3:20 "far more abundantly than all we ask" (#59), and the whole divine-initiative class establish an **outer bound**: genuine + persistent + aligned does not compel the asked-for outcome, and magnitude is ultimately God's.

## Phase 4 — Adjudication

### 4.1 Verdict on the three models
- **Model B (proportionality) — rejected.** Tables 3.1, 3.2, 3.3 all return **0** for the quantity reading, and B failed even when steelmanned (Phase 2b). The decision rule's primary test (any graded-output-by-faith-amount?) is met by no case in the canon surveyed.
- **Model A vs. C → C (synthesis).** Bare A (pure binary; all magnitude exogenous to the faith system) is too thin: Table 3.3 shows above-floor magnitude is **real and scalable** — but on persistence/authority/obedient-capacity, exactly the `h()`/`g()` region, not on `f()`. C is the precise form.

**Resolved model (proposed):** *A quality threshold on genuine trust sets a sharp floor (Model A for the floor). Below it, nothing operates. Above it, the magnitude and timing of the outcome are carried by alignment, persistence, authority, and obediently-prepared capacity — the resonance and authority factors — not by the quantity of faith. The whole is bounded by divine sovereignty: the conditions describe how God characteristically works, never a lever that obligates Him.*

### 4.2 Final disposition of the flagged cases
- **#7 "according to your faith"** — resolved as **quality/presence** ("in keeping with the faith you professed that I am able"), binary outcome; not a graded-magnitude datum. The resolution write-up should address κατὰ explicitly so the verse is not later misread as proportional.
- **#40 widow's oil** — resolved as **obedient-capacity scaling**, folded into the `h()`-region as a capacity/preparation term; *not* faith-amount.
- **#16 Peter / #52 doubter** — resolved as **floor-crossing**, illustrating the sharpness of the threshold.
- **#9 "only by prayer"** — resolved as a **type-dependent** threshold/resonance requirement; the floor is not uniform across miracle types.

### 4.3 The gift-of-faith sub-question — resolved
*Does the differentially-distributed "gift of faith" (1 Cor 12:9; Rom 12:3) reintroduce proportionality?*

**Resolution: No — it operates on a different axis.** The charismatic **gift of faith** is a sovereignly-assigned **operational capacity** (the God-given assurance to undertake a particular work), distinct from (a) saving faith and (b) the per-act trust that is the `f()` floor. The question this trail asks — "for a given faith-act, does output scale with faith-amount?" — is answered *no*. The gift-of-faith asks a different question — "whom does God enable, and for what works?" — which is about **distribution of calling/capacity**, sits on the **sovereignty side** ("as He wills," 1 Cor 12:11), and is consistent with the sovereignty bound (3.5). It does **not** put a continuous faith-quantity term back into `f()`.

**Disposition:** preserve as a **distinct, named observation** in the resolution — *differential, sovereignly-assigned operational capacity* — explicitly flagged as **orthogonal** to the threshold-vs-proportionality question, and as a candidate **preserved open question** in its own right (how differential gifting relates to the force law) rather than a finding that revives Model B.

### 4.4 Resolved functional forms — the deliverable for V3.Exp3
- **`f()` — trust:** a **sharp threshold / near-step** on the *genuineness* of trust. Best modelled as a step (or a very steep logistic admitting the "deficient → genuine" transition of Mark 9:24's "help my unbelief"), **not** a proportional ramp. The floor may be **type-dependent** (Mark 9:29).
- **`h()` — resonance** and **`g()` — authority:** carry the **above-floor magnitude and timing**, now explicitly joined by an **obedient-capacity / preparation** term (widow's vessels). This is where persistence (Elijah's seven, Daniel's 21 days), directional alignment ("according to His will," 1 John 5:14), and authority (the centurion, "in His name") act.
- **Sovereignty:** an **outer bound** on the product (a ceiling / veto God retains), not a factor the operator controls.
- **Net for the equation:** the multiplicative form `F_s = f × g × h × i` stands; this resolution fixes `f` as a threshold (not a continuous scalar) and relocates all genuine "magnitude" behaviour into `g`/`h` (+ capacity), with sovereignty as the bound. This is precisely the input V3.Exp3 flagged as its open trail.

- **V3.Exp3** (Force equation, 40%, frontier) — `f()`/`h()` forms now specified; this scriptural analysis is the kind of higher-rigor warrant the research-program track requires. Propose entering it into that track with a Council sponsor; no confidence change until the window closes.
