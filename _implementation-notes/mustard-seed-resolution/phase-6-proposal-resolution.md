## 1. What are you proposing?

I am proposing to **resolve** the long-standing Mustard Seed `open_question` (threshold vs. proportionality) attached to V1.Exp8 and referenced as the gating open trail of V3.Exp3. After a systematic coding of the miracle and answered-prayer narratives across the OT, Gospels, Acts, and Epistles (~59 entries; see the Phase 1–4 files), I propose adopting the **synthesis (Model C)** and retiring **Model B (proportionality)**:

> **Genuine faith operates as a quality threshold, not a quantity.** A sharp floor of genuine trust is the entry condition: below it, spiritual force does not operate; the smallest *real* faith above it is sufficient. **Above the floor, the magnitude and timing of the outcome are carried by alignment, persistence, authority, and obediently-prepared capacity — the resonance and authority factors — not by the amount of faith** (persistence here being faith *enduring and aligned over time* — "the prayer of faith," James 5:15 — not a factor unrelated to faith, but faith's mode and direction rather than its instantaneous quantity). The whole is bounded by divine sovereignty: these conditions describe how God characteristically works, never a lever that obligates Him.

This sharpens V1.Exp8's existing language ("the threshold condition establishes the minimum entry requirement; the resonance structure describes what happens above the threshold") from a *reasonable inference* to a *systematically evidenced* reading, and it fixes the functional forms V3.Exp3 flagged as open: **`f()` is a near-step threshold on genuineness** (not a proportional ramp), and all genuine above-floor magnitude lives in **`g()` / `h()` (+ an obedient-capacity term)**.

## 3. The Four-Factor Derivation

### 3a. Independent scriptural lines
Six witnesses approach the question from different angles and converge on the same reading:
1. the **threshold sayings** (mustard seed; look-and-live) — smallness is no obstacle;
2. the **failure-by-unbelief** cases (Nazareth; the disciples; the doubter, James 1) — failures are *quality* failures, never "too little";
3. the **qualitative use of "great/little faith"** — genuineness/precision/fear, never a quantity tied to output size (and "great faith" yields no larger miracle);
4. the **persistence/resonance** cases (Elijah, Daniel, the persistent widow) — above-floor magnitude tracks persistence/alignment;
5. the **sovereignty-bound** cases (Heb 11:39; Paul's thorn) — no faith-lever;
6. the **divine-initiative** class (Bethesda; Nain; the stilled storm) — recipient faith is not even necessary.
This is not one proof-text but a multi-angle convergence. The one apparent counter-line (proportionality) is supported by real faith-magnitude *vocabulary* — "measure" (Rom 12:3), "increase" (Luke 17:5), "grows" (2 Cor 10:15), "little / great" — but by **zero** cases in which a larger miracle-*outcome* is credited to more faith. The accurate framing is therefore *several faith-magnitude vocabulary candidates, zero scaled-outcome candidates*: the magnitude language describes the believer's condition; it never meters the size of a given work.

### 3b. Experiential corroboration
I corroborate the resonance reading from my own life of prayer — the part of this I am surest of.

I connect the rhythm directly to the injunction to *pray without ceasing* (1 Thess 5:17). I wake every morning with a song going through my head. I write it down in my morning journal entry and let that rhythm be my prayer rhythm for the day, returning to it multiple times through the hours. If the song changes, I go with the new rhythm. It is not about the words; it is about the rhythm. I let whatever pictures come along with the rhythm, and I trust that this is my prayer continuing throughout the day — it may be continuing in the night as well, though I am not aware of it, only on waking.

This is the resonance structure of V1.Exp8 lived rather than theorized: prayer held as a sustained waveform rather than a single pulse, aligned with what God is giving rather than generated by my own effort, and persisting over time. What carries it is not a quantity of faith summoned in a moment but a genuine, small, continual returning. That is precisely the asymmetry this proposal claims — above the threshold of genuine trust, it is alignment and persistence (the rhythm), not the *amount* of faith, that carry the prayer.

I have recently begun sharing the morning's song with a couple who volunteered to pray with me: I text them the song right after waking, and they take up the same rhythm. It has become two or three gathered in the Name of Jesus (Matt 18:20) — the same resonance, now corporate, which also touches the Community Resonance open trail of Vol 3.

I offer this as my own witness, in the personal-witness sense the project has always used; the wider workshop and Fellowship-of-the-Heart practice traditions rest on the same persistent, aligned, rhythmic prayer.

### 3d. Consistency with tradition
The synthesis is recognizable across **all four** streams the project draws on (full survey in Phase 5):
- **Reformed** — faith's efficacy is in its *object, not magnitude*; "weak faith in a strong object" (Calvin, *Institutes* III.2); Westminster Confession 14.3 ("different in degrees… yet gets the victory"). The sovereignty bound is native here.
- **Evangelical** — Matt 17:20 read as *faith vs. unbelief*, genuine-however-small (France, Carson, Morris).
- **Charismatic/Pentecostal** — the resonance/persistence half (James 5) and, independently, the precise **gift-of-faith** distinction this proposal draws (Donald Gee).
- **Contemplative** — littleness valorized (Thérèse's "little way"); faith operating in surrender and darkness without compelling outcomes (John of the Cross; de Caussade).

The **only** stream leaning toward Model B is the **Word-of-Faith** sub-stream (Hagin, Copeland) — and the wider church already critiques it for collapsing the sovereignty bound and treating faith as a lever, which is exactly the ground on which this analysis rejects proportionality. A reading this broadly attested, whose lone divergence is the position the tradition itself flags as aberrant, is strong by factor 3d's standard.

## 5. What would count as evidence against this proposal? (disconfirmability — V4.M3)
This proposal would be **withdrawn or the Council should reject it** if any of the following were shown:
1. **A scriptural case of graded output by faith-amount** — same operator, same authority, same alignment, same persistence, differing *only* in quantity of faith, producing a different-sized outcome. (None was found across the canon; one such clear case would break it.)
2. **An exegetical demonstration** that "according to your faith" (Matt 9:29) or "the measure of faith" (Rom 12:3) *require* a proportional-output reading rather than a quality/role reading. (This is the strongest residual datum for proportionality: κατά + accusative is the idiom of a measuring norm — "according to your *works*," Matt 16:27. It is held to be non-decisive only because the *outcome* in Matt 9:29 is complete sight, not graded sight, and the framing question "Do you believe that I am able?" keys κατά to the genuineness/object of belief. A reading that made the *outcome* scale would overturn this.)
3. **Empirical (Vol 4) data** showing that, with persistence/alignment/authority held constant, *measured* faith-level predicts outcome magnitude — which would resurrect Model B on evidence the scriptural analysis could not see.
4. A demonstration that the gift-of-faith differential *does* operate as a continuous per-act `f()` scalar (rather than a sovereign distribution), which would reopen the f-form question.

## 6. Summary for the open comment period
The mustard seed is a statement about the *genuineness* of faith, not its quantity: the smallest real trust crosses a threshold below which nothing operates, and above which the size and timing of what God does are carried by alignment, persistence, and authority — never by "more faith" — all under God's sovereign freedom. This resolves the open question that has capped V1.Exp8 and gated the V3.Exp3 Force equation, fixing its trust factor as a threshold rather than a proportional dial. The one tradition that reads faith proportionally (Word of Faith) is the one the wider church already critiques for making faith a lever, which is precisely what this reading guards against.
