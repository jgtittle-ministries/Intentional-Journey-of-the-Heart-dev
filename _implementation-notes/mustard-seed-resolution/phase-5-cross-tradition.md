# Mustard Seed Resolution — Phase 5: Cross-Tradition Check (Proposal factor 3d)

*Factor 3d of the Proposal Template asks whether the proposed reading is recognizable across the four streams the project draws on (Reformed, Evangelical, Charismatic, Contemplative). A reading at home in several streams is stronger; a reading drawn from only one is weaker. This file surveys how each stream takes (i) the mustard seed (threshold vs. proportionality), (ii) the sovereignty bound, and (iii) the "gift of faith." Positions are given as each stream's representative line, with figures I am confident hold them; specific page citations are left for the human write-up to verify rather than risk fabricating.*

---

## Reformed
- **Mustard seed → quality/threshold, decisively.** The Reformed instinct is that faith's efficacy lies in its **object, not its magnitude** — "weak faith in a strong object" saves as surely as strong faith. Calvin (*Institutes* III.2) holds that faith genuinely laying hold of Christ is true faith even when weak and mixed with unbelief. The **Westminster Confession (14.3)** is explicit: saving faith "is different in degrees, weak or strong," yet even weak faith "gets the victory" — i.e., *degree does not change saving efficacy*; the floor is genuineness. This is the threshold reading stated in confessional form.
- **Sovereignty bound → very strong.** The Reformed doctrine of God's decree is the natural home of the bound: faith is never a lever on God; outcomes rest on His sovereign will. Heb 11:39 and Paul's thorn are read here as paradigm, not anomaly.
- **Gift of faith → real but God-given.** Reformed (esp. cessationist-leaning) streams are cautious about charismatic miracle-faith, but where they affirm it they affirm it as **sovereignly given**, never self-generated — consistent with the resolution's "distribution" framing.

## Evangelical (broad)
- **Mustard seed → quality.** Mainstream evangelical commentary reads Matt 17:20 as a contrast of **faith vs. unbelief**, not large vs. small faith: even minimal *real* faith suffices, because the disciples' failure was unbelief/doubt. This is the line in standard commentaries (e.g., R. T. France, *Matthew*; D. A. Carson, *Matthew*; Leon Morris). The popular evangelical formula — "a little faith in a great God" — is the homiletic of the threshold reading.
- **Sovereignty bound → affirmed**, typically via "according to His will" (1 John 5:14) and the example of unanswered/redirected prayer.
- **Gift of faith → affirmed as a Spirit-gift**, with the same caution against treating it mechanically.

## Charismatic / Pentecostal
- **Mustard seed → quality + Spirit-empowerment + persistence.** The classic Pentecostal reading honors the genuine-faith floor *and* the persistent, fervent, Spirit-empowered prayer that the resonance term `h()` formalizes (James 5:16–18 is a Pentecostal touchstone). This stream most strongly corroborates the *resonance* half of the synthesis.
- **Gift of faith → most developed here.** Pentecostal teaching independently draws exactly the distinction the resolution needs: the **charismatic gift of faith** (1 Cor 12:9) is a *special, sovereignly given surge of God-given certainty for a particular work* — distinct from saving faith and from the fruit of faithfulness (e.g., Donald Gee, *Concerning Spiritual Gifts*; the phenomenon associated with figures like Smith Wigglesworth). This is independent confirmation of Phase 4's "gift of faith = sovereignly-assigned operational capacity, orthogonal to per-act faith-quantity."
- **The one genuine dissent — Word of Faith.** Within the broader Pentecostal/charismatic world, the **Word-of-Faith** sub-stream (e.g., Kenneth Hagin, Kenneth Copeland) reads faith far more **proportionally and mechanistically** — a "law of faith," a "measure of faith" one increases by technique, reliably producing results. *This is the living-church form of Model B*, and it is precisely the reading the resolution rejects: it collapses the sovereignty bound and treats faith as a lever, which Heb 11:39 and Paul's thorn refute. Mainstream Pentecostalism and the wider church critique this sub-stream on the same grounds.

## Contemplative / Mystical
- **Mustard seed → littleness valorized.** The contemplative stream prizes not the magnitude of faith but the **purity and simplicity of surrendered trust**; smallness is no obstacle and is even the path (Thérèse of Lisieux's "little way" — the smallest acts in great trust). This is the threshold reading in a devotional key.
- **Sovereignty bound → strong, via surrender.** John of the Cross's "dark night" has faith operating precisely when sensible evidence and consolation are withdrawn — faith does not command outcomes; it abandons itself to God (Jean-Pierre de Caussade, *Abandonment to Divine Providence*; Brother Lawrence). Persistent prayer (Luke 18) is the contemplative "prayer of the heart" maintained over time — the resonance half, devotionally.
- **Gift of faith → received, never seized** — wholly consonant with sovereign distribution.

A reading this broadly attested is, by factor 3d's own standard, **strong**. And the **only stream that leans toward Model B (Word of Faith) is the one the wider church already critiques** on exactly the grounds this analysis reached independently — which is itself corroboration: the proposal does not have to invent its objection to proportionality; the tradition supplies it.

**Recommendation for the write-up:** state the synthesis as a genuinely *catholic* (whole-church) reading, name the Word-of-Faith divergence explicitly and honestly, and show that the resolution's sovereignty bound is the historic safeguard the divergence lacks. This both satisfies 3d and pre-answers the most likely objection during the comment period.
