# Phase 7 — Gospel faith-cases as scriptural corroboration of the synthesis

*Added 2026-06-09 (during the 14-day comment period). This is **additional corroboration**, not a change to the resolution. It is recorded here in the trail rather than edited into the under-comment worked-resolution doc; **fold it into §5 "The witness behind it" on enactment (post-2026-06-15)** if it still stands. Originated in John's reflection on how human faith interacts with God's action. **Folded into §5 "The witness behind it" on enactment 2026-06-19 (it still stood); see commit 1524b82.***

## The claim being tested
The resolution holds: **genuine faith is a quality *threshold* (a near-step floor), not a quantity; above the floor, magnitude and timing are carried by alignment/persistence/authority/capacity — all bounded by divine sovereignty.** The pre-registered decision rule was: *is there any narrative where, all else equal, more faith yields a larger outcome?* If yes, proportionality lives.

## The cases, against that rule
- **Faith is never ignored (the threshold "floor" cleared):** the centurion (Matt. 8:5–13, "such great faith"), the Syrophoenician woman (Mark 7:24–30, "great is your faith"), the woman with the hemorrhage who is healed on a touch He had not initiated (Mark 5:25–34, "your faith has made you well"). In each, real faith — not its measured *amount* — opens access. None shows a *bigger* miracle for *more* faith; they show access granted to genuine trust. **Consistent with threshold, not proportionality.**
- **Bounded by sovereignty / His timing:** Cana (John 2:1–11) — faith asks, Jesus says "My hour has not yet come," then acts on His own timing, not under compulsion. The outcome's timing is His, not faith's to force. **Direct support for "bounded by divine sovereignty."**
- **God acts apart from prior faith:** Lazarus (dead, cannot believe), Saul on the Damascus road (an enemy), Cornelius (the Spirit falls before any "work," Acts 10:44). Faith is the necessary *reception* God never ignores; it is not what *causes* Him to act. **Reinforces: faith receives, it does not compel.**
- **Corporate scale (the threshold at the level of a people):** Kadesh-Barnea (Num. 13–14) — the generation's unbelief fell below the floor and they did not enter; the real faith of Joshua, Caleb, and Moses did not outweigh it. The threshold structure holds corporately, and corporate unbelief can shut a door that stood open.

## Verdict
Every case is consistent with the threshold-bounded-by-sovereignty synthesis and **none** satisfies the proportionality decision rule. This is experiential/scriptural corroboration, not a challenge.
