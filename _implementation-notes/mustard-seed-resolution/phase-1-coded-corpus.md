## Phase 0 — Pre-registration (locked before coding)

**The three models and what each predicts the texts will show:**

- **A — Threshold (step):** genuine faith of *any* size is sufficient; the constraint is **quality** (real trust vs. assent), never **quantity**. Predicts: failures are attributed to absent/counterfeit faith; "great/little faith" describes genuineness; outcome magnitude is **never** attributed to faith-amount.
- **B — Proportionality:** force scales with faith **quantity** — more faith, bigger outcome. Predicts: at least some texts grade the *size* of a miracle by the *amount* of faith.
- **C — Synthesis (threshold floor + resonance magnitude):** a quality floor (A), and **above** the floor the magnitude is carried by **alignment, persistence, and authority** (the `h()` resonance and `g()` authority factors), not by faith-quantity. Predicts: failures track the quality floor; magnitude variation tracks persistence/authority/alignment; "great faith" = precision, not volume; bounded by divine sovereignty (genuine + persistent + aligned still does not *compel* a specific outcome).

**Decision rule (locked):**
1. **Model B stands or falls on one question:** is there *any* clear text where a **larger** outcome is attributed to **more** faith (as opposed to more persistence, better alignment, proper authority, or genuineness)? If essentially none, B is rejected.
2. **A vs. C** is decided by the *above-threshold* cases: if magnitude/timing variation among successful miracles tracks **persistence/authority/alignment**, C is preferred over bare A.
3. A model must specify **whose** faith the gate applies to (recipient / intercessor / operator) and must accommodate the **no-faith / divine-initiative** miracles without special pleading.

**Coding conventions:**
- "Faith verdict" ∈ {genuine-present, deficient, absent, **not-mentioned**}. *Not-mentioned ≠ absent* — silence of the text is coded distinctly and never read as faith-absent.
- "Whose": **R** recipient · **I** intercessor/petitioner · **O** operator (Jesus / prophet / apostle) · **C** community · **—** none named.
- "P/A": **P** persistence/repetition present · **A** authority structure invoked.
- "Mag↔amt?": does the text correlate outcome *size* with faith *amount*? (the Model-B test)
- Synoptic/parallel accounts merged to one event (multiple witnesses), parallels in the Reference cell.
- This is a *first* pass by one coder who holds a prior toward A/C; it therefore requires the adversarial "argue-for-B" second read (Phase 2b) before any adjudication is treated as settled.

## Corpus inventory & inclusion criteria

Included: every narrative of healing, exorcism, raising, nature/provision miracle, judgment-miracle, or specifically-answered prayer in which faith/trust/persistence is **named or clearly in view**, plus the reflective/doctrinal faith texts of the Epistles. Pure divine-initiative wonders with no human-side element (e.g., most Exodus plagues) are sampled as the "—/divine-initiative" stratum rather than catalogued exhaustively. Strata: **Gospels** (faith-attribution core) · **Acts** · **Old Testament** · **Epistles/reflective**.

## Running tally after the first pass (~59 entries)

- **Clear cases where outcome-magnitude is attributed to faith-AMOUNT:** **0.** Every "scaling"/magnitude candidate resolves on inspection to genuineness, persistence, alignment, authority, obedience, or preparation — never to "how much faith." → **Model B is, so far, unsupported.**
- **Failure / blockage cases** (Nazareth #19, disciples #9, Sceva #28, Meribah #48, James doubter #52): **all** attributed to **absent / counterfeit / doubting** faith — a **quality** failure. → supports the **threshold floor (A)**.
- **"Great faith"** (centurion #1, Canaanite #5): **precision / persistent tenacity / structural understanding**, never volume.
- **Above-threshold magnitude & timing** (Elijah's seven #36, Naaman's seven #38, Jericho #41, Daniel's 21 days #43, Hannah #44, persistent widow, the woman who pressed through #2, Bartimaeus #4): tracks **persistence / alignment / authority** — i.e., the `h()` and `g()` factors — **not** faith-quantity. → supports **synthesis (C)** over bare A.
- **No-faith / divine-initiative** miracles are a *large* class (Bethesda #11, Nain #14, blind man #12, the storm #15, compassion healings #20–21, many OT deliverances #49, Acts raisings): recipient faith is **not** a universal necessary input. → the gate is on the **operator's** genuine trust + alignment + authority, and many miracles are **sovereign initiative** (the V3.Exp8 "noticing / responding" structure).
- **Sovereignty bound** (Heb 11:39 #50, Paul's thorn #57): genuine + persistent + aligned still does **not** compel a specific outcome. → the model must not become a faith-lever.

### Preliminary reading (to be tested, not yet concluded)
The first pass points **clearly away from Model B** and toward **Model C**: a **quality threshold** on genuine trust (the `f()` floor — and #18 mustard / #52 doubting / #34 look-and-live read as a near-**step**, not a smooth sigmoid), with **above-floor magnitude and timing carried by resonance/persistence/alignment (`h()`) and authority (`g()`)**, the whole bounded by divine sovereignty. This matches the author's standing lean — now with systematic, cross-canon support rather than a few proof-texts.

## Cases flagged for Phase 4 adjudication
- **#7 "according to your faith"** (Matt 9:29) — settle whether this is quality ("whether you believe I am able") or a genuine proportional datum. *Most load-bearing ambiguity for Model B.*
- **#40 widow's oil** — scaling to vessels: preparation/obedience vs. a true magnitude-of-faith datum.
- **#55 gift of faith** (1 Cor 12:9) — does a differentially-given charism of faith reintroduce proportionality at the gifting level (distinct from the per-act threshold)?
- **#16 Peter sinking** — gradient vs. floor-crossing reading of "little faith / doubt."
- **#9 "this kind only by prayer"** — type-dependent thresholds: are some miracles gated higher than others? (refines, does not break, C)
