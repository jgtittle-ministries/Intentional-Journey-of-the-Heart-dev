# Vol 4 Minimum Viable Protocol Restructure — Design Proposal

**Status:** brainstorm; companion to [sip-design-proposal.md](sip-design-proposal.md). Not yet committed to the published site.

**Date:** 2026-05-25 (draft 1)

**Goal:** Restructure Vol 4 §3 and §4 around a three-instrument core (SIP + NRP + EWI) — the Minimum Viable Protocol (MVP) — with the eight existing instruments that fall outside the core demoted to a new Appendix B as preserved research-grade extensions. The deciding argument is Companion load: the §4e Facilitator section already insists the Companion is the single most important variable in whether a PROAPT group produces genuine formation, and the current 11-instrument suite is incompatible with the kind of presence that role requires.

---

## The MVP core (recap)

1. **SIP (Individual + Group).** Forced-choice profile across the five AT stages, six dimensions each, current + preferred. Replaces PSFA-AT, CSFA, GCS, and (partially) STI-A. Surfaces the integration-vs-closure check natively rather than requiring qualitative caveats. See [sip-design-proposal.md](sip-design-proposal.md).
2. **NRP (Narrative Reflection Protocol).** Four monthly prompts, kept largely as currently designed. Irreplaceable qualitative texture; captures what no quantitative instrument can. Image of God 4Rs likely folded in as a recurring fourth prompt set rather than a standalone instrument.
3. **EWI (External Witness Interview).** Once at start, once at end. External triangulation that protects against self-report inflation in either SIP or NRP.

That's three instruments. The Companion runs PROAPT sessions, holds the room, maintains supervision, completes one annual SIP, supports participants on monthly NRP, and coordinates two EWI cycles per participant. Runnable.

## The new Vol 4 structure

### §1 — Why Testing Matters for the Laws of the Spirit Project
**No change.** The framing of personal/communal/academic testing and the falsifiability discussion stand on their own. The recently shipped LotS-H3 integration clarification stays.

### §2 — Testing Framework: Epistemological Foundations
**No change.** The four sources, quantitative/qualitative, Holy Spirit, Construct Validity, the §2 integration sub-section, and the Researcher's Own Formation sections all stay as-is. The §2 integration sub-section becomes more load-bearing because the MVP core is built around it.

### §3 — Individual Spiritual Formation Testing Protocol — substantially restructured

- **3a Defining the Testing Unit** — minor revision only. The four participant criteria stand; the twelve-month commitment stands.
- **3b Assessing the Starting State** — restructured. Currently lists four dimensions, each tied to an instrument (PSFA, STI-A, IoG-4Rs, SDFL). Restructure around what the MVP core measures at baseline:
  - **Dimension 1: AT Integration Profile (baseline SIP).** Replaces the existing PSFA-AT and STI-A baseline sections. The SIP profile gives both stage and integration check at once.
  - **Dimension 2: Image of God Reflection (baseline NRP-IoG prompt).** Folded into NRP as a recurring prompt rather than a standalone instrument. Baseline reading is the first NRP entry.
  - **Dimension 3: External Witness Baseline (start-of-study EWI).** The same EWI structure as the endpoint; the start reading gives the comparison point.
  - SDFL and dedicated STI-A → demoted to Appendix B.
- **3c The Primary Outcome Variables** — restructured around what MVP measures at twelve months:
  - **Outcome 1: AT Stage Progression with Integration Maintained.** Combines what is currently OV1 (stage movement) with the integration check from the §2 reframe. SIP current/preferred gap is the operational measure; SIP closure-flag is the integration check.
  - **Outcome 2: Image of God 4Rs Profile Shift.** From the NRP-IoG prompt set baseline vs endpoint comparison.
  - **Outcome 3: Belief-Behavior Integration as Observed Externally.** From the EWI pre/post comparison.
  - STI-A change-score and SDFL discipline-frequency change → demoted to Appendix B.
- **3d The Assessment Instruments** — becomes "The Three Assessment Instruments." Each gets a section: SIP-Individual (replacing the existing PSFA section), NRP (lightly revised to add the IoG prompt set), EWI (essentially as-is). Two paragraph cross-reference to Appendix B for STI-A, SDFL, and the other extensions.
- **3e The Testing Timeline** — minor revision. The three-phase structure (Orientation 1–3, Active Formation 4–9, Integration 10–12) holds. Assessment points become baseline (Month 1 SIP + first NRP + EWI), midpoint (Month 6 SIP + NRP review), endpoint (Month 12 SIP + NRP review + final EWI). Cleaner than the current schedule.
- **3f The Claims This Protocol Tests** — minor revision. LotS-H3, LotS-H4, LotS-F2, LotS-I2 all remain testable under the MVP. The wording shifts from "the PSFA tracks…" to "the SIP measures…" but the claim-test logic is unchanged.

### §4 — Small Group Spiritual Formation Testing — substantially restructured

- **4a Defining the Small Group Testing Unit** — no change. Five-to-seven participants, ninety-minute weekly PROAPT, twelve months, facilitator at Stage 3 or above.
- **4b Open Trail OT-6 / GATS Working Definition** — no change. The integration framing we just shipped (closure shadow rating) is load-bearing under the MVP.
- **4c Group Spiritual Formation Profile (GSFP)** — **demoted to Appendix B.** The GSFP is the facilitator-maintained distribution-of-individuals chart; it's useful for research teams but the Group SIP captures the group's collective culture profile more directly and with less facilitator load. The GSFP description becomes an Appendix B entry.
- **4d Group-Level Outcome Variables** — restructured around Group SIP. Currently has five outcome variables (Net Stage Progression, Cohesion/Safety, Epistemic Trust, Tell Rate, Fruit Observed by External Witnesses). Restructure as:
  - **Group Outcome 1: Group Culture Profile Shift with Integration Maintained.** From Group SIP baseline vs endpoint, with the closure-flag check.
  - **Group Outcome 2: Tell Rate.** Tracked as a single weekly count by the facilitator — keep this; it's lightweight and operationally important. (NOT demoted; it's part of MVP at the group level as a single facilitator-side metric.)
  - **Group Outcome 3: Fruit Observed by External Witnesses.** Aggregated from individual EWIs.
  - GCS (cohesion), separate Epistemic Trust items → demoted to Appendix B. Group SIP Dimensions 3 (welcoming new voices), 4 (prayer life), and 5 (conflict/correction) cover the same operational territory at the integration level.
- **4e The Facilitator as a Critical Variable** — minor revision. Facilitator PSFA becomes Facilitator SIP. The integrated-vs-closed caveat we just shipped reads more cleanly because the SIP itself produces the integration check.
- **4f Session Observation Protocol (SOP)** — **demoted to Appendix B.** This is the biggest single Companion-load reduction. SOP is the facilitator's weekly six-variable checklist; it's the kind of instrument that becomes the thing the facilitator does in the room instead of the thing the facilitator does outside the room. Move to appendix; recommend a much shorter weekly check (two questions, post-session) as an MVP-compatible substitute that lives inside the Group Outcome section rather than as its own instrument.
- **4g Collective Spiritual Formation Assessment (CSFA)** — **demoted to Appendix B.** Group SIP subsumes the operational territory; CSFA's three-domain structure becomes an alternative cut available in the appendix.
- **4h The Testing Timeline for Small Groups** — minor revision; mirrors the §3e simplification.
- **4i The Claims This Protocol Tests** — minor revision. LotS-F4, LotS-H2, OT-6, LotS-SF1/SF2 all remain testable under the MVP.

### §5 — Research Program Design — minor revision

The §5c instruments summary table currently summarizes all 12 instruments. After this restructure it summarizes 3 core + Appendix B reference. The multi-site research design discussion stays substantially intact; the §5 prose probably gains a brief paragraph naming the MVP/Appendix-B distinction and recommending that pilot churches run MVP only while doctoral-level studies may opt into Appendix B extensions.

The §3/§4 protocols are designed to run cleanly without them; adding any of them is an explicit choice with a known cost in facilitator and participant load. ## Open structural questions

1. **Appendix B placement.** Inside Vol 4 (as a new §6 or as Appendix B at the end of Vol 4) or as its own short Vol 4 appendix file? Probably its own file: `volume-4-testing-framework/appendix-b-research-extensions.md`. Cleaner than bloating any §1–§5 section, easy to add to mkdocs.yml nav.
2. **Image of God 4Rs disposition.** Three options:
   - (a) Fold entirely into NRP as a fourth recurring prompt set ("This month, where did you see new edges of growth in Reason, Rulership, Relationship, or Righteousness?")
   - (b) Keep as a tiny standalone instrument (4 questions, administered at baseline and endpoint only)
   - (c) Add as a fourth dimension to the SIP itself (distribute 100 points across the 4 Rs)
   - My lean: (a). Keeps the instrument count at three, keeps the 4Rs alive as a qualitative dimension, and the NRP register is well-suited to 4Rs reflection. (c) is interesting but conflates two different constructs.
3. **Tell Rate — keep as Group MVP measure or demote to Appendix?** I have it as Group Outcome 2 in MVP because it's a single weekly facilitator count (lightweight) AND it's the operational form of the Romans 10 chain at the group level. But it adds a facilitator weekly task. Argument for demoting: Group SIP Dimension 6 (the group's Tell) captures the same construct at the integration level. Argument for keeping: weekly count is a leading indicator that quarterly SIP cannot match. My lean: keep, but as a single-question check, not the multi-variable SOP-style record.
4. **Sequencing: one big rewrite vs incremental.** Two options:
   - **Big rewrite:** one PR that restructures §3 and §4 and adds Appendix B all at once. Cleanest result, but big diff.
   - **Incremental:** (a) ship Appendix B with current §3/§4 instruments duplicated there as "also available as research extensions"; (b) ship the SIP as an additional core instrument; (c) restructure §3/§4 to demote the appendix versions. Each PR leaves prod in coherent state; the user could stop at any point.
   - My lean: incremental. Matches the standing "make sure each pass leaves prod in readable condition" instruction. The big-rewrite version has too many simultaneous moving parts.
5. **Vol 5 cross-references in the existing demoted instrument descriptions.** Some Appendix B entries reference Vol 5 documents (e.g., GSFP has a Vol 2 connection callout about V2.Exp2C Standing Interior Agreements). Those callouts stay; they're still substantively right. The Appendix B disposition is about *protocol role* (optional vs core), not substantive correctness.
6. **Naming.** "Minimum Viable Protocol" (MVP) borrows lean-startup language and may not fit the IJH register. Alternatives:
   - "Core Testing Protocol" (and Appendix B is "Research Extensions")
   - "Pilot-Ready Protocol" (and Appendix B is "Extended Research Protocol")
   - "Three-Instrument Protocol" (and Appendix B is "Optional Instruments")
   - The pastoral register probably wants something like "Core Testing Protocol" or "Pilot-Ready Protocol" rather than MVP. Open.

## Sequencing recommendation (if you want to proceed)

If we go incremental (my lean), the natural sequence is three dev→mirror cycles:

**Pass A — Create Appendix B (additive only, no §3/§4 restructure).**
- New file: `docs/volume-4-testing-framework/appendix-b-research-extensions.md`
- Each of the 8 demoted instruments gets an entry here that *references* the current §3/§4 location for the canonical description ("see §3d for the full description"). The Appendix B header note frames them as preserved for optional/research-grade use.
- nav update in mkdocs.yml to add the new appendix.
- Strict build clean. §3/§4 unchanged. Prod still has the full 11-instrument protocol; Appendix B is just an additional indexing.

**Pass B — Publish SIP as new core instrument.**
- SIP-Individual and SIP-Group full text added as new §3d-i (or as a new sub-section in §3d) and parallel addition in §4. PSFA remains in §3d alongside SIP for this pass.
- New §3c/§4d outcome variables added (SIP-based) alongside existing ones; the integration calibration touches we shipped get the lighter rewrite to mention the SIP closure-flag.
- Strict build clean. Prod now has all 12 instruments (11 original + SIP-Individual + SIP-Group, treating the two SIP variants as one instrument family). Slightly heavier than current, briefly.

**Pass C — Demote the eight Appendix B instruments and rewrite §3/§4 around the MVP core.**
- §3d goes from "the four instruments" to "the three instruments" — PSFA, STI-A, SDFL descriptions move out (now they live in Appendix B with full text, not just stub references).
- §3b and §3c restructure as in the proposal above.
- §4c, §4f, §4g move to Appendix B; §4d restructures around the three Group SIP outcomes.
- Calibration touches finalize.
- Appendix B header note updates to reflect canonical residence ("the canonical descriptions of these instruments now live in this appendix rather than in §3/§4").

Each pass leaves prod in a readable, coherent state. After Pass A, prod has an extra index. After Pass B, prod has the SIP available alongside the existing protocol. After Pass C, prod has the MVP-structured protocol.

Total: three dev cycles, three prod mirror cycles, six commits per repo. Same cadence we've been running.
