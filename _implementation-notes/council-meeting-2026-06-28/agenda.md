## Framing

A first meeting's job is to **constitute itself, agree how it will work, take its founding votes, and make the few decisions that unblock real work** — not to finish everything. The large multi-session work (the full claim-by-claim registry walk-through, the research cycles themselves) is *scheduled*, not done today.

The agenda is built from the project's own governance artifacts — Volume 6 Part 1 §13 ("First Council actions"), the Research Register, the Peer-Review Panel Registry, and the Formation Companion's new safeguarding section — not invented.
