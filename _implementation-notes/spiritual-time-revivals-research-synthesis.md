# Spiritual Time & Revivals — Research Synthesis (genuine test of the Vol 3 model)

*Working artifact for a possible Vol 3 companion to "The Engine Beneath the Force." Compiled 2026-07 from five parallel research passes. Distinguishes primary-source anecdote from peer-reviewed analysis; flags CONFIRM / REFINE / CHALLENGE / THIN per model part.*

## The model being tested
Vol 3 ("The Engine Beneath the Force"): spiritual time **τ = ∫(engagement) dt** (kairos, not chronos); rate **dτ/dt spikes** in revival ("a weekend can hold years"); a 7-step "anatomy of an outpouring" (sovereign injection → time-acceleration → mass threshold-crossing → momentum → community/prayer cascade *without overriding wills* → high-resistance move last → maturation into "steadfastness / good inertia," fruit = "who is still moving a year later"); plus a **friction** term (world, flesh, uncleared wounds, isolation) for fade/backslide.

## Part-by-part

### 1. Kairos/chronos and the τ formalism
The *distinction* is well-established theology: Tillich (chronos = measured time; kairos = "qualitative time," the moment "history has matured… for the breakthrough of God"); Cullmann, *Christ and Time* (NT time-terms tied to redemptive acts); Eliade, *The Sacred and the Profane* (sacred vs. profane time). **But** these treat kairos *qualitatively* — a category of meaning, not a quantity with a rate. **The move to a rate dτ/dt that can "spike" is the model's own mathematization; scholarship offers no warrant for treating spiritual time as integrable.** (Note a tension: Eliade's sacred time is *cyclical/recoverable*, vs. the model's accumulative, forward-integrating τ.) → The *concept* is solid; the *math* is original and should be tiered **Speculative**. Sources: [Tillich (BU)](https://people.bu.edu/wwildman/bce/tillich.htm) · [Cullmann (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscar_Cullmann) · [Eliade (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eternal_return_(Eliade)).

### 2. Acceleration — the spike
**Phenomenon CONFIRMED.** Edwards, *A Faithful Narrative*: "more than 300 souls… in the space of half a year" in a town of ~1,100 — a genuine community phase-change ([jonathan-edwards.org](http://www.jonathan-edwards.org/RevivalandConversion.html)). Cane Ridge (1801): 10,000–20,000, ~10% of Kentucky's population ([Christian History Institute](https://christianhistoryinstitute.org/magazine/article/revival-at-cane-ridge)). Asbury 2023: 50,000–70,000 visitors over 16 days ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Asbury_revival)). William James: self-surrender conversions "burst through all barriers and sweep in like a sudden flood" ([Varieties IX](https://www.authorama.com/varieties-of-religious-experience-8.html)).

**Two challenges.** (a) *Timescale*: Edwards's own work unfolded over *months*; McLoughlin frames awakenings as "profound cultural transformations… extending over a generation or more." "A weekend holds years" is devotional overstatement of an event scholarship situates in a longer cycle. (b) *Experience ≠ formation*: Bleidorn, Lenhausen, Schwaba & Hopwood (2024, *Journal of Personality*) found "no evidence for linear post-conversion changes in any of the Big Five traits" and conclude conversion is "better conceptualized as *gradual* processes that unfold long before and after" ([Wiley](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jopy.12881)). The measurable *formation* may be gradual even when the *moment* feels instantaneous — the sharpest challenge to the spike-as-formation reading.

### 3. Triggers / readiness / "sovereign injection"
**CONFIRM.** Prayer as precondition is strongly attested: Hebrides — Peggy & Christine Smith (84, 82) and barn-praying deacons *preceded* Campbell ([christianbooknotes](https://christianbooknotes.com/2021/10/04/the-hebrides-revival-of-1949-1952-an-assessment/)); Herrnhut, Azusa (5 weeks' prayer), Welsh. Rambo's 7-stage model (context → crisis → quest → encounter → interaction → commitment → consequences) confirms conversion needs *crisis + prepared context*. **REFINE:** in the durable cases, structure *preceded* the spark (organized, covenanted community). Scholarship (McLoughlin, Wallace) foregrounds *cultural stress/dislocation*; "sovereign injection" is a theological claim history can neither confirm nor falsify — fine to hold by faith, but flag it as not empirically testable.

### 4. Cascade / community amplification
**CONFIRM (strong).** Durkheim's "collective effervescence": assemblies generate "a sort of electricity… every feeling expressed resounds in other consciences" ([Rimé & Páez 2023](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/17456916221146388)). Diffusion vectors are era-specific: Azusa's *Apostolic Faith* paper (5,000→40,000 copies); Welsh via the *Western Mail*; Asbury via TikTok (#asburyrevival 24M views by Feb 15, 63M by Feb 18) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Asbury_revival)); Vineyard via worship music. **A real quantitative literature exists**: a multi-strain SIR model of religious "contagion" reproduces transient waves, revival, and cyclical rise-and-fall, includes an apostasy (friction) rate, and finds a *phase transition* near β≈0.40 — validated against Swedish church data (R²=0.978) ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.11436v1)). The model's instinct to *quantify* revival has company (though these models are agency-agnostic).

**"Without overriding wills" — the weak point.** Le Bon's classic contagion is agency-*eroding* (anonymity → suggestibility → "deindividuation dissolves personal responsibility"); Cane Ridge's involuntary falling and "the jerks" raise exactly this; and Finney's engineered "new measures" invited the "manufactured revival" charge. Modern crowd theory partly rehabilitates the model (group membership can *enhance* purposeful agency, not only erode it) — but this is contested. The model should *engage* this tension, not assert non-coercion.

### 5. Durability — the biggest gap + the biggest challenge
Three literatures converge on a term the model lacks: **routinization of charisma** (Weber) — the charismatic outbreak "*must* be transformed into stable, institutionalized structures… or dissipate." Wallace's revitalization-movement model (1956) has a formal five-stage lifecycle *that explicitly includes a "routinization" stage*. Donald Miller's *Reinventing American Protestantism* (1997) explains why "new-paradigm" churches (Vineyard, Calvary, Hope) *endure*: distributed lay authority, cultural forms, small groups — routinization *diffused*, not leader-dependent.

**Primary-source anchor, added 2026-07-08 for traceability (VERIFIED — used in Vol 3's "Drift and the Source" and in *A Church in Revival*'s "How Revivals Were Lost"):** John Wesley, *Thoughts upon Methodism* (1786) — the inside-witness statement of routinization-with-dilution, from the builder of revival history's most successful routinization apparatus, and famously quoted by Weber himself (*The Protestant Ethic*, ch. 5): "I fear, wherever riches have increased, the essence of religion has decreased in the same proportion. Therefore I do not see how it is possible, in the nature of things, for any revival of true religion to continue long. For religion must necessarily produce both industry and frugality, and these cannot but produce riches. But as riches increase, so will pride, anger, and love of the world in all its branches. … So, although the form of religion remains, the spirit is swiftly vanishing away." Wesley's engineered counter-loop to the riches term — "gain all you can, save all you can, give all you can" — is Sermon 50, "The Use of Money".

**Key pattern: the founding EVENT declines while the MOVEMENT endures.** Azusa's mission decayed (gone by 1931), yet Pentecostalism claims 500M+; the Vineyard *survived Wimber's death* (1997) via planned succession; Herrnhut converted its moment into a *100-year prayer watch*. So durability is **organizational and engineered**, not automatic maturation. → The model's "still moving a year later" is the *right question* but the *wrong altitude* for multi-generational sustaining, and it needs a **routinization term distinct from friction.** Weber's twist to absorb: routinization is also *dilution* — "good inertia" and "codified, cooling institution" can be the same process with opposite valences.

**Disconfirming durability cases (honest counter-evidence).** The "burned-over district" of upstate NY = spiritual *exhaustion* from over-revival ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burned-over_district)). **Finney's own later confession**: revivals "of much shorter duration… fewer of the converts make stable and efficient Christians" — he "began to ponder the problem… of his revival converts who became backsliders" ([EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/literature-and-writing/lectures-revivals-religion-charles-grandison-finney)). Welsh church membership *peaked in 1905 and declined continuously thereafter*; Evan Roberts collapsed and withdrew ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1904%E2%80%931905_Welsh_revival)). Hebrides: no comparable revival in the region for 70+ years (contested by Lennie). Asbury: durability data genuinely thin/too-early ([Firebrand](https://firebrandmag.com/articles/the-asbury-outpouring-two-years-later-a-conversation-with-suzanne-nicholson)). *Partial support:* Starbuck's data (via James) — of 100 converts, most "backslid" emotionally but only ~6% fully relapsed in faith, so core commitment often persists ([Varieties X](https://www.authorama.com/varieties-of-religious-experience-9.html)).

### 5b. Billy Graham / BGEA — the measured durability case
The strongest real-world test, because the BGEA both *engineered* durability and got *measured* a year+ out.

**The measurement, and why the numbers diverge.** Retention figures split by *who counts and how they define "fruit."* BGEA-internal: Robert Ferm reported **95% of NY-1957 card-signers "adhered to their original decision,"** and later (*Do the Conversions Last?*, 1988) **70–80% "remain steadfast"** over 30 years ([CT 1958](https://www.christianitytoday.com/1958/06/whither-converts/); [Baptist Press](https://www.baptistpress.com/resource-library/sbc-life-articles/crusade-evangelism/)). Independent/measured: Stanley High found **72% of 1954 Harringay "converts" were already religiously engaged** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Crusade_(1954))); NY 1957 yielded only **6,000–10,000 new church members** out of ~57,000 decisions (<20%) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Crusade_(1957))); Win Arn's study of Seattle/Tacoma 1976 found only **15.3% incorporated into churches**, and **8 of 10 of those already had a friend or relative in that congregation** ([CT 1981](https://www.christianitytoday.com/1981/03/church-growth-leader-win-arn-responds-to-firebaughs/)). **The gap between "95% still committed" and "~15% joined a body" is definitional** — private steadfastness vs. visible movement in a body. The model's fruit-test implicitly wants the *harder, body-visible* denominator, where the honest one-year figure is ~15–20%, far below the peak.

**Routinization made visible — STRONGLY CONFIRMS.** The BGEA built an explicit apparatus to convert a peak into membership: the cooperative-crusade model (local churches sponsor, supply counselors, receive referrals); the multi-week "Christian Life and Witness Course" for counselors; decision cards mailed to a local pastor **within 24 hours**; Operation Andrew and nurture materials ([Beliefnet](https://www.beliefnet.com/faiths/christianity/protestant/2004/01/how-does-a-crusade-work.aspx); [Ministry Magazine 1972](https://www.ministrymagazine.org/archive/1972/06/a-look-at-the-billy-graham-crusades)). Decisively, **retention tracked the follow-up structure, not the peak**: only 23% of NY converts received a ministerial visit, yet churches that followed up aggressively retained **95–96%** of their referrals ([CT 1958](https://www.christianitytoday.com/1958/06/whither-converts/)). William McLoughlin, *Billy Graham: Revivalist in a Secular Age* (1960), reads Graham in the Finney lineage of *deliberately organized* revival technique. Mass-communication research (Klapper via Mathisen) adds that "mass communication rarely serves as an agent of attitude conversion" — it *reinforces* existing attitudes via selective exposure ([ASA/PSCF 1979](https://www.asa3.org/ASA/PSCF/1979/JASA3-79Mathisen.html)).

**Acceleration = formation — CHALLENGED.** Much of the spike was reactivation of the already-churched; genuinely new durable members were a minority and clustered where relational scaffolding pre-existed. Firebaugh's re-analysis: stripping members, ~**16% of non-member inquirers became new members**, with high subjective benefit (83% positive) — real but small durable formation, captured mainly where structure already held it ([CT 1981](https://www.christianitytoday.com/1981/03/how-effective-are-city-wide-crusades/)). *(Soft-number caution: BGEA figures are self-report/internal; Stanley High is journalistic; decision totals themselves vary by source.)*

### 6. Friction — confirmed, but needs two new modes
Friction (world/flesh/isolation) is well-attested. Two refinements the cases force: **(a) Productive friction** — the Vineyard's boundary-drawing controversies (MC510's cancellation, Kansas City Prophets, expelling the Toronto Blessing in 1995) *strengthened* institutionalization by pruning excess; resistance can be load-bearing, not only a brake ([CT on MC510](https://www.christianitytoday.com/1986/02/fuller-seminary-cancels-course-on-signs-and-wonders/); [Toronto split](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto_Blessing)). **(b) Administrative capping** — Asbury 2023 was deliberately *ended* by university leadership at 16 days, a stewardship decision, not friction-decay. The friction term is currently one-directional (drag only); reality has drag, pruning, and stewardship.

## What this means for a Vol 3 piece
1. **Add a routinization term** distinct from friction — the durability mechanism (Weber / Wallace / Miller, and empirically the Billy Graham follow-up machine, where one-year retention tracked the *structure*, not the peak). Name Weber's dilution valence honestly: institutionalization both *preserves and cools* the fire. This is the single most defensible, most important addition.
2. **Specify the denominator of the fruit-test.** "Who is still moving a year later" splits sharply between *private steadfastness* (~70–95% self-report, Graham/BGEA) and *visible movement in a body* (~15–20% church incorporation). The model wants the harder, body-visible denominator — and the Graham data show that fraction is captured mainly where *relational and institutional scaffolding already exists* to hold it. This ties the fruit-test to Community-as-Amplifier and the routinization term.
2. **Make friction multi-modal**: drag (fade), *pruning* (productive friction that strengthens), and *stewardship* (deliberate capping).
3. **Tier honestly** (fits IJH's confidence posture): the *anatomy* → **Reasonably Inferred** (broadly corroborated by Wallace, Blumer, Weber, Durkheim, SIR-diffusion); the *τ-as-integrable-rate formalism* → **Speculative / original** (the phenomenon is real; the math is the author's overlay with no academic footing). Separate acceleration-of-*experience* (attested) from acceleration-of-*formation* (challenged).
4. **Lean confidently on kairos as a category** (Tillich/Cullmann/Eliade) while flagging the τ-integral as the model's own extension. And note the *existing* quantitative revival-diffusion literature (SIR/phase-transition) as company — with the theological "no will overridden" as the thing those agency-agnostic models *cannot* supply, i.e., IJH's distinctive contribution.
5. **Include the counter-evidence** (burned-over exhaustion, Finney's confession, Welsh decline, the manufactured-revival and free-will tensions). A genuine-test chapter is *stronger* for them, and it matches the volume's self-critical posture.

## Foster, *Streams of Living Water* (1998) — fit test
Foster's thesis: six great "streams" of Christian life — **Contemplative** (prayer-filled), **Holiness** (virtuous), **Charismatic** (Spirit-empowered), **Social Justice** (compassionate), **Evangelical** (Word-centered), **Incarnational** (sacramental) — recur across church history as renewal, each traceable to Jesus, and the church's health is their convergence into one "great river" ([Renovaré](https://renovare.org/articles/six-streams-a-new-reformation)).

Verdict: **largely an ENRICHMENT of the model, not a challenge — and it does not touch the time claim.**
- **Recurrence — witness, not mechanism.** Foster corroborates *that* renewal recurs ("the Holy Spirit has a history") but shows it by spreading exemplars across centuries, not by arguing a cyclical mechanism. So he is a historical *witness* to the model's recurrence; McLoughlin/Wallace and the revival-anatomy supply the *how*. (Notably his charismatic and evangelical paradigms are **William Seymour/Azusa** and **Billy Graham** — the very cases the model was tested against.)
- **The six streams EXTEND the injection.** The model's "sovereign injection" is monochrome; Foster says renewal comes in distinguishable *kinds*. This adds a spectrum/character to the outpouring — a natural link to Vol 3's resonance language (streams ≈ modes/frequencies of the one Spirit) and the Country-of-the-Heart's "color for law-type."
- **A second durability condition beside routinization: BREADTH.** Foster's shadow-side is *not* temporal ossification (he doesn't develop that lifecycle) but **isolation** — a stream held alone turns pathological (legalism; emotionalism; performative justice). His cure is integration. So the wineskin picture gains a companion: a container can fail by being too **rigid** (Weber ossification) *or* too **narrow** (Foster's single-stream isolation). Healthy durability needs structure that is both supple *and* whole. This rhymes with IJH's own DNA — held tensions, integration-not-replacement, and the deliberately cross-tradition Council (institutionalized Foster-integration).
- **Boundary — silent on τ.** Foster is typological and normative, not temporal; he neither confirms nor challenges the spiritual-time / acceleration formalism.
- **Critique to note** ([Modern Reformation](https://www.modernreformation.org/resources/articles/streams-of-living-water-celebrating-the-great-traditions-of-the-christian-faith-by-richard-j-foster)): thin soteriology, ecumenical vagueness. Differentiation point for IJH — it can take Foster's integration insight and give it the doctrinal spine (the hear-and-obey core) his critics say he lacks.

**Net:** complementary layers. Foster maps the *watershed* (kinds of renewal) and the *ought* (make them one river); the model explains the *mechanics* of any one outbreak and its durability. One addition to absorb into the routinization term: **integration/breadth** as a health condition (the wineskin must be supple *and* whole).

## Brownsville / Pensacola Outpouring (added 2026-07-09, John's request)

The modern American low-κ case, added to the chapter's counter-evidence section. Three-agent verified research pass (sociological / journalistic / aftermath). Key facts as used, all page-verified unless flagged:

- **The event:** Brownsville Assembly of God, Pensacola FL; began Father's Day, June 18, 1995; revival meetings 4–5 nights/week; main phase ended 2000 (Steve Hill's departure); Friday-only services until 2006. Visitors: honest range **2.5M–4.5M** (AP 2012); Wikipedia's "4M+" is the high end; the church now claims 5M (inflation gradient — cite the range).
- **The count:** ~200,000 recorded decisions via signed **decision cards mixing conversions AND rededications** (CT Feb 1998: 122k cards "conversion or rededication" by early 1998); the Pensacola News Journal found the tally included **duplicate altar experiences** (via Garcia dissertation, p.-verified).
- **The absence finding (assert confidently):** NO academic study ever tracked Brownsville convert retention. Verified by full-text search of the two deepest treatments — Garcia, *Revivalism and Restorationism* (Baylor PhD, 2020, 398 pp.) and Poloma & Green, *The Assemblies of God* (NYU Press 2010, open-access) — plus multi-angle searching. Poloma's 1995/1997 two-wave panel (n≈1,000) followed **Toronto** pilgrims, not Pensacola converts; she never surveyed Brownsville attendees (her Pensacola work = participant observation + documents).
- **The κ verdict in numbers:** pre-revival congregation ~2,000–2,100 → peak 5,500/night → attendance **below 400 by 2006** (Grady, spring 2006) → 800–1,000 by 2012 in a 2,200-seat room. **$11.5M debt** from revival-era expansion (AP 2012; $6.5M still owed 2012; staff ~50→6; no record it was ever retired). BRSM: 1,200 peak → split Dec 17, 2000 (Michael Brown fired over AG-credentialing tied to the AG's multimillion-dollar loan; ~670 students left to FIRE) → 120 by 2006 → merged away/defunct. Garcia: "denominational influence due to financial involvement expedited the end of the revival."
- **Denominational stats (Poloma & Green, p.-verified):** white AG churches **slightly declined 1990–2000**; all net AG growth that decade came from ethnic congregations. AG pastors split on the revival; ~two-thirds never attended; only 30% of churches "actively engaged."
- **Epitaph quotes (AP 2012, p.-verified):** Evon Horton (successor pastor): **"This revival touched the world but not this community."** John Kilpatrick: "I resigned (from) the church, and I never would have left if I knew the struggles it was going to have."
- **Journalistic record:** PNJ series "The Money and the Myths," Nov 16–20, 1997 (4-month investigation; Crann/Streater/Blair/Allman; ed. J. Lowe Davis) — planned-not-spontaneous origin (the Father's Day videotape vs. the told story), leader finances, $60k+ unpaid sales tax, no medical documentation for healings, sheriff disputed the crime-drop claim (PD data: crime rose 1995→1996). **Award: 1997 George Polk (Local Reporting) — verified on LIU's winners list; other claimed awards (Headliner/Scripps/SPJ) unverified — Wikipedia's sentence is garbled, don't repeat it.**
- **Cautions for any future use:** PNJ full text survives only on critic-hosted mirrors (banner.org.uk, culteducation.com) — re-verify quotes against microfilm before print-grade citation; the "ex-attendees now in local Baptist churches" claim is an embellishment of one Grady 2006 sentence about **a single** Southern Baptist church — quote exactly or not at all; the "Toronto drew charismatics / Pensacola drew classical Pentecostals" flow claim is unverified verbatim — the safe form is Poloma's taxonomy (Toronto = charismatic renewal, Brownsville = classical Pentecostal revival, *Main Street Mystics* 148–159 via Garcia).
- **Chapter placement:** one paragraph in "The counter-evidence, kept in view" (chronological, between Welsh and Asbury) + Pensacola added to the intro case list. The paragraph reads Brownsville as walls-not-banks routinization (κ spent on the container, not the capture) and the Graham contrast (same nation, one generation apart, opposite κ).
