# v5_6_0_17 Pre-Pass Candidate Roster — Scriptural Anthropology of the Soul as Lens on the P0/GIII Territory

This document is the v5_6_0_17 pre-pass deliverable per the v5_6_0_16 closing-note Q2 working judgment ratification. The pass's exploratory lens — ratified at the pass-construction step — is the scriptural anthropology of the soul itself: the Hebrew *nephesh* and the Greek *psyche* as integrative-being concepts, the canonical articulation of the soul as a distinct dimension of the person, and the wisdom-literature and Pauline-Petrine articulations of the soul's life with God. The lens differs from the v5_6_0_14 attachment-theory lens in being internal to the catalog's scriptural framing rather than imported from a psychological or therapeutic framework; the internal-lens approach is appropriate because the GIII column's existing entries operate explicitly on what scripture treats as soul-level integration territory and the question for the pre-pass is whether a scale-invariant substrate-or-architectural law on that same scriptural-anthropology basis exists.

The lens is used as a candidate-surfacing heuristic in the catalog's standard methodology: every candidate must clear the inclusion bar on its own scriptural footing — multi-author support across at least two biblical authors, broad acceptance across mainstream Christian traditions, directional in form, principle-level — independent of how the lens framed the candidate's surfacing. The lens is a flashlight; the warrant is scripture.

This pre-pass discussion presents nine candidates with brief operational descriptions, anchor scriptural references with the multi-author check made explicit, and preliminary inclusion-bar evaluations. The candidates are surfaced from the territory the lens points at: the soul as integrative-being-substrate; the soul's relationship to its restoration; the soul's integration work requiring sustained operation; the soul's tethering to God as the integrating center; and adjacent territories where the lens surfaces operational candidates the existing catalog may or may not already hold. Your steering response — indicating priorities, demoting candidates that do not warrant the pass's attention, or surfacing additional candidates — will set the v5_6_0_17 pass's working scope.

## Candidate Roster

### Primary Candidates (highest finalist likelihood)

**Candidate 1: The Soul-Restoration Substrate Law**

Operational claim: The soul, under the pressures of life and the operations of the world, runs down — fragments, weakens, loses integration; the soul's restoration is God's particular operation, sustained over time, that returns the soul to its integrated state. The restoration operates through specific operations God names — rest in Christ; restoration through the Shepherd's care; hope as the anchor of the soul; renewal through Spirit's work; the soul's prospering as the substrate of all other prospering — with the restoration being neither self-generated by the soul nor a one-time event but a sustained relational operation between God and the soul over time. Absent the sustained restoration, the soul progressively deintegrates with operational consequences across all the dimensions the soul holds together.

Anchor scriptures: Ps. 23:3 ("he restores my soul"); Matt. 11:28–29 ("Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn from me, for I am gentle and lowly in heart, and you will find rest for your souls"); Heb. 6:19 ("we have this as a sure and steadfast anchor of the soul"); 1 Pet. 2:25 ("For you were straying like sheep, but have now returned to the Shepherd and Overseer of your souls"); 3 John 1:2 ("Beloved, I pray that all may go well with you and that you may be in good health, as it goes well with your soul"); 1 Thess. 5:23 ("may the God of peace himself sanctify you completely, and may your whole spirit and soul and body be kept blameless at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ"); Ps. 42:5, 42:11, 43:5 ("Why are you cast down, O my soul, and why are you in turmoil within me? Hope in God; for I shall again praise him"); Jer. 6:16 ("Stand by the roads, and look, and ask for the ancient paths, where the good way is; and walk in it, and find rest for your souls"); Isa. 40:31 ("they who wait for the LORD shall renew their strength").

Multi-author check: The Psalmist across multiple Psalms (Ps. 23, Ps. 42, Ps. 43); Matthew (presenting Jesus's articulation); the Hebrews writer; Peter; John (in 3 John); Paul; Jeremiah; Isaiah. At least eight biblical authors across both Testaments — substantially exceeding the two-author threshold.

Preliminary evaluation: Clears all four inclusion-bar criteria. The territory is operationally substantive and the empty P0/GIII cell is the structural target. The scale-invariance is operationally substantial — the soul-restoration dynamic operates at all the catalog's analyzed scales by the same mechanism. The Mirror form is anticipated as constitutive bidirectional or paired-statement (sustained refusal of God's restorative operation produces progressive soul-deintegration with operational consequences across the dimensions; the idol-ward face operates as the substrate-failure of the restoration dynamic). **High likelihood of finalist status; anticipated to be the primary admission of the v5_6_0_17 pass.**

**Candidate 2: The Wholeness/Shalom Substrate Law**

Operational claim: Shalom — the Hebrew concept of integrated wholeness that encompasses peace with God, peace with self, peace with others, and peace with creation — operates as the substrate condition under which the soul's integration becomes structurally possible; the shalom-substrate is itself produced by sustained right-relationship across the four directions of peace, and absent the substrate the soul fragments under the competing pulls of unintegrated relationships. The substrate operates scale-invariantly — individual shalom (the integrated person under God); dyadic shalom (the integrated covenant relationship); community shalom (the integrated body of Christ); generational shalom (the integrated household and lineage); cosmic shalom (the eschatological consummation of all things).

Anchor scriptures: Num. 6:24–26 (the Aaronic blessing — "The LORD bless you and keep you; the LORD make his face to shine upon you and be gracious to you; the LORD lift up his countenance upon you and give you peace"); Isa. 26:3 ("You keep him in perfect peace whose mind is stayed on you, because he trusts in you"); Ps. 85:10 ("Steadfast love and faithfulness meet; righteousness and peace kiss each other"); Jer. 6:14 ("They have healed the wound of my people lightly, saying, 'Peace, peace,' when there is no peace"); Mark 5:34 (Jesus to the woman healed: "Daughter, your faith has made you well; go in peace, and be healed of your disease"); John 14:27 ("Peace I leave with you; my peace I give to you. Not as the world gives do I give to you"); Phil. 4:7 ("And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus"); Eph. 2:14 ("For he himself is our peace, who has made us both one"); Col. 3:15 ("Let the peace of Christ rule in your hearts"); 1 Thess. 5:23 (already cited above).

Multi-author check: Mosaic (Numbers); Isaiah; the Psalmist; Jeremiah; Mark; John; Paul (across multiple letters). At least seven authors.

Preliminary evaluation: Strong multi-author footing; broad acceptance across traditions; clearly directional; principle-level. **The principal question is whether this is operationally distinct from Candidate 1 (Soul-Restoration) or whether the two are facets of one underlying substrate claim.** The shalom-substrate framing emphasizes the four-directions-of-peace as the substrate's content; the soul-restoration framing emphasizes God's restorative operation as the substrate's mechanism. The two may admit as distinct laws (different substrate contents) or may be absorbed (shalom as the substrate-content of the restoration that Candidate 1 names). **Moderate likelihood as distinct law; may be absorbed into Candidate 1 as the substrate-content articulation, with the shalom-language scriptures cited in Candidate 1's chapter rather than supporting a separate admission.**

**Candidate 3: The Soul-Anchored-in-Hope Substrate Law**

Operational claim: Hope in God operates as the soul's anchor — the operational substrate that holds the soul in integrated stability against the storms and pressures that would otherwise scatter it; the anchor's strength is the operational condition under which the soul maintains its integration through difficulty, with the absence of the anchor producing the soul's drift into despair or fragmentation under any sufficient pressure. The anchor's content is specific — hope grounded in God's character and his promises and his eschatological completion — not generic optimism; the anchor's operation is sustained over time through repeated returning-to-hope rather than achieved once for all.

Anchor scriptures: Heb. 6:18–19 ("we who have fled for refuge might have strong encouragement to hold fast to the hope set before us. We have this as a sure and steadfast anchor of the soul, a hope that enters into the inner place behind the curtain"); Ps. 42:5, 42:11, 43:5 ("Hope in God; for I shall again praise him, my salvation and my God"); Lam. 3:21–24 ("But this I call to mind, and therefore I have hope: The steadfast love of the LORD never ceases; his mercies never come to an end; they are new every morning; great is your faithfulness. 'The LORD is my portion,' says my soul, 'therefore I will hope in him'"); 1 Pet. 1:3–9 (the living hope; the inheritance kept in heaven; the rejoicing-though-grieved dynamic); Rom. 5:3–5 (suffering produces endurance, endurance character, and character hope; hope does not put us to shame); 1 Thess. 1:3 (the work of faith and labor of love and steadfastness of hope); Rom. 15:13 ("May the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, so that by the power of the Holy Spirit you may abound in hope"); Col. 1:5 (the hope laid up for you in heaven).

Multi-author check: Hebrews writer; the Psalmist; Lamentations writer; Peter; Paul (multiple letters). At least five authors.

Preliminary evaluation: Strong multi-author footing. **The principal question is overlap with FL.XXII Endurance-Hope Law at P1/GIII.** FL.XXII operates at the individual scale at the Soul-integration Group; the present candidate would name the scale-invariant substrate-level hope-anchor dynamic. The relationship parallels the FL.III-to-FL.XXXV pairing at GII — present-moment articulation at P1; scale-invariant substrate at P0. The two laws may stand as parent-substrate (P0) to individual-operation (P1) at adjacent Periods, with the present candidate providing the scale-invariant substrate that FL.XXII's individual-scale operation operates within. **Moderate likelihood; depends on operational distinctness from FL.XXII at the stress-test articulation. May be absorbed into Candidate 1 (Soul-Restoration) as the hope-anchor substrate-content articulation within the broader restoration framework.**

### Moderate-Risk Candidates (may admit; may absorb)

**Candidate 4: The Soul-as-Integration-Substrate Law (architectural)**

Operational claim: The soul operates as the integration-substrate of the person, holding the heart, mind, and body in coherent operation; the soul is itself a distinct dimension of operational territory (rather than reducible to the heart, mind, or body it integrates), and the soul's integration of the dimensions is itself a sustained operational accomplishment requiring ongoing engagement with God's integrative operation. The architectural claim is that the soul is the dimension within which the heart-orientation (GII), the cognitive-volitional renewal (GIV), the embodied-active operation (GV), and the spirit-communion (GI) are held together as one person under God; without the integrating soul, the dimensions operate in conflict (Rom. 7's "the good I want to do, I do not do" articulates the fragmented state).

Anchor scriptures: Gen. 2:7 ("the LORD God formed the man of dust from the ground and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life, and the man became a living soul/nephesh"); Deut. 6:5 ("you shall love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your might"); Mark 12:30 / Luke 10:27 / Matt. 22:37 (the greatest-commandment articulation adding "mind"); 1 Thess. 5:23 ("may your whole spirit and soul and body be kept blameless"); 1 Cor. 9:24–27 (the disciplined-body in service of the integrated-mission); Rom. 7:15–25 (the divided self); Rom. 8:5–13 (the renewed coherent operation); Jas. 1:8 ("a double-minded man, unstable in all his ways"); Ps. 86:11 ("unite my heart to fear your name").

Multi-author check: Mosaic; Deuteronomist; Mark/Jesus; Paul (multiple letters); James; Psalmist. At least six authors.

Preliminary evaluation: The territory is operationally real but **the candidate's claim is architectural rather than directional in the strict inclusion-bar sense.** The catalog's V1.Exp2 Nested Person Structure (at P1/GVI, Reasonably Inferred) already articulates the architectural relationship of soul-holding-the-other-dimensions. The present candidate would extend the architectural claim into a directional substrate-level law at P0/GIII; whether the extension produces a distinct directional law or whether it operationalizes V1.Exp2 at the scale-invariant scale is the principal stress-test question. The candidate may admit as a distinct law (the directional-substrate articulation of the architectural relationship V1.Exp2 names) or may be held as substrate-articulation within V1.Exp2 or within Candidate 1 (Soul-Restoration). **Moderate likelihood; structural-relationship question with V1.Exp2 is the principal stress-test territory.**

**Candidate 5: The Soul-Rest Substrate Law**

Operational claim: The soul's rest in God — distinct from the body's rest in Sabbath cessation (FL.XXIII) — operates as the integration-substrate that holds the soul in its integrated state; the rest is interior soul-state rather than external practice, achieved through sustained engagement with Christ as the rest-giver and maintained through continued returning-to-the-rest under the pressures of life. Absent the rest, the soul operates in chronic restlessness that fragments the dimensions under the cumulative stress of un-rested operation.

Anchor scriptures: Matt. 11:28–29 ("Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn from me, for I am gentle and lowly in heart, and *you will find rest for your souls*"); Heb. 4:9–11 ("So then, there remains a Sabbath rest for the people of God, for whoever has entered God's rest has also rested from his works as God did from his. Let us therefore strive to enter that rest"); Jer. 6:16 ("ask for the ancient paths, where the good way is; and walk in it, and find rest for your souls"); Ps. 62:1–2, 62:5–8 ("For God alone my soul waits in silence; from him comes my salvation"); Ps. 131:2 ("I have calmed and quieted my soul, like a weaned child with its mother"); Phil. 4:6–7 (the peace of God guarding hearts and minds).

Multi-author check: Matthew/Jesus; Hebrews writer; Jeremiah; Psalmist (multiple); Paul. At least five authors.

Preliminary evaluation: Strong scriptural footing. **The principal question is operational distinctness from FL.XXIII Sabbath Rest Law (P0/GVI) and from Candidate 1 (Soul-Restoration).** FL.XXIII operates at the structural-frame dimension (cessation in trust-of-provision); the present candidate operates at the soul-integration dimension (interior soul-state rest). The two are operationally related (the structural cessation of FL.XXIII may produce the interior soul-rest of the present candidate; the interior soul-rest may sustain the structural cessation). Whether the soul-rest framing produces a distinct law or operates as substrate-articulation within FL.XXIII or within Candidate 1 is the stress-test question. **Moderate likelihood; may admit as distinct law (the interior soul-state distinct from the structural cessation); may be absorbed into Candidate 1 as the soul-rest articulation of the broader restoration framework.**

**Candidate 6: The Soul-Tethering-to-God Substrate Law**

Operational claim: The soul's integration depends on the soul being tethered (anchored, grounded) to God as its center; absent the tethering, the soul fragments under the competing pulls of life (the heart's desires; the mind's reasonings; the body's impulses; circumstance's pressures) that operate against the soul's integration. The tethering's content is sustained relational engagement with God as the soul's center, not merely cognitive belief in God's existence; the operational tethering is what maintains the soul's integration under stress.

Anchor scriptures: Ps. 62:1–2 ("For God alone my soul waits in silence... He alone is my rock and my salvation, my fortress; I shall not be greatly shaken"); Ps. 131:2 (the weaned child with its mother); Heb. 6:19 (the anchor of the soul); Matt. 11:28–29 (rest for souls in Christ); Ps. 16:8 ("I have set the LORD always before me; because he is at my right hand, I shall not be shaken"); Ps. 73:23–26 ("Nevertheless, I am continually with you; you hold my right hand. You guide me with your counsel... God is the strength of my heart and my portion forever"); Col. 3:3 ("your life is hidden with Christ in God").

Multi-author check: Psalmist (multiple); Hebrews writer; Matthew/Jesus; Paul. Three or four authors.

Preliminary evaluation: Real territory. **The principal question is overlap with FL.XXXV Trust-Substrate Law at P0/GII.** FL.XXXV operates at the heart-orientation dimension; the present candidate would operate at the soul-integration dimension. The trust-substrate (heart) and the soul-tethering (soul) may be related-but-distinct substrate articulations at adjacent Groups of the Period 0 row — FL.XXXV operates on the heart's experienced participation in God's faithful character; the present candidate would operate on the soul's integrated state held by tethering to that same character. The relationship is plausible but the operational distinctness from FL.XXXV would need to be demonstrated at the stress-test. **Moderate likelihood; depends on operational distinctness from FL.XXXV. May be absorbed into FL.XXXV's articulation as the soul-level expression of the heart-level trust-substrate, or may admit as distinct law operating at distinct dimension of the nested structure.**

**Candidate 9: The Soul-Knows-Itself-in-God Substrate Law**

Operational claim: The soul's true self-knowledge — knowing what one is, who one is, what one is for — operates only in reference to God; absent God-reference, the soul's self-knowledge is distorted (Jer. 17:9 "the heart is deceitful above all things"; the soul cannot accurately diagnose itself without external help). The Calvin tradition's "knowledge of God and knowledge of self are inseparable" articulation; the Augustinian "Thou hast made us for thyself, and our heart is restless till it rests in thee" articulation.

Anchor scriptures: Jer. 17:9–10 ("The heart is deceitful above all things, and desperately sick; who can understand it? ## Structural Observations Surfaced by the Lens

The pre-pass has surfaced two structural observations worth noting regardless of which candidates admit.

**Observation 1: The Soul column's vertical articulation is unique in shape.** Unlike the Heart column (which holds substrate-level entries at P0/GII with FL.XXXV and FL.XXXVII alongside scale-specific orientation entries at P1, P3, P4, P5) and the Spirit column (which holds the Hardening substrate at P0/GI alongside the Miracle Frame architectural-frame relocated at v5_6_0_16 alongside the various scale-specific Spirit entries), the Soul column's existing entries at the scale-specific scales (Humility-Exaltation, Soul Disorder Awareness, Emotional Knot, Tool-Application, Corporate Emotional Integration, Communal Soul-Care for the Wounded, Generational Transmission, Suffering-as-Formation Loop) operate at the soul-integration territory but with varied specific operational claims. The proposed Soul-Restoration Substrate Law at P0/GIII would provide the scale-invariant substrate that the various scale-specific entries operate within. The vertical articulation pattern of the Soul column post-admission would be: scale-invariant restoration-substrate (P0/GIII, new) → individual integration work (P1/GIII: Humility-Exaltation; Soul Disorder Awareness) → dyadic integration work (P2/GIII: Emotional Knot; Tool-Application) → community integration work (P3/GIII: Corporate Emotional Integration; Communal Soul-Care) → generational integration transmission (P4/GIII: Generational Transmission) → cosmic-eschatological integration formation (P5/GIII: Suffering-as-Formation Loop). The vertical articulation would be the catalog's first articulation of a Group column's full scale-invariant substrate-to-eschatological-formation development.

**Observation 2: The substrate-to-operation pattern at P0 extends across multiple Groups.** The v5_6_0_14 admission of FL.XXXV produced the catalog's first articulated substrate-to-operation relationship across distinct Groups at the same scale (FL.XXXV at GII as substrate; FL.VI at GV as operation). The v5_6_0_16 admission of FL.XXXVII at P0/GII produced a second substrate at the same cell. The proposed Soul-Restoration Substrate Law at P0/GIII would extend the pattern further — substrate operations at GI (FL.XV), GII (FL.XXXV and FL.XXXVII), and GIII (proposed) operating at the scale-invariant scale, with the corresponding operational entries at GIV (FL.X), GV (FL.VI/IX/XVI), and GVI (FL.I/XXIII). The pattern teaches the catalog that the Period 0 row articulates substrate dimensions (the inner-three Groups: Spirit, Heart, Soul) and operational dimensions (the outer-three Groups: Mind & Will, Body & Action, Structural Frame), with the substrates operationally producing the operations the outer Groups articulate.

The two observations together suggest that the v5_6_0_17 admission of a Soul-Restoration Substrate Law would not only fill the empty P0/GIII cell but would also complete a structural pattern the recent expansion arc has been articulating implicitly — the Period 0 row as the catalog's scale-invariant substrate-and-architecture row, with the inner-three Groups holding substrate-level mechanisms and the outer-three Groups holding the operational consequences those substrates produce.
