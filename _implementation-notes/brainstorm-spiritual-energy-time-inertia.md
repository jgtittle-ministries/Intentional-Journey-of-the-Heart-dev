## The scaffold already in the corpus
The physics web is tight: distance, velocity (dd/dt), inertia (mass), force (F=ma), work (W=F·d), energy (potential + kinetic), power (P=dE/dt=F·v). Three nodes are already placed:
- **distance** `d` — position relative to the Glory attractor (V3.Exp9), proxied by the Affective Taxonomy (V3.Exp4): high AT ≈ low distance.

The honest framing for adding time/inertia/energy: not "new physics," but "if those three relationships hold, the other web-nodes are forced — here is the most defensible shape for each."

## Spiritual inertia (mₛ) — resistance to a *change* of spiritual motion
Components, all already in the corpus: entrenchment/hardness (FL.XV; Sower hard path), bondage/besetting patterns (FL.XVI), the weight of wound and false self (Vol 2 knots), attachment (ATB — insecure raises mₛ, secure lowers it).

**Key new idea — inertia is dual-valenced.** It resists leaving the local minimum (the liability: the hardened heart won't move) *and* resists leaving the Glory trajectory once established (the gift: steadfastness, FL.XXII endurance, the Stage-5 saint who can't be deflected). Same quantity, opposite blessing, depending which side of the barrier you stand on. Maps to held-tensions/shadow-pairs and the "easy down, hard up" asymmetry (Properties of Spiritual Law).

**Relationship — a spiritual second law:** `aₛ = Fₛ / mₛ`. The same outpouring moves the soft heart and barely budges the entrenched one; the difference is **inertia, not the force, and not the person's worth.** (Pastoral guard, Peterson-shaped: never a verdict.)

## Spiritual time (τ) — the clock ticks on encounter, not on chronos
Chronos is the wrong clock (already flagged: the "spirit-time vs. natural-time" open trail, 2 Pet 3:8). Proposal:
- **τ = ∫ (engagement) dt** — kairos as the integral of genuine hearing/obeying/trusting acts (PROAPT cycles, surrenders, encounters).
- **dτ/dt is variable:** spikes in revival/crisis/"suddenly" (huge τ in little chronos); ≈ 0 on the dry path (years pass, almost no τ).
- The **delay property** falls out: the harvest needs enough τ, which may take many chronos-years (Elijah's seven prayers = τ accumulating to a threshold).
- Velocity toward God is cleaner per τ: `vₛ = dd/dτ`.

## Spiritual energy (Eₛ) — and the equation being hunted
Work-energy shape:
- **Eₛ = Uₛ(d) + ½ mₛ vₛ²** — potential (position in the Glory field) + kinetic (momentum toward God).
- **Activation barrier Eₐ** (the mustard-seed threshold) traps a soul in its local minimum.
- **Source = G, not the soul.** "It is God who *works in you*" (energeō, Phil 2:13); the *energoumenē* of James 5:16 already cited in Exp 3. Energy is **received**; the system is **open** — which is the honest answer to the Exp 6 conservation question: conservation holds *only with the Source included.* This is the term that keeps it from being a closed machine.
- **Power:** `Pₛ = Fₛ · vₛ = dEₛ/dτ` — the rate of formation; spikes in revival (Exp 3 already says "power is the rate at which force produces work").

## Revival, run through the whole apparatus
1. **G injects energy** (sovereign outpouring, not summoned).
2. **dτ/dt spikes** — spiritual time accelerates; a weekend holds years.
3. Many souls clear **Eₐ** and leave their local minima at once.
4. Potential → kinetic: **momentum builds** (revival's self-feeding feel).
5. The moving body **lowers the barrier for others** (V2.Exp9 community amplifier; resonance, Exp 8) → a **cascade / phase transition**, not a sum of individuals.
6. **High-inertia souls move last/least** — observed, never a verdict; just mₛ.
7. **Afterward:** those reaching Stage 4–5 acquire *good* inertia (steadfastness) that holds them on the Glory trajectory when the outpouring recedes. **The fruit of a revival is who keeps moving, not the peak** — which the energy/inertia picture predicts.

## The three next pieces (riff)
### Spiritual friction / damping (γ) — why momentum decays after revival
Distinct from inertia: friction is a **velocity-dependent drag** (`F_drag = −γ·vₛ`) that opposes *current* motion; inertia resists *change* of motion. Spiritually, γ = the drag of the world/flesh, unhealed knots, and (above all) isolation. Damping regimes:
- **Underdamped** community → oscillates: revival → backslide → revival.
- **Critically/over-damped** in the *good* sense → motion settles smoothly into the new state ("it stuck").
- **Community lowers γ** (sustains motion); **isolation raises it.** This is the standard "revival fades" problem and why discipleship/community/follow-up is precisely what converts a spike into lasting formation: low γ + acquired good-inertia. Ties to FL.XXX (communal soul-care), V2.Exp9.

### The gift of faith as a localized inertia/barrier drop
Exp 3 left "the differential gift of faith (1 Cor 12:9)" as open question V3.Exp3.Open2. Candidate structural account: the **gift of faith = G transiently, sovereignly collapsing mₛ (or Eₐ) for a specific person in a specific moment** — the barrier that stood yesterday is simply gone, and movement that was impossible becomes effortless. Not earned, not generated — gifted; the exact inverse of hardening (which *raises* inertia). Gives Open2 a candidate shape without over-claiming.

### Spiritual temperature (Tₛ) of a community — the collective face of kinetic energy
Thermodynamic analog: temperature = average agitation / kinetic energy across the body.
- **Hot** body = high average kinetic energy, many in motion, barriers crossed en masse (revival is a high-Tₛ state). Scripture: "did not our hearts burn within us" (Luke 24:32); "fervent (ζέοντες) in spirit" (Rom 12:11); tongues of fire (Acts 2).
- **Lukewarm** = the dangerous middle Christ spits out (Rev 3:15–16) — settled in local minima, low Tₛ.
- **Arrhenius-shaped intuition:** barrier-crossing rate ∝ e^(−Eₐ/Tₛ) — raise the temperature and the *same* barriers nobody crossed last year get crossed at once. Revival = the Spirit raising the body's temperature.
- **Heat spreads by contact** — one on-fire saint warms neighbors (coupling); the corporate version of resonance.
