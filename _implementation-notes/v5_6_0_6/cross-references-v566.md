# Cross-Reference Additions to Existing Foundational Law Chapters (v5_6_0_6)

The v5_6_0_6 implementation produces four new Foundational Laws (FL.XVIII Bitter-Root Community Law, FL.XIX Spirit Anointing Transmission Law, FL.XX Gathered-Body Discernment Law, and FL.XXI Household Formation Law). Two of the new laws pair with existing Foundational Law chapters in a manner that warrants reciprocal cross-references — the Bitter-Root Community Law with FL.V Reciprocal Forgiveness (as its community-scale parent in the Group V vertical family) and the Household Formation Law with FL.XVII Substitution-Cascade (as the constitutive bidirectional counterpart at the same P4/GII cell). This file specifies the cross-reference additions needed in the two existing chapters.

The additions are presented as: (1) the anchor — a distinctive substring in the existing chapter that locates where the addition goes; (2) the addition — the sentence(s) to insert.
