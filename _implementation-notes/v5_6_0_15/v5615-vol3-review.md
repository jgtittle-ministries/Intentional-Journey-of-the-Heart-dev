# Vol 3 Forward-References Review

*Standalone review document for v5_6_0_15 consideration*

*Prepared at the end of v5_6_0_14*

## Purpose

This document reviews the six Vol 3 forward-references currently held at P5/GV and P5/GVI of the Periodic Table against the matured catalog state at v5_6_0_14. The review's purpose is to determine whether each forward-reference still warrants its current placement, should be absorbed into existing Foundational Laws now that those Laws hold the territory the forward-reference was placeholder-holding, should be admitted as a new pastorally-named Foundational Law in the active catalog, or should be removed from the operational table entirely.

The review operates under two principles ratified for this work:

- **Conservative absorption.** Absorb a forward-reference only where the territory is fully held by existing Foundational Laws with no remainder. Where any genuine new operational content exists, preserve the forward-reference or admit a new Foundational Law on its own scriptural footing. The disposition is the inverse of the catalog's conservative inclusion bias and operates with the same care.

- **Use the noted-but-not-recommended mechanism to preserve interesting options.** Where a forward-reference's framing surfaces operational territory or structural-observation territory that does not warrant separate admission but is worth preserving, document it in the "Noted for Future Consideration" section rather than discarding it. The mechanism is the same the v5_6_0_14 pass used for the thirteen demoted attachment-theory candidates and operates as institutional memory for subsequent revisions.

The review is a recommendation document. The actual implementation — Vol 5 PT chapter edits, any new FL chapter at the matured chapter length, Vol 1 overview chapter update, and the closing-note and residual-questions work — is left to a subsequent revision (anticipated as v5_6_0_15 if the recommendations are ratified). This document's output is a recommended action for each of the six forward-references plus a small set of preserved notes for future consideration.

## Current State of the Vol 3 Forward-References

The six Vol 3 forward-references currently sit at two cells of the Period 5 row:

**P5/GV (Body & Action, Cosmic-Eschatological):**
- Spiritual Force Equation
- Miracle Threshold Events

**P5/GVI (Structural Frame, Cosmic-Eschatological):**

The forward-references were placed when the catalog held approximately seventeen Foundational Laws at v5_6_0_4. The matured catalog at v5_6_0_14 holds thirty-five Foundational Laws across substantially-completed Period 0, Period 1, and Period 4 rows, with the community-relational substrate triplet at P3/GV, the corporate-person work at P3, and the dyadic-architectural work at P2/GVI all developed. The expansion arc has thickened many of the territories the Vol 3 forward-references were placeholder-holding, and the present review tests whether that thickening has overtaken each forward-reference or whether each still names operational content the active catalog does not hold.

## Review Methodology

For each Vol 3 forward-reference, the review proceeds in four steps:

**Step 1 — Pastoral re-articulation.** State the forward-reference's underlying operational claim in pastoral language without the physics-borrowed vocabulary. This is not a translation exercise but a substance-recovery exercise — the question is what operational content the forward-reference is actually trying to name, set apart from how Vol 3 plans to articulate it.

**Step 2 — Catalog coverage check.** Identify the existing Foundational Laws (and Vol 1/Vol 2 Explorations where relevant) that already hold the operational territory the forward-reference names. Test whether the coverage is full (the existing laws hold the whole operational claim) or partial (the existing laws hold the core but leave operational content the forward-reference still uniquely holds).

**Step 3 — Recommendation.** Recommend one of four actions: ABSORB (fully covered by existing laws; forward-reference is no longer needed); ADMIT (operational territory warrants a new pastorally-named Foundational Law on its own scriptural footing); HOLD (forward-reference still warrants its place; Vol 3 work proceeds on its own terms); REMOVE (entry is methodological rather than operational and does not belong in the operational table).

**Step 4 — Notes preserved.** Where the review surfaces interesting operational or structural observations that do not warrant separate admission, document them for future consideration rather than discarding them.

## Review of Each Forward-Reference

### 1. Spiritual Force Equation (P5/GV)

**Pastoral re-articulation.** The underlying operational claim is that spiritual cause-and-effect operates with structural proportionality — that the strength of an input produces a correspondingly strong output, with the proportionality holding across multiple operational dimensions. Sown more, reap more. Forgave more, was forgiven more. Trust more deeply, obey more substantially. The "Force Equation" framing names this proportionality and seeks to articulate it in a unified operational form.

**Catalog coverage check.** The proportionality claim is articulated across the entire Period 0 row of the matured catalog. FL.I Sowing-and-Reaping (P0/GVI) names the proportionality directly: "the harvest's character determined by the seed's character and its quantity proportional to the sowing's quantity." FL.IX Generosity-Provision (P0/GV) names the proportionality directly: "generosity produces provision proportional to the generosity's measure; stinginess produces the inverse proportional withholding." FL.VI Hear-and-Obey (P0/GV) names the proportionality through the issuing-in-doing condition — operational benefit follows hearing-issued-in-doing in proportion to the doing's substance. FL.XV Hardening names the cumulative proportionality on the idol-ward side — sustained refusal compounds across the period of refusal. FL.XVI Bondage names the cumulative proportionality on the idol-ward operational side. FL.XXXV Trust-Substrate (new at v5_6_0_14) names the proportionality through the substrate's depth conditioning the operations' substance. The entire Period 0 row articulates the proportionality the Force Equation forward-reference names, with the additional resolution of which Group's dimension the proportionality operates within.

**Recommendation: ABSORB.** The Spiritual Force Equation's operational content is fully held by the Period 0 row's six Foundational Laws taken together. The proportionality claim is articulated at each of the row's populated Groups; the Force Equation's unified-form framing is a useful structural observation but does not warrant separate admission as a Foundational Law. The forward-reference is no longer needed at P5/GV.

**Notes preserved.** The proportionality pattern across the Period 0 row may warrant a structural-observation sub-section in the Vol 5 PT chapter (paralleling the "What the Attachment-Theory Pass Revealed" sub-section at v5_6_0_14) — the observation that the scale-invariant row articulates proportionality at the Spirit dimension (FL.XV), the Heart dimension (FL.XXXV), the Mind & Will dimension (FL.X), the Body & Action dimension (FL.VI / FL.XVI / FL.IX), and the Structural Frame dimension (FL.I / FL.XXIII) is structurally instructive about how the scale-invariant scale holds the proportionality dynamic across multiple dimensions of the person. This is the Period 0 row's distinctive structural feature and may be worth articulating explicitly in future revision.

### 2. Miracle Threshold Events (P5/GV)

**Pastoral re-articulation.** The underlying operational claim is that the formation life has critical points at which a structural transition occurs — points where a condition is met that opens what was previously closed, points where a previously-available trajectory becomes structurally unavailable, points at which the soul's state shifts categorically rather than continuously. The "Threshold" framing names these discrete transitions and seeks to articulate them as a recognizable operational pattern.

**Catalog coverage check.** The threshold/gateway pattern is operationally visible at multiple places in the matured catalog. V1.Exp5 Fear of the Lord is explicitly designated as the "Gateway" entry condition for the Wisdom cluster. V2.Exp7 Hearing Development (5-Stage Affective) holds the Affective Level 2→3 transition as a structural threshold within affective hearing formation. FL.XIII Pure-Heart Vision is designated as "Gateway" — the threshold past which the heart can see God. FL.XV Hardening articulates a threshold on the idol-ward side — "reaches a point past which external intervention is required." Regeneration operates as a threshold the catalog implicitly references across multiple laws. The threshold/gateway pattern is therefore a recognizable structural feature of the catalog already, articulated at the entry level for several laws but not articulated as a structural-pattern observation in the table's prose.

**Recommendation: ABSORB.** The Miracle Threshold Events forward-reference's operational content is held distributively across the existing Gateway-designated entries and the threshold articulations within laws like FL.XV and V2.Exp7. The forward-reference itself does not name a single operational law in the catalog's inclusion-bar sense — it names a structural pattern that recurs across multiple laws operating at multiple scales. The forward-reference is no longer needed at P5/GV.

**Notes preserved.** The threshold/gateway pattern across the catalog may warrant a structural-observation sub-section in the Vol 5 PT chapter — articulating where the pattern appears (Gateway entries at V1.Exp5, FL.XIII; threshold articulations at FL.XV, V2.Exp7; implicit thresholds at regeneration and at multiple FL operations), how the pattern operates (discrete categorical transitions rather than continuous changes; conditions met opens what was closed; conditions sustained closes what was open), and what the pattern teaches the formation work (the importance of recognizing threshold conditions and meeting them rather than treating formation as uniformly-continuous improvement). This is a structural pattern the catalog has been articulating piecemeal and that the matured table could now make visible in unified articulation. The Vol 3 framing of "Miracle Threshold Events" may overstate the pattern's cosmic-eschatological specificity — most of the catalog's threshold instances are at the individual or community scales rather than at the cosmic-eschatological scale, suggesting the pattern is scale-invariant rather than P5-specific. The future articulation should test the scale of the threshold pattern explicitly.

### 3. TFT Structural Law (Transcendental Field) (P5/GVI)

**Pastoral re-articulation.** The underlying operational claim is that spiritual reality permeates material reality rather than operating as a separate realm alongside it — that the natural order is held within the larger spiritual order, with the spiritual operating through the natural where God's purposes warrant. The "Transcendental Field" framing names this permeation-and-holding relationship and seeks to articulate it as the architectural structure underneath the catalog's entire operational treatment.

**Catalog coverage check.** The operational claim is directly held by V1.Open Miracle Frame at P5/GI: "The natural order is held within the larger spiritual order rather than the other way around; miracles are not violations of the natural but the spiritual order's operation through the natural where God's purposes warrant." Miracle Frame's Mirror is explicitly orientation-neutral (architectural) — the framing the TFT forward-reference would have provided is already articulated as Miracle Frame's structural character. The two entries (Miracle Frame at P5/GI and TFT at P5/GVI) articulate the same operational claim from two Group locations, with Miracle Frame holding the Spirit-dimension articulation and TFT holding the architectural-frame articulation. The matured catalog's Mirror Discipline already articulates Miracle Frame's architectural character without requiring a separate P5/GVI entry to do the same work.

**Recommendation: ABSORB.** The TFT Structural Law's operational content is held by Miracle Frame at P5/GI, with the architectural-frame character already named in Miracle Frame's Mirror articulation. The forward-reference is no longer needed at P5/GVI; the territory is fully covered.

**Notes preserved.** Two related observations are preserved for future consideration.

*First*, the operational claim that "spiritual reality permeates material reality" may warrant scale-invariant articulation rather than P5 placement. The matured catalog's pattern (FL.I, FL.VI, FL.IX, FL.XV, FL.XVI, FL.XXIII, FL.XXXV) is that operations operating at all scales by the same mechanism are placed at Period 0 rather than at Period 5. The spiritual-permeates-natural claim arguably operates by the same mechanism at all scales — the spiritual realm's relationship to the natural realm is not different at the individual, dyadic, community, generational, or cosmic-eschatological scale. The Miracle Frame entry sits at P5/GI by historical placement rather than by re-test under the matured scale-invariance criteria. Whether Miracle Frame should be relocated to P0/GI as a scale-invariant law is a structural question the future revision may want to address. The TFT forward-reference's existence may have been pointing at this relocation question implicitly.

*Second*, if Miracle Frame is eventually relocated to P0/GI under the scale-invariance criterion, the resulting Period 0 row would hold operational laws at all six Groups (P0/GI Miracle Frame; P0/GII FL.XXXV Trust-Substrate; P0/GIII still empty; P0/GIV FL.X Faith-Sight; P0/GV FL.VI / FL.IX / FL.XVI; P0/GVI FL.I / FL.XXIII). The Period 0 row would become the catalog's most fully-developed scale-invariant articulation. Whether this is desirable is a separate question, but the structural possibility is worth noting.

### 4. Spiritual Distance Metric (P5/GVI)

**Pastoral re-articulation.** The underlying operational claim is that nearness to God and distance from God are real spiritual conditions of the heart, with the heart's experienced state corresponding to operational realities the catalog's other laws name — proximity to God's faithful presence enables certain operations; distance from God's faithful presence makes those operations structurally unavailable. The "Distance Metric" framing names the continuum between proximity and distance and seeks to articulate it as a measurable spiritual dimension.

**Catalog coverage check.** The nearness-and-distance dynamic is articulated across multiple matured catalog entries. FL.VII Drawing-Near Reciprocity (P1/GI) directly articulates the drawing-near direction: "drawing near to God produces God's drawing near to the participant; the relational direction is reciprocal under the conditions of approach the scripture specifies." FL.XV Hardening (P0/GI) articulates the moving-away direction at the scale-invariant scale: "sustained refusal of God's address produces the heart's operational hardening; the hardening compounds across the period of refusal and produces the structural state in which the address can no longer be heard." FL.XXXV Trust-Substrate (P0/GII, new at v5_6_0_14) articulates the substrate's depth as the heart's experienced participation in God's faithful presence — the substrate's strength corresponding to operational nearness. FL.XIII Pure-Heart Vision (P1/GII, Gateway) articulates the heart's purity as the condition for seeing God — the operational form of nearness at the heart-orientation dimension. The "Distance" framing the Vol 3 forward-reference holds is therefore a structural observation about the relationships among these existing laws rather than a separate law in the inclusion-bar sense.

**Recommendation: ABSORB.** The Spiritual Distance Metric's operational content is held by FL.VII Drawing-Near Reciprocity, FL.XV Hardening, FL.XXXV Trust-Substrate, and FL.XIII Pure-Heart Vision combined. The forward-reference's "metric" framing is field-theoretic vocabulary that does not earn its place in the pastoral catalog and that the existing laws articulate without requiring quantification language. The forward-reference is no longer needed at P5/GVI.

**Notes preserved.** The structural-relationship among FL.VII, FL.XV, FL.XXXV, and FL.XIII may warrant explicit articulation in future revision. The four laws together hold the catalog's articulation of nearness-or-distance from God at multiple scales and across multiple Group dimensions: FL.XV at scale-invariant Spirit dimension (the moving-away substrate); FL.XXXV at scale-invariant Heart dimension (the trust-substrate's depth as nearness's heart-face); FL.XIII at individual Heart dimension (the purity condition for vision); FL.VII at individual Spirit dimension (the drawing-near reciprocity). The cross-Group, cross-scale articulation of nearness-or-distance is a structural pattern the matured table makes visible and that may benefit from explicit structural-relationship articulation. This is the second of two structural-observation sub-sections this review surfaces as worth future articulation (the first being the Period 0 row's proportionality pattern under the Force Equation review).

### 5. Quantification Program (P5/GVI)

**Pastoral re-articulation.** The Quantification Program is not an operational law in the catalog's inclusion-bar sense. Its name describes a methodological program — how Vol 3 might quantify the operational relationships among the catalog's laws — rather than naming an operational law that Vol 3 would articulate. The forward-reference's placement in the operational table at P5/GVI was structurally awkward when first held and is more obviously awkward now that the matured catalog has clarified the distinction between operational laws (the catalog's content) and methodological discussions (how the catalog and its successor volumes do their analytical work).

**Catalog coverage check.** No catalog entry articulates a methodological program because the catalog does not hold methodological programs as operational laws. The forward-reference's content does not correspond to any operational claim in the catalog's inclusion-bar sense.

**Recommendation: REMOVE.** The Quantification Program does not belong in the operational table. Its territory — whether and how Vol 3 might pursue quantification of spiritual operations — is a Vol 3 question that Vol 3 may pursue on its own terms in its own volume's methodological discussion. The forward-reference is removed from P5/GVI without absorption (because there is no operational content to absorb into existing laws).

**Notes preserved.** Two observations on Vol 3 methodology are preserved.

*First*, the question of whether Vol 3 ultimately pursues a quantification program is a Vol 3 methodological question, not a catalog question. The removal of the forward-reference from the operational table does not preclude Vol 3's pursuit of quantification work; it merely removes the placeholder from a place it does not structurally belong. If Vol 3 develops a quantification methodology and that methodology surfaces specific operational claims, those claims would enter the catalog through the standard inclusion-bar process rather than through pre-deferred forward-reference.

*Second*, the awkwardness of the Quantification Program's placement in the operational table at v5_6_0_4 may have been a signal that the catalog at that early stage had not yet drawn a clean distinction between operational laws and methodological discussions about the catalog. The matured catalog at v5_6_0_14 holds this distinction cleanly — operational laws articulate cause-and-effect operations of the spirit-and-heart life; methodological discussions articulate how the catalog and its successor volumes do their analytical work. The forward-reference's removal is consistent with the matured catalog's articulation of this distinction.

### 6. Glory Attractor / Eschatological Law (P5/GVI)

**Pastoral re-articulation.** The underlying operational claim is that the eschatological completion — God's glory revealed at the consummation of all things; the marriage supper of the Lamb; the new heavens and new earth; the eschatological people of God gathered to see Christ as He is — operates as the ultimate direction-of-pursuit toward which the heart of the people of God is drawn through the present life. The future glory pulls forward; the heart's anticipation of what is coming shapes how the heart navigates difficulty, makes sacrificial choices, and sustains hope through trial. The "Attractor" framing names this forward-pulling dynamic; the underlying operational claim is real and pastorally substantial.

**Catalog coverage check.** The matured catalog does not hold this operational claim at the Foundational tier. The closest existing entries are partial:

- **FL.XXII Endurance-Hope Law (P1/GIII):** Names the soul's history with God under trial as the ground of hope, but operates at the individual scale and at the soul-integration dimension. The eschatological-glory orientation is implicit in FL.XXII's hope but is not its explicit content — FL.XXII names hope grounded in the soul's history with God, not hope grounded in anticipation of the eschatological completion.

- **FL.III Heart-Throne Law (P1/GII):** Names what the heart enthrones as the structuring center of formation at the individual scale. The eschatological glory as the heart's throne is an instance of FL.III's operation, but the eschatological-specific dynamic is not articulated as such.

- **FL.VIII Desire-for-God Law (P1/GII):** Names sustained desire for God as producing increased capacity to receive God at the individual scale. The eschatological dimension of the desire (desiring the seeing-Him-as-He-is at the consummation) is implicit but not explicitly articulated.

- **Worship Alignment Law at P5/GII (Speculative ◆):** Names worship's alignment work at the cosmic-eschatological scale. The eschatological dimension is present here but the forward-pulling dynamic of the not-yet-completed glory on the heart of the people of God is not articulated.

The closest existing articulation of the forward-pulling dynamic the Glory Attractor names is at Rom. 8:18–25 (the hope of the coming glory; creation groaning with us for the revealing), 2 Cor. 4:17–18 (this light momentary affliction preparing for us an eternal weight of glory; looking to things not seen), Heb. 11:13–16 (they saw the promises from afar and welcomed them; sought a better country, a heavenly one), Phil. 3:13–14 (forgetting what lies behind and straining forward to what lies ahead; the upward call of God in Christ Jesus), 1 John 3:2–3 (we shall be like him, for we shall see him as he is; everyone who thus hopes in him purifies himself), Col. 3:1–4 (seek the things that are above, where Christ is), and Rev. 22:17 (the Spirit and the Bride say, "Come"). The scriptural canvas is substantial — at least Paul across multiple letters, the Hebrews writer, John (in 1 John and Revelation), with the Old Testament background at Isaiah's eschatological visions providing supplementary witness. The catalog's existing laws hold partial articulations but no Foundational Law articulates the forward-pulling dynamic of the eschatological completion on the present life of the people of God as its primary operational claim.

**Recommendation: ADMIT.** The Glory Attractor forward-reference, articulated pastorally, names operational territory that the matured catalog does not yet hold and that warrants admission as a new Foundational Law on its own scriptural footing. The recommended pastoral name is **The Eschatological Glory Law** — naming the operational claim directly without field-theoretic vocabulary. The proposed placement is **P5/GII (Heart, Cosmic-Eschatological)** rather than P5/GVI, because the operational mechanism is heart-orientation (what the heart of the people of God is drawn toward) rather than architectural-frame. The placement at P5/GII would extend the Heart column's cross-scale articulation: FL.XXXV Trust-Substrate at P0/GII (scale-invariant heart-substrate); FL.III / FL.VIII / FL.XIII / FL.XIV at P1/GII (individual-scale heart-orientation cluster); FL.XXXII Communal Worship Heart-Alignment at P3/GII (community-scale corporate-heart); FL.XVII / FL.XXI at P4/GII (generational-scale heart-formation pair); new Eschatological Glory Law at P5/GII (cosmic-eschatological heart-orientation). The Heart column would hold scale-invariant articulation across all six scales with the proposed admission.

**Proposed operational claim (working draft for the implementation pass):** *The eschatological completion — God's glory fully revealed, the people of God seeing Christ as He is, the new heavens and new earth established — operates as the forward direction of pursuit toward which the heart of the people of God is drawn through the present life; the heart's anticipation of the not-yet-completed glory produces operational consequences in the present (sustained hope through trial; sacrificial choices in service of what is coming; purifying-of-self in anticipation of seeing Him as He is; orientation toward "things above" rather than "things on the earth"); the absence of eschatological anticipation produces present-life over-investment in what is passing away.*

**Proposed Mirror (constitutive bidirectional, statement form):** *The Eschatological Glory Law's idol-ward face operates wherever the heart's forward-pulling orientation is directed toward a competing eschatological vision rather than toward God's revealed completion — political utopias, cultural progressions, technological transformations, individual self-actualization arcs, family-dynasty continuations, or institutional permanences that operate as substitute eschatologies of the heart. Each competing eschatology produces the heart's forward-pull toward itself rather than toward God's glory, with the operational consequences (sustained hope grounded in the false vision; sacrificial choices in service of the substitute completion; orientation toward the substitute's "things above") mimicking the law's God-ward operation while pursuing the wrong end. The matured catalog's FL.III Heart-Throne articulates the throne dynamic at the present-moment dimension; the present law articulates the parallel dynamic at the forward-pulling-toward-completion dimension. The two laws together hold the heart's orientation in both its present-moment and its forward-pulling articulations.*

**Multi-author check.** Paul across Romans (8:18–25), 2 Corinthians (4:17–18, 5:1–10), Philippians (3:13–14, 3:20–21), Colossians (3:1–4), 1 Thessalonians (4:13–18), and 2 Timothy (4:7–8); the Hebrews writer (11:13–16, 12:1–2, 13:14); John (1 John 3:2–3; Revelation 21–22, 22:17); Peter (1 Pet. 1:3–9, 2 Pet. 3:11–13). At least four New Testament authors plus the Old Testament background in Isaiah's eschatological visions (Isa. 25:6–9; 65:17–25). The multi-author footing substantially exceeds the catalog's two-author minimum.

**Broad acceptance check.** The operational claim that the eschatological completion draws the heart forward is held with substantial uniformity across mainstream Christian traditions. The Catholic tradition's articulation of the beatific vision as the heart's telos; the Orthodox tradition's eschatological liturgy and the resurrection-hope centrality; the Reformed tradition's articulation of the *eschaton* as the consummation of the covenant; the Lutheran tradition's emphasis on the kingdom-yet-to-come; the Wesleyan tradition's articulation of glorification; the Anabaptist tradition's discipleship-oriented eschatology; the broader evangelical tradition's emphasis on the second coming as the heart's hope — all hold the operational claim that the eschatological completion's forward-pull on the present life is real and pastorally substantial. Tradition-differences exist on specific eschatological questions (the millennium; the rapture timing; the relationship between the present kingdom and the future kingdom; the universalism question) but the underlying operational claim that the eschatological completion draws the heart forward in the present life is held with substantial uniformity.

**Confidence:** *Clearly Taught.* The multi-author scriptural footing is substantial, the broad acceptance across traditions is substantial, the operational claim is directionally structured (the forward-pulling direction is named explicitly across the scriptural canvas), and the principle-level articulation is clean (the principle is the forward-pulling dynamic of the eschatological completion on the heart; the operational pathway is the heart's sustained orientation toward "things above" through scripture-shaped imagination, communal worship that anticipates the marriage supper, and sacramental practice that anticipates the consummation — natural operational expressions of the principle rather than scripturally-specified specific pathway requiring Band 2 or Band 3 articulation).

**Notes preserved.** Three observations are preserved for the implementation pass.

*First*, the FL.III Heart-Throne / Eschatological Glory Law structural relationship may warrant explicit articulation. The two laws together hold the heart's orientation in both its present-moment dimension (FL.III) and its forward-pulling-toward-completion dimension (new law) — a structural pairing across Periods (P1 and P5) at the same Group (GII). The pairing pattern is parallel to but distinct from the parent-child and constitutive bidirectional patterns the catalog has already articulated; it is a temporal-dimension pairing across Periods at the same Group rather than a scale-pairing or same-cell-pairing relationship. Whether this temporal-dimension pairing warrants explicit articulation as a fourth structural-relationship pattern (alongside parent-child, constitutive bidirectional, substrate-to-operation) is a structural question worth flagging for the implementation pass.

*Second*, the Worship Alignment Law at P5/GII (currently Speculative ◆) may warrant re-examination in light of the proposed Eschatological Glory Law admission. The Worship Alignment Law and the proposed law would coexist at P5/GII as two distinct cosmic-eschatological heart-orientation laws — one articulating worship's alignment work (Worship Alignment) and one articulating the eschatological completion's forward-pull (proposed). Whether the two laws stand as distinct entries or whether one is structural articulation within the other is a question the implementation pass would need to resolve.

*Third*, the cross-scale Heart column (GII) articulation that the proposed admission would complete is structurally significant. The Heart column would hold scale-invariant articulation across all six scales: P0 (FL.XXXV); P1 (FL.III + FL.VIII + FL.XIII + FL.XIV cluster); P2 (currently empty, with the Pair-Bond Attachment Substrate candidate held without prejudice at v5_6_0_14); P3 (FL.XXXII); P4 (FL.XVII + FL.XXI pair); P5 (proposed Eschatological Glory Law). The Heart column's cross-scale completion (with P2/GII as the remaining cell) would teach the catalog that the heart-orientation dimension operates at every scale with operational articulation, with substrate at P0, individual-scale cluster at P1, dyadic-scale at P2, community-scale at P3, generational-scale pair at P4, and cosmic-eschatological at P5. Whether the matured catalog can articulate the cross-scale completion as a structural observation in future revision is worth preserving as a flag for subsequent work.
