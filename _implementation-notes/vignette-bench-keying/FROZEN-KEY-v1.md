## Methodological findings for the Vol 4 chapter and the eventual paper

1. **The DECLINE verdict works.** Seven independent raters, blind, converged on refusing to score the thief — and articulated the same reason in five different vocabularies. The claim ceiling is not a rule imposed on raters; competent raters demand it.
2. **The controls cannot be passed by piety-detection.** The panel separated V06 from V12a (same night-scene shape) and V07 from V29 (same spotless-service shape) using exactly the guards the instruments will need: direction of grief, and what remains when the scaffold is withdrawn.
3. **Movement pairs are essential.** Baxter: "one panel is a photograph; a soul is a road" — adopted as standing rater doctrine.
4. **The 4/5 boundary is the live one** (V14b, V15b, V08-minority): unself-consciousness vs willed fidelity. This is Edwards's kind-marks surfacing empirically, and it must be written into rater training for the top of the scale.
5. **Instrument-design implication (Fowler, V30):** the bench's own Valuing exemplar is undetectable without other-report. External Witness data is senior, again.
