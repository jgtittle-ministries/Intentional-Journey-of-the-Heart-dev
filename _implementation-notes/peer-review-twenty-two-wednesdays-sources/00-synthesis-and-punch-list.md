**Target:** "Twenty-Two Wednesdays as a Fellowship of the Heart," JSFSC protocol paper, draft v2.3
(17 pp / 7,299 words / 15 footnotes)
**Run:** 2026-08-18 · **Method:** IJH Peer-Review Panel Registry standing protocol
**Panel:** six author-personas seated on their own cited work, plus a verified citation sweep
**Verification:** two adversarial refuters; every HIGH and MEDIUM finding given a verdict before weighting

> **Status of this document.** These are findings, not instructions. No manuscript edit has been
> made. Every item below is the author's to accept, modify, or decline.

---

## 1. The headline

Six reviewers and a citation sweep were asked one question: *is your work represented correctly?*
They filed 60+ findings. After adversarial verification, one finding was refuted outright, several
were downgraded, two were upgraded, and the following picture emerged.

**The paper's scholarship is sound in the places that matter most and loose in a small number of
places that are cheap to fix.** All nine Scripture references check out. Every bibliographic entry
but two is correct. No source was fabricated, and no persona invented literature to support its own
objection. The reviewers repeatedly credited the paper for candour they did not expect: the two
abandoned field designs reported as failures, the limits paragraph, the pre-committed language of
"this ran, not this works," and the author's own line that *a protocol that cannot fail is a
testimony, not a design.*

**The one structural finding.** Four reviewers, reasoning from four unrelated premises
(psychometrics, coding reliability, researcher degrees of freedom, and theory-in-use), observed the
same fact: **no channel in the design is independent of the designer.** The refuter's ruling is
precise and should govern how this is weighted — *this is one undisputed structural fact observed
four times, not four confirmations.* It is one finding with four consequences and one shared remedy.

**The one convergent recommendation.** Unprompted, and from different literatures, four reviewers
independently named the same restructure:

> **Let the behavioural traces carry the argument. Let the judgment-based instruments carry the formation.**

Break-survival — what survives the breaks when nobody is watching — was named by Vazire, Krathwohl,
and Lewicki independently as the paper's best instrument, and it is currently presented as one
supporting trace among several while the room's spoken self-read is called the best instrument in
the pilot. Reversing those two emphases is the single highest-value change available, and it costs
nothing to run because both instruments already exist.

### Tier 3 — the one structural fact, four consequences, one remedy

**2.8 — No channel in the design is independent of the designer.** Observed by Vazire (unblinded
invested raters), Lewicki (no codebook, no second coder), Nosek (researcher degrees of freedom),
and Schön (no theory-in-use detection). Weight as **one finding**, not four.

**The remedy is inside the paper's own cited methodology.** Cameron and colleagues' theological
action research — cited at footnote 15 for the paper's legitimacy — is an *insider–outsider
partnership*. The pilot has the designer as sole observer. The paper names the conflict honestly
without noting that the method it cites carries the standard remedy for it.

Cheapest forms proposed and surviving: a surprise line on the debrief sheet ("one thing tonight that
did not go as I expected"); one genuine outsider, two hours a quarter, answering *judging only by
what this program does, what does it appear to believe?*; and blind coding of the three self-reads.
All must respect the covenant constraint at 2.5.

### Tier 4 — downgraded but worth a sentence each

- Ordinal arithmetic on "one level or more" — real but not load-bearing; appears in no disconfirmation
  condition and is never reported. Note: the "never a scorecard" guard answers an *ethical*
  objection while this is a *psychometric* one; they pass without touching.
- The structural-ceiling claim is stronger than Handbook II supports and stronger than SOKA supports.
  Sharpened form: *if the ceiling sentence were true as written, break-survival could not work.* One
  clause, one occurrence; the abstract does not repeat it.
- Disconfirmation conditions: several have undefined terms or disjunctive escapes. Two reviewers
  audited independently and agreed on the pattern while disagreeing on the count.
- *Move* and *Natural Church Development* are offered as the instrument landscape for an
  individual-maturity argument; one is congregational self-report segmentation, the other
  organisational church health.
- Pre-existing parent–child dyads already sit at identification-based trust on day one, and are the
  ones disclosing first.

## 4. Remedies the panel proposed that would damage the paper

Recorded because a punch-list without this section is dangerous to a pastoral design.

- **Unsigned index cards at the self-reads** — crosses the permission bright line and undoes two
  field revisions.
- **Instrumenting Organization as real value-conflict** in a room where parents sit with their own
  teenagers.
- **An outside coder on confidential verbatim transcripts** — breaks the Measurement Covenant.
- **Numeric primary-outcome thresholds** — contradicts "no target is ever announced," a commitment
  warranted by the very Strathern argument the paper cites.
- **The full nine-item OSF deposit** — three of its contents collide with the no-targets commitment.
- **Stacking five separate framework-status declarations** before the five principles; consolidate to
  one paragraph or the opening becomes a methods preamble.

Two findings reach into published material and should be adjudicated together with the paper, not
after it.

- **2.1 (held / known / owned)** is the spine of the *Watching Trust Grow* card, live on
  fellowshipoftheheart.org, and the basis of IJH Research Register trail 6.

  words.

Neither is a safety issue and neither is urgent this week. But the card is a teaching instrument
that Companions will start using in September, and it currently teaches the overclaim.
