Placement when adopted: an appendix to Exploration 6, or the front of Volume 5 Part VI, cross-linked both ways. Under the light rule this is a tell, an organizing reference like the Periodic Table itself, held at the Tool Map's own tier, Reasonably Inferred. The Map's own caution governs the page: matching help to hurt is discernment, not a lookup, and in every case it is the Holy Spirit who heals; the tools only open the door He walks through.

# How to read it

**Rows** are what is blocked, the diagnostic laws of Volume 2 Part I plus the two upstream governors the Tool Map names, the corporate knot, and the outer limit. **Columns** in Table A are the roles a tool plays for that row: the primary tool, its complement, the access mode when the default verbal entry does not reach the person, and what the text warns against. **Table B** is the same information as a grid: **P** primary, **S** secondary or complement, **A** access mode, **X** contraindicated or guarded, a dot for no pairing in the text. Each row carries the Connect it lives in and each protocol its Companion level where Volume 5 states one.

Three things the Map says to hold at once, which no grid can: *what* is blocked, *how* this person is reached, and *what governs from upstream*. The last two are rows 9 and 10 and the access-mode column. Read them before the knot rows, because a standing agreement or an occupied throne keeps every other knot re-forming.

# What the grid makes visible

- **The most upstream blockage has the least tooling.** Row 10, the idol on the throne, is the governor the Map says keeps every other knot re-forming, and it has no numbered protocol. The move is described in prose in Exploration 6 and borrowed in part from A25 and confession. This is the clearest gap on the page.
- **Two rows lean on imports that were never written down in Part VI.** Wound and false-self work (row 11) runs on Payne and Bennett inner healing, and anger work (row 5) runs on psychodrama and voice dialogue. Both are Part III sources under the tool-import discipline; neither has an A-numbered operational form. The Companion runs them from lineage, not from a page.
- **Three A-list protocols have no clearing row of their own.** A22 onboarding, A16 the group mission, and A21 saying your mission serve the body or the Mission Connect rather than a blockage; that is right, and the grid shows it.
- **One column is a wall of X.** Row 15 is the outer limit the Map names in its first paragraph. Every tool is contraindicated there except containment, and containment is a Level III specialty followed by a referral.
- **Contraindications are uneven.** The B-list entries state their when-not conditions clearly; several A-list entries carry a when-not section that this draft did not verify cell by cell. Those cells are left unmarked rather than guessed.
- **Every primary in rows 2, 3, 4, and 7 ends at the same place.** The Spirit speaking truth into a memory, whether reached by grief, shame, fear, or the lie directly. That is the Map's own law restated as a pattern: different doors, one Healer.

# What this page is not

It is not the Map. Exploration 6 says the right entry point can never be reduced to a chart, and this chart agrees; it is an index into the chapters, kept so that gaps and contraindications are visible, and so that each pairing can be tested as the claim it is. A Companion at Level 1 or 2 will use it in the order it is printed. The discernment the Map asks for is a Stage 4 competency, and no grid supplies it.
