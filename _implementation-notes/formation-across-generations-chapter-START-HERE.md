## The chapter outline (as designed in-session, confirmed/refined by the research)

1. **The problem: τ is non-transferable** — faith non-heritable, conditions heritable; John 1:13 "not of bloods" (the canon's own grammar for "God has no grandchildren"); Ezek 18 + Jer 31 (non-heritability *inside* the new-covenant oracle); the triple "did not know the LORD" formula (Judges 2:10; Eli's sons in the sanctuary; Samuel's "not yet"). Niebuhr's one-generation law and Smith's "we'll get what we are" as the sociological echo.
2. **The ledger: what the child actually inherits** — position, soil, friction environment, exemplar repertoire (Ezek 18:14 "sees"), story, practices, writings ("able to make you wise… through faith" — exposure delivers "able to," never "did"), habitus (Bourdieu via JKA Smith), blessing — and the idols by default. Not inherited: guilt, righteousness, knowledge, velocity, τ, the new birth. No will overridden at family scale: the parent warms the room, cannot compel the yes.
3. **The asymmetry as dynamics** — passive diffusion downhill (idols; bounded to a household's radius) vs active pumping uphill (faith; compounds to a thousand generations where the work continues); the nones' 73% retention as the passive channel winning now; the Shema as κ-engineering (soak-the-day = engagement-density design).
4. **The three-generation problem = the wineskin problem** — Weber's *Erbcharisma* (routinization into bloodline = his own Type 5; and Weber quotes Wesley verbatim); the Half-Way Covenant → Stoddard → Edwards-fired-in-1750 as one continuous wineskin story (30 years to crisis); the declension debate held honestly (Pope's scrupulosity finding; Bercovitch's jeremiad-as-genre); MTD as the degraded product in Smith's own words ("parasitic… the new spirit living within the old body"); Dean's "the church is the problem — and has the solution."
5. **The warmth findings — the carrier medium** — Bengtson ("parental piety will not compensate for a distant dad"; closeness doubles transmission; interfaith marriage the worst breaker; zealots produce rebels); Grusec & Goodnow's two steps (**talk gives accuracy; warmth gives acceptance**; coercion wins step 1 and loses step 2); Scripture's anti-provocation clause first (Eph 6:4; Col 3:21 *athymeō*); Smith & Adamczyk's triad (authoritative warmth + natural faith-talk at home + authentic practice; outsourcing fails); gender honesty (mothers modal; father the high-leverage margin; warmth master for both). JKA Smith's *ex opere externo* gap filled here: liturgy transmits only when embodied love is credible.
6. **The engineering: what the successful designs share** — parent-officiated daily thick practice ("the table is the altar"; Luther's Hausvater headings; the 1647 Directory); **question-provoking ritual** (Pesachim 116a's explicit design intent; the Four Sons; Gilgal's stones; the dark twin at 1 Kgs 1:6 — the question-less house); **threshold as entry-into-obligation** (bar mitzvah vs confirmation-as-graduation; Amish ~85–90%; nobody is born a member — Niebuhr answered by design; cost+agency+timing; means of grace, never grace); high-boundary community; the thickness gradient (Orthodox 83% vs 22% by era; the Conservative 41% "demand without practice" dead zone); the baptismal fork with symmetric failure modes (Bushnell vs believer's baptism; each side invented the other's corrective).
7. **The counter-cases: the canon refuses the formula** — Hezekiah→Manasseh→Josiah (the only full Deut-6:5 doer, taught by no one); Samuel as both counter-cases in one man; David's question-less house; Prov 22:6 honestly (proverb-not-promise; Hildebrandt's commissioning reading = the handoff of agency). **The pastoral rail: a child's unbelief is not a verdict on the parent** — by the same verse (Ezek 18) that says the parent's sin is not a verdict on the child. The praying parent of the prodigal is this chapter's weary reader.
8. **The grace pathway** — compensation meta-analyzed (sudden conversions among the insecurely attached, N=1,465: God finds the children of broken chains); Samuel and Josiah called directly; Ps 68:5–6 (conditions re-issued to those who inherited none); the grandparent backup channel (survives divorce — the peer-reviewed base for what a Granddaddy's testimony is for); return dynamics (Bengtson's prodigal factors + Wave 9's 21% later-life returners, driven partly by "the desire to pass beliefs to grandchildren"; Luke 15's deposit germinating in famine; Monica/Augustine ~17 years; Isa 55:10–11 → FL.XLVII).
9. **A word to the ones who carry it** — closing by pointing at FL.XXI's Granddaddy note rather than repeating it; Bengtson's own story as the last case (the researcher of prodigals was a prodigal who returned — "the weak link in the chain" who came back singing in the choir).
