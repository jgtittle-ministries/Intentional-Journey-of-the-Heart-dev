# Foundational Laws: Thirteen Operational Laws of Wide Consent

Before I begin the personal explorations that make up the body of this volume, I want to set out thirteen operational laws that are already broadly accepted across mainstream Christian tradition. These are not my discoveries. They are the floor I am building on.

The Explorations that follow this section are my own investigations into territory I have found less well mapped: how faith is generated by hearing, what the structure of a person is, how the Fear of the Lord functions as a gateway, how prayer operates as a resonance phenomenon. Those are propositional claims in the Reasonably Inferred to Clearly Taught tiers. They need testing. The community has not yet weighed in.

The thirteen laws in this section are different. They have been weighed in on for two thousand years. Scripture states them with directional clarity, multiple authors across the Old and New Testaments witness to them, and every mainstream Christian tradition I am aware of accepts the directional relationship even where traditions differ on the mechanism or on the interpretation of the consequent. I am holding each of them in the Clearly Taught tier. Any residual uncertainty reflects the discipline of not claiming more than the texts plainly say.

Why begin here? Two reasons. First, grounding. If a reader is going to follow me into the more exploratory work in the Explorations, they should first see that the same investigative method I use there is applied to material everyone already accepts. If the method handles received laws cleanly, that builds the case for trusting it on the harder ones. Second, taxonomy. These thirteen are operational laws in the same sense as the Obedience Channel and Prayer Resonance are. Placing them first in the volume gives the Operational category an anchor at a confidence level that the Explorations cannot yet reach.

Each of the thirteen follows the same format as the Explorations: a Type tag, the Scriptural Ground, the Mechanism, the Proposed Law statement, and a Certainty rationale. The Type tag for these thirteen reads “Operational Law of Wide Consent” rather than “Operational Law” or “New Discovery” to distinguish them. They are received, not discovered.

Several of the thirteen Foundational Laws reveal features about the shape of the Periodic Table itself. The Sowing-and-Reaping Law (Foundational Law I), the Hear-and-Obey Blessing Law (Foundational Law VI), and the Generosity-Provision Law (Foundational Law IX) do not fit at a single Period — they operate identically at every social scale. The top row of the table is Period O (Scale-Invariant), holding laws of this family. The Confession-Restoration Law (Foundational Law II) and the Heart-Throne Law (Foundational Law III) form a structural pair at the sin-clearance altitude: confession clears the sin act, while the Heart-Throne Law dethrones the false god the sin was serving. The Reciprocal Forgiveness Law (Foundational Law V) and the Confession-Restoration Law (II) also complete mechanisms that the Sin Blockage and Forgiveness-Debt Transfer laws identify on the negative side; together, these laws form positive-and-negative pairs at the channel-opening level. The Drawing-Near Reciprocity Law (Foundational Law VII) is the canonical example of a fourth periodicity — reciprocity-of-response — alongside the three (channel, attention, threshold) already named in this volume. The Desire-for-God Law (Foundational Law VIII) pairs with FL.VII Drawing-Near as its inward-signal complement (the longing is the engine of the seeking) and with FL.III Heart-Throne as its inverse (what the heart was made to want before idolatry redirected it); it also exhibits a designed-orientation pattern that may signal a fifth periodicity (creational-pointer) the catalog should watch for. The Humility-Exaltation Law (Foundational Law IV) is the cleanest example of a law that is bidirectional in its source texts, with humbling and being humbled stated in the same sentence. The Ask-Seek-Knock Law (Foundational Law X) is the threshold form of the prayer mechanism, parent to the Prayer Resonance Law’s resonance dynamics at Period 2. The Renewal-of-Mind Transformation Law (Foundational Law XI) gives the Mind/Will column its highest-confidence anchor and forms a Period 1 / Period 5 parent-child pair with the speculative Attention Economy of the Soul. The Honor-Authority Flourishing Law (Foundational Law XII) is the testing case for the principle-vs-situational-command clause in the inclusion criteria: a specific commandment with promise (Exod. 20:12) whose causal structure generalizes beyond parents to civil and ecclesial authority. The Pure-Heart Vision Law (Foundational Law XIII) is the second Gateway-type entry in the table alongside Fear of the Lord — purity of heart is the prior condition for the seeing of God in both present and eschatological senses. These structural observations are why the Foundational Laws come first: they orient the table before the Explorations populate it.
