# Foundational Laws: Thirty-Three Operational Laws of Wide Consent

Before I begin the personal explorations that make up the body of this volume, I want to set out thirty-three operational laws that are already broadly accepted across mainstream Christian tradition. These are not my discoveries. They are the floor I am building on.

The Explorations that follow this section are my own investigations into territory I have found less well mapped. Those are propositional claims in the Reasonably Inferred to Clearly Taught tiers. They need testing. The community has not yet weighed in.

But these thirty-three are different. Each one has been recognized as foundational across nearly the entire breadth of Christian thought — from the patristic period through the medieval doctors, through the magisterial Reformation, through the modern theological recovery, and into contemporary pastoral practice. They are the operational grammar within which the church's various traditions hold their disagreements about other matters.

I will state each one in a single sentence. The single-sentence statement is deliberate: it forces the law into its most operational form, the form in which the cause-and-effect relationship is plainly named and the principle-level operation is plainly visible.

## The Inclusion Bar

A law qualifies as Foundational only if it clears four criteria. First, *multi-author scriptural support* — the cause-and-effect relationship stated or strongly implied across multiple scriptures from at least two different biblical authors. Second, *broad acceptance* — generally affirmed across mainstream Christian traditions, not contested doctrine. Third, *directionality* — explicitly causal or conditional, not merely descriptive. Fourth, *principle-level* — operating at the level of a governing principle rather than a specific situational instruction, with band-of-closeness articulation (Band 1 clearly-distinguishable principle; Band 2 principle-with-pathway-specified; Band 3 principle-very-close-to-commandment-instantiation with the specific operational forms held tradition-by-tradition) addressing the principle-vs-command edge cases.

The catalog's Group designations (Spirit, Heart, Soul, Mind & Will, Body & Action, Structural Frame) are scripturally grounded at both the individual and corporate scales. The corporate-scale grounding — corporate spirit through the Rev. 1–3 lampstand, corporate heart through Acts 4:32's "one heart" and Ezek. 11:19's God-given new heart, corporate soul through Acts 4:32's "one soul" and Phil. 1:27's "one soul striving together," corporate mind through 1 Cor. 1:10's "same mind" and Phil. 2:5's "mind of Christ," corporate body through the extensive Pauline body-of-Christ material, corporate structural frame through the temple-household-polity articulations — is held in the Vol 5 Periodic Table chapter's body-of-Christ master-frame sub-section at v5_6_0_11. The Group designations operate consistently across the catalog's full scale range.

## The Thirty-Three Laws

The thirty-three Foundational Laws are summarized below in numerical order, with the Period/Group placement, the directionality tag, and the single-sentence operational statement. Each law has its own chapter in this volume.

**FL.I — The Sowing-and-Reaping Law (P0/GV, V).** What is sown is what is reaped (Gal. 6:7–9; Hos. 10:12; 2 Cor. 9:6).

**FL.II — The Confession-Restoration Law (P1/GV, V).** Spoken confession of sin to God produces forgiveness; concealment produces compounding interior load (1 John 1:9; Prov. 28:13; Ps. 32:3–5).

**FL.III — The Heart-Throne Law (P1/GII, B).** What the heart enthrones becomes the structuring center of formation (Matt. 6:21, 6:24; Exod. 20:3).

**FL.IV — The Humility-Exaltation Law (P1/GIII, B).** Humbling before God precedes exaltation; self-exaltation precedes humbling (Jas. 4:10; 1 Pet. 5:6; Luke 14:11; Prov. 3:34).

**FL.V — The Reciprocal Forgiveness Law (P2/GV, H).** Forgiveness extended to others is the condition for forgiveness received from God (Matt. 6:14–15; Matt. 18:21–35; Mark 11:25).

**FL.VI — The Hear-and-Obey Law (P0/GV, V).** Hearing the word produces operational benefit only where the hearing issues in doing (Jas. 1:22–25; Matt. 7:24–27; Luke 11:28).

**FL.VII — The Drawing-Near Reciprocity Law (P1/GI, B).** Drawing near to God produces God's drawing near (Jas. 4:8; Heb. 10:22; Zech. 1:3).

**FL.VIII — The Desire-for-God Law (P1/GII, V).** Sustained desire for God produces increased capacity to receive God (Ps. 42:1–2; Ps. 63:1; Matt. 5:6).

**FL.IX — The Generosity-Provision Law (P1/GV, V).** Generosity produces proportional provision; stinginess produces proportional withholding (Luke 6:38; 2 Cor. 9:6–11; Prov. 11:24–25).

**FL.X — The Faith-Sight Law (P0/GIV, V).** Faith is the operational substance of things hoped for, evidence of things unseen (Heb. 11:1, 6; 2 Cor. 5:7; Rom. 1:17).

**FL.XI — The Renewal-of-Mind Transformation Law (P1/GIV, V).** Renewal of the mind produces transformation of pattern-of-life (Rom. 12:2; Eph. 4:23; Col. 3:10).

**FL.XII — The Honor-Authority Flourishing Law (P2/GV, H).** Honor of legitimate authority produces flourishing in the relational unit (Exod. 20:12; Eph. 6:1–3; Rom. 13:1–7).

**FL.XIII — The Pure-Heart Vision Law (P1/GII, B).** Purity of heart produces operational seeing of God (Matt. 5:8; Ps. 24:3–4; 2 Tim. 2:21–22).

**FL.XIV — The Vanity-of-Substitutes Law (P1/GII, B).** Substitutes for God produce the structural emptiness their idol-status guarantees (Eccl. 1:2; Jer. 2:13; Ps. 115:4–8).

**FL.XV — The Hardening Law (P0/GI, V).** Sustained refusal of God's address produces the heart's operational hardening (Heb. 3:7–15; Rom. 1:18–28; Pharaoh narrative).

**FL.XVI — The Bondage Law (P0/GV, V).** Sustained sin produces operational bondage to the sin (John 8:34; Rom. 6:16; 2 Pet. 2:19).

**FL.XVII — The Substitution-Cascade Law (P4/GII, V, paired bidirectional with FL.XXI).** Idolatry transmits across generations through the household's implicit-learning channel by default (Exod. 20:5–6; Deut. 5:9–10; 2 Kings 17:41).

**FL.XVIII — The Bitter-Root Community Law (P3/GV, H, bidirectional).** Unresolved offense calcifies into a defiling root; Matt. 18 pathway is the corrective (Heb. 12:14–15; Matt. 18:15–20; Eph. 4:26–27).

**FL.XIX — The Spirit Anointing Transmission Law (P4/GI, V).** The Spirit's anointing passes across generations through deliberate impartation (Num. 27:18–23; 2 Kings 2:9–15; 2 Tim. 1:5–7).

**FL.XX — The Gathered-Body Discernment Law (P3/GI, V).** The Holy Spirit speaks distinctively through a gathered body operating in unity (Acts 13:1–3; Acts 15:1–29; Matt. 18:18–20).

**FL.XXI — The Household Formation Law (P4/GII, V, paired bidirectional with FL.XVII).** Faithful covenant-keeping transmits blessing across generations through deliberate teaching (Gen. 12:1–3; Deut. 7:9; 2 Tim. 1:5).

**FL.XXII — The Endurance-Hope Law (P1/GIII, V).** Sustained endurance under God-ward orientation produces character that grounds hope (Rom. 5:3–5; Jas. 1:2–4; 1 Pet. 1:6–7; Heb. 12:1–11).

**FL.XXIII — The Sabbath Rest Law (P0/GVI, V; Band 3).** Structured cessation in trust-of-provision produces flourishing; absence produces compounding diminishment (Gen. 2:2–3; Exod. 20:8–11; Mark 2:23–28; Heb. 4:1–11).

**FL.XXIV — The Confession-in-Community Law (P3/GV, B).** Spoken confession in trusted community produces healing solitary confession does not produce (Jas. 5:16; Prov. 28:13; 1 John 1:9).

**FL.XXV — The Restoration-of-the-Erring Law (P3/GV, H).** Active pursuit of the wanderer in gentleness restores to communion (Gal. 6:1; Jas. 5:19–20; Matt. 18:12–14; Luke 15).

**FL.XXVI — The Doctrinal Calcification Law (P4/GIV, V).** Traditions transmitted without disciplines of return-to-scripture calcify into substitutes for the truth (Mark 7:8–13; Col. 2:8; 2 Tim. 4:3–4; Jude 3; Isa. 29:13).

**FL.XXVII — The Thick Practice Transmission Law (P4/GV, V).** Embedded practices in regular rhythm transmit substance deliberate teaching alone cannot convey (Deut. 6:4–9; Exod. 12; Josh. 4; 1 Cor. 11:23–26; Acts 2:42).

**FL.XXVIII — The Generational Nested Structure Law (P4/GVI, V).** Formation operates within a nested architecture; integrity at each scale is the operational condition for the transmission mechanisms (Deut. 4:9–10; Ps. 78; Eph. 3:14–15; Heb. 11–12).

**FL.XXIX — The Corporate Emotional Integration Law (P3/GIII, B).** The corporate soul integrates shared experience through gathered community's collective engagement; suffering-together and rejoicing-together are scripturally co-articulated (1 Cor. 12:26; Rom. 12:15; Acts 4:23–31; Neh. 8–9; Ps. lament-and-praise corpus; Heb. 10:24–25; John 11:33–36).

**FL.XXX — The Communal Soul-Care for the Wounded Law (P3/GIII, H).** The gathered community holds the integration the soul-wounded member cannot accomplish alone, sustained over the duration the wounding requires (Acts 9:9–25; 2 Cor. 1:3–7; Jas. 5:13–16; Gal. 6:2; Mark 2:1–12; Job 2:11–13).

**FL.XXXI — The Corporate Scriptural Reception Law (P3/GIV, V).** A gathered community's collective reception of scriptural address produces operational reception that solitary reading does not (Deut. 31:9–13; Neh. 8:1–12; Acts 2:42; 1 Tim. 4:13; Col. 3:16; Rev. 1:3; Luke 4:16–21).

**FL.XXXII — The Communal Worship Heart-Alignment Law (P3/GII, B).** A gathered community's collective worship operates as the structural form of the corporate-heart's God-ward alignment, with constitutive-bidirectional mutual constitution paralleling FL.III at the corporate-heart scale (Ps. 22:22–25; Ps. 100; Heb. 12:22–24; Heb. 10:24–25; Acts 2:46–47; Rev. 4–5; Rom. 15:5–6).

**FL.XXXIII — The Community Polity Structure / Ecclesial Architecture Law (P3/GVI, V; Band 3).** Some polity-structure operates as the architectural framework within which the gathered community's other operations are conducted; the framework is Christ-given, operates for the body's building-up, and operates within eschatological horizon; the specific polity-form (episcopal, presbyterian, congregational, etc.) is tradition-by-tradition theological work (Acts 6:1–7; Acts 14:23; Acts 15; Acts 20:28; 1 Tim. 3; Titus 1:5–9; Eph. 4:11–13; 1 Cor. 12:27–31; Heb. 13:7, 17; 1 Pet. 5:1–4). *New at v5_6_0_26.*
