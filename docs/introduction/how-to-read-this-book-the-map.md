# How to Read This Book: The Country of the Heart

This is a big book — six volumes, forty-eight laws, a good deal of machinery. But before any of that, I want to hand you the whole of it on a single page. Everything in these volumes is a closer look at one map: the country of the heart, and how a heart grows toward God. If you keep this one picture in your head, you will never be lost in the rest of it.

![The Country of the Heart — the whole book on one map. Grace comes down from outside the map as the Father's free gift; a central engine of hearing, trusting, and obeying carries the heart toward Home, which is becoming like Christ; some places are pits, like a hardened heart, that trap you and need help from outside; and a legend gives four ways to read the map.](images/country-of-the-heart-map.svg)

## The whole book in five sentences

If you remember nothing else, remember these. They are enough to live on, and everything else is only detail:

1. **This is a map of how your heart grows toward God.**
2. **Everything on it slopes toward one Home — becoming like Jesus.**
3. **At the center is an engine:** you *hear* God, you *trust* Him, you *obey* — and obeying opens you to hear more, round and round, and it carries you toward Home.
4. **Some places are pits** — a hardened heart, a sin you are stuck in — and you cannot climb out alone; you need a hand from outside.
5. **The most important thing is not machinery at all:** grace falls from *outside* the map, because God is not a system you operate — He is a Father who freely gives.

## Four ways to read the one map

You never need all four at once — a child can read the terrain; a scholar can read the grid. But the same picture holds all four:

1. **The grid → *where* a law lives.** Every law has an address, the way a spot on a map has a grid reference. That address (its scale, and which part of you it works on) is what the Periodic Table lays out in full.
2. **The hills and pits → its *shape*.** Some laws are high ground that carries you along; some are pits that trap. The shape tells you whether a law is helping you home or holding you back.
3. **The arrows → *how* the laws move together.** No law works alone. The arrows show the currents — how hearing leads to trusting, trusting to obeying, obeying back to hearing.
4. **The colour → *what kind* of law it is.** Whether it names how things are built, how they move, or how they are healed.

## The rules of the whole country

A few things are true of the *entire* map — the deep truths that govern every law on it. In plain words:

- **Grace comes from outside.** God's free giving powers everything, and it is never a lever you pull. However well you walk every path, His nearness is a gift, not a wage. *There is no mechanism; there is a Father.*
- **Every good road has a shadow road.** The same path walked toward God builds you up; walked toward a substitute, it hollows you out. Drawing near, or hardening. Worship, or idolatry. One road, two directions.
- **The same law shows up at every size.** What is true in you is true in your family, your small group, your church, across the generations. The map zooms in and out, and the shapes hold.
- **The traps need outside help.** Some pits cannot be escaped from the inside. That is not a failure in you; it is the design that keeps us leaning on Him.

## How to use this map

Start here, then go where you need:

- **If you are weary or dry,** read [If You Come Weary, Read This First](if-you-come-weary-read-this-first.md) before anything else. The map will keep.
- **For the whole story the map sits inside** — creation, fall, redemption, the wedding feast at the end — see [The Larger Story](../volume-2-knowing-to-doing/larger-story-where-this-volume-sits.md).
- **For the two deeper maps this one grew out of:** [The Grammar of Creation](../volume-3-quantitative-framework/the-grammar-of-creation.md) (why the world is law-shaped at all) and [The Role Atlas](../volume-3-quantitative-framework/the-role-atlas-where-each-law-lives.md) (where each law lives in the field). This front map is those two, told simply.
- **When you meet a hard chapter,** come back here. Find where it sits on the map — which part of the country you are in, and which way it flows — and it will make sense again.

The map is the doorway. The laws are the rooms. Take your time; the Father is not in a hurry with you.

***A word from your Granddaddy.*** *I made this map for you the way I wish someone had made one for me — so you could see the whole shape of it before the details ever crowded in. Hold the five sentences close. Everything else in these volumes is just me walking you slowly around the country, one place at a time. And remember the rule that matters most: the map only ever shows how His world tends to run. It never measures how loved you are. That was settled at the cross, before you read a word.*
