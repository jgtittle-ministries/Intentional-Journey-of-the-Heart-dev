# The Four Foundational Principles

Before I get into any specific explorations, I want to lay out the four principles I treat as axioms for this investigation. I borrowed the axiom language from mathematics — just as Euclidean geometry builds outward from a small set of starting assumptions, I am working from four starting assumptions that I hold with roughly Clearly Taught certainty. Further exploration may refine or deepen these, just as Einstein’s work refined Newton without discarding him. That is fine with me. These are working axioms, not dogma.

## The ground the four stand on

Before the four, one thing I have assumed on every page of this work and never said out loud. I hold that Scripture is true and reliable: the norming norm, as the Reformers called it, the rule that measures every other rule, including every law in these volumes (Ps. 19:7–9; Ps. 119:160; Isa. 40:8; John 17:17; 2 Tim. 3:16–17). It is not a fifth axiom. I hold the four below as working assumptions I expect to refine, and I am not willing to refine this one. It carries no confidence tier, because it is the thing the tiers are measured against. Every test I use assumes it: the first of my four tests for a law is "does it line up with Scripture," Clearly Taught is defined as what Scripture plainly teaches, and the five tests of the Scripture-Grounding Standard in Volume 6 are all tests of how faithfully a chapter handles a text that is presumed true. So I name it here once, plainly. Where a law of mine and the Word disagree, the law is wrong.

With it comes its twin, which I also hold and also will not argue: the God who spoke in Scripture still speaks, to His children, now, never against what He has written, and He can be heard (John 10:27; John 16:13; Heb. 1:1–2; Isa. 55:10–11; Rom. 10:17). Take that away and there is no engine in the middle of the map, and nothing in Volume 2 to clear. I know that some traditions I love and learned from hold this more narrowly than I do. I say it so you know where I stand, and so you can test everything that follows against it.

Two things I am not claiming. I am not claiming a hermeneutic. How I read is disciplined by the Scripture-Grounding Standard, and I can be wrong in my reading, and the tiers exist to say how sure I am of a reading, not of the text. And I am not claiming a doctrine of inspiration in any one tradition's form; this work belongs to none of them. I am claiming trust. The Word is true, it is reliable, it does its work, and it outranks me.


I want to register a caution about my own title before you go further. C.S. Lewis used legal language brilliantly in Mere Christianity to point toward God — but he was careful to note that once you arrive at God via the law, you discover you have not arrived at a lawful system. You have arrived at a Person who wants you as a child, not as a subject. The laws I am describing in these volumes are real and discoverable and operable — but they are the structural features of a love relationship, not a spiritual technology to be mastered. A person could learn all forty-eight Foundational Laws in Volume 1, execute the training plan in Volume 2 with discipline and precision, and still have missed the entire point if they have pursued the laws rather than the Lawgiver. I keep this caveat close because I know my own tendency — the engineer’s tendency — to love the system more than the source. The laws are meant to get you closer to Him, not to substitute for Him.

**1. Laws of the Spirit exist. **The spiritual world operates according to regular, predictable, lawful relationships — as orderly as the physical world, because the same God created both.

2.	**These Laws are knowable. **We will not see all of them, but we should be able to see more than we do today. The wind blows where it wishes — but we should be able to see more than just the sound of it passing.

3.	**These Laws are knowable in all three “worlds” simultaneously **— in my spirit, in my mind and emotions, and in my body and actions. If I have truly grasped a law of the Spirit, my doing will change. Otherwise, I have only memorized it, which is different. Said another way, if I am not changed by God’s word, then I haven’t yet heard God’s word.

4.	**We can operate in these Laws.** This is not merely an intellectual exercise. The goal is to live closer to the life Jesus modeled — where miracles are not the exception but the expected fruit of operating in alignment with how God’s world actually works.
