# What I Found: The Life Jesus Invites Us Into

For over two decades one question has driven me: *what if the world of the Spirit works in a way that is just as real and discoverable as the laws of physics?* Not identical to physics — but every bit as orderly, every bit as real, and every bit as open to someone willing to pay attention.

That question is not arrogant. It is the opposite. Jesus rebuked Nicodemus in John 3 — not for a lack of devotion, because Nicodemus was a serious student of Scripture, but because, as a teacher of Israel, he should have understood how things work in the Spirit, and he didn't. *"Are you the teacher of Israel, and yet you do not understand these things?"* That stings. And it is the honest starting point of this whole work: if the teacher of Israel should have seen more, then surely we — with the full canon, the indwelling Holy Spirit, and two thousand years of the church behind us — can see deeper than we usually do. We live so far below the life Jesus actually invites us into: a life of walking in the Spirit, of miracles in the natural, of intimate conversation with Him.

So here, in four plain steps, is the life I went looking for — and what I found.

**Why it is worth wanting.** Jesus keeps inviting us deeper — the way He kept inviting His disciples, who kept missing it — into a life lived *with* Him and *in* Him: a miracle life, where His hand at work becomes an ordinary, everyday thing. What is at stake is not just knowing *about* God — it is walking and talking with Him, close.

**What the life is like.** It is not a place you arrive; it is a walk — step by step, deeper into knowing Jesus. And the wonders along the way are not random. They follow God's deeper design, the way creation follows the laws of nature — His ways are simply the larger, truer set. To see more and live deeper is to learn how His world really works.

**How you actually walk it.** You learn to hear His voice and do what He says — better, and quicker. That is the engine of the whole thing: hearing Him grows your faith (Romans 10:17), and without faith we cannot please Him (Hebrews 11:6) — so every time you hear and obey, your trust grows and carries you further in. And because it helps to know where you stand, this work lays out a simple map of the growth — one you can walk on your own, and walk together in a small group.

**What it is all for.** A life *in Him* — lit up, doing the very thing you were created to do (Ephesians 2:10). Not a heavier load of religion — the joy of finally being who He made you to be.

The one thread through all of it: **hear His voice, and obey it.**

---

A caution I keep close, and offer to you. The goal of mapping this life is never to replace the living of it. C. S. Lewis said the person who has only a map of the Atlantic has something useful — but the person who has sailed it has something the map can never give from the paper. Everything in these volumes is my best map of water I have been crossing for over forty years. The map is here to help you find your way in — not to stand in for the crossing. Pursue the Lord, not the laws. The laws are only ever meant to bring you closer to Him.
