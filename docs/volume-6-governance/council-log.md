## Toward 6 December 2026 — the draft agenda, held here until the meeting

*This paragraph holds agenda items as they gather during the month, so that the agenda is the Council's and not mine alone. It is a draft, not a record: the meeting's own entry replaces it. November is skipped, since its first Sunday fell only a week after the October meeting. The first three items carry over from 25 October, which did not reach them.*

1. **The briefing's next doors** (John, 22 September; counsel). The church-authority briefing, now in a second edition draft — the running pilot, the published warrant, and an open invitation to come sit in an evening — has a target map filed with the packet: the CCA conversation, Living Hope, our own congregations, the home group, and the relationship-first names. Dave is already in process toward some in his church; Barry may consider his. I would value counsel on the map and on who is missing from it.

2. **The Seeing Round** (John, 28 September; counsel, and an invitation). A practice drafted this week for Connect with Others: fifteen minutes in which a room says aloud what it has seen in each other, in two rounds and two sentences — "What I see in you that I wish for myself…" and "What I've seen you do that I don't think you noticed…". Everyone gives twice and receives twice, to the left and then to the right, so no one chooses whom to affirm; the only answer is thank you. It is aimed at the Quarter 2 close of the evening series, where there will be enough shared history to make it worth anything, and it is tested first with the leading team. I would welcome counsel — and since we meet as a Fellowship of the Heart, forming a container and working within it, we may wish to run it ourselves rather than only talk about it.

3. **Laws from a friend's book** (John, 14 October; asks and counsel). Terry Allen's *Who Do You Think You Are?* went through the law-catalog test, with the effect on Volumes 2 and 3 read alongside. I am asking the Council to admit three laws — Image-Dignity, Faithfulness-Entrustment, and Comparison-Envy — and seeking counsel on what the test showed about the table: a direction for the adversary, a horizon for laws whose effect comes later, and a name for FL.XLVIII that matches what it holds. A fourth candidate, Foothold-Resistance, waits on item 13. The same test on Dave Smith's *Relational Discipleship*, partly this work's own source, adds three more for the Council's word: Log-First, Comfort-Transmission, and Willing-Heart.

4. **Spiritual authority — the open question** (standing, from 25 October). A Fellowship of the Heart needs both a Formation Companion and organizational authority, with cross-checks and accountability. I need a scriptural companion who can challenge me from Scripture: not as an authority, but as a call to back up and rethink. How to build that out of people is still open.

5. **The personal leadership process** (John, 26 October; for review and discussion). The process I walked the Council through on 25 October, written up as a described process with my own results as the worked example. The write-up went to the Council on 27 October, and we look at it together at this meeting. There is more to do on the process itself.

6. **How would we tell it is working?** (John, 27 October; a report, and counsel). On 26 October I asked the teens how they would tell whether internalization is working. Their answers did not quite reach it, and we did not get to the group question at all. After some back and forth, I put one measure in front of them: when life interrupts the practice, do you return to it? Being turned the wrong way is not a great distance; it is a pivot in place. You stop, turn, and seek Jesus. This is a first word, not a definitive one. On 10 November I ask the same question of the group: how would we tell whether we are internalizing it together? I will bring what comes of both.

Members add items through the month as they think of them; they appear here as they arrive.

## 25 October 2026 — Council Meeting 3

**Present:** Barry Boyle, Dave Smith, Epi, John Tittle. Face to face.

**How we met.** We began by forming a container with the Fellowship of the Heart protocol, this time in pairs, face to face. Each of us said to the other, in our own words, what safe, present, clear, and intentional mean. It made the four personal; I came away understanding each of them in a new way, and I think it is a good alternative to the usual form. We worked from the [working agenda](https://github.com/jgtittle-ministries/Intentional-Journey-of-the-Heart-dev/blob/0574756/_implementation-notes/council-meeting-2026-10-25/agenda-items.md), and the discussion ran where it needed to. Items 5 and 11 became one conversation. We did not reach items 9, 10, or 12.

**Asked, and answered.**

1. **The held asks of 6 September: approved.** Ask 3, the [two foundation inserts](https://github.com/jgtittle-ministries/Intentional-Journey-of-the-Heart-dev/blob/0574756/_implementation-notes/council-meeting-2026-09-06/draft-foundations-inserts.md), with the theory-of-action sentence as I refined it on 8 September. Ask 5, the hear-and-obey traceability study, promoted to the Research Register. Ask 6, A34, The Throne Question. I asked whether we were good on all three, and the Council agreed. All three will be published.
2. **The theory of action: approved.** I read the theory-of-action sentence, and we talked it through. It explains the how in order to come to the why. The work stays the Holy Spirit's; we open the door and invite Him to do it. And where nothing seems to happen, that is the place to go deeper in faith. When I asked, the Council agreed. With it, [Part I of the placements](https://github.com/jgtittle-ministries/Intentional-Journey-of-the-Heart-dev/blob/0574756/_implementation-notes/council-meeting-2026-10-25/draft-theory-of-action-placements.md), the Volume 4 research questions and the pointer in the Note on Proof, was approved along with ask 3. The Council accepted the theory of action as a defining foundational thought of the work. Part II, the Fellowship of the Heart placements, was before the Council for counsel only.
3. **Our common practice: decided.** It came out of the opening round, in which each of us said what we do daily that draws us deeper into God. Three of us already keep a standing morning practice. One of us adds something the rest of us did not have. He takes one word from his morning reflection, carries a smooth stone all day to remind him of it, and in the evening reflects on the word and what it did to his day. I am taking it up, and the others may. The pattern is simple. In the morning, whatever fits: reflection, listening, journaling, reading, meditation. During the day, some reminder to pay attention. In the evening, a reflection. This replaces the draft Rule of Life in Part 2, which none of us was using. Part 2 has been revised to say so.
4. **A standing rule for asks: approved.** I proposed that when I put an ask to the Council and 30 days pass with no comment, it is accepted. One of us first held that an ask needs a comment of acceptance. I answered that we can always back up if we need to, and the rule was approved.

**Counsel.**

- **The Fellowship of the Heart relaunch shapes** were discussed and accepted. (The decisions on the Fellowship of the Heart are mine; the Council's counsel stands behind them.)
- **Spiritual authority, and the direction circle.** I brought this as an ask for counsel. We talked about our own leadership, and about what I am actually looking for. I do not need classic spiritual direction, and the term has not landed with the men I have asked. I need a scriptural companion who can challenge me from Scripture. I don't take the challenge as authoritative, but as a call to back up and rethink. The shape I have in mind is four men, each willing to sit with me once a year, so that one of them does every quarter. I also walked the Council through the personal leadership process I developed. It sets down my intention, my energy, and my focus, so it gives a scriptural companion a reference point to ask me: am I really doing what I say matters to me? That can be the pivotal feedback on whether I am doing what God has called, equipped, and energized me to do. We distinguished direction for those who lead groups from direction for the one who leads the organization. Epi offered to serve as the financial auditor of the work, to keep it honest. Asked how a church built on this work is kept from a bad leader who bends it to his agenda, we answered with the Council's own governance and with oversight of the money. We came to a shared understanding that a Fellowship of the Heart needs both a Formation Companion and organizational authority, with cross-checks and accountability. That is hard, because it is made of people. The question remains open. For the role itself, I will use the name that was suggested: scriptural companion.
- **The articles, and the book.** Good back and forth on how the book, the foundation article, and the follow-on articles fit together. Where I have landed: every Fellowship of the Heart is an experiment, because each is a unique group of people, and pastoral needs come before academic rigor. So the articles report from the middle of an ongoing experiment. For the evening series, my hypothesis is that adult processes can be simplified so that teens can use them and teach them, and that over seventeen evenings each will move to a greater internalization that can be observed. A book from this work is now in view, probably a five-year project.
- **The Crucible Project.** Dave gave us a full summary of the weekend. He is discerning what to do next.
- **Spiritual warfare.** The Council agreed that praying in the Spirit can be part of spiritual warfare. Dave pointed us to Paul Billheimer's *Destined for the Throne* (1975), on the scriptural basis of our authority in Christ. The spiritual-warfare additions I published on 19 October, ahead of this meeting, were confirmed as they stand.
- **How this work is recorded.** Every change to this work is made with an AI assistant and recorded with the reason for it. What research of this kind usually loses is not what changed but why; here the why is kept. For a project like this one, I think that is a new kind of research record.

**Who carries what.** I ask the teens at the next evening, 26 October, how they would tell whether the series is working. I send the Council the draft of my personal leadership process. Dave sends the name of the book (done, 26 October). Epi sends his summary of the meeting, made from its recording. Epi's audit offer stands; how it works is for a later meeting.

**Not reached.** Item 9, the briefing's next doors; item 10, the Seeing Round; item 12, the laws from Terry Allen's and Dave Smith's books. All three carry to the next meeting.

**Dissent.** None recorded.

**Going forward.** We skip November, since its first Sunday falls only a week after this meeting. Next meeting: Sunday 6 December 2026.
