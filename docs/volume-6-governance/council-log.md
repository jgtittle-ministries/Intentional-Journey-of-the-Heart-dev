## 6 September 2026 — Council Meeting 2

**Present:** Barry Boyle, Dave Smith, John Tittle. Face to face.

**How we met.** We began by forming a container, using the protocol developed for the Fellowship of the Heart, and it landed well. The [packet](https://github.com/jgtittle-ministries/Intentional-Journey-of-the-Heart-dev/blob/9286a6e/_implementation-notes/council-meeting-2026-09-06/packet-contents.md) served as the agenda and we took it in order: the spiritual-authority question, the record of 28 June, the signing, and then the asks. The meeting was a working session from beginning to end. Every item was questioned and discussed, insights were offered and weighed, and nothing below was approved without being examined first.

**Spiritual authority.** Counsel, not decision. The Council heard the four candidates in my order of preference and approved proceeding stepwise. I have already spoken with Bill Fairback and Ryan Hammond, elders at Living Hope. I asked each to serve as a spiritual director for me, and asked Ryan Hammond to be the spiritual authority over a Fellowship of the Heart at Living Hope; both promised an answer within two weeks. If that covering is granted, the next ask is a covering over this work itself. In parallel, I will ask Andrea Sponsler, the head of school at Covenant Christian Academy, whether a CCA club could meet in the evenings at my home, since I cannot be away from the house in the evenings. The Crucible Project has a path of its own: Dave Smith has connected with Tom Wooten, its director, and will explore the possibilities when they meet later in October. AJ McGraw remains open for later. Barry Boyle observed that too much taken on at once could be more than can be handled. The Council had no other suggestions and approved the steps.

**The record of 28 June.** No minutes exist. The Council confirmed my recollection as set out in the [changes note](https://github.com/jgtittle-ministries/Intentional-Journey-of-the-Heart-dev/blob/9286a6e/_implementation-notes/council-meeting-2026-09-06/changes-note-2026-09-06.md): on 28 June the Council approved by consent the two new Foundational Laws, FL.XLVII and FL.XLVIII, Volume 4's *Before You Measure*, and the Formation Companion's credentialing limit; the Meta-Law Layer was not discussed. That confirmation stands as the June record.

**The signing.** I re-signed the [Succession Letter](https://github.com/jgtittle-ministries/Intentional-Journey-of-the-Heart-dev/blob/9286a6e/docs/volume-6-governance/part-3-succession-letter.md) before the Council, dated 6 September 2026, witnessed by Barry Boyle and Dave Smith. David R. Smith is Theological Successor; John David Tittle is Literary Executor. The signed original is kept with my estate papers, and copies go to each named successor as the letter provides.
