## 6 September 2026 — Council Meeting 2

**Present:** Barry Boyle, Dave Smith, John Tittle. Face to face.

**How we met.** We began by forming a container, using the protocol developed for the Fellowship of the Heart, and it landed well. The meeting was a working session from beginning to end. Every item was questioned and discussed, insights were offered and weighed, and nothing below was approved without being examined first.

**Spiritual authority.** Counsel, not decision. The Council heard the four candidates in my order of preference and approved proceeding stepwise. I have already spoken with Bill Fairback and Ryan Hammond, elders at Living Hope.
