# Cultivating Spiritual Maturity in Hearing God

The full paper — *Cultivating Spiritual Maturity in Hearing God: An Affective-Domain Pedagogy of Formation for Individuals and Small Groups* — is the prescriptive member of the **Hearing-God Papers** and is available here [(pdf)](source-pdfs/csm-cultivating-spiritual-maturity-in-hearing-god.pdf){: .pdf-popup data-pdf-label="CSM — Cultivating Spiritual Maturity in Hearing God" }. The summary below names what the paper does, why it matters to the LotS project, and how it is used across the volumes.

## What CSM Argues

Where [MSM](measuring-spiritual-maturity-in-hearing-god.md) diagnoses *where* a heart sits in the Affective Taxonomy and *why* instruments cannot see the higher levels, CSM turns from diagnosis to cultivation. Its thesis is that **the same taxonomy that diagnoses also prescribes**: each transition between levels has a characteristic *barrier*, and therefore a characteristic *intervention* — so that the failure of much formation is not too little effort but effort applied at the wrong transition. The formational rule that answers MSM's "match the instrument to the level" is **"match the intervention to the transition."**

The pedagogy is transition-by-transition: fallow-ground **plowing** (toward Receiving), **rhythm**-building (Receiving into Responding), the **watering of desire** (the decisive Responding-to-Valuing hinge, which cannot be forced), the **weeding** of disordered loves (Augustine's *ordo amoris*, into Organization), and patient **waiting** for the harvest (into Characterization). Two scriptural anchors discipline the whole: the parable of the seed growing secretly (Mark 4:26–29, "the earth produces by itself… he knows not how") and Paul's "I planted, Apollos watered, but God gave the growth" (1 Cor 3:6–7). The paper relocates the church's formative power in the historic **means of grace** and the doctrine of **secondary causes** — the means are efficacious instruments precisely because God ordinarily works through them, so the taxonomy adds no new mechanism, only a map. It draws the reformed, evangelical, charismatic, and mystical traditions into a single **formational toolkit** (each supplies the intervention another lacks at its plateau) and extends the pedagogy to the small-group scale, where the leader is a deliberately receding presence.

## How CSM Is Used in the LotS Project

CSM is the cultivation backbone of the Hearing-God arc and the prescriptive complement to MSM. Its transition pedagogy is grounded in, and feeds back into, the diagnostic and developmental work of Volume 2 — the heart-soil mapping of [V2.Exp1](../volume-2-knowing-to-doing/exploration-01-heart-soil.md), the hearing-journey taxonomy of [V2.Exp7](../volume-2-knowing-to-doing/exploration-07-hearing-with-understanding.md), and the [Four Connects](../volume-2-knowing-to-doing/exploration-05-four-connects.md). Its two halves are developed at length in the applied papers: the lower three transitions in [CTG](clearing-the-ground-lower-levels.md) and the upper two in [BFP](bearing-fruit-with-patience-upper-levels.md).

!!! note "Integration, not replacement"

    CSM's pedagogy is governed by the integration-not-replacement reading ([V2.Exp7](../volume-2-knowing-to-doing/exploration-07-hearing-with-understanding.md#reading-these-levels-as-integration-not-replacement)): the goal is never to push a person up a ladder but to keep every region of the heart open as it matures. The cultivation tends conditions; the Spirit gives the growth.
