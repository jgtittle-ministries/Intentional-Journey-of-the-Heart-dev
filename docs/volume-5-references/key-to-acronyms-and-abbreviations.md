# Key to Acronyms & Abbreviations

*A single reference for the acronyms, short-forms, and notation used across the six volumes. Most are expanded on first use within a chapter; this key gathers them in one place so any term can be looked up cold. It is grouped by kind — the Formation Documents, the frameworks and source models they draw on, the tools and practices, the Volume 4 testing instruments, and the project's own structural notation. For the fuller treatment of the structural notation (law types, certainty tiers, the Periodic Table's Periods and Groups), see each volume's **Taxonomy Key**.*

## Frameworks & Source Models

Conceptual models the corpus draws on from outside.

| Acronym | Stands for | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **AT** | Affective Taxonomy | Krathwohl, Bloom & Masia's five-level internalization model (Receiving → Responding → Valuing → Organization → Characterization); the corpus's core developmental spine. |
| **VIM** | Vision, Intention, Means | Dallas Willard's account of how transformation actually proceeds. |
| **QFT** | Quantum Field Theory | The physics whose interpretive *posture* (not its predictions) Volume 3 borrows by analogy. |
| **NCD** | Natural Church Development | Schwarz's church-health assessment approach; a measurement reference. |
| **REVEAL** | the REVEAL study | Willow Creek's spiritual-growth study; a measurement reference. |
| **MOVE** | the MOVE study | Hawkins & Parkinson's (2011) follow-on study; a construct-validity anchor in Volume 4. |
| **SISRI** | Spiritual Intelligence Self-Report Inventory | King's (2008) spiritual-intelligence measure. |
| **OCAI** | Organizational Culture Assessment Instrument | Cameron & Quinn's forced-choice instrument whose structure the Volume 4 SIP adapts. |

## Project & Structural Notation

The corpus's own short-forms. For the fuller treatment of law types, certainty tiers, and the Periodic Table axes, see each volume's **Taxonomy Key**.

| Short-form | Means | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **IJH** | Intentional Journey of the Heart | This project and book. |
| **FotH** | Fellowship of the Heart | The community of practice carrying the work forward (the pilot's living form). |
| **BoB** | Band of Brothers | The earlier men's-group form from which FotH grew. |
| **BSCP** | Balanced Scorecard Process | The author's consulting methodology, cited as a forerunner in Volume 1. |
| **CCA** | Covenant Christian Academy | Warrenton, VA; host of the first Fellowship of the Heart pilot cohort. |
| **FL** | Foundational Law | A numbered law of Volume 1 (e.g., FL.XIX = the nineteenth). |
| **V1 / V2 / V3 …** | Volume 1 / 2 / 3 … | Cross-volume references — e.g., V2.Exp7 = Volume 2, Exploration 7. |
| **Exp** | Exploration | A chapter within a volume. |
| **A## / B##** | protocol codes | Catalogued operational protocols (Vol 5, Part VI) — e.g., A25 Breaking the Contracts, B14 Kinesthetic Metaphor. |
| **CT / RI** | Clearly Taught / Reasonably Inferred | Two of the three certainty tiers (the third is **Speculative**); see the Taxonomy Keys and the [Scripture-Grounding Standard](../volume-6-governance/scripture-grounding-standard.md). |
| **P0–P5** | Periods (scale of operation) | The Periodic Table's vertical axis, from scale-invariant (P0) to cosmic-eschatological (P5). |
| **GI–GVI** | Groups (dimension of the person) | The Periodic Table's horizontal axis (which dimension of the person a law primarily addresses). |
| **ESV** | English Standard Version | The Bible translation quoted throughout. |
