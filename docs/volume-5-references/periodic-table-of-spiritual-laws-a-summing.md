# The Periodic Table of Spiritual Laws — A Summing and Organizing Reference

This chapter is the consolidated Periodic Table reference for the entire Laws-of-the-Spirit investigation. It was originally drafted as the closing chapter of Volume 1 and now lives here in Volume 5, alongside the Tool Inventory and the Companion-formation chapters, because the table's primary function is reference-and-orientation across the whole corpus rather than further argument within any single volume. Volume 1 introduces the Foundational Laws as a tier ahead of its eight Explorations; Volume 2 maps the Diagnostic, Tool-Application, and Developmental laws that operate downstream of the Vol 1 framework; Volume 3 reframes both sets in field-theoretic terms; and this table is the consolidated organizer that holds all three views in one frame so a Companion in training or in formation work can find any specific law by its place in the structure.

The Periodic Table is not merely a summary. It is itself an analytical instrument that sums (every analyzed law arrayed in one frame), organizes (by the two axes that the investigation has earned the right to use — Period 0–5 for scale of operation and Group I–VI for dimension of the person primarily addressed), and reveals (gaps where a law must exist but has not yet been found; family resemblances among laws sharing a Period or Group; periodicities that recur across scales and dimensions). The chapter retains its original Vol 1 structure for that reason: the Two Axes section explains the analytical instrument's shape; What the Shape Reveals is the insights section that surfaces the patterns the table makes visible; the Slim Format and Reference List are the consolidated data; the Six Group Families closes structurally; and the Six Priority Open Unknowns is the suggested-actions section that names what the table tells the corpus to look for next. Vol 5 v5_6_0_4 added a sixth subsection — Reading the Table Through Classical Doctrine — that applies a second analytical lens to the consolidated set. Vol 5 v5_6_0_5 (the present revision) adds a seventh subsection — The Third-Axis Test and Mirror Discipline — that documents the test of an orientation-of-pursuit candidate third axis and the implementation that followed from its findings; the test added four new Foundational Laws (FL.XIV–FL.XVII) and one new Speculative entry (Counter-Resonance), and established Mirror Discipline as a required field on every entry going forward.

The table currently holds 33 analyzed laws drawn from across the three primary volumes and the associated formation documents, including 17 Foundational Laws of wide consent (FL.I–XVII), 10 speculative extensions marked with ◆, 5 open unknowns marked [ OPEN UNKNOWN ] (down from 6 in the previous version — the Household Formation Law's idol-ward face has now been articulated as FL.XVII Substitution-Cascade, while the Household Formation Law itself remains Open), 1 anomaly, and 6 forward-references to Volume 3's field-theoretic appendix. Confidence is expressed in qualitative tiers, not statistical probabilities. The three tiers Vol 1 defines — Clearly Taught (taught directly by scripture or confirmed by community testing), Reasonably Inferred (reasonably inferred from scripture, still in need of community testing), and Speculative (interesting enough to pursue, not yet solid enough to build on) — do the actual epistemic work; see Vol 1's tier description for the full definitions. Volume 1 v5_6_0_19 added four new Foundational Laws since the previous version of this table (FL.XIV Vanity-of-Substitutes Law, FL.XV Hardening Law, FL.XVI Bondage Law, FL.XVII Substitution-Cascade Law), and the table is synced to current Vol 1 numbering as of this volume's v5_6_0_5.

I want to explain the reasoning behind this structure before I show you the table itself — because the structure is a hypothesis, not a presentation. If I have chosen the wrong axes, the table will look tidy, but it won't teach anything new. If I have chosen the right ones, it will show us where the investigation is dense, where it is sparse, and most importantly, **where there are laws that must exist but that we have not yet found**. Those gaps are the most valuable thing the table can produce.

The Periodic Table of Elements was a scientific breakthrough, not because Mendeleev was a better cataloguer than his contemporaries, but because he noticed something his contemporaries missed: *periodicity*. The same chemical behavior — the same valence, the same reactivity — kept reappearing at regular intervals as atomic number increased. Organizing by that repeating pattern revealed a structure that **predicted the existence and properties of undiscovered elements**. The gaps in the table were not failures. They were research assignments.

I believe the Laws of the Spirit have a periodicity. The same underlying mechanism keeps reappearing at different scales and in different dimensions of the person. Let me name three instances before I defend my axis choices.

**The channel-opening / channel-blocking mechanism** appears at the individual level (Obedience Channel, Sin Blockage, Forgiveness-Debt Transfer, and now FL.XV Hardening Law as a wider-scope channel-blocking law), at the small group level (Container Law, Community Amplification's gnostic warning), and — speculatively — at the generational level (Generational Transmission). Same mechanism, different scale. A law governing channel dynamics at the congregational level almost certainly exists, but we have not found it yet.

**The attention-conformity mechanism** appears in the soul's movement toward its sustained object of attention (the Attention Economy of the Soul), in the Faith-Hope-Love triad's reinforcing loop, and in the Worship Alignment Law's claim about perception recalibration. Same underlying dynamic: what you direct sustained attention toward, you move toward and are changed by.

**The threshold / gateway structure** appears in the Fear of the Lord as the entry condition for the Wisdom cluster, in the Affective Level 2→3 transition as the critical formation threshold, in regeneration as the entry condition for spirit formation, and in the Miracle Threshold Events model. Each is logically prior to what follows it; each requires a specific condition to be met before the next stage becomes available.

These repetitions are the periodicity. The table is built around them. Also note that the elements are drawn from all three volumes of this IJH work.

A fourth periodicity belongs alongside the three above: reciprocity-of-response. God's response is structured in kind by the person's posture. The locus classicus is Ps. 18:25–26 (ESV): "With the merciful you show yourself merciful; with the blameless man you show yourself blameless; with the purified you show yourself pure; and with the crooked you make yourself seem tortuous." This is distinct from channel-opening (which is one-directional), from attention-conformity (which shapes the attender rather than the attended), and from threshold (which is binary). It surfaces in the Drawing-Near Reciprocity, Humility-Exaltation, Reciprocal Forgiveness, and Generosity-Provision dynamics. I am naming it as a fourth periodicity rather than reorganizing the table around it; it functions as an additional analytical lens for reading the table.

A possible fifth periodicity — designed-orientation with structural-failure-of-alternatives — is now more sharply posed by the FL.VIII Desire-for-God / FL.XIV Vanity-of-Substitutes pairing produced by the third-axis test. The corpus reserves the fifth-periodicity question for future revision but notes that the paired-law pattern (designed-for-X engine + structural-failure-of-every-non-X engine) may itself constitute the fifth periodicity rather than any single law. See the Third-Axis Test section below for the full treatment.

## The Two Axes

**Axis 1 (the rows — the 'Period' analog): Scale of operation.** Individual → Dyadic/Small Group → Community/Congregation → Generational/Historical → Cosmic/Eschatological. My argument for this axis: almost every law we have found has a version that operates at multiple scales simultaneously, and the laws at each scale form a genuine family — they share mechanism but differ in their unit of analysis, their time constant, and their feedback dynamics. The Obedience Channel law operates in an individual's daily walk; it also operates in a congregation's collective faithfulness over decades; and it arguably operates in a civilization's relationship to revealed truth across centuries. Those are the same law at three different scales, which means the table should show them in the same column.

**Axis 2 (the columns — the 'Group' analog): Dimension of the person primarily addressed.** Spirit → Heart → Soul → Mind/Will → Body/Action → Structural Frame. My argument is that the Formation Documents establish that each dimension has its own developmental logic, its own scriptural vocabulary, and its own relationship to the Spirit's work. Laws that primarily operate in the spirit dimension (Fear of the Lord, Miracle Frame, Prophetic Imagination) share a family resemblance different from laws operating in the heart dimension (Faith Generation, Hearing Development, Community Amplification). The Structural Frame column catches laws that are primarily architectural — they describe the landscape within which the other laws operate.

I considered two alternative arrangements. *Directionality of causation* (God→Human / Human→God / Human→Human) would reveal different patterns, particularly around laws governing divine initiative vs. human cooperation — a theologically important axis I am not capturing here. *Law type* (Structural / Operational / Diagnostic / etc.) would make the existing taxonomy visible, but would reproduce what the Master Law Index already shows, without generating new predictions. I chose Scale and Dimension because they produce the most interesting gaps, which is the test.

A third candidate axis was considered and tested at length in the third-axis test documented later in this chapter: *orientation of pursuit* (God-ward vs. idol-ward), with the first table of the Decalogue (Exod. 20:1–11) as its scriptural anchor. The test found the dimension to be real and operationally important across the table, but the analysis of four candidate forms (entry attribute, systematic Mirror discipline, paired-row treatment, true third axis) concluded that Mirror Discipline at the entry level is the right structural integration for the dimension at the present density of inverse-law content. The two-axis instrument therefore stands; the third dimension is named through the Mirror field rather than promoted to a structural axis. See the Third-Axis Test and Mirror Discipline section below for the full reasoning.

## What the Shape of the Table Reveals

**Period 1 (Individual) is the densest row.** It holds 12 of the 32 confirmed laws (FL.XIV Vanity-of-Substitutes added at P1/GII in this revision). This is not surprising — the investigation began with my own interior life and expanded outward. But it means that individual-level laws are well lit, while community, generational, and cosmic laws remain largely dark — somewhat less dark, with this revision, at Period 4 in the Heart column.

**Group VI (Structural Frame) is the most confirmed column.** We have structural frame laws at Individual, Dyadic, Community, and Cosmic scales — the architecture of the system is becoming visible. The Period 5 / Group VI cell has four entries: TFT, Spiritual Distance, Quantification Program, and the Glory Attractor. This density in the frame column is appropriate. You need the map before you can navigate.

**Period 4 (Generational) remains underdeveloped but less so.** The previous draft held five open unknowns and speculative entries at P4. This revision articulates one of them: the inverse-direction (idol-ward face) of the Open Household Formation Law at P4/GII is now named as FL.XVII Substitution-Cascade. The Household Formation Law itself remains Open pending its full articulation, and the other four P4 Open Unknowns remain Open. Scripture has a great deal to say about generational dynamics — the Deuteronomic narrative, the Abrahamic covenant, the transmission of blessing and curse, Paul's language about Timothy — and the third-axis test established that the orientation-of-pursuit dimension is the operational frame the remaining P4 laws have been waiting for. This is probably still the most practically important period for family formation and long-horizon discipleship.

**Group I (Spirit) has a striking gap at Period 3.** We now have the Fear of the Lord (individual entry condition), the Miracle Frame (cosmic), the Prophetic Imagination and Prayer Resonance (dyadic, the latter confirmed), and FL.XV Hardening Law (scale-invariant P0 channel-blocking law in the Spirit column). But we still have no confirmed law governing how the Spirit operates distinctively through a gathered body (Period 3). The Elijah/Elisha double-portion pattern, the Acts 13 gathered-body discernment moment, the Pentecost pattern — these are pointing at something not yet articulated. The Spirit Anointing Transmission Law at P4/GI also remains Open.

**Group V (Body/Action) now contains FL.XVI Bondage Law** at P0/GV, paired with FL.VI Hear-and-Obey Blessing as its idol-ward counterpart at the same scale-invariant location. The two laws are the same mechanism (sustained obedience as constitutive of the obeyer's state) viewed from two directions of pursuit. Together with the Sin Blockage Law (V2.Exp4) and Forgiveness-Debt Transfer Law (Speculative) at P2/GV — which form an analogous pair at the dyadic scale — the Body/Action column now displays the orientation-of-pursuit pattern across two periods.

**The Heart column (Group II) is the densest column for the orientation-of-pursuit dimension after this revision.** At P1/GII the column now holds FL.III Heart-Throne (which Object the heart enthrones), FL.VIII Desire-for-God (the engine of God-ward longing), FL.XIII Pure-Heart Vision (single-Object orientation as gateway), and the new FL.XIV Vanity-of-Substitutes (structural failure of every alternative engine), with V1.Exp1 Faith Generation and V1.Exp3 Faith-Hope-Love Triad as supporting Reasonably-Inferred entries. At P4/GII the new FL.XVII Substitution-Cascade Law completes the idol-ward face of the territory the still-Open Household Formation Law occupies. The Heart column's density in the orientation dimension is appropriate — orientation of pursuit is by its nature a heart-level dynamic, and the table now reflects that.

**The anomaly entry deserves continued attention.** The Skill Development Law (V2.Exp10, Reasonably Inferred) does not fit cleanly into any single cell — it spans Individual and Community scales simultaneously, because skills develop in individuals, but the conditions for that development are almost entirely community-dependent. In chemistry, hydrogen's ambiguous placement (it could go in Group I with the alkali metals, or Group VII with the halogens) was a clue to its unique properties. The Skill Development Law's ambiguous placement may point to a missing meta-law about how formation systems transfer across *scales* — a law about the interface between individual development and community context.

### PERIOD 0 — Scale-Invariant

**P0/GI · FL.XV (Foundational) — Hardening Law (V), Clearly Taught**

Sustained refusal of God-ward orientation progressively reduces the heart's responsiveness to truth, eventually closing the channel; the closure is incremental, cumulative, and reaches a point past which external intervention is required (Heb. 3:7–15; Prov. 29:1). Asymmetric in time and operating without conscious recognition by the participant in the middle of it. Scale-invariant (P0); operates at individual (Pharaoh, Mark's disciples), community (the wilderness generation), and arguably generational scales. Pairs with V1.Exp6 Obedience Channel as a wider law in the same family. New in v5_6_0_5 from the third-axis test.

***Mirror:*** *The Hardening Law has no Mirror in the orientation-of-pursuit sense; it is itself the idol-ward face of the responsiveness-growth dynamic that V1.Exp6 Obedience Channel and FL.VII Drawing-Near Reciprocity name from the God-ward side. The God-ward face is the sustained responsiveness that grows the heart's capacity to receive (Heb. 3:13 "today" structure; Prov. 4:18 path of the just shining brighter); the idol-ward face is the cumulative-trajectory reduction this law names. Same mechanism, two directions of pursuit.*

**P0/GV · FL.VI (Foundational) — Hear-and-Obey Blessing Law (V), Clearly Taught**

Hearing the word of God and doing it produces blessedness and flourishing of the form proper to the unit operating in the law; hearing without doing forfeits the blessing. Scale-invariant (P0).

***Mirror:*** *FL.XVI Bondage Law at P0/GV is the structural counterpart of FL.VI in the same scale-invariant location: the same obedience-constitutes-state mechanism viewed from the other direction of pursuit. Sustained obedience to God's word produces blessedness; sustained obedience to sin's claim produces bondage (Rom. 6:16). Hearing without doing forfeits the blessing in the FL.VI register and operates as the entry point of the FL.XVI bondage trajectory under sustained refusal.*

**P0/GV · FL.XVI (Foundational) — Bondage Law (B), Clearly Taught**

Sustained obedience to sin constitutes the obeyer as the slave of sin; the obeyer becomes the slave of whatever the obeyer obeys, with the slavery growing in proportion to the duration and intensity of the obeying (Rom. 6:16; John 8:34; 2 Pet. 2:19; Prov. 5:22). The obedience constitutes the slavery rather than producing it as consequence. The bondage compounds across instances; freedom from a master to whom one has been enslaved requires deliverance, transferred obedience to another master, or both (Rom. 6:22). Scale-invariant (P0). New in v5_6_0_5 from the third-axis test.

***Mirror:*** *FL.VI Hear-and-Obey Blessing is the God-ward face of FL.XVI's idol-ward bondage. Same obedience-constitutes-state mechanism; opposite Object of obedience; opposite output (blessing vs. bondage). The two laws together cover the bidirectional whole of the obedience-constitutes-state dynamic at P0/GV.*

**P0/GV · FL.IX (Foundational) — Generosity-Provision Law (B), Clearly Taught**

Generous giving evokes provision toward the giver, in varied forms, with characteristic time delay. Withholding evokes the inverse. Specialization of Sowing-and-Reaping in the material/relational substrate. Scale-invariant (P0); bidirectional in the relational sense (operates both vertically as God's provision to the giver and horizontally as the giver's effect on the receiving community).

***Mirror:*** *The idol-ward face of FL.IX is mammon-as-throne — the love of money operating as a competing sovereignty that closes the generosity channel at its source (Matt. 6:24 "you cannot serve God and money"; 1 Tim. 6:10 the love of money as a root of all kinds of evils; Luke 12:15ff the rich fool's possession-anxiety). The withholding the law names in its negative direction is not merely the absence of generosity; it is the operation of an alternative sovereignty over the same heart that the FL.IX positive direction has invited to operate under God-ward provision.*

**P0/GVI · FL.I (Foundational) — Sowing-and-Reaping Law (B), Clearly Taught**

What a person, community, or generation sows, that unit reaps — in kind, in degree, with characteristic time delay. Scale-invariant; bidirectional in the relational sense. Anchor entry for the Period 0 row.

***Mirror:*** *The orientation-of-pursuit reading of FL.I distinguishes two directions of sowing. Sowing to the Spirit (Gal. 6:8b) reaps the harvest of righteousness (Hos. 10:12) and eternal life; sowing to the flesh (Gal. 6:8a) reaps corruption, with the wind sown reaping the whirlwind (Hos. 8:7) and injustice sown reaping calamity (Prov. 22:8). The Pauline "flesh" names idol-ward orientation in its embodied expression — the heart oriented away from the Spirit's leading toward self-rule. Same mechanism (sowing constitutes reaping in kind); two directions of pursuit (Spirit-ward, flesh-ward); two characteristic outputs (life, corruption).*

### PERIOD 1 — Individual (sole actor)

**P1/GI · V1.Exp5 (Gateway) — Fear of the Lord (V), Clearly Taught**

Logically prior entry condition for the entire Wisdom cluster; without it, apparent wisdom is cleverness from a distorted reference point.

***Mirror:*** *"Fools despise wisdom and instruction" (Prov. 1:7b). Despising the Lord is the gateway-blocking condition: it does not produce a different output; it produces the absence of the Wisdom-cluster output entirely. The orientation reading: fear of the Lord vs. fear of man (Prov. 29:25; Isa. 8:12–13) is the operational orientation distinction. The heart oriented under right reverence of God enters the Wisdom cluster; the heart oriented under fear of any other Object (typically self-protective fear of social consequence) is structurally blocked from the same cluster.*

**P1/GI · FL.VII (Foundational) — Drawing-Near Reciprocity Law (V), Clearly Taught**

Active movement toward God evokes God's movement toward the person; turning away evokes withdrawal. The clearest example of reciprocity-of-response, the fourth periodicity.

***Mirror:*** *Turning away from God produces God's withdrawal — the law's negative direction, named in the same verse as its positive at 2 Chron. 15:2 ("the Lord is with you while you are with him. If you seek him, he will be found by you, but if you forsake him, he will forsake you"). The turning-away is structurally idol-ward: the heart turns toward an alternative Object. The canonical biblical pattern of the prophets — Israel turning to other gods evokes the withdrawal the prophets repeatedly diagnose — is the corporate-scale operation of the same dynamic FL.VII names at the individual scale.*

**P1/GI · FL.X (Foundational) — Ask-Seek-Knock Law (V), Clearly Taught**

Persistent, faith-filled asking in alignment with God's character produces receiving; asking from wrong motive (James 4:3) does not. Threshold form of the prayer mechanism; parent-child pair with the Prayer Resonance Law at P2/GI.

***Mirror:*** *Asking from wrong motive (Jas. 4:3 "to spend it on your passions") is the idol-ward face the law itself names. The request is shaped by the heart's competing Object; the asking does not produce receiving because the asking is in the service of the competing Object rather than in alignment with God's character. Jas. 4:4's immediate sequel ("friendship with the world is enmity with God") names the orientation-of-pursuit dynamic explicitly. The idol-ward face is not non-asking; it is asking-for-idol-ward-ends, which produces non-receiving by the same mechanism FL.X describes.*

**P1/GII · V1.Exp1 — Faith Generation (Hearing Chain) (V), Reasonably Inferred**

Word → Hearing → Faith; genuine willingness to obey activates the chain; disobedience degrades it.

***Mirror:*** *The hearing chain operates wherever sustained attention to a source produces faith in what the source says. Hearing chains directed toward false gospels produce faith in falsehood: 2 Tim. 4:3–4 names the dynamic explicitly (people accumulating teachers to suit their own passions, turning away from listening to truth, wandering off into myths); 2 Thess. 2:11–12 names the divine-side correlate (God sending a strong delusion to those who refused to love the truth). The mechanism is the same Word → Hearing → Faith chain; the input determines the output. The God-ward face is faith generated by hearing the word of Christ (Rom. 10:17); the idol-ward face is faith generated by sustained hearing of any other Object that claims the heart's allegiance.*

**P1/GII · V1.Exp3 — Faith-Hope-Love Triad (V), Reasonably Inferred**

Mutually reinforcing triad: Faith generates Hope; Hope enables Love; Love deepens Faith. Self-reinforcing upward and self-degrading downward.

***Mirror:*** *The triad's idol-ward face is the self-degrading downward direction the law itself names. Misplaced trust corrupts hope; corrupted hope deforms love; deformed love undermines further trust. Jer. 17:5–8 names both directions in a single oracle: the man whose trust is in man, who makes flesh his strength and turns the heart from the Lord, is "like a shrub in the desert" — the inverse of the man whose trust is in the Lord, who is "like a tree planted by water." Same triadic mechanism; opposite directions of trust; opposite outcomes (flourishing vs. withering).*

**P1/GII · FL.III (Foundational) — Heart-Throne Law (B), Clearly Taught**

## Reading the Table Through Classical Doctrine

A reader trained in classical systematic theology may approach this table with a question: where in the table do the canonical doctrines of the faith — the Trinity, the Incarnation, the work of the Holy Spirit, the doctrine of regeneration, justification by faith, ecclesiology, the sacraments, eschatology — appear? Some appear directly. The Miracle Frame at P5/GI carries the structural claim about the relationship between the natural and supernatural orders. Spirit Anointing Transmission, Gathered-Body Discernment, and the Hear-and-Obey Blessing Law touch pneumatology. The Confession-Restoration Law and Forgiveness-Debt Transfer Law operate downstream of the doctrine of justification. The Periodic Table is not, however, a table of doctrines. It is a table of *operational laws* — claims about how the formation territory behaves under specific conditions, expressed in the form a law-statement takes. The Trinity is not a law in this sense; the Trinity is the doctrine of who God is, the precondition of every law in the table being intelligible. The Incarnation is not a law; the Incarnation is the historical-doctrinal event that makes the laws available to the participant Christ now indwells. The table assumes the doctrines and operates within them.

What the table does add, that classical doctrinal organization does not, is the *operational law-form treatment* of phenomena the classical doctrines name without operationalizing. Regeneration is doctrinally located but operationally underdescribed; the Pure-Heart Vision Law (FL.XIII) names the operational structure of the regenerated heart's gateway-condition for the seeing of God. The work of the Holy Spirit is doctrinally rich but operationally distributed; the Prayer Resonance, Prophetic Imagination, Hearing Development, and the new Counter-Resonance laws operationalize the Spirit's work in specific registers. The Communion of Saints is ecclesiologically held but operationally underdescribed; the Container Law, Community Amplification, and the Four Connects operationalize the community's role in the formation work the doctrine names. The table's contribution is the operational law-form, not the doctrinal structure.

Two observations follow. First, classical doctrines that the table does not address directly are not therefore unimportant — they are pre-supposed. A future reader who asks "where is the doctrine of the Trinity in the table?" is asking a category-mismatch question; the right answer is that the doctrine of the Trinity is the precondition for every entry in the table being intelligible, not an entry in the table itself. Second, several entries in the table do operate at the intersection of operational law and classical doctrine, and these intersections are themselves interesting research territory. The Heart-Throne Law (FL.III) intersects with the doctrine of idolatry and with the Reformed treatment of the *cor incurvatum*. The Desire-for-God Law (FL.VIII) intersects with the imago Dei doctrine and the contemplative tradition's *eros* for God. The new Vanity-of-Substitutes Law (FL.XIV) intersects with Augustine's *Confessions* and Pascal's *Pensées*. The new Hardening Law (FL.XV) intersects with the divine-human-agency interplay the major traditions theologize differently. The new Substitution-Cascade Law (FL.XVII) intersects with the federal-headship, social-character-of-sin, inherited-corruption, and ancestral-sin doctrines. The table does not adjudicate among these doctrinal treatments; it states the operational law that the differing doctrines theologize differently, and lets the operational claim stand at the level the differing traditions hold in common.

What is *not* on the table is therefore as important as what is. The table does not contain the doctrine of God; it operates within it. The table does not contain the doctrine of Christ; it operates within it. The table does not contain the work of the Spirit in his metaphysical fullness; it contains operational law-statements about how the Spirit's work shows up in formation. The table does not contain ecclesiology in its full doctrinal form; it contains operational law-statements about how the gathered community shapes formation. The third-axis test added a further "what is not on the table" item: the table does not contain a doctrinal statement of the orientation-of-pursuit dimension; it contains operational law-statements about how orientation operates within the laws the table catches. The Mirror discipline established in v5_6_0_5 makes the orientation dimension consistently visible at the entry level without making it a doctrinal claim in its own right. The reader who treats the table as a substitute for systematic theology will mistake the kind of work the table does; the reader who treats the table as a complement to systematic theology — operational where the systematic is doctrinal — will find the two reinforcing rather than competing.

## The Third-Axis Test and Mirror Discipline (new in v5_6_0_5)

The previous version of this table (v5_6_0_4) closed with the doctrinal-reading section above. The present revision (v5_6_0_5) adds this seventh section to document the third-axis test — the investigation of whether orientation of pursuit (God-ward vs. idol-ward) should be promoted from an entry-level attribute to a structural dimension of the table — and the implementation that followed from the test's findings.

The test was motivated by an observation about the first table of the Decalogue. Exod. 20:1–11 organizes the foundational commandments along a dimension the table did not previously make structurally visible: which Object the heart is oriented toward in pursuit. "You shall have no other gods before me" (20:3) is the first commandment because it names the dimension along which every subsequent law's operation can be inverted. The two great commandments Jesus identifies (Matt. 22:37–40) preserve the same priority structure: love God with the heart's whole orientation, and love neighbor as oneself within that prior orientation. The orientation-of-pursuit dimension is canonically prior, and the table's structure did not make this priority structurally visible.

The test examined four candidate forms for the dimension's integration: (A) entry-level attribute on each law (status quo, with the dimension named in individual entries where it appears); (B) systematic Mirror discipline (every entry carries a Mirror field that names the orientation dimension explicitly); (C) paired-row treatment (each Period would have a paired God-ward row and idol-ward row); (D) true third axis (the table becomes three-dimensional, with the orientation dimension as the third axis). Each form was evaluated against four criteria: scriptural support, operational density of inverse-law content, structural cleanness, and pedagogical clarity for the Formation Companion using the table in practice.

The test concluded that Form B (Systematic Mirror Discipline) is the right structural integration for the dimension at the present density of inverse-law content. Form A is structurally inadequate because the orientation dimension is canonically prior and warrants visible treatment in every entry, not only the ones where the inverse direction has been independently named. Form C and Form D are both prematurely committed structures — they would force every cell of the table to commit to a bidirectional treatment when many of the table's entries are not yet developed enough to support the commitment, and would impose a treatment-pattern that does not match the corpus's actual textual density. Form B is the form that matches the corpus's current state: the orientation dimension is visible on every entry through the Mirror field, but the field's three forms (Mirror statement / Constitutive marker / Orientation-neutral marker) allow the field to carry the orientation reading that fits the specific entry rather than forcing a one-size-fits-all bidirectional commitment.

The test also identified candidate laws whose articulation had been blocked by the absence of the orientation-of-pursuit dimension's explicit treatment. Four cleared the inclusion bar for Foundational-tier addition: the Vanity-of-Substitutes Law at P1/GII (paired with FL.VIII Desire-for-God), the Hardening Law at P0/GI (peer to V1.Exp6 Obedience Channel with wider scope), the Bondage Law at P0/GV (paired with FL.VI Hear-and-Obey Blessing), and the Substitution-Cascade Law at P4/GII (paired with the still-Open Household Formation Law). One additional candidate cleared the inclusion bar for Speculative-tier addition: the Counter-Resonance Law at P2/GI (paired with V1.Exp8 Prayer Resonance). These five new entries are integrated into the table above and into the reference list.

The test produced three further outcomes worth noting. First, the directionality tag (V/H/B/I) is now reserved for relational vector only; the previous draft's (B) tag had been ambiguous between three kinds of symmetry (relational-vector, posture, and orientation-of-pursuit), and the revision separates them — orientation lives in the Mirror field, relational vector lives in the tag. Second, the fifth-periodicity question the FL.VIII Desire-for-God entry had reserved for future revision is now more sharply posed: the paired-law pattern (designed-for-X engine + structural-failure-of-every-non-X engine) may itself constitute the fifth periodicity rather than any single law, and the question's reservation is tightened by the test's findings without being resolved. Third, the test's anchor in the first table of the Decalogue (Exod. 20:1–11) names the scriptural ground for treating orientation-of-pursuit as a canonically prior dimension; future revisions may identify additional law-forms in the second-table register (Exod. 20:12–17) whose orientation-of-pursuit treatment requires further articulation.

The Mirror discipline established in v5_6_0_5 is intended to be permanent. Future revisions of the table that add new entries should add the Mirror field to the entry at the time of addition, in whichever of the three forms (Mirror statement / Constitutive marker / Orientation-neutral marker) fits the entry's content. The field's purpose is to make the orientation dimension consistently visible at the entry level, per the test's recommendation, without committing the table to structural complexity that does not match the corpus's current state. The third axis, in this sense, is now present in the table — not as a structural axis, but as a structural discipline that operates on every entry the table holds.
