# Clearing the Ground: The Lower Levels of Internalization

The full paper — *Clearing the Ground: Cultivating the Heart Through the Lower Levels of Internalization — Receiving, Responding, and Valuing* — is the applied lower-level member of the **Hearing-God Papers** and is available here [(pdf)](source-pdfs/ctg-clearing-the-ground-lower-levels.pdf){: .pdf-popup data-pdf-label="CTG — Clearing the Ground (Lower Levels)" }. The summary below names what the paper does, why it matters to the LotS project, and how it is used across the volumes.

## What CTG Argues

CTG develops the lower half of [CSM](cultivating-spiritual-maturity-in-hearing-god.md) — Receiving, Responding, and Valuing — where the great majority of formation actually happens and where the decisive duty-to-desire threshold lies. Its governing claim is that in the lower levels **formation is mostly *clearing*, not building**: breaking the hard path, deepening the rocky ground, and removing the thorns. The right clearing depends on reading the ground, which the paper takes directly from the IJH corpus's reading of the Sower — the four soils are **four conditions co-present in one heart**, not four kinds of people, which dissolves the developmental-ladder objection and grounds the integration-not-replacement reading. Its lower-level rule, **"match the clearing tool to the soil,"** is the cousin of CSM's "match the intervention to the transition."

The paper's most distinctive borrowing is the IJH account of the thorns as **emotional knots** — "a sustained energetic load on the heart, created by an unresolved wound, an incomplete communication, or a lie believed" — with the weight-versus-sin discrimination (Heb 12:1) and the tool map that matches a clearing practice to each kind of knot. This specifies what CSM could only name: much of what looks like absent desire is desire *obstructed*, so that the watering of the Responding-to-Valuing threshold is, in large part, a clearing of the thorns that choke it.

CTG also extends the map **below** the hard path to a **Level −1** — the condition of those who actively work against not only their own receiving of the word but others' (Matt 23:13; the adversary "active at the point of reception," Mark 4:15). Here the Companion's task is first **discernment** (telling mere hardness from active contest, both misreadings costly) and then, where genuine, **spiritual warfare** (2 Cor 10:4; Eph 6:10–18) **in community** — never solo. Because the enemy "targets the isolatable… the one who has drifted from community" (1 Pet 5:8), the Companion who does this work must themselves be under spiritual direction and inside a Fellowship-of-the-Heart community — not best practice but existential necessity. Throughout, the boundary holds: the tools clear the ground; the Spirit gives the growth.

## How CTG Is Used in the LotS Project

CTG is the cohort-walked, lower-level operational form of the Hearing-God arc, grounded in Volume 2 — the heart-soil diagnostic of [V2.Exp1](../volume-2-knowing-to-doing/exploration-01-heart-soil.md), the emotional-knots and clearing-tools work of [V2.Exp2](../volume-2-knowing-to-doing/exploration-02-emotional-knots.md), the adversary's role and spiritual-warfare framing of [V2.Exp2B](../volume-2-knowing-to-doing/exploration-02b-adversarys-role.md), and the [Four Connects](../volume-2-knowing-to-doing/exploration-05-four-connects.md) — and illustrated by the Fellowship of the Heart's "Getting Started" curriculum, whose stated aim is to bring a cohort "to the threshold of the Level 2-to-Level 3 transition." It is paired with [BFP](bearing-fruit-with-patience-upper-levels.md), which develops the upper levels.
