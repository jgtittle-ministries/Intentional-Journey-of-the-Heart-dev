# Read Me First

The Intentional Journey of the Heart — IJH for short — started as a small group of guys in 2002 trying to figure out, together, what it would actually look like to live the kind of life Jesus described in the Gospels. Not the abridged version. The full version — with miracles as a normal part of life, with hearing God clearly, with the kind of interior transformation Scripture talks about and most of us see only in flashes. We didn't know what we were getting into. Twenty years later, I am still exploring.

What began as a small group has slowly grown into this: six volumes, several academic papers, a governance framework, and a community of practitioners who have been workshopping the material for years. But you don't need any of that to begin. The honest summary in one sentence: I have spent two decades looking for the lawful structure underneath the spiritual life — the way physics has lawful structure — and writing down what I have found, what I am still finding, and what is still uncertain. The **Laws of God's Love**, as I have come to call them, are the subject. The **journey of the heart** is what happens when a person tries to live by them. This work is the record of both.

I wrote it, first of all, for my kids and grandkids — a father's notes on what he has come to see. If that is you, welcome. If you found your way here some other way, welcome too: take what is useful and leave the rest.

One honest word about posture before you go further. I am not asking you to accept any of this on my authority. I am asking you to test it — in your own life, in your own small group, in conversation with people you trust and with your own pastor — and to bring back what you find. Where I am sure of something, I say so; where I am only guessing, I say that too. The point of the whole thing was never a system to master. It was a Person to know. The laws are only ever meant to bring you closer to Him.

A few things this work is *not*, because the difference matters. It is **not a systematic theology** — for that I would point you to Grudem; this is one person's exploration of how a few spiritual things actually work, not comprehensive doctrine. It is **not a denominational program** — I write from a mix of Reformed, Evangelical, Charismatic, and contemplative streams, and it belongs to none of them. And it is **not finished**, and never will be, because we serve an infinite God who is always doing more than we can ask or think; the work is versioned, and Volume 6 exists so it can keep growing after I am gone.

- If you have come tired — dry, in a hard season, unsure God is even near — read **[If You Come Weary, Read This First](introduction/if-you-come-weary-read-this-first.md)** before anything else. Nothing here was ever meant to measure how loved you are.
- Otherwise, just keep going. The next few pages tell the story simply: **[where this came from](introduction/founders-genesis-how-this-work-came-together.md)**, **[the life I went looking for — and found](introduction/introduction-what-i-am-trying-to-do.md)**, and **[the one picture that holds it all: the Country of the Heart](introduction/how-to-read-this-book-the-map.md)**.
