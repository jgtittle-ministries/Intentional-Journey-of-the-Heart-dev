# The Complete Causal-Loop Set: How the Laws Move Together

*A modeling companion to the Seventh Exploration. The qualitative loop structure is well-supported; the claim to a complete set is a research proposal, offered at Speculative tier.*

The [Seventh Exploration](exploration-07-systems-dynamics-as-modeling-language.md) named three feedback loops — the Faith-Obedience engine, the Knot-Bandwidth trap, and the Community Amplification loop — and said plainly that there are "likely additional loops not yet identified." This companion takes up that invitation and asks what a *complete* set would look like. If the [Role Atlas](the-role-atlas-where-each-law-lives.md) maps *where* each law sits in the field, this maps *how the whole system moves* through it — the dynamic complement to the static map. (Systems Dynamics, as the Seventh Exploration put it, is "the dynamic description of how systems move through" the manifold the field theory describes.)

A complete set is not a pile of diagrams. It is a **layered library** sitting under a handful of rules — and a single picture can hold the whole of it:

## The Six Rules the Whole Set Obeys

This is the real value of drawing the whole set at once — it reveals what the laws, taken one at a time, do not.

1. **Shadow-pairs are the same loop with the polarity flipped.** Vol 1's "Held Tensions / Shadow Pairs" structure is, in this language, a fact about feedback: Hear-and-Obey ↔ Bondage, Drawing-Near ↔ Hardening, true-Worship ↔ idol-Worship, Generosity ↔ Hoarding, Humility ↔ Pride — each is *one* loop drawn twice, sign-reversed. The set is **bipolar**, not doubled.
2. **The heart's throne (FL.III) is the master switch.** What sits enthroned sets the sign of *every* vertical loop at once. Repentance and cleansing (FL.II, FL.XLI) flip the switch, and the whole bank reverses from vicious to virtuous. One node governs the lot.
3. **G is exogenous — outside every loop.** God's free self-giving is never a variable inside the feedback structure; it is the input that can override it (a miracle injects regardless of loop state; the dark night withholds felt nearness while the loops still run). This is "there is no mechanism; there is a Father," drawn in systems notation — exactly the Seventh Exploration's warning that a systems graph "cannot show the Hand."
4. **Substrate laws are loop-*gains*, not loops.** Trust-Substrate (FL.XXXV), the Fear of the Lord (Exp 5), and the Pure Heart (FL.XIII) do not cycle — they set *how strongly* each loop responds. (The Role Atlas calls the same laws "the landscape.")
5. **Delays are load-bearing.** The obey-then-receive lag, the slow creep of hardening, the third-and-fourth-generation delay — these are *why* people misread cause and effect, and *why* the dry season gets misread as failure.
6. **Suffering is the swing-input.** FL.XXII: the same input produces character-and-hope *or* bitterness, depending on the switch — the Seventh Exploration's barrier-crossing, "down before deeper-down."

## The Engine at the Center

Every other loop feeds, or is fed by, one core engine: the Faith-Obedience loop of the First and Sixth Explorations.

![The Faith-Obedience engine as a causal loop diagram: Word of Christ to Hearing to Faith/Trust to Obedience to Revelation and back, every link positive, forming a reinforcing loop; a time delay sits on the Obedience-to-Revelation link; God's free self-giving feeds the loop from outside; and the heart's throne is the switch that, flipped idol-ward, reverses the same loop into the Hardening/Bondage spiral.](images/cld-engine.svg)

*The engine. A reinforcing loop with a delay, an exogenous Giver, and a switch that can run it either way.*

Read it as a single reinforcing loop: the Word is heard with a will to obey; hearing builds faith; faith issues in obedience; obedience opens the channel for the next word — and round again, each turn opening the channel wider. Two things keep it honest. A **delay** sits between obeying and the next revelation (which is why the link is so often missed). And the loop has a **dark twin**: flip the throne idol-ward and the very same arrows reverse — disobedience clogs the channel, hardening reduces hearing, faith thins, and the spiral runs down instead of up (FL.XV Hardening, FL.XVI Bondage). The engine and its shadow are not two laws; they are one loop with the sign reversed.

## What the Map Buys Us

Three things, beyond the pleasure of seeing the whole.

**Leverage points become obvious.** Two kinds of intervention actually move the system. The first is the **throne switch** — one flip (repentance, cleansing) reverses an entire bank of loops at once, which is why conversion changes everything downstream and not just one habit. The second is the **external-input breaker**: a balancing *trap* (the emotional knot, the bitter root, the calcified tradition) cannot free itself from inside the loop — it takes an input from outside the degraded channel (the Spirit speaking truth into the knot, the Matt. 18 process into the offense, living re-engagement into the dead form). Knowing which loops are traps tells you where effort is wasted and where it lands.

**It is testable.** This set is precisely the structure the Seventh Exploration's open trail proposes to simulate — operationalized stocks, measured flow rates, identified delays, validated against longitudinal community data (the Vensim / REVEAL proposal). The map is the model you would build.

**And it has a hard limit, drawn right into it.** Because G sits outside every loop, no amount of loop-tracing predicts what God will freely do. The map sketches how His world *tends* to run; it never measures how loved you are, and it cannot show the Hand. A believer perfectly aligned on every loop, before a God who freely withholds for a season, is not a broken mechanism — there is no mechanism. There is a Father.

**Proposed framing (Speculative — a research-grade extension of V3.Exp 7): The Foundational Laws can be assembled into a single, layered causal-loop library — a core Faith-Obedience engine coupled to vertical, interior, horizontal, and generational loop-families, all nested toward the Glory attractor. The library obeys six structural rules: shadow-pairs are sign-flipped loops; the heart's throne is the master polarity switch; God's free self-giving (G) is exogenous to every loop; substrate laws are loop-gains rather than loops; delays decouple cause from visible effect; and suffering is the swing-input whose effect depends on the switch. The set is the dynamic complement to the field-map of the Role Atlas, and the structure the volume's quantitative program would simulate.**

**Certainty: Reasonably Inferred (the structure) / Speculative (its completeness).** *The qualitative loop shapes are well-supported by scripture and by the Seventh Exploration's already-stated three loops, and the six rules restate patterns the matured catalog already holds (the Shadow-Pair discipline, the Role Atlas's field-roles, the force equation's leading G). The claim that these layers and rules constitute a* complete *set is a hypothesis — there are likely loops and couplings still unnamed — and the quantitative simulation remains a research proposal, not an achievement.*
