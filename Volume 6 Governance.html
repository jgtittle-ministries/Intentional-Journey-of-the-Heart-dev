Governance.

Where Volumes 1–5 are my personal record of what I believe the Lord has shown me, Volume 6 is how this work is stewarded, extended, refined, and, where necessary, corrected by others. What follows first is the governance for this stage of the project, in a few pages. Then the Council itself, the Succession Letter, and the Council's public record. A fuller governance model for a larger season is kept in the reference section at the end.

I wrote Volume 6 in a more institutional voice on purpose. It is meant to outlast me and serve people who will come to this work long after I am gone. The Council of Stewards it describes is how this record stays honest once it is no longer just me keeping it honest.

The work belongs to Christ. I have been its steward, not its owner.
